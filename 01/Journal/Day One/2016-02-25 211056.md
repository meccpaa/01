Stupid things I did
There are actually many stupid I did in my life. 

If I really want to be a better man, I have to control myself

What is stupid? To want to get the things in a wrong way, which I won't get it in such a way
E.g. In my birthday last year, I walked lonely on the bridge, then there is a guy from California fitness invited me to have a look on their fitness center. 

Since it was the 2nd time he asked me, I thought just take a walk is fine. So I enter. 

I didn't aware that it was one of the costliest decision I ever made which have zero value to me. Or even negative value. 

A woman brought me along to introduce the center and then she was supposed me that I would join them, but in reality I never had such intention. 

Firstly, a guy as me to join the membership. 
It is a few hundred a month. For 5 years. 

Well I actually don have the interests to going to the gym room.  I just want to be free and don't want to limited by time and location to do exercise. It was just not my type to do any exercise in gym room. 

The only reason I could think of was to build up my core muscle to run faster and more durable. 

But still there are just so many other better way that's suitable to me.

I am okay with every thing. So I just accepted that. And signed the contract and pay the pre- installment. 

Then they ask me to do muscle building. 

Well, I just want to train myself. No need for anyone bothering me. 

But then I was persuaded. 

If I don't really decide myself, all my life would just be random decision made my the other. And that would never be my life. 

I have to cut my low lever decision out of 
- stupid fear, cowardice, lust, food, money

What I really want in my deepest heart I had always know and it would never be gotten in a wrong way.
