# 圓明圓

![](ac3d5b9f61d80d916cdfeb50fed94e54.jpeg)

We intended to visit Great Wall, but missed the train, so we took a detour to here. It is a Garden for the Emperor, but destroyed in Ching Dynasty by foreign countries invasion.

We walked around and took a lot of photos.

This garden has a lot of variety, could take days to visit. We just browse through. Nearby is the universities. But after the visit, it was already turned dark. 

So we left around 5-6pm, and had dinner at a noodle restaurant near our hotel.
