After a long rest due to COVID, finally I went for hiking with Kammy to Clear Water Bay Area. Haven’t been to there for a long while. The weather was good. We started around 10am. We walked from Hang Hou, through High Junk Hill, then passed the turnaround. Walked along the road beside the sea.

We saw a wide sea, it was beautiful. We had lunch there. It was comfortable sitting there. There is a 古蹟. And 4-5 people fishing. On the sea, some people were playing boat.

After a short break, we started our journey. It was already very close, only take around 5 more minutes, we arrived the entrance to the trail.
