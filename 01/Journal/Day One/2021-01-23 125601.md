# Saturday hike

![](e0931b3eea6c81ab9629d6b208d09837.png)

In the morning, we walked from the hotel to Sum Tseng to have breakfast at 陳記粉麵. It has good milk tea. Price is economic. Around $35 for breakfast. I had egg and sausage, bread plus macaroni. As usual, I drank lemon water. 

The seats were full, we waited for a short time. 

After that we bought 2 breads nearby. 

We went to toilet before the hike.
