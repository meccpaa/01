# The process for admission to listing on the Main Board [[QP]]

![](e04861957a742c56d45c63168f6d74c4.jpeg)

1. The company decides to make an application for listing. This will include an assessment of how much capital it wants to raise from the share issue and how any of its existing shares will be made available for sale to the public.
2. The company appoints a sponsor (and reporting accountants and legal advisers).
3. The prospectus is prepared and checked for accuracy and completeness. It includes an accountant's report and may also include a profit forecast. Several drafts of the prospectus are prepared until the final version is completed.
4. Other documentation is prepared, including a formal application for listing.

An underwriting agreement is prepared and underwriting is negotiated with underwriters. All documentation and agreements are reviewed and approved by the company’s board of directors.

5. The application for listing is made to the Listing Committee of the Exchange (by the sponsor on behalf of the company).
6. The Listing Committee approves the application and the admission of the shares to listing. It also approves the prospectus, and the company must then file a copy with the Companies Registry.
7. The prospectus is published, together with application forms for investors to apply to buy shares in the issue.
8. The applications from investors are processed and shares are allocated to the applicants.
9. Dealing in the shares begins on the SEHK.

For a new applicant this process usually takes about six to twelve months
