# Consignment arrangement

common in the motor industry

- the dealer does not obtain control of the cars at the point of delivery indication include:
- the product is controlled by the seller (manufacturer) until a specified event occurs
- the seller (manufacturer) can require the return of the product or transfer it to another party
- the customer (dealer) does not have an unconditional obligation to pay for the product
