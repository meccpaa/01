# Role of Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Ltd (HKEx)

The HKEx and its subsidiary bodies, is responsible for the listing of securities both on the main board and GEM. It sets out the requirements and procedures for listing, including pre-listing requirements and post-listing requirements.
