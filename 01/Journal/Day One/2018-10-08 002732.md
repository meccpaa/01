# CFA Code of Conduct [[QP]]

The SFC seeks to promote professional and ethical business conduct among corporate finance advisers in Hong Kong, via the CFA Code of Conduct

Corporate finance advisers engaging in corporate finance advisory work under

- the Listing Rules,
- the Takeovers Code or
- the Share buy-backs Code are required to observe the specific requirements under the respective codes and rules as regards their conduct.

It is not statutory but members are required to consider the spirit as well as the letter of the Code
