# Step 4: allocate transaction price to performance obligations [[QP]]

Proportion to the individual stand-alone selling price attached to each performance obligation

If a stand-alone selling price is not directly observable —> estimate using a suitable method
(a) adjusted market approach
(b) expected cost plus margin approach
(c) residual approach - appropriate only if (i) price is not discernible, or (ii) has not yet established a price
