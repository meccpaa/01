# Hiking to see 吊鐘花
It’s 9:49am. I’m on minibus 12 to Sai Kung. I’m listening the story from Kindle. Audio book. I have read many books in the past. But little spent on listening. It made my reading is better than listening. I shall improve in listening.

Today hiking was to see 吊鐘花. So we went to 大枕蓋. The beginning path is the same as before we went 2 years ago. i.e. from Lee Tomb to the trail. However, we found many people already in the bus, and in the sites. So we made some detour during the hike. We found a secret area where we can appreciate the flowers quietly. 

Just that during the process, I accidentally got a grass probe into my eye. Hope there are no big issue.

![](5e202b199ea0640e7d3e3e00cdf5bb06.jpeg)

After appreciating the flowers, we made a detour. We didn’t go to 鯽魚湖, but went to the other side (east), and then go north. We passed an areas with burnt grass, then toward 北潭凹 starting point direction. We could see 赤徑 from the far. 


![](e2bd2cd0c630f0866d489f4c8fc452fc.jpeg)

We also saw a number of 吊鐘花 in the trail. Not very difficult to walk. 
We left at the camping site of 北潭凹. Then took 94 bus back to Sai Kung pier. We had lunch there at 4:30pm. I ate 黑椒牛柳絲炒意粉. Other than too much black pepper, it was a nice dish. 

Tired after the hike. Slept after dinner. Going to sleep again now.
