# HKEx regulation and reporting requirements [[QP]]

There are a large number of reporting requirements in respect of listing on the HKEx. There are two major areas that affect CPAs; the accountants' report and pro forma financial information required in relation to listing, and the continuing obligations to provide reports of interest to investors.
