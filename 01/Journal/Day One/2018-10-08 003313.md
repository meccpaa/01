# Role of the SFC

The SFC maintains and promotes the fairness, efficiency, competitiveness, transparency and orderliness of the securities industry.

- The SFC has responsibility for supervision of merger and takeover activity involving listed companies.

Its Corporate Finance Executive deals with any matters referred to it concerning a takeover or merger, or buy-back of shares by a company, and makes rulings on the matter in accordance with the Codes on Takeovers and Mergers and Share Buy-Backs('the Codes').
