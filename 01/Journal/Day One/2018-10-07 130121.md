**Objective of the auditor** [[QP]]

`HKSA 315 (Revised 2016)`  states that the ​**objective of the auditor**​ is to identify and assess the risks of material misstatement whether due to fraud or error, at the financial statement and assertion levels, through understanding the entity and its environment, including the entity's internal control, thereby providing a basis for designing and implementing responses to the assessed risks of material misstatement.
