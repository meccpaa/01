# Hiking from Pak Tam Au to Hoi Ha
星期五晚上工作直至零晨3:45am. 終於可以專心完成OTT SG TB 2020, can provide it to auditor.
I got a lots of disturbance during the week. Only until the weekend I could start on it. It was not as difficult as expected. 

Since I expected today weather was good, so I asked Kammy to go hiking somewhere. 
When I woke up this morning, the first trail came to my view was to 雞麻峒 https://www.oasistrek.com/kai_ma_tung.php.

![](47072a139f9a2f434fec9043790463e8.jpeg)

*Saw a boar when I heading to the minibus station.*

I went out at around 11am. Took no. 1A minibus at Cheng Lan Shui. The trip including 25min walking was around 45 min.

The weather as expected was beautiful.

![](eb1e8058d755d6365dcaee15ef2a20d4.jpeg)

We took no. 94 bus to Pak Tam Au, then through Cheung Sheung, to the route we intend to go this time. The new route include
- 石屋山 > 蛇石坳 › 雞麻峒 › 白角仔 › 荔枝莊 › 南山洞 › 白沙澳

![](6cf9dac2bde7c6a0e1b23b2d3348a0bc.jpeg)

I like this tree at Cheung Sheung. It is big in this type of tree.

However, we took the wrong route. Could only reached 石屋山. Then we went to Hoi Ha when we left the mountain.

![](3257df5afdbfd7d3eb7e10b4f45a9411.jpeg)

We took some photos at the helicopter site at 石屋山. It is beautiful. The sky was blue.

Although we didn’t go to the places we intended to go, the view at Hoi Ha was beautiful. It is not contaminated by Human. Water was very clean and clear.

![](54394891f508064cd7a11b7f1c3d8f4b.jpeg)

We had a tea at a cafe called 好景茶座. The foods were not very good. But it was the only choice. It is good enough. 

After a walk at the seafront, we took minibus no. 7 to Sai Kung Pier and then went home separately.
