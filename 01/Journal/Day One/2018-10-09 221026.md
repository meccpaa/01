# Accounting treatment - principal and agent

Principal - @ goods / services are transferred to the customer and is measured as the “gross amount” of consideration expected to be entitled and received from the customer

Agent - @ arranging for goods/ services to be provided by another party
Measured at the “fee or commission” that the seller expects to be entitled to in exchange for arranging for the provision of goods / services to the customer
