# Contract [[QP]]

Contract: an agreement between ≥ 2 parties that creates enforceable rights and obligation

Contract asset: an entity's right to consideration in exchange fo G/S that the entity has transferred to a customer when that right is conditioned on something other than the passage of time

Contract liability: an entity's obligation to transfer G/S to a customer for which the entity has received consideration (or the amount is due) from the customer
