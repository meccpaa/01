To make my life more interesting. 

These days, months, years, I have been reading many many manga. Seem every time I got bored at home, I would turn into manga. If I have finished old one, then I would keep on finding new one, until I found one interesting. 

From the beginning, **one piece, Naruto, Bleach, Touch, H2, History Strongest Disciple, Beelzebub, the world only god know, Gamaran, Minimum, Detective Conan, Yamada-kun to 7 Nin no Majo, Fairy Tail, Sankarea, Cage of Eden, Minamoto-kun Monogatari, Ippo, ...**

If I continue, it would be much more than that. But is it worth to spend wo much time in it? It seems that I found my life was boring, so I just keep seeking the life of others, the life in the virtual world. To get the excitement. To escape from where I belong. 

It has been my conclusion for a long time. I want to make my life more interesting instead. However, I would turn back to manga again and again. 

I know that if I do not focus on what I can do to make a difference, I would not be able to change my life to what I really want to be in my deepest heart. 

It was painful to get the pity from the others, even more painful not to able to help or protect the ones I love, make them worry. 

I really want to be stronger, to be the strongest. I determine to focus on it. To grasp every moment of my life again.
