# Substantive procedures, including analytical procedures

![](3e458fa969725eb2514b500b006b1ac0.jpeg)

Substantive procedures are designed to ensure that the balances in the financial statements are not materially misstated, that is, to detect whether there are any material errors in the financial statements which have not been prevented by the entity's internal controls. Auditors should consider whether they should use analytical procedures as substantive testing or use only tests of details or even a combination of the two.
