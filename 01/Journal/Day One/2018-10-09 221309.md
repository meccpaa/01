# Standard warranties or Extended warranties [[QP]]

![](93d8145b9707a9d0295d7260ffd3d4f3.jpeg)

- standard warranty: provides assurance that a product will function as intended (often for a specified period of coverage) - HKAS 37
- additional (or extended) warranty - separate performance obligation - HKFRS 15

HKFRS 15 -  factors to be considered:

1. legal requirements
2. longer period of warranty
3. nature of the tasks promised - function as intended?
