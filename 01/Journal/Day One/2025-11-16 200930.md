# Wish comes true
A few months ago, I rejioned Achievers and saw a photo shared by Kathy in our club social group. It reminded me of a wish I had when I was young... (show the photo)
 
My wish was to look older... (sigh)
 
The photo was taken 17 years ago... I was there. 

The photo not only expose my age, but also made me depressed...
 
Because when I returned to our meeting, many members said that ... I look the same...

What's more, when I looked at our club flag at the entrance, and compare it 18 years ago, it has become a seasoned toastmaster flag. Getting more wrinkle and wearing more the ribbons on it,...  leaving trace of time that I can't even compare with. 
 
I was depressed and reflected on what I have done wrong ... and summarize into 3 points.  I hope that my speech can also be beneficial to you on making your wish come true.
 
My first mistake was,
 
- my wish was just a wish crossing my mind, no actionable plan. Just like new year resolutions. 
 
- so I checked what is the cause of aging ... it is basically because of the damage of our cells that cannot be repaired.
 
... understanding the science, I can take appropriate actions...
 
That lead me to find my second mistake...
 
all the suggestions I got, is opposite to what I have been doing. For examples:
 
- ​**A diet high in processed foods, sugar, and unhealthy fats**​,... I am not particularly interested in eating unnecessary foods, ... 
- ​**drinking exceesive alcohol**​ - my favorite drink are water... and tea... 
- **Less Sleep** - just like my computer, I can be turned into sleeping mode in a minute.
- **loneliness and social isolation** ... I just joined achievers...  achievers are never lacking of connection - can I abandon achievers? of course not, I just paid my membership fee, ...
 
all are in **conflict** with my current goals, and interests.
 
Achieving something new is difficult, but as an achiever I believe ... every cloud has a silver lining ...
 
That lead me to my 3rd point. If there are something that are in conflict with my current goals/ interest, there could be something the are a​++**ligned with (in harmony) my current goals / interests. So that I do not need to sacrifice something and even achieve 2 goals at once.**++​ 
 
- I am interested in woman, ... I can go to lan kwai fong, drinking alcohol and flirting with woman and staying late...
- that's a promising and attractive goal ... 2 birds, one stone. the problem is I don't know how to flirt with women...
- ​++**So I need to Break it down, do it progressively ...**++​
- Join a toastmaster club, learn to have eye contact, smile and speak confidently and fluently, learn to praise people adequately...
- ....

In conclusion, actionable plan, take action, eliminate the conflicts. Start easy and step by step to achieve the ultimate goal. 

I believe I will look older one day... and I hope my reflection can also help you to make your wishes come true.
