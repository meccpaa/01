Farewell

Perhaps because of my past experience, I am either too sensitive or too indifferent.

I have been hiding my true feeling all the time. 

Long long ago, to now.

If I could choose, I would love a person who would show her truth self, rather than someone like me.

It does not matter whether the others know my feeling or not, what matter most is what I really want to do.

It does not matter if she understand me or not, what matter is I wish her all the best. Just that simple.
