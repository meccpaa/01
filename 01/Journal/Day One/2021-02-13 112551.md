# Hiking in Tai Po
Taking taxi now to the waiting point. I think it would cost around $200. But want to save time. We would take a new route today. 

UM5609 is the car no. its final cost is $180.

The weather turned out to be nice today. I was worried that it would be cloudy.

We went to Hung Shek Mun and Ma Tao Fung. I took my Lumix camera with me. But I use “raw” file format. It took a lots of space, so only could take a few photos. It is better to use it to do zooming.

Below are on the way to Hung Shek Mun. We turned left when we reached the dangerous sign, after 觀音峒. Then view when going down were beautiful. Sky and ocean were blue. The ocean can reflect the image of the sky.
