My Trailwalker advice
Just a few points if you want to be faster:

1. It is preferable to keep walking. No matter you are drinking or eating.

No matter how slow it is. It would be better than stop. As it takes time to restart the engine. And starting too fast would get injury easier.

Don't stay and eat at any check point.

It would be a big achievement itself if you can keep walking 100km.

2. If you couldn't walk in fast pace from the beginning to the end. You better not run until the last section.

I tried in my training in my first year, since that time my IT Band was hurt. I tried different ways not to induce the pain and still keep walking. I found that keep walking itself is fast enough to complete in 24hr.

3. Keep focus and feel every part of your body. All the muscle, breathing and pace should be in sync with rhythm.

Also feel the environment and make good use of it esp the gravity.

4. Make each training a little more challenging than the last one. And the purpose of training is to get stronger instead of getting faster. As getting faster in one training doesn't necessarily mean you would be faster next time.

So on training, don't choose the easier way. And there may be some technique to help you save energy, e.g. Smaller step when walking uphill. You may apply it on the Day, but you could choose to stride a larger step in your training.
