# Cooking red rice
Of course! Cooking red rice congee (porridge) is easy, but achieving a soft, creamy texture requires one crucial step because red rice has a tougher, fibrous bran layer than white rice.
Here is a detailed guide on how to cook perfect red rice congee.
### **The Golden Rule: Soak First!**
Unlike white rice, **red MUST be soaked** before cooking to soften the grains and reduce the cooking time. Soak for at least **4 hours**, or ideally **overnight**.
* * *
### **Method 2: Rice Cooker (Easiest & Hands-Off)**
This is the most convenient method for a worry-free meal.
**Instructions:**
1. **Soak:** Don't skip this step, even for a pressure cooker. Soak the red rice as described above.

2. **Combine:** Place the drained rice, water, and any add-ins (like dates or sweet potato) into the inner pot of your appliance.

3. mix 1/5 white rice for better texture

4. **Cook:**

    - **Standard Rice Cooker:** Use the "Porridge" or "Congee" setting. If it doesn't have one, use the regular "Cook" setting and let it run for a full cycle. Check the consistency afterward; you may need to run a second short cycle.

    

5. **Release Pressure:** Once cooking is complete, allow the pressure to **release naturally (NPR)** for at least 10-15 minutes before turning the valve. This is key for a creamy texture.

6. **Season:** Open the lid, stir well, and season as desired.


* * *
### **Pro Tips for Success**
- **Toppings are Key:**

    - **Sweet:** Fresh fruit, nuts, seeds, a splash of coconut milk.

    - **Savory:** Sliced green onions, fresh cilantro, crispy fried shallots, a soft-boiled egg, or shredded chicken.

    

- **Water Ratio:** The ratio is flexible. **1:8** is a standard starting point, but adjust to your preferred thickness. Remember, it will continue to thicken as it cools.
