Hiking - Ching Shan
It was the first time I went to this mountain. Originally, thought, it was a well-developed place, not much surprise. And at the beginning, only saw temples and asphalt roads. After 15 min walk, we got closer to the slope.

I went ahead first to reach the peak to see how it look like there.

![](341515a0a05bed8428ac9f63cefce109.jpeg)

it was beautiful. Saw many mountains at the top. I was a bit hungry, but we still continue to walk, we want to find another way to leave. They way we used is similar to what I found from the web http://www.walkonhill.com/route.php?area=4&seq=6

After I reached the peak, I came back and saw her struggling to climbing up.
Well, in fact, all the way to the peak was climbing stairs. 550m. It wasn’t easy for one who didn’t walk much. It was how she look like when she arrived the peak.

When she was taking rest, I walked around and discovered the other ways to left. So I got back took some photos and grabed her. The other way turn out to be playful, interesting to hike. It is a bit challenging for some people, but overall is save, as there were not much sharp cliff. 

We continue to walk for another 3 hours. leaving around 2 pm.

Had lunch at Saizeriya Italian Restaurant in a shopping mall of Yeun Long and then took bus to Tsuen Wan and then back home with 290.
