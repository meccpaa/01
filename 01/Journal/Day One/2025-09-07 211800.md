# Long vacation
Do you like vacation?
How long was your longest vacation?

I will share something about me in 3 of my longest vacation, and explain why I rejoin Achievers.

---

My first vacation lasted for ​==a year==​, ...

Self-study - University Entrance exam

many years ago, I took my ==entrance exam== to the university.. I wasn't satisfied with my performance , so retook it. Self-study for a year.

- a long vacation before my university study
- at that time I felt that my body was getting weaker.

Here was my ​**==plan==**​ to deal with both...
- study 12 hours a day. 
- everytime when I finished a chapter or a target, I would do 30 - 50 push up, 30 sit up, while doing it, sum up what I have learnt.
- daily, would go running / hiking for 5km to breathe some fresh air, to have an overall conclusion what I have learnt, or solve the problem that I couldn't solve.
- the exercise seem not balance enough to me, I took the opportunity to pursue one of my childhood dreams, to learn Chinese Kung Fu... the one that I learnt is popular --- called Tai Chi. I always found it to be beautiful and sophisticated.

I searched from the web and found master Eddy. Learnt from him once a week for a year and practice it twice a day. 

After a year, the result of the exam was fine. I got a ​==stronger body==​ and entered into an university.

HKICPA exam.

my next long vacation was started around 10 years later... spanned for 9 years. last for around 160 days. 

This time, I was really travelling, and ​==travelled a lots==​. 

It was started in an early morning, sunny day, I packed my luggage, heading to the airport. 

I travelled further away from the airport, not to another country, but to the next mtr station ​==asia world expo==​. 

I unpacked My luggage filled with the studying materials for certified accounting exam... 

I am living in TKO, it took me 3 hours to travel back and fro. ... the exam conducted every half year ... 

it took me 9 years 18 attempts ... to reach the final destination, passed all the papers...

The total travelling time was 18 x 3 hours = 54 hours ...I checked deepseek..  if I were taking plane with the same time it was enough to travel around the world. 

It was really a long long travel and vacation. I learnt one crucial thing from this vacation -- I don't like exam.

Half year ago, I finally got some leisure time, 

I decided to pursue my childhood dream again - I ​==reached master Eddy==​. Texted him I wanted to learn tai chi again. To my delighted, he is still alive ... and teaching. I found that after 20 years of practice, I can still find areas of improvement and still like it. 

It made me thought that if I can ==practice== tai chi for more than 20 years, can learn to control my body from head to toe, 
why not practice more before speaking on stage ? they both require practice and body control. 

I left Achievers 6 years ago,  because my office relocated away from HK Island, it is not convenient to get here.
