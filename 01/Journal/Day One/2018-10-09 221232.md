# Loyalty scheme - Options for additional goods and services

![](5a5644ac0f98972f0f09e60951df9ab1.jpeg)

free or at discounted price, material right

- a separate performance obligation

proportion of revenue deferred and recognised as revenue at the earlier of:

- the date on which the future G/S are provided, or
- the date on which the option to acquire the additional G/S expires

the estimate adjusted for:

- any discount that the customer could receive without exercising the option, and
- the likelihood that the option will be exercised
