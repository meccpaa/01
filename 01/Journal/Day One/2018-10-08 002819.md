# The board of directors [[QP]]

- accountable to its shareholders
- Has the duty
	- to present a true account of the company’s financial performance through the periodic reporting system and
	- to ensure effective internal control systems are put in place in the company
- Chairman
- Executive directors
- Non-executive directors (No legal distinction between executive directors and non-executive directors.)
	- one of the roles: monitor executive pay levels
	- 
- INEDs:
	- provides shareholders and stakeholders with greater assurance that the business strategies adopted and the decisions made by the board are likely to have been premised on independent views
	- Apart from directors’ fees and shareholdings, independent of management and free from any business or other relationships which could materially interfere with the exercise of the independent judgment
	- Roles:
		- (1) Participate in formulating strategy of the company;
		- (2) Oversee the company's management and check the company has established a proper and effective internal monitoring system;
		- (3) Take independent decisions, based on all available information, and not influenced by any private interests;
		- (4) Protect the interests of all shareholders

Listing Rules: requires companies listed on the Main Board to have

- ≥3 INEDs (≥1 have appropriate professional qualifications or accounting or related financial management expertise)
- audit committee (comprising NEDs only, ≥3 members, ≥1 hold a relevant qualification)
