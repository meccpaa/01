# Bill and hold arrangements [[QP]]

customer is billed for a product but it is physically retained by the seller until it's transferred to the customer in the future.

recognise the revenue --> control has passed, ALL the following criteria:

1. the reason for the bill and hold must be substantive (e.g. requested by the customer)
2. the product must be identified as belonging to the customer
3. the product must be ready for physical transfer to the customer
4. the entity can not use the product or transfer it to another customer

the seller should consider whether to allocate a proportion of the transaction price to the provision of a "storage service".
