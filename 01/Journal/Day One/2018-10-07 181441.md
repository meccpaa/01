# Reasons for listing shares [[QP]]

![](fb0fa24021061c436b6d6af62ee24a7c.jpeg)

Following advantages:

(a) Access to capital for growth with opportunities to raise funds both at the time of listing and at later stages.

(b) Broader shareholder base could potentially lead to a more liquid market in the trading of the company's shares.

(c) Employee incentive and commitment resulting from the grant of employee share options to tie in the company's key staff.

(d) Higher profile and visibility in the market could generate reassurance among the company's customers and suppliers.

(e) Increased corporate transparency could lead to the grant of credit lines on more competitive terms from the company's bankers.

(f) Greater efficiency resulting from rigorous disclosure standards demanded of listed issuers will lead to an improvement in their control, management information and operating systems.
