# Royalties [[QP]]

Consideration in a contract to license IP may be a royalty
That is measured by reference to “sales or usage”

Recognized revenue when the later of the following events occurs:

- the subsequent sale or usage arises
- The performance obligation to which some or all the sales or usage based royalty has been allocated has been satisfied (or partially satisfied)
