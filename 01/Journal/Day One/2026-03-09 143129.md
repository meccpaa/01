# I am not a woman
2 weeks ago, I heard a table topic response from our new member, Victor, saying, he was not a funny guy, couldn't give funny question. 

This statement inspire me that ==i am not a woman, no wonder I couldn't get a girlfriend.== 

==It was long ago, when I were not a woman, and not even a man, but an innocent boy,== 
I thought everything were simple. 

in the first year of secondary school, I read a manga, called - "Touch", it is a story baseball. It had a **training menu** - including doing push up 50 times, sit up 50 times. twice a day. I didn't play baseball, but as a boy I did arm wrestling with classmates and interested in bodybuilding. So I tried the training menu, I do it when I was free, normally during advertising period when I was watching TV. That year PE class final test was ​**fitness test**​. Two of the tests were doing push up and sit up in 15 seconds... I did 30 times push up .... so I came first in the year.

In the next year, I watched a TV programme, it taught about painting. It amazed me how a **beautiful painting** can be created so effortlessly. I felt for that. For every assignment in art and design class in school, I would try all the skills I learnt from the TV, and from books borrowed from library. I forgot about time, do it from day to night, just absorbed in it, made many drafts before final pieces. ... so I got the first place in the year.

In the following year, I ​**felt for a girl**​. I thought about her from day to night.  ​==When I saw her back, wanted to see her front; when I saw her external appearance, wanted to see her in... inner heart.==​ ==That year.., I got a “good student” remark from her, and literally meant I was rejected.== "I hated to be good student."

​++**"If I were a woman, I might know more about her ... and get her heart "**++​ 

==But if I were a woman, I probably would not be interested in a woman.==

It made me thought, it is ​**the same interest**​, I didn't think my interest in a girl would be less than that of sport or art. So what are the difference?

When I compare the difference, it seems that I **Focused on the wrong thing.**  For sport or art, I'm interested in the process, as much as even more than the result; but for a relationship, I was trapped that it was all or nothing. My fear of failure stop me from taking any concrete action. It had no process at all, just ending. ... rarely speak, and even eye contact ... without action, there is no feedback, and no improvement. 

How can I overcome my fear? ​==After being an==​ ​**==accountant==**​​==, I understand more about cost and benefit. I found that being rejected was a good thing.==​ 
  1.   it meant that she can live happier without me. In a relationship, I do wish a person has a happy life;
  2.   ​==it costs me nothing==​. I do not need to spend time on her anymore. I can focus on ==being a better person, getting a better girlfriend...==
==it is a win-win situation.==

Fellow toastmaster and guests, I'm not a woman, nor a good speaker. But it would not stop me from getting a girlfriend or being a better speaker.

Because I like that or want to do that. And would do something on that. 

How about you?
