# Investment Rules (Warren Buffet)
Rule 1: if the Business Does Well, The Stocks Eventually Follows
Rule 2 : Most News is Noise, Not News!
Rule 3 : When you buy a stock, Plan to hold it forever
Rule 4 : Only listen to those you know and trust
Rule 5 : Understand what you are investing in
Rule 6 : Be fearful when others are greedy and be greedy when others are fearful
Rule 7 : Buy at price below intrinsic value
