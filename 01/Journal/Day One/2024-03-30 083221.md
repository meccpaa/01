# The best tool for note taking
It seems to me that no device is better than paper. Which is flexible. I can draw picture, write Chinese, turn back to previous page to compare current writing. Could be colorful. More memorable. 

But the drawback is that it’s not searchable and find what I write in the future. And slower. 

Today I want to keep writing to summarize what is in my mind. I found that what I speak is lacking of substance. 

我其實想用中文寫更多 ，因為這是的母語，更真切的表達我的想法 。如果可以的話，我希望用普通話拼音輸入法，因為可以學習更多。

要定下來好好想想 。不要總是填滿每一分一秒 。
