Time goes, life flow
There are many different people in the world. They have different character, experience, and choice in their life. 

However, we share the same destiny in our life. We have limited time and would be old and death one day. 

When are not sure when would be our last day. What would happen in the next moment. That make life interesting. That should make us focus on the current moment. 

I don't really hate anyone because of what they do. They just make their own choice in their limit lifetime and want to choose their best solution. 

Anyone might be wrong, or do we have the standard correct one solution? 

In my life, I just want to get stronger and much stronger. 

To connect the dots: design the ways to learn or accumulated useful information or better ways of learning. Personally, corporately, academically, relationship.  

If we can keep on improving on something that really matter, then all of us, and our environment, shall be better and better. 

There must be ways to make our life much better. 

Technologically and human interaction. No matter what it is, I want to find the better solution. A better product. 

That's my goal.
