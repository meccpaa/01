# Hiking at Sai Kung North
Started from M3 through 嶂上, 石屋山 (which has very nice view of South China Sea, Sai Kung !). Then to 蛇石. In the path, we did many climbing. But it was worthwhile.

![](52f1b738adc5bc3aa3f89da2f57cbf56.jpeg)

![](c6130a06a17476ca7a5f503b603d0ed6.jpeg)

After that we turn right and go to 荔枝莊. At the seashore, we saw some special rocks there. It started raining again. To be safe, we didn’t go to the path to 蛇石 thru 雞麻峒。

![](e713df9d8e38a2c60569206548424267.jpeg)

![](d0132a1053704f5e19bd2255defbe9df.jpeg)

So we return to 蛇石through the main road. It wasn’t long. Took around 1.5 hours. Then we walk along the path to 深涌，to 榕樹澳，then to 水浪窩. Found that my mobile still didn’t work… We took 99 bus to Ma On Shan, then have McDonald as dinner and then I took 89D bus to Kwun Tong and 98A to home.

It was cold at night. I took a hot bath. Felt much better.
