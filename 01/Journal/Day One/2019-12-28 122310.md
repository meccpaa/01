# Heading back to Hong Kong

![](c0f625b5e219042bc6a45ea36602901d.jpeg)

We woke up around 6:30 in the morning for breakfast, then took train back to HK.
In the MTR station, we couldn’t use the ‘Octopus’, because only RMB2 left in each of our card.

And when we were passing through the checking gate, security found a knife in Kammy’s bag, so it was confiscated by the ‘Police’’.

Other than these, everything went smoothly.

We arrived HK around 7pm, and had dinner at Kowloon Station. 

HK was warm and humid comparing to Beijing!
