To have a prepared mind
Is not just about thinking but action
Only when I am able to do everything for the purposes, I would have a prepared mind.

For **strength**, it means
- rest enough, choose the right foods, correct posture, 
- adequate exercise - muscle, stretch, running, hiking, balance, focused mind, tai chi

To be strong so that I could **focus** on things that really matter and would will not be sick

For **work**, it means 
- pass QP this year
- achieve something **visible and sustainable** for the company. 

To do things that really **matter**. Protect the environment, connect the dots
