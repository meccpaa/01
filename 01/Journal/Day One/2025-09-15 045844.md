Doubt,
Difficulty is not at the beginning , but from how to continue, and be better
Lingering fear, expectation -> work , don’t run away, fun, different aspects

recently, I'm taking leaves to learn more about myself.
what I want? where do I want to go? how can I move on.

I found that I have doubt all the times.
But if that's the direction I decided to go, no matter how much doubt I  have. I shall go for it. 
My heart will not stop questioning myself... similarly the world could also questioning me...
when I were on stage, ... look at the face... make it fluent, so that I can feel what it is like. If I'm too busy to deliver my speech, then I would miss the feeling. The feeling is important, to allow me to improve it in the next stage time.

When can i have a peaceful mind? 
- as long as I don't satisfy with the current state, as long as I explore more,
- I shall always face the doubt.
- but I can't stop because of the past failure or because I have never done it.
- who do I want to be? 
- as long as I'm alive, I shall not give up.
- to live the life that I naturally want to be.
