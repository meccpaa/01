# Performance obligation satisfied over a period of time [[QP]]

Reasonably measure the outcome of a performance obligation

- early stages of a contract: not always possible —> recognized to the extent of costs incurred that are expected to be recovered

—> until the outcome can be reasonably measured —> based on progress

- input methods: proportion of costs incurred, labour hours or machine hours worked or time elapsed
- Output methods: the number of units produced, work certified or contract milestones

Wasted materials - do not contribute towards meeting a performance obligation —> ignored when measuring progress towards satisfaction of the performance obligation
