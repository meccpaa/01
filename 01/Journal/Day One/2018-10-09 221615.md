# Step 5: Recognize revenue when performance obligations are satisfied [[QP]]

When a G/S is transferred to the customer i.e. when the customer obtains control of the asset

Control is the ability to direct the use of, and obtain substantially all of the remaining benefits from, an asset.

Depending on the performance obligation

- at a single point in time /
- over a period of time
