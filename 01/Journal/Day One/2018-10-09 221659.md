# Allocation of discount [[QP]]

Total price for the bundle is less than the sum of individual prices of each of the items included in the bundle —> allocated proportionately to all performance obligations

— Unless there is observable evidence that the discount only relates to certain performance obligation - ALL the following conditions are met:

- regularly sells each distinct G/S in the contract on a stand-alone basis’
- Regularly sells on a stand-alone basis a bundle of some of those distinct G/S at discount
- The discount attributable to each bundle of G/S is substantially the same as the discount in the contract and an analysis of the G/S in each bundle provides observable evidence of the performance obligation to which the entire discount in the contract belongs
