# Petty cash
1. Introduction            
 
	Petty Cash is used for payment of expenses of a minor and non-recurring nature.  Normally, they are not exceeding HK$500 per transaction. The Manager concerned should consider whether payment could be made by a more favourable method such as on credit terms through purchase orders, if the aggregate amount payable is far beyond the aforesaid amount.  
 
	Items should NOT be paid by Petty Cash include: -
 
a) Staff allowances
b) Mobile invoice
c) PCCW / HKT invoice
d) Business gift or business entertainment such as lunch / dinner / drinks with client. (Refer to Entertainment Policy in HR homepage)
e) Roadshow payment
f) Cash advance 
g) Octopus add value
h) Office Equipment such as Desktop PC / Notebook / Monitor / Microsoft Software / Copier / Printer etc (Refer to GSP Office Equipment Policy)
i) Purchase with credit term i.e. payment can be made via cheque afterwards
j) Balance Sheet items such as Capital Expenditure & Liability (Account codes starting from 0XXXXX to 3XXXXX)
k) Inter-Company settlement (Account codes starting with 78XXXX)
		 
2. Establishing and Changing Petty Cash Float
 
	The VP is responsible for the control of the petty cash.  However, he/she can delegate the custody of the petty cash to a staff member as a float holder who can be entrusted to hold cash or VP can select the float holder not to hold cash (i.e. $0 cash float).
	 
	The respective VP or above signs and sends an User Access Application Form for Petty Cash to the Payments Unit when a petty cash float has to be established, increased, cancelled or transferred if necessary.  
	 
	In general, all approved petty cash in the system on / before Friday will be reimbursed to the float folder on / before next Friday. 
 
	It is the responsibility of the respective Manager to ensure that the float in his/her area is operated in compliance with the guidelines contained herein and the Authorization Grid of the group. 
	 
	 
3. Collection of Cash and Reimbursements
 
	a)     Petty cash float in respect of new float or increase of float will be arranged payable by cheque and then sent to the petty cash float holder through internal mail. For cancellation of float, the responsible Manager must ensure the Petty Cash float to be returned to the company by cheque in a timely manner.    
 
a) All petty cash reimbursements will be paid directly into cash float holder’s designated bank account by Autopay. A bank notification will be sent to the cash float holder via email upon Autopay done.   
	 
	c)    The cut-off time of Autopay for petty cash reimbursement is at 17:00 hours on every Friday.  If it falls on a holiday, the preceding working day will be taken as cut-off day.  Autopay will be made on or before the coming Friday. 
	 
		To confirm whether your petty cash reimbursement has been approved for processing for Autopay or not, simply login the PCCW Internet Expenses (Petty Cash) system and open Expenses Home.  The following statuses denote the current status of each expense report.
		 
· Ready for Payment  - already approved 
· Pending Manager Approval – pending for approval
· Rejected – having been rejected by respective approver
· Paid – reimbursement made
· Pending System Administrator Action – system rejected the claim automatically due to excess HKD2,000.00
4. Safe Custody
 
	Overnight cash should be held under lock.
5. Reimbursement and Supporting Documents
 
	To reduce your effort and time in inputting data and also computer storage space and processing time, it is recommended that petty cash expenses of similar nature are to be grouped together as far as possible for data input, and float holder is required to input petty cash nature / details in description field.
 
	After inputting of expense data into the Petty Cash System, the Float Holder can submit the expense reports to approver for approval.  The petty cash expense reports must be approved by the appropriate level of authority On-Line before being submitted for replenishment.
	 
	Please also note that each petty cash claim must not exceed HK$2,000.  Deviation from this limit will result rejection of the expense report concerned even approved by approver. 
	In order to ensure that all petty cash expenses are included in the General Ledger in a timely manner, users are requested to complete the expense reports before month end cut-off time.
6. Expenses Control and Handling Petty Cash Expense Supporting Documents
 
	It is a very important principle that employees at all level must have their expenses signed and approved by their supervisor.  Employees cannot approve their own expense form.
	 
	The Float Holder must ensure that all supporting invoices/receipts are duly reviewed and approved by the appropriate level of authority.  He/she should stamp "PAID” with date on each supporting document after it has been paid to the staff.
	 
	Individual sections should then file the documents, which make up a Voucher Claim Form, with the approved Petty Cash Expense Report copy in voucher number order.  These must be kept for at least 2 years to cater for any audit review.
7. Month End Closing
 
	Due to tight month-end closing time table, all petty cash expense vouchers intended to be included in the current month General Ledger must be input and approved in the system at least 2 working days before the month end.
