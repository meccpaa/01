# SBP Example Modification cash-settled becomes equity-settled
On 1 January 20X6 Park Constable Co (CPC) grants 250 share appreciation rights (SARs) that will be settled in cash to each of 100 employees on the condition that employees remain employed for the next four years.

On 31 December 20X6 CPC estimates that the fair value of each SAR is $20. On 31 December 20X7 the estimated fair value of each SAR is $22.

On 31 December 20X7 CPC cancels the SARs and, in their place, grants 250 share options to each employee on the condition that each employee remains in its employ for the next two years. On this date the fair value of each share option is $23.20. All of the employees are expected to and ultimately do provide the required service.

Required

How should CPC recognise the share-based payment scheme in each of the years ended 31 December 20X6 to 20X9?
