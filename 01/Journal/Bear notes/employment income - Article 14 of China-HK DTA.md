# employment income - Article 14 of China-HK DTA
[[Double taxation arrangement - HK, China - 183 days]]

Pursuant to Article 14 of China-HK DTA, employment income derived by a **resident** of One Side is **taxable** in ~that Side~ ::unless:: the **employment** is ~exercised in the Other Side~. Remuneration derived by resident of One Side in respect of employment exercised in the Other Side will be ::exempt:: from tax in the Other Side provided that:

(a) the taxpayer stays in the Other Side for a period or periods ~not exceeding~ in the aggregate 183 days in any 12-month period commencing or ending in the taxable period concerned; and

(b) the remuneration is ~paid by or on behalf~ of an employer who is not a resident of the Other Side; and

(c) the remuneration is ~not borne by~ a [[permanent establishment]] which the employer has in Other Side.
