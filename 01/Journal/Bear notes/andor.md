# and/or
### Interpretation:
When a section contains a list, it is necessary to determine whether the series end with an 'and' or an 'or'. If there is an 'and' before the last item in the series, all the elements or conditions listed must be met or be present for the provision to apply.

If there is an 'or', only one element in the series must exist in order for the provision to apply.
- - - -
### Example
S.41(1) of the IRO:
Subject to subsection (1A), an individual:
(a) of or above the age of 18 years, or under that age if both his or her parents are dead; and
(b) who is or, if he or she is married, whose spouse is either a permanent or temporary resident, may elect for personal assessment...
