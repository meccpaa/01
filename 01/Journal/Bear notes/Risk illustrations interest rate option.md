# Risk illustrations: interest rate option
Zuma has US$20 million of borrowings at a floating rate, US$ base rate + 0.75%, with a threemonth rollover. The treasurer is considering hedging the interest rate for the period starting on the next rollover date and has been offered a cap at 10% interest for a premium cost of 1% per annum payable quarterly in arrears.
