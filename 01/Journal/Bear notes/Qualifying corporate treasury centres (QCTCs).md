# Qualifying corporate treasury centres (QCTCs)
A CTC qualifies as a 'qualifying corporate treasury centre' ('QCTC') if (ss.14D(2) and (3)):

(a) It is a [[standalone entity]] carrying out one or more of the following [[corporate treasury activities]] in Hong Kong:
	* (i) Carrying on an [[intra-group financing business]];
	* (ii) Providing [[corporate treasury services]] to a [[non-Hong Kong associated corporation]];
	* (iii) Entering into [[corporate treasury transaction]]s with a [[non-Hong Kong associated corporation]]; or
(b) It satisfies the specified [[safe harbour rule under s.14E]], i.e. not lower than 75% of its profits and assets are corporate treasury profits and assets; or
(c) It has obtained the CIR's determination that it is a QCTC under s.14F(1).

The CTC must also meet the **business substance requirements** in s.14D(5):
(a) The central management and control of the CTC must be exercised in Hong Kong (the central management and control requirement).
(b) The activities that produce the qualifying profits must be either
	* (i) Carried out in Hong Kong by the CTC; or
	* (ii) Arranged by the CTC to be carried out in Hong Kong (the substantial activity requirement).

S.14D(9) specifies that a FI is not eligible to be a QCTC.
