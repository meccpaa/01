## 3.3.2 Subsequent measurement of the lease liability
The lease liability is subsequently:
1. ~Decreased~ in each period of the lease term by **payments** made by the lessee to the lessor
2. ~Increased~ in each period of the lease term by **interest**, calculated at a ~constant rate~ on the outstanding balance (being the interest rate used to discount the lease payments).
