# Lease Example 5: variable payments linked to an index
On 1 January 20X8, Knowles Crowther Co (KCC) entered into a lease agreement to obtain use of an asset for 4 years. Lease payments are required annually in advance; the first payment on 1 January 20X8 is $10,000 and payments in future years increase by the consumer price index (CPI) for the previous 12 months. The CPI at the start of the lease is 102 and the interest rate implicit in the lease is 5%.

Required

How is the lease liability measured?

- - - -
Solution

At the start of the lease the liability is measured based on the current CPI i.e. at annual lease payments of $10,000.
