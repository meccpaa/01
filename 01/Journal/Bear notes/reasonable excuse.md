# reasonable excuse
In D13/85, the Board of Review described the concept of '**reasonable excuse**' as follows:

Reasonable excuse is what one would expect a reasonable person to do in all of the circumstances. A reasonable person is not a perfect person, but an average person using the reasonable skill and care in handling his tax affairs which one would expect to see from such an average person.
