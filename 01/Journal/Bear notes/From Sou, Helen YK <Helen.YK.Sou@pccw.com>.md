For the T&C that issue on Direct IO,I had clarified with Adrain.  In terms of payment, basically,
 
Whoever signs the IO is liable to pay us. If advertiser signs, it has to pay us. If agency signs, it has to pay us (regardless whether the advertiser behind pays the agency or not).
If BOTH advertiser and agency signs, both or each of them has to pay us. That’s why in Clause 12.1 it says “the Advertiser and Advertising Agency shall be jointly and severally liable for the payment of Fees”. This means that if they both sign, but the advertiser goes bankrupt, we can chase the agency, and vice versa.
