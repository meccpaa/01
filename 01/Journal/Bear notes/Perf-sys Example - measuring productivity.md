# Perf-sys Example - measuring productivity
A manufacturing company produces a single item of product. Actual results for a particular month were as follows:
![](Perf-sys%20Example%20-%20measuring%20productivity/1D3E685E-045B-4217-BC29-96659766B92C.png)
Value-added processing time is the amount of time spent producing the goods (and so producing value).

Manufacturing productivity in the period will be measured as the volume of good output per labour hour worked.

So productivity = (3,269,520/130,780) = 25 units per hour The productivity ratio can be analysed into three elements:

(1) Manufacturing cycle efficiency = (Value added processing time/Total hours worked) × 100% In this example: (81,738/130,780) × 100% = 62.5%

(2) Process productivity = (Total units produced, including faulty units/ Value added processing time) In this example: (3,420,000/81,738) = 41.841 units per value-added hour

(3) Process quality yield = (Good units produced/Total units produced, including faulty units) × 100% In this example: (3,269,520/3,420,000) × 100% = 95.6% Manufacturing cycle efficiency × Process productivity × Process quality yield = Manufacturing productivity In this example: 62.5% × 41.841 × 95.6% = 25 units per hour Productivity overall was 25 units per hour. This was because 41.841 units in total were produced in each value added hour, but only 62.5% of hours worked were value added hours and due to faulty production, good output was only 95.6% of total output produced.
