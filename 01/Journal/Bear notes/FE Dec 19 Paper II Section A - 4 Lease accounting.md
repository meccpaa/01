# FE Dec 19 Paper II Section A - 4 Lease accounting
**You are Mr Joe Wong, the Finance Manager of the Group.**
**(16 marks)**

**Write a memo to advise Mr Paul Lee, the Finance Director, on the accounting treatment of the transaction entered into with PIHKL in relation to the Fo Tan Premise upon the lease commencement date with reference to the applicable accounting standards.**
Marks will be awarded for appropriateness of the form and presentation of the memo.

## Answer
To: Paul Lee, Finance Director
From: JoeWong,FinanceManager
Date: dd / mm / yyyy
Subject: Accounting treatment of transaction entered with PIHKL 

In accordance with HKFRS 16, the transaction entered with PIHKL is a sale-and-leaseback transaction since CHL (the seller-lessee) transfers the Fo Tan Premise to PIHKL (buyer- lessor) and leases the Fo Tan Premise back from PIHKL. 

Since the control of Fo Tan Premise would be transferred to PIHKL, the transfer of the Fo Tan Premise shall be accounted for as a sale of the asset on 1 February 2019. 

Since the consideration of HK$16.5 million is higher than the fair value, the Group shall make the adjustment to make the sales proceed at fair value and the above-market terms (excess sale price of HK$1.5 million) is accounted for as additional financing provided by PIHKL to CHL. 

Right-of-use asset of HK$1.2 million arising from the leaseback is recognised at the proportion of the previous carrying amount of Fo Tan Premise that relates to the right of use retained by CHL [HK$9 million (carrying value) / HK$15 million (fair value) x HK$2 million (present value of the lease payments – excess sale price)] and depreciated over five years. 

Furthermore, the carrying amount of the building of HK$9,000,000 would be derecognised and CHL shall recognise only the amount of gain of HK$5.2 million that relates to the rights transferred to PIHKL in the profit or loss [HK$6 million – (HK$6 million (gain on disposal HK$15 million – HK$9 million) x HK$2 million / HK$15 million)] = HK$5.2 million upon the control of the building is transferred to PIHKL on 1 February 2019. 

Financial liabilities of HK$3.5 million, representing the present value of the lease payments would be recognised, of which HK$1.5 million relates to the additional financing and HK$2 million relating to the lease and measured at amortised costs using the effective interest rate method.
