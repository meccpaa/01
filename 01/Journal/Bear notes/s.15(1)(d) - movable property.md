# s.15(1)(d) - movable property
Sums received by or accrued to a person by way of hire, rental or similar charges for the use of or right to use movable property in Hong Kong.
- - - -
Hire or rental received for the use of or right to use movable property in Hong Kong.

There is no specifically identifiable assessable profit, which must be ascertained as a question of fact. Failing an ascertainment of the true profit arising in Hong Kong, s.21 provides that it may be computed on a fair percentage of the receipt.
