# 3.6 Monitoring
`HKSA 220.23`
The audit engagement partner is required to take account of the results of monitoring the firm's quality control systems and consider whether they have any impact on the specific audit to which he is assigned. The engagement partner considers:
	- Whether deficiencies noted in that information may affect the audit engagement; and
	- Whether the measures the firm took to rectify the situation are sufficient in the context of that audit.
