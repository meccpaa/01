# FE Dec 19 Paper I Section A - 4(b) Exchange rate risks
(b) With respect of the Group’s expansion plan in Singapore, (i) explain the need to manage the exchange rate risk in response to Ms Gabriella Fung’s concern; (ii) assume that the commission income in Singapore is SGD5million, the Group plans to commence the operation in the next 12 months. Determine the gain or loss on the one-year forward contract related to the commission income, one year from now, in Hong Kong Dollar based on the information available below:
	* the current spot exchange rate is HKD/SGD 0.1725.
	* the one-year forward exchange rate quoted by a bank is HKD/SGD 0.1628.
	* the spot exchange rate for one year from now is HKD/SGD 0.1933.
	(5 marks)
- - - -
## Answer
From the Group’s perspective, managing exchange rate risk is necessary because the **cost involved in hedging the exchange rate movement is not very substantial compare with its benefits**. Also, in the short run, it can ~mitigate the risk of financial distress and bankruptcy~. In the medium and long run, it can ~enhance the sustainability of the business and maintain a relative stable cash flow~. Given the **high volatility** of the HKD/SGD exchange in the past year, it is necessary for the Group to manage the exchange rate risk from the uncertainty of the exchange rate movement and ~reduce the risk~ to the Group. 

The gain on the one-year forward contract 
= SGD5 million / 0.1628 – SGD5 million / 0.1933 = HKD30.7125 million – HKD25.8665 million
= HKD4.846 million
