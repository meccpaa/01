# Section 61 – Certain transactions and dispositions to be disregarded
Pursuant to s.61, where an assessor is of the opinion that:

(a) Any transaction which reduces or would reduce the amount of tax payable by any person is [[artificial]] or [[fictitious]], or that

(b) Any disposition is not in fact given effect to;

he may disregard any such transaction or disposition and the person concerned shall be assessed accordingly.

Commercial realism can be one of the considerations for deciding artificiality (see Cheung Wah Keung v CIR [(2002) HKRC 90-116]), and it is necessary to scrutinise the terms of the particular transaction to be impugned and the circumstances in which it was made and carried out.

DIPN 15 (Revised) provides guidance on the application of s.61.
