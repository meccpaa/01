# SIT: overdue fees
By allowing fees to remain overdue, the professional accountant runs the risk of ::effectively offering a loan:: to an entity. The professional accountant may then run the risk of being in breach of the rules set out above.

Appropriate **safeguards** include an independent review to ensure that the ~fees charged are fair~ for the work performed. Policies should be in place to ensure that unpaid fees do not build up to unfeasibly high levels. Unpaid fees should be discussed with the entity's senior management promptly. If the fees ~remain unpaid~ then the firm should consider **resignation**.
