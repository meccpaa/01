# amount specified in Schedule 3B of the IRO
The maximum annual tax deduction for mandatory contributions to MPF schemes specified in Schedule 3B of the IRO:
- $12,000 for the years of assessment 2001/02 to 2011/12 (inclusive) 
- $14,500 for the year of assessment 2012/13 
- $15,000 for the year of assessment 2013/14 
- $17,500 for the year of assessment 2014/15 
- $18,000 for the year of assessment 2015/16 and onwards
