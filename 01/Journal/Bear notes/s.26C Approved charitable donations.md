# s.26C: Approved charitable donations
Approved charitable donations made by the taxpayer or his/her spouse are allowable for salaries tax if the aggregate amount is:

(a) Not less than $100; and
(b) Not exceeding **35% of assessable income** minus allowable outgoings and expenses minus depreciation allowances; and
(c) Not allowable as a deduction under profits tax.

*10% for years of assessment prior to 2003/04; 25% for 2003/04 to 2007/08; and 35% for 2008/09 onwards.

The recipient organisations must be **charitable institutions approved by the IRD** and **exempt under s.88 of the IRO**. Otherwise, the donations made would not be deductible for salaries tax purpose. Furthermore, the basic criteria governing the granting of the salaries tax deduction are:

- The payment must be a donation 
- The donation must be a **donation of money** 
- The donation must be for **charitable purposes** 
- A deduction in respect of the same donation **cannot be granted to more than one person**

The term 'donation' is not defined in the IRO. However, the IRD has taken the view that the ordinary meaning of 'donation' is **'gift'** (CIR v Sanford Yung (1977) 1 HKTC 959). To constitute a 'gift', property must have been ~transferred voluntarily and no material advantages or benefits should have been received by the transferor.~

DIPN No. 37 (Revised) provides guidance on the concessionary deduction for approved charitable donations under salaries tax.
