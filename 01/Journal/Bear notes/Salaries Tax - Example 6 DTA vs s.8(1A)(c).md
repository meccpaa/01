# Salaries Tax - Example 6: DTA vs s.8(1A)(c)
Example 6: Mr. Y

Mr. Y (a single person not entitled to any concessionary deductions) is under a Hong Kong employment. He is entitled to a monthly salary of $30,000 (i.e. annual salary $360,000).

During the year ended 31 March 2017 (i.e. the year of assessment 2016/17), he spent eight months (from 1 April 2016 to 30 November 2016) in the Mainland.

In the absence of any tax relief, the Hong Kong salaries tax payable of Mr. Y for the year of assessment 2016/17 in Hong Kong will be computed as follows:

[($360,000 – $132,000 (basic allowance) – $120,000)] x 17% + $8,400 = $26,760

Tax reduction for 2016/17 = $20,000 (capped) 
Net tax payable for 2016/17 = $(26,760 – 20,000) = $6,760 
Effective salaries tax rate in Hong Kong: 6,760/360,000 = 1.88% The 
PRC individual income tax ('IIT') Mr. Y paid is computed as follows:
	- Assume the exchange rate is $1 = RMB0.8 
	- $30,000 x 0.8 = RMB24,000 
	- RMB24,000 – RMB4,800 (monthly standard deduction) = RMB19,200 

Monthly PRC Individual Income Tax paid will be computed as follows:
	- = RMB[1,500 x 3% + 3,000 x 10% + 4,500 x 20% + (19,200 – 9,000) x 25%] 
	- = RMB3,795 

Total PRC IIT payable for the 8 month's services in the PRC = RMB3,795 x 8 = RMB30,360 

Total PRC IIT payable for the 8 months' service in the PRC = RMB30,360 x 0.8 = $37,950 

Gross income subject to PRC IIT = $30,000 x 8 months = $240,000 

Net income after PRC IIT = $240,000 – $37,950 = $202,050 

Tax credit limit for PRC IIT paid:
($202,050 x 1/(1 –1.88%)) – $202,050 = $205,921 – $202,050 
= $3,871 

PRC IIT paid not allowed as tax credit = $37,950 – $3,871 = $34,079 

**With tax relief under s.8(1A)(c)**
