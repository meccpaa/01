# Profits tax concession to qualifying aircraft lessors
On 10 March 2017, the Inland Revenue (Amendment) (No.2) Bill 2017 was gazetted which provides profits tax concession to qualifying aircraft lessors and qualifying aircraft leasing managers. This will strengthen the development of offshore aircraft leasing activities in Hong Kong and enhance the status of Hong Kong as an international aviation hub. The Bill includes relevant anti-avoidance provisions to ensure that the proposal is consistent with the international standards to combat BEPS.

Refer to chapter 3 for details.
