# Responses to the assessed risks of material misstatement - accounting estimates
`HKSA 540.12`
An auditor's point estimate or auditor's range is the amount, or range of amounts respectively, developed by the auditor in evaluating management's point estimate.

A management's point estimate is the amount selected by management for recognition or disclosure in the financial statements as an accounting estimate.

This is arguably the core of HKSA 540. The auditor must either:
`HKSA 540.18`
- 1.5.1 [[Obtain evidence from subsequent events (up to the date of the auditor's report)]]; 
- 1.5.2 [[Test how management made its estimate]]; or 
- 1.5.3 [[Develop an auditor's estimate ('auditor's point estimate’)]]
