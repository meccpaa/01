# s.20AD loss sustained by a tax-exempt offshore fund
Profits from the specified transactions are exempt from tax. As a matter of symmetry, losses sustained by a tax-exempt offshore fund from the specified transactions in a year of assessment are not available for set off against any of its assessable profits for any subsequent years of assessment (s.20AD and DIPN 43 (Revised), para 41).
