# Revenue Example Variable consideration (1)
In January 20X6 College Properties Ltd (CPL) enters into a contract to build an extension to an existing warehouse for an agreed fee of 40 million. The contract terms require completion by 30 September 20X6. The price will decrease by $100,000 for each day after this that the project remains incomplete. At the year-end of 30 June 20X6, CPL expect that there is a 75% chance of the project being completed on time, a 15% chance of it being completed a day late, a 6% chance of it being completed two days late and a 4% chance of it being completed three days late.

Required

What is the transaction price?

Solution

The consideration is variable due to the price concession, i.e. the fact that CPL will accept an amount that is less than the price stated in the contract if the project overruns.

Here the calculation of transaction price is based on expected values.
