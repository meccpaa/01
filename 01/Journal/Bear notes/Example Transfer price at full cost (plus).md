# Example: Transfer price at full cost (plus)
In the previous example, if transfers are priced at cost plus a margin of 25%, A's sales to B would be $7,500 (= $12,000 x 50% x 1.25).

![](Example%20Transfer%20price%20at%20full%20cost%20(plus)/0AABA8B4-C0A3-4838-BCF1-76BA6E8B7BE9.png)

Compared to a transfer price at cost, A gains some profit at the expense of B. However, A makes a bigger profit on external sales in this case because the profit mark-up of 25% is less than the profit mark-up on open market sales. The choice of 25% as a profit mark-up was arbitrary and unrelated to external market conditions.
