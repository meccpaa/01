# exchanges of assets
### Initial measurement
Fair value is used to measure **the cost of the intangible asset acquired** if one intangible asset is exchanged for another unless:

(a) The exchange lacks commercial substance; or
(b) The fair values of both the asset received and the asset given up cannot be measured reliably.

In such cases, the cost of the intangible asset acquired is measured at the carrying amount of the asset given up.
