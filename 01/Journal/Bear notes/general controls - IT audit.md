# general controls - IT audit
As we saw in Chapter 11 Section 4, the overall audit objective in reviewing the general controls is to ensure that the controls and procedures are adequate to provide secure, effective and efficient day-to-day operation of the computer facilities.

In auditing general controls, the auditor verifies that:
(a) There is **segregation of duties** to reduce the risk of employee fraud or limitation of the scope of authority of any individual
(b) There are **comprehensive written standards** and any ~changes~ are appropriately documented
(c) ~Access~ to and use of computer terminals is properly **authorised**

Remember, [[application controls]] may be useless when [[general controls]] are ineffective. Therefore, most auditors will review the design of general IT controls first, before reviewing the application controls.

## Audit risk assessment procedures
### Development of computer applications
* Inspecting standards over system design, programming and documentation
* Performing full testing procedures using test data
* Observing approval by computer users and management
* Observing performance of procedures for evidence of segregation of duties
* Observing and inspecting documentation regarding installation procedures 
* Inspecting training documentation

### Prevention or detection of unauthorised changes to programs
* Observing performance of procedures for evidence of segregation of duties 
* Inspecting records of program changes 
* Testing password protection of programs 
* Inspecting physical access controls 
* Inspecting program logs 
* Inspecting evidence of virus checks on software 
* Inquiring into back-up procedures 
* Inspecting documentary evidence of control copies of programs being preserved and regularly compared with actual programs

### Testing and documentation of program changes
* Inspecting documentary evidence of testing procedures 
* Inspecting documentation standards 
* Inspecting evidence of approval of changes by computer users and management 
* Inquiring into training of staff using programs

### Controls to prevent wrong programs or files being used
* Inquiring into and testing operation controls over programs 
* Inquiring into and inspecting evidence of job scheduling

### Controls to prevent unauthorised amendments to data files
* Observing and testing password protection 
* Observing existence of restricted access to authorised users 
* Inspecting evidence of authorisation prior to processing (eg authorised data input forms)

### Controls to ensure continuity of operation
* Inquiring into and inspecting the storage of back-up copies of programs and data files off-site 
* Inspecting the protection of equipment against fire and other hazards 
* Inspecting evidence of disaster recovery procedures 
* Inspecting documentary evidence of maintenance agreements and insurance

### Controls over network security
* Inspecting evidence of prior authorisation of the Network Administrator 
* Inquiring into and observing restricted authorised access to communication and network systems 
* Inspecting for existence of network diagnostic tools 
* Inquiring into the existence of firewalls and inspecting evidence of firewall testing and maintenance 
* Inquiring into and testing for evidence of data encryption for secure data transmission and controls over the identification of corrupt data.
