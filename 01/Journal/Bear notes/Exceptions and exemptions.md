# Exceptions and exemptions
One of the objectives of HKFRS 1 is to generate first-time HKFRS financial statements at a cost that does not exceed the benefits. With this in mind, the standard provides a number of mandatory exceptions and optional exemptions from the retrospective application of HKFRS, as well as a short-term exemption.

## 3.3.1 Mandatory exceptions
`HKFRS 1,14-15, Appendix B`

Mandatory exceptions apply to topics for which HKICPA believe that retrospective application could not be applied with sufficient reliability. In these instances, HKFRS must not be applied retrospectively and HKFRS 1 provides alternative guidance.
