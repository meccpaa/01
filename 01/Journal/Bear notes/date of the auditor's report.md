# date of the auditor's report
Date of the auditor's report is the date the auditor dates the report on the financial statements in accordance with HKSA 700 (Revised) Forming an Opinion and Reporting on Financial Statements.

This date cannot be earlier than the date on which the auditor has **obtained** [[sufficient appropriate audit evidence]] for ~forming the audit opinion~ and the [[date of approval of the financial statements]].
