# Tax assessment based on falsified accounts - CIR v Roderick John Sutton, Joint and Several Liquidator of Moulin Global Eyecare Trading Ltd [2010] 4 HKLRD 283
A tax assessment is held to be final and conclusive under s.70, even if the liquidator considers that the accounts of the company were not real and did not disclose true profits
