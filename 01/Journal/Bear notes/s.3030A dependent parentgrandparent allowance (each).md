# s.30/30A dependent parent/grandparent allowance (each)
## Dependant parent allowance
### Scope: 
A parent of a marriage under which the taxpayer or his/ her spouse is the child; the natural parents, adopted parents, step parents of the taxpayer or his/her spouse or deceased spouse(s) maintained by the taxpayer or his or her spouse

### Conditions
- Parent is ordinarily resident in Hong Kong; 
- Under s.30(1), age of parent ≥60; or ≤60 but eligible for Government's Disability Allowance; 
- Under s.30(1A), age of parent ≥55 and <60;and was not eligible for Government's Disability Allowance; 
- Parent either resides with the taxpayer for a continuous period of not less than six months in the year of assessment for less than full valuable consideration; or not less than $12,000 has been made for the maintenance of that parent in the year of assessment; 
- The allowance in respect of a parent can only be granted to one taxpayer although more than one taxpayer may be eligible for the allowance; and 
- The taxpayer cannot claim dependent parent allowance if deduction of elderly residential care expenses has been allowed to him/her or other person in respect of the same person.
- - - -
## Additional dependant parent allowance
### Scope: 
Same as above

### Conditions
- same as above
- Parent resides with the taxpayer for less than full valuable consideration continuously throughout the year of assessment.
- - - -
## Dependant grandparent allowance
### Scope: 
Natural/adopted/step grandparents of the taxpayer or his/her spouse or deceased spouse(s) maintained by the taxpayer or his or her spouse

### Conditions
- same as above (replace parent with grandparent)
- - - -
## Additional dependant grandparent allowance
### Scope: 
Natural/adopted/step grandparents of the taxpayer or his/her spouse or deceased spouse(s) maintained by the taxpayer or his or her spouse

### Conditions
- same as above
- Grandparent resides with the taxpayer for less than full valuable consideration continuously throughout the year of assessment.
