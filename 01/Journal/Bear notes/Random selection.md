# Random selection
Ensures that all items in the population have an equal chance of selection, eg by use of random number tables or computerised generator. 
#QP/C/Audit/evidence

Random selection is a sample selection procedures whereby each [[sampling units]] making up the account balance or class transactions has a ~known chance of selection~; often each item has an equal chance of selection. The concept of random selection requires that the auditor selecting the samples does not influence or bias the selection either consciously or unconsciously.
