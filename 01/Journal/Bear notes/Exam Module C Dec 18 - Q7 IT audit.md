# Exam Module C: Dec 18 - Q7 IT audit
Company E is a newly acquired subsidiary of Company Z. The current operation and finance system of Company Z, named AI, is self-developed by the IT team of Company Z. Company E currently uses a packaged software purchased from the market, named HoEasy, for daily operation.

There is a plan that Company E will convert its system from HoEasy to a modified version of AI that will be developed by Company Z’s IT team with user inputs provided by Company E.

Required:

(a) Explain the differences between bespoke software and off-the-shelf software in terms of the following areas: [[Bespoke or off-the-shelf?]]
(i) Control procedures
(ii) Data and file structures
(iii) Ongoing maintenance
(iv) Customisation
(4 marks)

(b) Based on the information provided in the case, state whether AI and HoEasy is bespoke or off-the-shelf software. (2 marks)

(c) Advise six internal controls that should be in place to oversee the conversion of Company E’s finance system from HoEasy to AI. (6 marks) 
- - - -
## Solution
(a) (i) Control procedures
The control procedures of bespoke software are usually separately designed, documented and implemented, of which the degrees of reliability varies. Off-the-shelf software is usually with in-built control procedures which are well-documented.

(ii) Data and f ile structures
Data and file structures of bespoke software are usually chosen by the organisation. Data and file structures of off-the-shelf software are usually dictated by the package design.

(iii) Ongoing maintenance
The ongoing maintenance and support of bespoke software is usually maintained by the organisation which is usually more costly than off-the-shelf software. Bespoke software should be able to be updated and extended to meet future needs. Off-the-shelf software will usually be updated continuously by the software manufacturer. The organisation is dependent on the supplier for maintenance of the package.

(iv) Customisation
Bespoke software is usually designed to meet the organisation’s special needs and therefore the software can be customerised to a larger extent and can be tailored to be compatible with other existing systems being used within the entity. Standard off-the-shelf software can usually be customerised to a very limited extent only.
- - - -
Answer 7(b)

AI is bespoke software.
HoEasy is off-the-shelf software.
- - - -
Answer 7(c)
The relevant internal controls should include the following:
* A **working team** should be set up with both 
	* (i) IT experts from Company Z who have extensive knowledge on AI and experience on AI development and 
	* (ii) business users from Company E who thoroughly understand the business needs, processes and controls of Company E.
* The **standards** over system design, programming and documentation should be clearly defined and documented.
* Thorough **testing procedures** should be developed to assist the working team to test the functions of the new system.
	* A user **testing environment** should be developed for testing purposes.
	* A thorough ~testing~ over the new system should be performed by the ~working team~ using test data.
	* ~User-acceptance test~ should be carried out.
	* ~Approval~ of the user-acceptance test by system users and management should be obtained before the launch of the system.
* **Segregation of duties** should be in place so those responsible for ~design~ are not responsible for ~testing~.
* **Installation procedures** should be in place so that ::data is not corrupted in transition.::
* A **user manual** should be developed for the new system with clear and adequate user guidance.
	* **Sufficient trainin**g should be provided to the affected staff to assist them to understand the new system and new procedures in place in response to the new system.
* Business processes and controls may have to be updated when the new system is implemented.
