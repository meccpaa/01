# qualifying asset
An asset that necessarily takes a substantial period of time to get ready for its intended use or sale.
- - - -
The standard says that any of the following may be qualifying assets:
- Inventories 
- Manufacturing plants 
- Power generation facilities 
- Intangible assets 
- Investment properties

Financial assets and inventories manufactured or produced over short periods are not qualifying assets, nor are assets ready for sale or their intended use when purchased.
