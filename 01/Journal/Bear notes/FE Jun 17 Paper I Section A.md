## Question 5 (8 marks – approximately 14 minutes) In order to raise funding, its management has decided to issue perpetual bonds with a coupon rate of 7%, payable annually. The one-year interest rate is 7%. There is a 35% probability that interest rates next year will rise to 9% and there is a 65% probability that they will drop to 6%. The par value of the bond is HK$1,000.

Required:

(a) Calculate the market value of the perpetual bonds if they are noncallable (irredeemable). (3 marks)

(b) If the Group decides to make the bonds callable (redeemable) in one year.

(i) Explain what the company’s actions will be if the interest rate changes (increase or decrease) in future.

(ii) Determine the required coupon rate for the bondholders to sell the bond at par. Assume the bonds will be called if interest rates decrease and that the call premium is equal to the annual coupon. (5 marks)
