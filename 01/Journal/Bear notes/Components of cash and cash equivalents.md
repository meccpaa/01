# Components of cash and cash equivalents
`HKAS 7.45,46`
The components of [[cash and cash equivalents]] should be disclosed and a reconciliation should be presented, showing the amounts in the statement of cash flows reconciled with the equivalent items reported in the statement of financial position.

It is also necessary to disclose the accounting policy used in deciding the items included in cash and cash equivalents, in accordance with HKAS 1, but also because of the wide range of cash management practices worldwide.
