### 2.4.2 Gordon growth model
Alternatively, the growth rate can be estimated using Gordon's growth approximation. The rate of growth in dividends is sometimes expressed, theoretically, as:
g = br

Where: 
g is the annual growth rate in dividends
b is the proportion of profits that are retained 
r is the rate of return on new investments

So, if a company retains 71% of its earnings for capital investment projects it has identified and these projects are expected to have an average return of 5%, growth can be estimated as:

g = br = 71% x 5% = 3.55%
