# Disclosure EPS
In addition to EPS figures, an entity must disclose:

HKAS 33. 70

(a) The profit attributable to ordinary shareholders used in the EPS and diluted EPS calculations and a reconciliation of this to profit for the period.

(b) The weighted average number of ordinary shares used in the EPS and diluted EPS calculations and a reconciliation of these amounts to each other.

(c) Instruments that could potentially dilute basic EPS but which are not included in the calculation of diluted EPS because they are anti-dilutive.

(d) Any transactions after the reporting date which would have changed significantly the number of ordinary shares or potential ordinary shares at the period end had the transactions occurred before that date.
