Subject: Balanced Scorecard that details the four key measurements

The **key measurements** of Balanced Scorecard include the Financial Perspective, Customer Perspective, Internal Business Process Perspective, and Innovation and Learning Perspective.

### Financial Perspective
The key measurements will identify relevant financial measures, such as sales growth, operating income, and cash flow etc. The key measurements will indicate whether the Company is able to **charge premium prices** and **increase the operating margin** especially for the ~Joint Support supplements~ since there are the best-selling product.

### Customer Perspective
The key measurements will identify relevant customers and stakeholders measures, such as **customer satisfaction**, and **market share** etc. The key measurements will indicate whether there is an increase in the number of ~new customers~ and increase in the ~sales from new products~ etc.

### Internal Business Process Perspective
The key measurements will identify relevant improvements that lead to superior financial performance, such as ~frequency of the introduction of new products~, and the ~time between concept and delivery of new products~ etc. The key measurements will indicate whether the **quality of the products and spoilage rate** are acceptable etc.

### Innovation and Learning Perspective
The key measurements will identify relevant measures for **how to create value and innovate**, such as ~product lifecycle~. The key measurements will indicate whether ~additional value~ is created and the ~product lifecycle is improved~.

Overall, the Balance Scorecard **links** the Financial Perspective, Customer Perspective, Internal Business Process Perspective, and Innovation and Learning Perspective to provide management with ~different information~ to enable ~effective performance measurement and implementation of the business strategy~.

For example, the key measurements will indicate whether the Company is able to **produce distinctive products** that ~fit the customer needs~ that have a **cause-and-effect relationship** with improvements in ~internal business processes~ (i.e. internal business process perspective), and the management team will find out how to ~create more value and innovate~ (i.e. innovation and learning perspective) that in turn leads to ~customer satisfaction~ (i.e. customer satisfaction perspective), and ~superior financial performance~ (i.e. financial perspective).

In addition, measurement of ~employee satisfaction~, ~ability to attract and retain customers~, ~investment in the new project~ (Project A), and ~employee training~ in sales and marketing (especially in the supermarkets and consignment stores) etc. are also key measurements of the Balanced Scorecard.
