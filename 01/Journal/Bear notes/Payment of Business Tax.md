# Payment of Business Tax
## 7.1 Timing of tax liability
Subject to the transitional arrangement as discussed above, BT liability, where applicable, arises on the date which the taxpayer provides taxable services, transfers intangible assets or sells immovable property and the business proceeds are received or the date on which the taxpayer obtains documentary evidence of the right to collect business proceeds.

Where a taxpayer transfers land use rights or sells immovable property, and receives payments in advance, BT liability arises on the date on which the advanced payments are received.

## 7.2 Tax periods
According to PRBT, the time period for paying BT shall be 5 days, 10 days, 15 days, one month, or one quarter. In case where BT cannot be assessed in regular periods, BT may be assessed on a transaction-by-transaction basis.

## 7.3 Location of tax payment
Locations for the tax payment of BT vary depending on the types of business conducted by the taxpayers:

(a) Taxpayers providing taxable services, transferring intangible assets and selling/leasing immovable properties should report and pay tax to the local tax authorities in-charge where their business establishment or the immovable properties are located;

(b) Taxpayers providing construction services should report and pay tax where the taxable services are provided;

(c) Taxpayers transferring or leasing land use rights should report and pay tax to the local tax authorities in-charge where the land is located.
