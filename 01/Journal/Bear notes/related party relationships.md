# related party relationships
`HKAS 24.10,12`
When considering each possible related party relationship, attention must be paid to the substance of the relationship, not merely the legal form.

The standard also clarifies that in the definition of a related party:

- An associate includes subsidiaries of the associate 
- A joint venture includes subsidiaries of the joint venture 

For example, an associate's subsidiary and the investor that has significant influence over the associate are related to each other.
