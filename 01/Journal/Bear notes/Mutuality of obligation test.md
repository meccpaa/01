# Mutuality of obligation test
This refers to whether there has been some forms of mutual obligation between the individual and the organisation. For example,

(a) Is the organisation obliged to pay a wage or remuneration?

(b) Is the individual obligated to provide his/her work or skills?

(c) Is the organisation under an obligation to provide work?

(d) Is the individual under an obligation to carry out the work?

(e) Can either the organisation or individual terminate the relationship without incurring any liabilities?

(f) Can either the organisation or individual apply any sanction to the other in the event that work is refused or not offered?
