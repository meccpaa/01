# Producing a statement of cash flows
Remember the steps involved in preparation of a statement of cash flows using the indirect method:

**Step 1** Set out the proforma leaving plenty of space.

**Step 2** Complete the reconciliation of profit before tax to net cash from operating activities, as far as possible.

**Step 3**
Calculate the following where appropriate.
- Tax paid 
- Dividends paid 
- Interest paid/received 
- Purchase and sale of non-current assets 
- Issues of shares 
- Repayment of loans

**Step 4**
Work out the profit if not already given using: opening and closing balances, tax charge and dividends.

**Step 5**
Slot the figures into the statement and any notes required.
