The key factors to determine the entity as a principal or agents are set out in B37 of HKFRS15. As discussed, I take Moov vs CSL service agreement as the model:

Factors                 Recommendations/ remarks
B37(b)                   Not applicable to our case, as there is no inventory involved.

B37(a)                   I suggest to elaborate more in the contract the role and responsibility in maintaining the Moov platform/ service in order to demonstrate that Moov (HK) Limited has the ultimate/ primary responsibility to delivery the music/ content to the end customer.
                                Are there any service commitment offered to end customer in the customer agreement with CSL? If such are breached, who (CSL/ Moov HK) bear the penalty/ refund?
                                Any mechanism to handle refund

B37(c)                   I suggest to elaborate more on Moov HK’s ability to modify the price to customer.
                                It seems that the price to customer is subject to approval by CSL, which is not supportive to revenue recognition as Principal.
