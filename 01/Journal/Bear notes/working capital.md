# working capital
~Increases in working capital reduce the net cash flow of the period to which they relate~. The relevant cash flows are the incremental cash flows from one year's requirement to the next. So for example, if a project lasts for five years with a $200,000 working capital requirement at the end of year 1, rising to $300,000 at the end of year 2, the DCF calculation will show $200,000 as a year 1 cash outflow and $100,000 (300,000 – 200,000) as a year 2 cash outflow.

**Working capital is assumed to be recovered at the end of the project**. In the example above, this will be shown by a $300,000 cash inflow at year 5.

~Assume there are no associated tax effects~ for working capital flows.
