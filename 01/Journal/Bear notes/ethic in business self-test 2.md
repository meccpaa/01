# ethic in business self-test 2
What are the benefits for shareholders of receiving annual integrated reports from a company?
- - - -
Shareholders should be aware of matters that affect the long-term sustainability of a company, and not solely informed about matters relating to short-term profitability. A company should therefore report on environmental and social issues as well as financial issues.

Integrated reports should also provide forward-looking information about the company's strategies and business plan, and about risk as well as financial returns.

The benefit of an integrated report is that it brings all these aspects of performance together into a unified report, which gives greater clarity about the relationship between financial and non-financial performance and enables shareholders (and other stakeholders) to make a more complete assessment of the company's performance and prospects.
