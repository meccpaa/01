# existence - PPE
	- Confirm that the entity physically inspects [[inspection]] all items in the [[non-current asset register]] each year.
	- [[inspection]] assets, concentrating on **high value items** and **additions** in-year. Confirm that items inspected:
		- Exist
		- Are in use
		- Are in good condition
		- Have correct serial numbers
	- Review records of income-yielding assets.
	- Reconcile opening and closing assets by numbers as well as amounts.
	- Note that inspection of a **building's title deeds** ::does not give audit evidence:: about existence and if there is doubt that a building actually exists, the auditors should physically inspect it.
