# Qualifying profits at concessionary tax rate of 8.25%
The half rate concession only applies to **qualifying profits** (s.14D(1)), i.e. profits derived from the following qualifying transactions or services:
(a) Money lent to a [[non-Hong Kong associated corporation]] in the ordinary course of an [[intragroup financing business]].
(b) Qualifying [[corporate treasury services]] provided to a [[non-Hong Kong associated corporation]].
(c) Qualifying [[corporate treasury transactions]] entered into by a QCTC on its own account and related to the business of a [[non-Hong Kong associated corporation]].

1. [[invest in HK bonds (s.14D(8))]] - not qualify
2. [[overseas bank deposit (s.15(1)(ia)]] - deem

### non-qualifying profits not eligible for the concessionary tax rate
Even though a corporation satisfies the conditions of a QCTC, the following non-qualifying profits are not eligible for the concessionary tax rate (DIPN 52, para 82):
(a) Income from intra-group financing transaction under which money is lent to a ::Hong Kong associated corporation::;
(b) Corporate treasury service income from a ::Hong Kong:: associated corporation;
(c) Income from corporate treasury transaction that is related to the business of a ::Hong Kong:: associated corporation;
(d) Income from corporate treasury transaction that is ::not related to the business of an associated corporation::.

### apportion
Where the corporate treasury transaction relates to both the business of ::Hong Kong:: and **non-Hong Kong** associated corporations, profits will be ~apportioned~ and only profits related to the business of non-Hong Kong associated corporation can be taxed at the concessionary tax rate (DIPN 52, para 84).
