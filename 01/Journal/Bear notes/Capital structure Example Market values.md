# Capital structure Example: Market values
A company is financed by 2,000,000 equity shares with a market value of $5 each and debt with a current market value of $4 million. The rate of tax on company profits is 16.5%

Required

(a) What would be the value of an identical company that is all-equity financed?

(b) What would be the value of the company, if it replaced some of its equity capital with an additional $2 million of debt capital (market value)?
