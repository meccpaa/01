# FE (Jun 18) Paper II: Q5(b) intra-group stamp duty
(b) Analyse if intra-group relief for stamp duty is possible. If not, state the reason and advise whether there is another way to mitigate the stamp duty payable. Advise as to how it could be carried out, if applicable, and state any assumption(s).

(7 marks)
[[FE (Jun 18) Paper II: case]]
- - - -
## Solutions
`Stamp duty relief under s.45` is ::not applicable:: because (i) there is a plan that Newco Ltd (as transferee) would ~leave the group~; or (ii) Newco Ltd (as transferee) would ~leave the group two months~ after the transfer, which does not exceed the required **two years’ period** under s.45.

It is possible for WT Tours to **distribute a portion of the retained profits** of HK$19 million in the form of a **dividend**, provided such portion is distributable, before the transfer of shares of WT Tours. After the distribution, the consideration or the market value of the shares of WT Tours would be lower, hence this could mitigate the stamp duty payable.

This may also save the efforts of the buyer of Newco Ltd in ascertaining how much retained profits of WT Tours are distributable or not, thus simplifying the [[due diligence]] process. This ~reason should be documented~ in order to avoid any ::general avoidance challenge:: (Ramsay step plan challenge under Arrowtown case).
