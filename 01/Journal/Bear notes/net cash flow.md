# net cash flow
Net cash is the real cash flow, the change in borrowings for the year. Depreciation and other non-cash items are included in the determination of retained income and in the change in net assets.

Net cash flow is the operating cash flow less interest, tax, dividends and extraordinary items (if applicable) and changes in equity, provision for tax and provision for dividend (if applicable).
