# Standalone entity
To qualify as a QCTC, the CTC cannot carry out other non-corporate treasury activity, subject to the safe harbour rule. Only activities that generate income to the CTC are considered for the purpose of determining whether the CTC has carried out any non-corporate treasury activity s.14D(4)). Therefore, a Hong Kong headquarter of an overseas corporation may qualify as a QCTC even though it performs corporate treasury activities as well as other corporate supporting functions for its group companies provided that those corporate supporting activities do not generate any income. Moreover, expense transactions (e.g. borrowings from FIs) would not preclude the CTC from being a QCTC. Therefore:

(a) A trading or manufacturing company with a corporate treasury division carrying out corporate treasury activities for group companies cannot qualify for profits tax concession, unless the [[safe harbor rule under s.14E]] is satisfied (DIPN 52, para 48).

(b) If a corporation takes a lease of office premises for carrying out corporate treasury transactions in relation to a non-Hong Kong associated corporation's business, the transaction of leasing premises would not preclude the corporation from being qualified as a QCTC because this is an expense transaction (DIPN 52, para 49).
