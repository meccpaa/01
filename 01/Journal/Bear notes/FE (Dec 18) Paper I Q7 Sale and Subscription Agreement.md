# FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Q7 Sale and Subscription Agreement
Assuming the Sale and Subscription Agreement is completed by 1 October 2017, analyse the accounting impact of the draft key terms on the consolidated financial statements of SSHI for the year ended 31 December 2017 with reference to the relevant accounting standards.

### ~Sale Shares and the option to repurchase the Sales Shares~
Since the transaction is entered between the Pre-IPO Investor and Andy, there is **no impact** on the consolidated financial statements of SSHI.

### ~Subscription Shares and the option to repurchase the Subscription Shares~

In accordance with HKAS 32.23, a contract that contains an ~obligation for an entity to purchase its own equity instruments~ for cash or another financial asset **gives rise to a financial liability** for the present value of the redemption amount (for example, for the present value of the forward repurchase price, option exercise price or other redemption amount). This is the case even if the contract itself is an equity instrument.

SSHI has the ::obligation:: to repurchase the Subscription Shares from the Pre-IPO Investor if SSHI ~failed to complete~ the IPO by the agreed timeline; therefore, the contract for the Subscription Shares shall be ~classified as a liability~ and **recognised** initially at the present value of the redemption amount (i.e. present value of HK$26.5 million (i.e. HK$20 million + the accrued interest of HK$6.5 million) (Accrued interest HK$6.5 million = HK$20 million x 10% x 3.25 years)).

Subsequently, the financial liability is measured at amortised cost in accordance with HKFRS 9 and interest to be accrued using the effective interest rate method. The effective interest rate amortisation is included as the financial costs.

If the contract expires (i.e. the IPO is completed and the Company is listed before the agreed IPO timeline) and the Company no longer has the obligation to repurchase the Subscription Shares from the Pre-IPO Investor, the carrying amount of the financial liability is **reclassified to equity.**

### Subscription Option
Since the Company’s obligation under the contract of Subscription Option is to deliver a fixed number of its shares (1 million shares) in exchange for a fixed amount of cash (HK$28 million) if the Pre-IPO Investor exercises the Subscription Option, the contract for the Subscription Option satisfied the definition of equity instruments. The premium of HK$1,500,000 received is recognised in equity.

When the Pre-IPO Investor exercises the Subscription Option, the Company will issue 1 million shares in exchange for HK$28 million and the cash received would be recorded as equity.
