### Five-step approach
![](HKFRS15%20Revenue/Attachment.jpeg)

HKFRS 15 takes a five-step approach to recognising revenue:
![](HKFRS15%20Revenue/image.png)
Revenue is therefore recognised when control over goods or services is transferred to the customer.

1. **[[Step 1: Identify the contract(s) with a customer]]**
Enforceable rights and obligations - 
	1. Approved; 
	2. With commercial substance; 
	3. Rights and payment terms; 
	4. Probable collect consideration
[[Contracts are combined and accounted for as a single contract if]]

2. **[[Step 2 Identify performance obligations]]**
	1. One or separate
	2. Distinct?

3. **[[Step 3: Determine transaction price]]** - variable, significant finance component, non-cash, payable to customer, refund liabilities
4. **[[Step 4 Allocate transaction price to performance obligations]]** - standalone price, discount, variable consideration, changes
5. **[[Step 5: Recognise revenue when performance obligations are satisfied]]** - single point, period of time

## 2.4 Modifications to contracts
![](HKFRS15%20Revenue/Attachment%202.jpeg)
A [[contract modification]] is a change to the scope/price of a contract such that it either **creates new** or **changes existing** enforceable rights and obligations of the parties to the contract. Both parties to the contract should ~approve~ such a modification.

## 2.8 Disclosure
![](HKFRS15%20Revenue/Attachment%205.jpeg)

HKFRS 15 requires that an entity provides both qualitative and quantitative information about:
(a) Its [[contracts with customers - revenue disclosure]];
(b) The [[significant judgments - revenue disclosure]] and changes in judgments made in applying HKFRS 15 to those contracts; and
(c) Any [[assets recognised - revenue disclosure]] from the costs to obtain or fulfil a contract with a customer.

## 3. Application of HKFRS 15
![](HKFRS15%20Revenue/Attachment%206.jpeg)
HKFRS 15 includes Application Guidance, which explains how the provisions of the standard should be applied to a number of situations. These include:
- [[Sales with a right of return]] - refund liability
- [[Extended warranties]] - separate performance obligation
- [[Transactions involving an agent]] - control
- [[Royalties]] - 
- [[Licensing]] - access as it exists at any given time (over time) or at the date license is granted (at a point in time)
- [[Repurchase agreements]] - keep the asset (—> not recognise revenue) + loan + interest expense
- [[Consignment arrangements]] - not recognise revenue
- [[Bill and hold arrangements]] - billed but physically retained
- [[Options for additional goods and services]]  - loyalty scheme - material right for free or at discounted
- [[Non-refundable upfront fees]] - prepayment?
