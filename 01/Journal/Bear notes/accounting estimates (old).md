# accounting estimates (old)
An accounting estimate is an approximation of a monetary amount in the absence of a precise means of measurement.
- - - -
## The nature of accounting estimates
`HKSA 540 Auditing Accounting Estimates, including Fair Value Accounting Estimates, and Related Disclosures`  provides guidance on the audit of accounting estimates contained in financial statements. The auditor's **objective** is to obtain [[sufficient appropriate audit evidence]] about whether accounting estimates are ~reasonable~ and related disclosures are ~adequate~.
