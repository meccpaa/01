# Non-creditable input VAT
The following categories of input VAT are not creditable against output VAT (PRVAT, Article 10):

- Input VAT related to activities subject to Business Tax;
- Input VAT related to tax-exempt items;
- Input VAT related to goods or taxable services purchased for group welfare or personal consumption;
- Input VAT related to abnormal or extraordinary losses. 'Abnormal losses' are losses other than the normal wear and tear in the ordinary course of business. For example, losses due to theft, spoilage or deterioration or losses from improper management.
