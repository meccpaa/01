# Basis period for a normal or continuing business
The IRD accepts the taxpayer's annual account as its basis period for that year of assessment.

(1) For accounts made up to 31 March, the basis period is the same as the fiscal year.

(2) For accounts made up to a date other than 31 March, the basis period is the accounting year ended within the year of assessment.
