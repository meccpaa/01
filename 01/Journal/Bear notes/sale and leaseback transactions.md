# sale and leaseback transactions
In a sale and leaseback transaction, an asset is transferred to another party and then leased back by the same seller. The lease payment and transfer price are normally linked because they are part of the same package in the negotiation. The accounting treatment depends on ~whether the transfer of the asset meets the HKFRS 15 criteria~ for determining whether a performance obligation is satisfied.

The transfer of an asset **qualifies as a sale** if the customer ~obtains **control** of the asset~. In determining this, the following should be considered:
1. Whether the selling entity has
	1. a present right to payment for the asset
	2. transferred legal title to the asset
	3. transferred physical possession of the asset
	4. transferred the risks and rewards of ownership of the asset to the customer
2. Whether the purchaser has accepted the asset

In assessing whether a transfer prior to a leaseback arrangement is a sale, the fourth criterion, related to the **risks and rewards** of ownership, is likely to be key.
- - - -
## 5.1 Transfer of the asset is not a sale
HKFRS 16.103 - If the transfer does not qualify as a sale, the transaction is, in substance, a **financing arrangement**. Therefore:

Seller/lessee
- The transferred asset is ::not derecognised::
- The transfer proceeds are recognised as a **[[financial liability]]** in accordance with [[HKFRS 9:  Recognition of financial instruments]] and [[lease payments]] are ~payments of the financial liability~.

Purchaser/lessor
- The transferred asset is ::not recognised::
- The transfer proceeds are recognised as a **[[financial asset]]** and HKFRS 9 is applied to account for lease receipts.

## 5.2 Transfer of the asset is a sale
HKFRS 16.100-102 If the transfer does qualify as a sale:

~**Seller/lessee**~ 
- The transfer proceeds are recognised as **sale proceeds**
- The carrying amount of the asset is allocated between
	- The transferred rights 
	- The retained right to use the asset
The transferred rights are derecognised and the retained right is transferred to be a [[right-of-use asset]].

- That part of the **gain or loss arising** that relates to the ~transferred rights~ is recognised; the part of the gain or loss relating to the ::retained rights:: is not recognised
- A [[lease liability]] is recognised. 

**~Purchaser/lessor~**
- The acquired asset is recognised in accordance with applicable HKFRS.
- The [[lessor accounting]] requirements of HKFRS 16 are applied to account for the lease (see section 4).

### 5.2.1 Above or below market terms
Adjustment is required if sale proceeds or lease payments are not on market terms:
![](sale%20and%20leaseback%20transactions/5E95812E-0E52-45D6-968A-0CE25C7ADBB2.png)
The adjustment is equal to the more readily determinable of:
- The difference between the fair value of consideration and the fair value of the asset, and
- The difference between the present value of contractual lease payments and the present value of lease payments at market rates.
