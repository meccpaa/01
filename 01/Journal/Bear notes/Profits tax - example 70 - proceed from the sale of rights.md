# Profits tax - example 70 - proceed from the sale of rights
Co HK purchased a patent at a cost of $500,000 during 2013/14. The patent was used partly (50%) in the production of chargeable profits. A deduction of $250,000 ($500,000 x 50%) was allowed in the year of purchase.

During 2016/17, Co HK sold the patent for $600,000. The relevant sale proceeds are $300,000 ($600,000 x 50%), which exceed the deduction previously allowed ($250,000). Therefore, the deemed trading receipt chargeable under s.16E(3) is restricted to $250,000.
