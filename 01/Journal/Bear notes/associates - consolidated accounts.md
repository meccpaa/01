# associates - consolidated accounts
An associate is an entity over which an investor has [[significant influence]] but not control. This is assumed where at least 20% of the voting power is held by an investor but may be achieved in other ways.

Note that in the separate financial statements of the investor, an interest in an associate is accounted for either:
- At cost, or 
- In accordance with [[HKFRS 9]], or 
- Using the equity method.
