# Strategy self-test 1
Using the BCG matrix categorise each of the following products for Apple stating your reasoning.
- Personal computers and laptops 
- Smart phones 
- Smart watches 
- MP3 players
- - - -
## Solutions
Personal computers and laptops – Cash cow

Apple has a significant share in the premium segment of this market. Although still very large, the worldwide markets for PCs and laptops are either static or in slow decline. Apple invests relatively less in this product group and generates cash flow that can be invested in development of other products.

Smart phones – Star/Cash cow

Apple has a high market share in this market. The smartphone market has grown very rapidly, (in fact the fastest adopted new technology in human history), and is expected to continue to grow. Apple can be seen to be investing heavily in this product producing regular and significant updates in the form of new models. Although in the last few years the iPhone has undoubtedly been a star, it could be argued that lower future predicted growth rates, (due to the already high penetration in most markets), mean that the iPhone should be considered a cash cow rather than a star.

Smart watches – Question mark

Apple has a relatively small share of this developing market. Although some analysts still predict very high growth for smart watch sales the market remains relatively small and is yet to produce high levels of growth. The iWatch has yet to enjoy the success of Apple's main products.

MP3 players – Dog

Apple iPods used to be one of its largest revenue generators. However, although Apple’s market share is still relatively high sales have declined very significantly due to the contraction in the mp3 player market (largely driven by the growth in smartphones offering this functionality). Apple no longer invests in regular updates for these products and in fact no longer even separately reports iPod revenue.
