# specific borrowings
These borrowing costs would have been avoided had the expenditure on the qualifying asset not been made. This is straightforward where funds have been borrowed for the financing of one particular asset.

Once the relevant borrowings are identified with a specific asset, the amount of borrowing costs to be capitalised during a period will be the actual borrowing costs incurred on those borrowings, less any investment income on the temporary investment of those borrowings. It is not unusual to invest some or all of the funds before they are actually used to finance the qualifying asset.

If specific borrowings remain outstanding after the qualifying asset to which they relate is ready for use or sale, the borrowings are transferred to be part of general funds for the purposes of calculating a capitalisation rate, as described below. [[general borrowings]]
