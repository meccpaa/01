# FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Q2 business and operation risks, mitigation
SSHI business is subject to numerous business and operation risks that may have a significant and adverse impact. With the case background information, identify three risk factors related to business and operations, and formulate strategies to mitigate the identified risks.

## Solutions:
SSHI business is subject to numerous risks that may have a significant and adverse impact. Below are the risk factors and strategies to mitigate the identified risks.

### Risk factor 1:
The SSHI business can be adversely affected by the ::change of customer choice,:: customer preference or spending habits. SSHI is well-known for its Vitamins and Minerals products and many tourists from mainland China purchase those products when they visit Hong Kong; however, if the customer preference or spending habits switch, then it could have an adverse impact on the business.
[[threat of substitute products or services]]

Strategy to mitigate the risk factor:
SSHI can enrich the Vitamins and Minerals products or develop new products that meet the customer preference. [[product development]]

### Risk factor 2:
The management of SSHI understands that the Joint Support supplements are the best selling products in the Hong Kong market due to the aging problem and SSHI will focus on those products in the coming years; however, SSHI business can be adversely affected if the distribution agreement is terminated. [[bargaining power of customers]]

Strategy to mitigate the risk factor:
SSHI can explore if the distribution agreement can be extended to ensure the suppliers that there is an enough supply of the Joint Support supplements products in the coming years so that the Sales and Promotion Team can continue to focus on those products without affecting the operation of the business.

### Risk factor 3:
SSHI incurs a lot of **Slotting fees** and the sponsorship fees to the design and renovation of the consignment stores. However, if the supermarkets and consignment stores change their business or pricing strategies, then the Hong Kong expansion plan of SSHI can be adversely affected. [[bargaining power of customers]]

Strategy to mitigate the risk factor:
SSHI can discuss with the supermarkets and consignment stores about the quantities and price for the coming years to ensure that the expansion plan will not be affected.

### Risk factor 4:
SSHI relies heavily on the sales through the supermarkets and consignment stores and SSHI does not have much control over the supermarkets and consignment stores.

Strategy to mitigate the risk factor:

SSHI can find more sub-distributors to sell the products or explore the possibility for e-commerce business.

### Risk factor 5:
SSHI will provide training to the distributors about the ingredients and functions of the products. SSHI may be exposed to litigation claims if the distributors can not explain the ingredients and functions of the products to the customers clearly and so cause health problems.

Strategy to mitigate the risk factor:
SSHI can purchase an **insurance** to cover or partially cover the claims.

### Risk factor 6:
The Customer Relationship Management (CRM) System may be hacked and SSHI can lose all the data.

Strategy to mitigate the risk factor:
SSHI can maintain and secure the CRM System by **recruiting IT experts** to maintain the IT security of the CRM System. SSHI can also **back up** all the data off site as a disaster recovery plan. In case, the CRM System is down, the back up off site can ensure the System and servers can be reloaded with the latest data.

### Risk factor 7:
The fluctuation of the pound currency (i.e. the new investment) against the Hong Kong dollar can affect SSHI’s business and its profitability. [[8.2 the causes of exchange rate fluctuations]] 

Strategy to mitigate the risk factor:
SSHI can manage the foreign currency fluctuation risk through currency futures, forwards, options, and currency-focused exchange-traded funds etc. [[8.3 foreign currency risk management]]

### Risk factor 8:
SSHI increases the long-term bank loan by 10 million and the fluctuation of the interest rate can affect SSHI’s business and its profitability. [[Interest rate risk]]

Strategy to mitigate the risk factor:
SSHI can manage the interest rate risk through entering the interest rate futures and purchasing the high-yield bonds. [[9.6 interest rate risk management]]

### Risk factor 9:
SSHI may not receive the outstanding payments from its debtors and this credit risk can affect SSHI’s business and its profitability. [[credit risk]]

Strategy to mitigate the risk factor:
SSHI can discuss the terms of repayment with its debtors in order to manage the credit risk.
