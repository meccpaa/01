# Poon Cho-ming, John [HCIA 2/2015] - Termination payments
The CFI held that (a) payment in lieu of a discretionary bonus made to the taxpayer and (b) stock option gains derived by the taxpayer from the accelerated vesting of previously granted options upon termination of his employment are treated as his employment income and hence subject to salaries tax despite that such payment and accelerated vesting were not provided in the taxpayer's employment contract.
