# Significant components
> Significant component – A component identified by the group engagement team (i) that is of individual financial significance to the group, or (ii) that, due to its specific nature or circumstances, is likely to include significant risks of material misstatement of the group financial statements.  

`HKSA 600.A5-A6`
It is important to consider whether the component is a significant component or not. The following factors should be considered for determining a significant component:

### (a) Individually significant component

The risks of material misstatement of the group financial statements would **increase** when the individual financial significance of a component increases. The group engagement team, using professional judgment, may identify a **benchmark** and determine a **percentage** to be applied to identify which are significant components. Depending on the nature of the group, benchmarks might include group assets, revenue or profit.

### (b) Component likely to include significant risks of material misstatement of the group financial statements

The group engagement team may identify a component that is likely to include **significant risks** of material misstatement of the group financial statements due to its specific nature or circumstances.
