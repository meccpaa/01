# Comparative information
Other than where HKFRS permit otherwise, comparative information should be presented for the preceding period for all amounts reported in the current period's financial statements. Comparative narrative information should also be provided where this is relevant to an understanding of the current period's financial statements.

As a **minimum**, an entity should present:

- Two statements of financial position
- Two statements of profit or loss and other comprehensive income 
- Two statements of cash flows
- Two statements of changes in equity, and 
- Related notes.
