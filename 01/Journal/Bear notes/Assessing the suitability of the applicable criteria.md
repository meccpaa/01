# Assessing the suitability of the applicable criteria
The practitioner must assess whether:
`HKSAE 3429.14`
(a) The unadjusted financial information has been extracted from an appropriate source
(b) The pro forma adjustments are directly attributable to the event or transaction, factually supportable and consistent with the entity's applicable financial reporting framework and relevant accounting policies
(c) Appropriate presentation and disclosures have been made

Sources for factual support for adjustments include:
- Purchase and sale agreements 
- Financing documents, such as debt agreements 
- Independent valuation reports 
- Other documents relating to the event or transaction 
- Published financial statements 
- Other financial information disclosed in the prospectus 
- Relevant legal or regulatory actions e.g. taxation 
- Employment agreements 
- Actions to those charged with governance
