# Taxable scope - business tax
According to article 1 of the Provisional Regulations on Business Tax of the People's Republic of China ('PRBT'), all units and individuals providing prescribed taxable services, transferring intangible assets, or selling immovable properties within the territory of the People's Republic of China are subject to BT.

However, starting from 1 August 2013, China has been implementing a VAT reform programme applying to various services on a nationwide basis. Majority of **services** such as transportation services, modern services and telecommunication services have been subject to VAT instead of BT.

With effect from 1 May 2016, VAT reform programme has expanded to cover **sales of immovable properties**, leasing of immovable properties and transfer of land use right, as well as provision of construction services, financial services and consumer services (see section 14 below for details).

As BT and VAT systems currently are in a state of transition, some business services previously subject to BT will continue to fall under BT until completion of service contracts.
