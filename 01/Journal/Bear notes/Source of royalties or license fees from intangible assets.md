# Source of royalties or license fees from intangible assets
Pursuant to [[DIPN 21 (Revised)]], the locality of royalties  would be 
1. the **place of acquisition and granting** of the licence or right of use.
2. other than those **deemed chargeable** under ss.15(1)(a), (b) or (ba)

In July 2012, the IRD issued [[DIPN 49]]. Part B 'Taxation of royalties derived from licensing of intellectual property rights' (para 71 – 76) outlines the IRD's interpretation and practices in relation to royalties derived from intellectual property rights ('IPRs') by taxpayers carrying on business in Hong Kong

Royalties deemed chargeable under ss.15(1)(a), (b) and (ba) are discussed in section 5.1 on [[Sums specifically chargeable to profits tax]].

[[CIR v HK-TVB International Ltd [(1992) 1 HKRC 90–064}]] - sublicense —> s15(1)(b)
[[Lam Soon Trademark Limited [(2005) HKRC 90–171]] -licensing —> s.15(1)(b) 
[[CIR v Emerson Radio Corporation [(2000) HKRC 90-102]] royalty income (trademark) —> s.15(1)(ba)
