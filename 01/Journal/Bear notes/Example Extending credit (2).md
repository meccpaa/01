# Example: Extending credit (2)
Another company currently enjoys sales of HK$5m per month. Variable costs of sales are $4m per month (all payable in the month of sale). It is estimated that if the credit period allowed to accounts receivable were to be increased from one month to two months, sales volume would rise by 20%. All customers would take the extended credit.

If the company's cost of capital is 12.5%, is the extension of the credit period justifiable in financial terms?
