# compliance adviser
A newly-listed company must appoint a compliance adviser for the **period** **beginning** on the date of the ~listing of its shares~ and **ending** on the ~publication of its results for the first full financial year~ that begins after listing. A compliance adviser must be ~licensed by the SFC~ to be a [[sponsor]], but ::need not be the same:: firm that acted as the company’s sponsor for the application for listing.
