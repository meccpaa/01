# Intra-group transactions
`HKFRS10.B86`
As the consolidated financial statements are prepared for a group as a single economic entity, intra-group balances and transactions **should be eliminated** as part of the consolidation process. As we have already said, these amounts may be distorted due to the group relationship, and so may result in the presentation of misleading group performance or position.

To take an example, assume that a group company bought an asset from a non-group company paying the market price of $100 and sold this to another group company for $300, so making a $200 profit. The second company then sold the asset to a third group company for $600, so making a $300 profit. If the effects of this intra-group transaction were not eliminated from the consolidated accounts, assets and profits would both be overstated by $500.
