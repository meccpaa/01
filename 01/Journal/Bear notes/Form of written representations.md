# Form of written representations
`HKSA 580.15`
The written representations shall be in the form of a representation letter.

The representation letter should:
* Be addressed to the auditors
* Be appropriately dated (see below)
* Contain specific information
* Be signed by the members of management who have primary responsibility for the entity and with specific relevant knowledge

The date of the written representations shall be as near as practicable to, but not after, the date of the auditor's report on the financial statements. The written representations shall be for all the financial statements and periods referred to in the auditor's report.
