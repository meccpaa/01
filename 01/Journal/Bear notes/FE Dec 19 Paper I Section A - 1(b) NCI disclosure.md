### Panel discussion
The question required candidates to explain the disclosure requirement in relation to the **non-controlling interest** of MPHZ (supported with the information provided in the case background) on the consolidated financial statements for the year ended 31 December 2018 with reference to the relevant accounting standard(s). 

The candidates’ performance was unsatisfactory. Around one-third of the candidates did not attempt to answer the question. For those answered the question, notwithstanding it is clearly stated in the question that the answer shall be ~supported with the information provided in the case background~, many of them simply stated the disclosure requirements without applying these requirements by relating the information provided in the case background. It demonstrated the candidates are generally weak in the disclosure requirements and applying the requirements to the case.
