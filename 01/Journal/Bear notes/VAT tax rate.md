# VAT tax rate
The current tax rate of VAT is divided into four classifications:
- Reduced rate of 13% (see section 11.1 for details);
- Various VAT rates lower than 17% (e.g. 6%, 11% and 13%), depending on the industries or activities from which the income is derived (see section 14.1 for details);
- Basic rate of 17%; and
- Zero rate which only applies to export goods.

## 11.4 VAT rate for small-scale taxpayers
For small-scale taxpayers, the applicable VAT rate is 3% on the sales amount. No input VAT can be claimed for deduction.

Prior to 1 January 2009, a tax rate of 4% is applicable to the small-scale taxpayer who is engaged in trading business while a tax rate of 6% is applicable to other businesses except for trading business.

## 11.5 Import VAT calculation
The VAT payable on goods imported by a taxpayer is calculated by applying the applicable VAT rate to the composite taxable value of the imported goods.

The composite taxable value includes (a) transaction value on which customs duty is paid, (b) customs duty and (c) consumption tax (where applicable). (For consumption tax, please see section 15 below.)
