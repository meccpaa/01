# CCY Example: Exchange
Silk Co. sells goods to a Japanese company on 1 May 20X9, and agrees that payment should be made in Japanese Yen at a price of 116,000 at the end of July 20X9. Silk Co. prepares its accounts to 30 June. Relevant exchange rates are:
1 May 20X9 		Yan10.75 to $1
30 June 20X9. 	Yan11 to $1
31 July 20X9		Yan11.6 to $1
Silk Co. would record the sale as follows:

DEBIT AR (Yan116,000 ÷ 10.75) = $10,791
CREDIT Sales

At the period end, Silk Co must re-translate the receivable amount based on the spot rate on 30 June 20X9. The re-translated receivables balance will be $10,545 (yan 116,000 ÷ 11). Therefore, 
DEBIT Exchange loss $246
CREDIT Receivable account ($10,791 - $10,545)

An exchange loss arises as the receivables amount has lost value in $.

When the Yan116,000 are paid at the end of July, Silk Co. will convert them into $, to obtain (Yan116,000 ÷ 11.6) $10,000. Therefore, a further loss arises:

DEBIT		Cash				$10,000
DEBIT		Exchange loss		$545
CREDIT		Receivables account	$10,545
