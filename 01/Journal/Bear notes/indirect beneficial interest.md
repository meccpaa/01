# indirect beneficial interest
S.20AB(5) provides that a person is to be regarded as having an indirect beneficial interest in a corporation, a partnership, a trustee of a trust estate or any other entity if, **through an interposed person or a series of two or more interposed persons**:

- CORPORATION: (a) he is interested in any of the issued share capital (excluding those that do not entitle their holders to receive dividends and a distribution of assets upon dissolution) of the corporation (where the corporation is not a trustee of a trust estate);
- PARTNERSHIP: (b) he is entitled to any of the profits (or a distribution of its assets upon its dissolution) of the partnership (where the partnership is not a trustee of a trust estate); or
- TRUST: (c) he-
	- (i) benefits under the trust estate; or
	- (ii) not being a trustee of the trust estate (or a director of the trustee where the trustee is a corporation), is able or might reasonably be expected to be able to control the activities of the trust estate or the application of its corpus or income; or
- OTHERS: (d) he has any of the ownership interests in the entity which is not a corporation, a partnership and a trustee of a trust estate.

- - - -
Under Part 2 of Schedule 15 of the IRO, the extent of a resident person's indirect beneficial interest in a non-resident person is determined as follows:

(a) Where there is **one interposed person**, the percentage arrived at by multiplying the ~percentage of the resident person's beneficial interest~ in the interposed person by the percentage of the interposed person's beneficial interest in the non-resident person; or

(b) Where there are a series of **two or more interposed persons**, the percentage arrived at by multiplying the percentage of the resident person's beneficial interest in the first interposed person by the percentage of the first interposed person's beneficial interest in the next interposed person and so on; and finally by the percentage of the last interposed person's beneficial interest in the non-resident person.
