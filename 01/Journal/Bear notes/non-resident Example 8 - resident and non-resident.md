# non-resident Example 8 - resident and non-resident
(Adapted from DIPN 43 (Revised), Examples 5 and 6)

N Ltd and O Ltd were resident companies with the same holding company. They held 20% and 25% respectively of the issued shares of D-Fund Ltd, a tax-exempt offshore fund.

N Ltd and O Ltd were associates as they were under the control of the same company. As their beneficial interests in D-Fund Ltd in total exceeded the 30% threshold (20% + 25% = 45%), the deeming provisions under s.20AE were applicable to both of them. Hence, 20% and 25% of the exempt profits of D-Fund Ltd were deemed to be the assessable profits of N Ltd and O Ltd respectively.

However, if O Ltd was a non-resident company, it should not have any tax liability since the deeming provisions would not apply to a non-resident person. However, the deeming provisions continued to be applicable to N Ltd as its beneficial interest together with that of O Ltd in D-Fund Ltd exceeded the 30% threshold. 20% of the exempt profits of D-Fund Ltd would be deemed to be the assessable profits of N Ltd.
