# Example: Early payment discount
A company has annual credit purchases of HK$20m from its major supplier. The credit terms are payment must be made in 60 days of the invoice without discount but a cash discount of 4% will be given if payment is made within 10 days of the invoice. The company's required return on investment is at 15% per annum.

Is the cash discount of 4% worth undertaking from a financial viewpoint?
