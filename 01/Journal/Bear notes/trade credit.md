# trade credit
Trade credit is one of the main sources of short-term finance for a business. Current assets such as raw materials may be purchased on credit with payment terms normally varying from between 30 to 90 days. Trade credit therefore represents an **interest free short-term loan**. In a period of high inflation, purchasing via trade credit will be very helpful in keeping costs down. However, it is important to take into account the loss of discounts suppliers offer for early payment.

Unacceptable delays in payment will worsen a company's credit rating and additional credit may become difficult to obtain.
