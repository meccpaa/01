# identified asset
- The asset must be ~explicitly or implicitly identified~ in the contract.
- The asset within the contract may be a portion of a physical asset if ~physically distinct~ e.g. a floor of a property. A ::capacity or portion of an asset:: that is not physically distinct is not an identified asset ~unless it represents substantially all of the capacity~ of the asset and so provides the customer with the right to obtain substantially all of the economic benefits of the asset.
- A contract is not a lease if the ::supplier has a substantive right to substitute the identified asset:: at any time throughout the period of use. 
 This is the case where the supplier:
	- Has the ability to substitute alternative assets, and 
	- Would benefit economically from doing so.
