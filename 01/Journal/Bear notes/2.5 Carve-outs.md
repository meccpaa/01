# 2.5 Carve-outs
A carve-out is the creation of a **new company**, by detaching parts of the company and ~selling the shares of the new company to the public~.
- - - -
In a carve-out, a new company is created whose shares are owned by the public with the **parent company retaining a substantial fraction** of the shares.

Parent companies undertake carve-outs in order to **raise funds** in the capital markets. These funds can be used for the repayment of debt or creditors or they can be retained within the firm to fund expansion. Carved out units **tend to be highly valued**. as they often tend to be the 'key' assets within a business and the carve-out is used to maximise their value.
