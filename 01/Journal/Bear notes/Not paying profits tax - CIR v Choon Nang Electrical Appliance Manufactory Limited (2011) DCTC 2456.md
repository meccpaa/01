# Not paying profits tax - CIR v Choon Nang Electrical Appliance Manufactory Limited (2011) DCTC 2456
The taxpayer made various defences for not paying the profits tax when they fall due, including that the amount was excessive or incorrect as the matter was under objection. However, it was held that the taxpayer did not have a reasonable defence, which was struck out accordingly.
