# agent
s.2 IRO: 'Agent', in relation to a non-resident person or a partnership in which any partner is a **non-resident person**, includes:

(a) The agent, attorney, factor, receiver, or manager in Hong Kong of such person or partnership; and
(b) Any person in Hong Kong through whom such person or partnership is in receipt of any profits or income arising in or derived from Hong Kong.
