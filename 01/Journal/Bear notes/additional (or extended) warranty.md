# additional (or extended) warranty
An additional (or extended) warranty that a customer has the option to purchase.

This is accounted for as a separate performance obligation and allocated a portion of the transaction price in accordance with HKFRS 15.
- - - -
 An additional (or extended) warranty at no cost that provides an additional service beyond the assurance that a product will function as intended.

Again this is accounted for as a separate performance obligation and allocated a portion of the transaction price in accordance with HKFRS 15.
