# investment property
Investment property is property (land or a building - or part of a building - or both) held (by the owner or by the lessee as a right-of-use asset) to **earn rentals** or for **capital appreciation** or both,

rather than for:
(a) Use in the production or supply of goods or services or for administrative purposes [[HKAS 16: Property, plant and equipment - PPE]]
(b) Sale in the ordinary course of business. [[HKAS 2 Inventories]]
