# risk
risk can be applied to a situation where there are **several possible outcomes** and, on the **basis of past relevant experience**, **probabilities can be assigned** to the various outcomes that could prevail.
- - - -
In general, risky projects are those whose **future cash flows**, and hence the project ~returns~, are ::likely to be variable::. The **greater** the variability is, the ::greater the risk::. The problem of risk is ::more acute with capital investment:: decisions than other decisions for the following reasons:

(a) Estimates of capital expenditure might be for ::several years ahead::, such as for major construction projects. Actual costs may escalate well above budget as the work progresses.

(b) Estimates of ::benefits will be for several years ahead::, sometimes 10, 15 or 20 years ahead or even longer, and such long-term estimates can at best be approximations.
