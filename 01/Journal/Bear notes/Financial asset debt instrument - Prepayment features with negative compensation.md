# Financial asset debt instrument - Prepayment features with negative compensation
Some financial asset debt instruments have a prepayment feature with negative compensation:
- A prepayment feature is the option to terminate the instrument before maturity;
- Negative compensation arises where the party exercising the early termination option receives compensation from the other party rather than paying it to the other party.

HKFRS 9 was amended in 2017 to clarify that where a debt instrument has a prepayment feature with negative compensation, its contractual terms can still give rise on specified dates to cash flows that are solely payments of principal and interest on the principal outstanding. Therefore a financial asset with such a feature is not necessarily measured at fair value through profit or loss.
