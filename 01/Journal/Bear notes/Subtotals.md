# Subtotals
The guidance on subtotals applicable to the statement of financial position (see section 1.2.2) is also applicable to the statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income.

For any subtotals presented in the statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income, the line items should be disclosed that reconcile those subtotals back to the line items required by HKFRS.
