# benefits-in-kind
Benefits-in-kind (i.e. a reward in a form other than money) will not be subject to salaries tax if they are:

- Not convertible into cash; 
- Provided by the employer in such a way that the employer has a sole and primary liability to pay for that benefit (with the exception of education benefits for children of employees – see s.9(2A)(b) below); 
- A settlement of the employer's liability not guaranteed by any other person (not discharging the employee's personal liability); 
- Not benefits specifically chargeable to salaries tax (e.g. under s.9(2A)).
