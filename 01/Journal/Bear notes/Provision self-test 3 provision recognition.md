# Provision self-test 3: provision recognition
A corporate customer launched a legal case against Pioneer Machinery Co ('PMC') on 2 June 20X4 on the basis that a machine supplied by the company was faulty and as a result goods produced by it were substandard, resulting in lost contracts and wasted materials.

PMC's legal team has advised that the company has a 75 chance of losing the case and as a result would have to pay 25,000,000 in damages.

PMC is covered by insurance for such an eventuality and if it loses the case against the customer it is virtually certain that it can claim for the full amount of the loss net of a 10 excess.
- - - -
Required

(a) Explain how the requirements of HKAS 37 are applied to this scenario and state the necessary journal entries that should be made in the year ended 30 June 20X4.

(b) Identify amounts to be recognised in the financial statements of PMC in the year ended 30 June 20X4.
