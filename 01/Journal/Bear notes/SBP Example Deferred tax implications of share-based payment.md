Lamar Co. has the following share option scheme at 31 December 20;1:

The price of the company's shares at 31 December 20;1 is 7 per share and at 31 December 20;0 was 7.50 per share.

The directors must be working for Lamar on the vesting date in order for the options to vest.

No directors have left the company since the issue of the share options and none are expected to leave before December 20;3. The shares can be exercised on the first day of the month in which they vest.

In accordance with HKFRS 2 an expense of 25,000 has been charged to profit or loss in the year ended 31 December 20;0 in respect of Yip's share options, and 100,000 has been charged to profit or loss in the year ended 31 December 20;1 in respect of both Yip and Chan. The cumulative total is therefore 125,000.

Tax allowances arise when the options are exercised and the tax allowance is based on the option's intrinsic value at the exercise date.

Assume a notional tax rate of 16.

Required

What are the deferred tax implications of the share option scheme"

## Solution
![](SBP%20Example%1D%20Deferred%20tax%20implications%20of%20share-based%20payment/EB825361-DFEF-4141-806A-A8B111B28596.png)
The cumulative remuneration expense is 25,000, which is less than the estimated tax deduction of 37,500. Therefore:

- A deferred tax asset of 6,000 is recognised in the statement of financial position 
- There is deferred tax income of 4,000 (25,000 u 16) 
-  The excess of 2,000 goes to equity
![](SBP%20Example%1D%20Deferred%20tax%20implications%20of%20share-based%20payment/956F34FB-0FB9-4300-B3E4-63166E33E0E1.png)

The cumulative remuneration expense is 125,000, which is equal to the estimated tax deduction. Therefore:
- A deferred tax asset of 20,000 is recognised in the statement of financial position at 31 December 20;1
- Deferred tax income of 14,000 is recognised in the statement of profit or loss in the year ended 31 December 20;1
