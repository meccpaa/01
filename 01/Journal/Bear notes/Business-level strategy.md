# Business-level strategy
Business strategy is about how to compete successfully in particular markets (JS&W). Business-level strategy is about the ~particular and distinct combination of products and markets~ dealt with by **one business unit**.

A business unit might be a small, independent organisation or part of a larger one. In the first case, business and corporate strategy merge with one another; in the second, SBU level strategies must be co-ordinated with corporate strategy and with each other.
