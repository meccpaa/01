# resident person - s.20AB(2)
Under s.20AB(2), a 'resident person' means:

(a) An individual who 
	- (i) ordinarily resides in Hong Kong (has a permanent home in Hong Kong where he or his family lives in that year of assessment), or 
	- (ii) stays in Hong Kong for more than **180 days** in the ~relevant year~ of assessment **or** more than **300 days** in ~two consecutive years~ of assessment, one of which is the relevant year of assessment (in counting the number of days, part of a day is treated as one day);

(b) A company, partnership or trustee of a trust estate whose central management and control is exercised in Hong Kong in the relevant year of assessment.
