# Cessation of auditor - new CO s.418
If, while holding office as auditor of a company, a person ceases to be eligible, or becomes disqualified, for appointment as auditor of the company, the person:
(a) Immediately ceases to be auditor of the company; and
(b) Must notify the company of the cessation in writing within 14 days from the date of the cessation.

Without complying to the section, the person would be penalised.
