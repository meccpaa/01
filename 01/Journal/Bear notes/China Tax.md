* (Note for VAT reform programme)

Starting from 1 August 2013, China has been implementing a VAT reform programme applying to various services on a nationwide basis. Majority of services such as transportation services, modern services and telecommunication services have been subject to VAT instead of BT.

With effect from 1 May 2016, the VAT reform programme has expanded to cover sales of immovable properties, leasing of immovable properties and transfer of land use right, as well as provision of construction services, financial services and consumer services.

As BT and VAT systems currently are in the state of transition, some business services previously subject to BT would continue to fall within BT until completion of service contracts.
