# 6. Circumstances responsible for the realisation
Assets realised in a sudden emergency may show that there is no plan to carry on a trade in respect of the subject matter.

In Cohan's Executors v IRC [(1924) 12 TC 602], the executors of the deceased sold a ship after completing a shipbuilding contract which was entered into by the deceased before he died. It was held that the executors did not continue the trade but were performing their duty to realise an asset to the best advantage.
