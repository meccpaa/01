# s.12(1)(a): All outgoing and expenses
The test for deductibility is **much stricter in salaries tax than in profits tax** [[Deduction]]. In order to be qualified for salaries tax deduction, the expenditure must satisfy each of the following tests in addition to not being expenditure of a domestic, private or capital nature:
- It must have been **'incurred'**; 
- It must have arisen **’[[wholly and exclusively]]’** in the ~production of the income~ (apportionment); and 
- It must have been **'necessary'** in the production of the income. [[test of necessity]]
