# measurement date
Measurement date is the date at which the [[fair value]] of the [[equity instrument granted]] ~is measured~. For transactions with **employees** and others providing similar services, the measurement date is [[grant date]]. For transactions with ::parties other than employees:: (and those providing similar services), the measurement date is the date the entity **obtains the goods or the counterparty renders service**.
