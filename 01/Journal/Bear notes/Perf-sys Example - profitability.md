# Perf-sys Example - profitability
Example A company has the following summarised income statements for two consecutive years.

![](Perf-sys%20Example%20-%20profitability/E680C77B-CA2A-460D-8D1E-6621F5A4E1FC.png)
Although the net profit margin is the same for both years at 10%, the gross profit margin is not.
![](Perf-sys%20Example%20-%20profitability/EF5C7719-C621-4860-BA51-81FC991D653F.png)
Is this good or bad for the business?

An increased profit margin must be good because this indicates a wider gap between selling price and cost of sales. Given that the net profit ratio has stayed the same in the second year, however, expenses must be rising. In year 1 expenses were 30% of revenue, whereas in year 2 they were 35% of revenue. This indicates that administration, selling and distribution expenses or interest costs require tight control.
