# Probity and honesty
Hopefully this should be the most self-evident of the principles, relating not only to **telling the truth,** but also **not misleading** shareholders and other stakeholders by presenting information in a biased way.

Probity can be defined in terms of receipt of ~gifts or hospitality~ by trustees. They should certainly not accept gifts or hospitality which may seem likely to influence their decisions.
