# Corporate Governance Report (CGR)
Listed companies are required to include a CGR in each annual report and summary financial report (if any). The rules on the CGR set out two levels of disclosure:
- [[Mandatory disclosure requirements]]: Failure to include these mandatory disclosure in the CGR will be regarded by the Hong Kong Stock Exchange as a ::breach of the Listing Rules.::
- [[Recommended disclosures]]: The Hong Kong Stock Exchange notes that the list of recommended disclosures is provided for listed companies' references and is not intended to be exhaustive or mandatory. The level of detail needed varies with the nature and complexity of issuers' business activities. Issuers are ::encouraged to include:: the recommended disclosure information in their Corporate Governance Report.
