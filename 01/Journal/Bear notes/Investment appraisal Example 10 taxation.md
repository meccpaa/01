# Investment appraisal Example 10: taxation
A company is considering whether or not to purchase an item of machinery costing $400,000 payable immediately. It would have a life of four years, after which it would be sold for $50,000. The machinery would create annual cost savings of $140,000.

The company pays tax one year in arrears at an annual rate of 16.5% and can claim tax-allowable depreciation on a 25% reducing balance basis. A balancing allowance is claimed in the final year of operation. The company's cost of capital is 8%.

Should the machinery be purchased?
- - - -
## Solution
![](Investment%20appraisal%20Example%2010%20taxation/510DD0B1-C161-4829-ACCA-09E9491C456C.png)
There are extra tax payments on annual cost savings of $140,000 = 0.165 x 140,000 = $23,100

Calculation of NPV
![](Investment%20appraisal%20Example%2010%20taxation/3D57F59F-7D3E-4CE1-B640-97CD2DEC47AA.png)
The net present value is $73,713 and so the purchase appears to be worthwhile.
