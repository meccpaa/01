# prescribed course of education
'Prescribed course of education' means a **course undertaken to gain or maintain qualifications for use in any employment** and being:

(i) A course of education provided by an education provider;
(ii) A training or development course provided by a trade, professional or business association; or
(iii) A training or development course accredited or recognised by an institution specified in Schedule 13 of the IRO (commencing from 1 April 2004).
