# for any period
The law allows a deduction of expenses incurred in the production of chargeable profits 'for any period'. It is not necessary that an expense is only deductible in the basis period in which the related income is assessed. An expense may be deducted in the basis period in which the expense is incurred, and the related income **may be charged to tax in a prior or subsequent year of assessment** (Lo and Lo and Secan).
