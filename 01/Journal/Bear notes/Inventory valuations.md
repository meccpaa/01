# Inventory valuations
Inventories should be valued in the same manner in both interim reports and final accounts, though the valuation in interim reports may rely more heavily on estimates.

The net realisable value of inventories should be estimated from selling prices and the related costs to complete and dispose of at interim dates.

HKAS 34.41
