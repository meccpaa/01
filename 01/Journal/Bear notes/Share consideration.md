# Share consideration
Where consideration takes the form of equity or other shares of the investor, the transaction may be referred to as a **share-for-share exchange**. In other words the shareholders of the acquiree are exchanging their shares in that company for shares in the acquirer.

Shares that are issued by the acquirer as consideration are measured at their fair value on the acquisition date, regardless of whether they are issued immediately or at some date in the future.
