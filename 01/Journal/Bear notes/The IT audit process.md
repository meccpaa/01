# The IT audit process
The audit of computerised business systems follows the same process as a standard audit, from gaining an understanding of the environment and carrying out a risk assessment to performing further audit procedures to support the audit conclusion.

In auditing a computerised system, the auditor may audit around a computer, or audit through a computer.
