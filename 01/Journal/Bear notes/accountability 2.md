# accountability
In the context of agency, accountability (agency) means that the agent  
	- is ~answerable under the contract to his principal~ and
	- must ~account for the resources of his principal~ and the ~money he has gained working on his principal's behalf~.

Two ::problems:: potentially arise with this:
- How does the principal ~enforce~ this accountability (the agency problem see below)? As we shall see, the ~corporate governance systems~ developed to monitor the behaviour of directors have been designed to address this issue; and
- What if the agent is **accountable to parties other than his principal** – how does he reconcile possibly conflicting duties?
