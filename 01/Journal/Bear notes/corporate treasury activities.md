# corporate treasury activities
'Corporate treasury activity' is defined in s.14C(1) as

(a) The carrying on an [[intra-group financing business]] (the business of the borrowing of money from and lending of money to an associated corporation);
(b) Providing a [[corporate treasury service]] to an associated corporation; or
(c) Entering into a [[corporate treasury transaction]] undertaken on its own account and related to the business of an associated corporation.
