# Non-sampling risk
Non-sampling risk is the risk that the auditor reaches an erroneous conclusion for any reason not related to sampling risk.
- - - -
For example, most audit evidence is persuasive rather than conclusive, the auditor might use ::inappropriate procedures:: or the auditor might ::misinterpret evidence:: or ::fail to recognise a misstatement or deviation::.

non-sampling risk, may be reduced by effective engagement planning, supervision and review.
