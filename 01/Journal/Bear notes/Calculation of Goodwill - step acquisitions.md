# Calculation of Goodwill - step acquisitions
Where control is achieved any previously held interest is revalued to fair value; the gain or loss is recognised in profit or loss.

On the date on which control is achieved, previously held interests (measured in accordance with HKFRS 9 or HKAS 28 (2011)) are remeasured to fair value and this value is included in calculating goodwill.

Any gain or loss arising from the remeasurement will be recognised in profit or loss.
