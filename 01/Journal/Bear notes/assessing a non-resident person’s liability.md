# assessing a non-resident person’s liability
There are three alternative methods of assessing a non-resident person's liability to tax in Hong Kong:
- **[[s.20A direct assessment of non-resident / in the name of agent]]**
	1. s.20A direct assessment on a non-resident person 
	2. s.20A assessment raised in the name of a non-resident person’s agent in Hong Kong
		- [[s.20A(3) Goods on consignment]]
		- [[s.20AA brokers and approved investment advisers not to be treated as agents of non-resident persons]]
- **[[s.20B - withholding obligations on resident persons paying or crediting certain payments to non-resident persons]]**
	3. s.20B Assessment raised in the name of any person in Hong Kong who has directly or indirectly paid or credited a non-resident person who is entitled to receive payment of royalties or license fees from Hong Kong, or who is an entertainer or sportsman and has performed in Hong Kong
- [[s.20 business with closely connected resident persons]] - the IRD seldom invokes s.20, other than in ~blatant avoidance~ cases
