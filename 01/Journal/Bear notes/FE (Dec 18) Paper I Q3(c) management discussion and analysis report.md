# FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Q3(c) management discussion and analysis report
(c) Discuss YK & Co’s responsibility regarding the Management Discussion and Analysis Report with reference to the relevant auditing standards.
[[FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Case]]
(5 marks)
- - - -
The Management Discussion and Analysis Report is attached with the financial statements; it is considered as “Other Information”.

According to HKSA 720 (Revised) The Auditor’s Responsibilities Relating to Other Information, YK & Co shall read the other information and consider whether there are:

(i) ::material inconsistencies:: between the other information and the **financial statements**; and
(ii) material inconsistencies between the other information and the **auditor’s knowledge** obtained in the audit.

Since the management stressed that the Joint Support supplements are the best-selling products rather than the Vitamins and Minerals products which are always the best-selling products of the Company, a ~material inconsistency appears to exist~.

YK & Co shall **discuss with the management** why such a representation is made in the Management Discussion and Analysis Report. If necessary, YK & Co shall perform **other procedures** to conclude whether a material misstatement exists in the Management Discussion and Analysis Report.

If YK & Co concludes that there is a material misstatement in the Management Discussion and Analysis Report, they should ask the **management to correct** the other information. If the management ::refuses::, they should **communicate** the matter with those charged with governance and **consider the implication** for the auditor’s report.
