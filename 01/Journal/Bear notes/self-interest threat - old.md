# self-interest threat - old
The HKICPA Code of Ethics highlights a great number of areas in which a self-interest threat might arise.
![](self-interest%20threat%20-%20old/image.png)
- [[SIT: financial interests]]
- [[SIT: close business relationship]] - so significant no safeguards
- [[SIT: employment with the entity]]
- [[SIT: partner on entity board]] - should not serve, unless purely admin roles - Company secretary
- [[SIT: family and personal relationships]] - depend on (1) the responsibilities on the assurance engagement; (2) closeness; (3) role of the other party at the entity —>
	- significant influence —> removing the individual
	- close family relationship —> remove the individual
- [[SIT: gifts and hospitality]] - unless trivial —> decline the gifts
- [[SIT: loans and guarantees]] - arm’s length; not with an entity that is not a bank or similar institution
- [[SIT: overdue fees]] - effectively offering a loan to an entity; ensure fees charged are fair, not to build up
- [[SIT: percentage or contingent fees]] assurance engagement - should not enter
- [[SIT: undue dependence on total fees]]
- [[SIT: lowballing]] - significant low fee
- [[SIT significant low fee]]
- [[SIT: recruitment]]
- [[SIT: receiving or paying referral and commission]]
