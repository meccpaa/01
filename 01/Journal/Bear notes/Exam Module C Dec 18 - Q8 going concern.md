# Exam Module C: Dec 18 - Q8 going concern
Fast Growth Limited (“Fast Growth”) was incorporated in Hong Kong in 2016. The principal business of Fast Growth is the trading and manufacturing of high performance gaming hardware, software and systems.

During the year ended 2018, the revenue and operating costs of Fast Growth grew by 5 times and 20 times, respectively. While Fast Growth benefited from the rapid growth of the gaming industry, its research and development expenditure was huge.

As at 31 December 2018, Fast Growth incurred net losses of HK$8 million and its current liabilities exceeded its current assets by HK$10 million. The current liabilities of HK$20 million mainly represented a loan from a bank which will mature in November 2019.

Management of Fast Growth has prepared a cash flow forecast up to 31 December 2019 and drafted certain disclosures in the financial statements explaining the uncertainty relating to going concern and management’s plan to address the uncertainty. Based on their **forecast**, management is ~confident that the use of the going concern basis of accounting~ in preparing the financial statements is appropriate. Management explained the following:
* Forecasted net profit of 2019 approximates HK$20 million
* They are in discussion with the bank to roll over the HK$20 million loan for one year. There is currently no indicator that the roll over is not possible
* Management expects that there will be an additional HK$10 million fund from a venture capital available in February 2019

Required:

(a) Assume you are the auditor of Fast Growth for the year ended 31 December 2018, propose audit procedures in evaluating the **appropriateness** of the management’s use of the [[going concern basis of accounting]] in the preparation of the financial statements by Fast Growth. (8 marks)

(b) Assume there is no significant audit findings identified during the audit and the conclusion of the management’s use of going concern basis of accounting assumption is appropriate, prepare the sections on the auditor’s opinion, basis of opinion and material uncertainty related to going concern in the auditor’s report. (7 marks)
