# Exam practice - Capital structure - EFG Limited
38 minutes

(a) EFG Limited, a Hong Kong listed company, has 200,000 bonds outstanding that are selling at 97.5% of face value of $1,000 each. Bonds with similar characteristics are yielding 6.75% pre-tax. The company also has 2,250,000 shares of 7% preferred stock and 7,500,000 shares of common stock outstanding. The preferred stock sells for $50 a share. The common stock has a beta of 1.34 and sells for $62 a share. The risk free rate is 2.8% and the return on the market is 11.2%. The corporate tax rate is 16.5%.

(i) What is the firm’s weighted average cost of capital?
(5 marks)

(ii) Given the cost of debt is generally lower than the cost of equity, why will a company only leverage (borrow) to a certain level instead of exhausting its borrowing power before going to equity? (3 marks)

(b) EFG Limited plans to continue further investment to expand its business. One of the directors suggested to use retained earnings first, followed by debt before issuing new shares to finance investment. This priority is based on increasing cash issuing cost. EFG Limited has been in business for only a few years and it considers its current capital structure as optimal. It has not accumulated substantial amount of cash. Due to its success, its share price is at an all time high.

(i) Explain the capital structure theory on which the director based his suggestions and the characteristics of a company using this theory in managing capital structure. (5 marks)

(ii) Comment on the director’s capital structure theory in (i) from the perspective of its implications for company valuation, capital budgeting analysis, actual cost of financing and financial flexibility. (8 marks)

(Total = 21 marks) HKICPA December 2014
