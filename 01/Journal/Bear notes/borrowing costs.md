# borrowing costs
Interest and other costs incurred by an entity in connection with the borrowing of funds.
- - - -
The standard lists what may be included in borrowing costs:
(a) Interest expense calculated using the effective interest method as described in HKFRS 9 Financial Instruments
(b) Interest in respect of lease liabilities recognised in accordance with HKFRS 16 Leases (see Chapter 9)
(c) Exchange differences arising from foreign currency borrowings to the extent they are regarded as an adjustment to interest costs
