# First set of HKFRS financial statements
An entity's first set of HKFRS financial statements are the first financial statements to which it applies HKFRS. In this set of financial statements the entity must make an explicit and unreserved statement of compliance with HKFRS.

Financial statements prepared in accordance with HKFRS are an entity's first set of HKFRS financial statements if, for example:

(a) The entity presented its most recent financial statements:
	- In accordance with national requirements that are not consistent with HKFRS in all respects;
	- In conformity with HKFRS in all respects except that the financial statements did not contain an explicit and unreserved statement that they complied with HKFRS;
	- Containing an explicit statement of compliance with some, but not all, HKFRS;
	- In accordance with national requirements inconsistent with HKFRS, using some individual HKFRS to account for items for which national requirements did not exist; or
	- In accordance with national requirements, with a reconciliation of some amounts to the amounts determined in accordance with HKFRS;

(b) The entity prepares financial statements in accordance with HKFRS for internal use only;

(c) The entity prepared a reporting package in accordance with HKFRS for consolidation purposes without preparing a complete set of financial statements in accordance with HKAS 1;

(d) The entity did not present financial statements for previous periods.

HKFRS 1 does not apply when:

(a) An entity stops presenting financial statements in accordance with national requirements, having previously presented them as well as another set of financial statements that contained an explicit and unreserved statement of compliance with HKFRS;

(b) An entity presented financial statements in the previous year in accordance with national requirements and those financial statements contained an explicit and unreserved statement of compliance with HKFRS; or

(c) An entity presented financial statements in the previous year that contained an explicit and unreserved statement of compliance with HKFRS, even if the auditors qualified their audit report on those financial statements.

## 3.1.1 Transition date
HKFRS must be applied not only to current year amounts in the first set of HKFRS financial statements, but also comparative amounts. Therefore the date of transition is the start of the comparative period. An opening statement of financial position must be prepared as at this date.

## 3.1.2 Contents of first set of HKFRS financial statements
The first set of HKFRS financial statements must include:
- Two statements of profit or loss and other comprehensive income (for the current and prior year)
- Two statements of cash flows (for the current and prior year)
- Two statements of changes in equity (for the current and prior year)
- Three statements of financial position (at the end of the current reporting period, at the end of the prior reporting period and at the transition date) 

A third statement of financial position at the transition date is a requirement of HKAS 1, because the transition to reporting under HKFRS is a change in accounting policy.

Note that financial statements in respect of the prior year will not agree to those previously reported for the same period under another GAAP.
