# FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Q8 Office taxability
Tom Wong spent only 10 days in Hong Kong during the year ended 31 March 2018. He would like to claim that the remuneration of HK$0.3 million he received from being an Independent Non-Executive Director for the year of assessment 2017/18 should not be taxable in Hong Kong.

Evaluate whether this is a valid argument and what other factors, if any, should be considered.

(6 marks)
- - - -
[[Salaries Tax]]
[[director - taxable?]]

Since Tom was an INED, he received income in his capacity as a director, i.e. income from an office under s.8(1)(a), and the 60-day rule under s.8(1B) would not be applicable.
[[S.8(1B) ≤ 60 days]]

Instead, the source of Tom’s office income depends on the ~residency of SSHI~.

If SSHI is ~generally managed and controlled~ in Hong Kong (e.g. **directors’ meetings** are generally held in Hong Kong), Tom’s income will be onshore sourced and taxable.
