# discount rate - lease
The discount rate applied to the [[future lease payments]] to calculate their present value is the **rate implicit in the lease agreement** or, if this cannot be determined, the **[[lessee’s incremental borrowing rate]]**, defined as 'the rate of interest that a lessee would have to pay to borrow over a similar term, and with a similar security, the funds necessary to obtain an asset of a similar value to the [[right-of-use asset]] in a similar economic environment'.
