# investment appraisal self-test 1: PV
QPR Limited is considering undertaking an investment in an infrastructure project to build a bridge in a country where inflation is currently running at 12% and the currency is the florin (FL). The project is expected to take four years and QPR expects to invest FL250,000 at the beginning of the project, the project is expected to require further investment at the beginning of each subsequent year, this amount is expected to reduce by 10% per annum in real terms. At the end of the project QPR expects to receive payment for the bridge of FL2 million. QPR uses a discount rate of 16% to appraise infrastructure investments.

Required

Calculate the present value to QPR of undertaking this project.
