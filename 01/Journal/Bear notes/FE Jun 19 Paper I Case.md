# FE Jun 19 Paper I Case
CASE 

Brilliant Education Holding Ltd (“BEHL”) was incorporated in Hong Kong in 2007. BEHL and its subsidiaries (the “Group”) were founded and wholly owned by Dr Albert Kam, a child psychologist. The Group operates under the trade name “Brilliant Kids”. 
Recognising that children learn a second language the most between birth and five years of age, the Group has been engaging in developing and selling children’s English educational kits in Hong Kong. After a decade of growth, Brilliant Kids has become a common household name in Hong Kong. 
The Group has a financial year end of 30 June, and plans to list on the Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited in the coming few years. The principal activities of BEHL and its subsidiaries include: 
* Design and develop English learning materials, and license the use of the materials to fellow subsidiaries of the Group and third parties. 

* Sell English educational kits to retail customers at its online store and store outlets in Causeway Bay, Kowloon Tong and Shatin.

The principal activity of BE Consulting is designing and developing English learning kits for use by fellow subsidiaries. BE Consulting self-develops and owns all the copyrights of the educational content developed.

## License arrangements concluded by BE Consulting 
### License arrangement between BE Consulting and BE Trading 
Under the license agreement between BE Consulting and BE Trading, BE Consulting grants a license to BE Trading, which in turn, allows third-party contract manufacturers to use the copyright for the manufacturing and sales of educational kits traded by BE Trading. The license fees or royalties payable to BE Consulting are based on the number of educational kits sold in each quarter. 
### License arrangement with Sommana Group (Thailand) Limited 
BE Consulting has entered into a license agreement with Sommana Group (Thailand) Limited (“SGL”), a third party Thailand publisher for the content of the “Sing along with Kocky” series. SGL is involved in publishing English learning materials in Thailand. Besides, it also has around 30 learning centres in Bangkok delivering English learning courses which targets children and young adults in Thailand. Under the License agreement, BE Consulting granted the rights for SGL to assess and publish all the latest content of “Sing along with Kocky” series exclusively in Thailand for a license period of three years from 1 January 2016 to 31 December 2018. There is no minimum guaranteed level of payment under the license agreement and the Group would receive a share of 3% of the proceeds in return. The term of the license agreement stated that either parties can terminate the contract by paying a penalty of four million Thai Baht (THB). 
### License arrangement with Advance Press Pte Ltd 
BE Consulting has further entered into a license agreement with Advance Press Pte Ltd (“Advance Press”), a third party Singapore publisher for the content of the “Sing along with Kocky” series on 1 February 2018. Advance Press is a subsidiary of Future Press Group Limited (“Future Press”), a listed company in Singapore with sound financial background. The Group has had a business relationship with Future Press for more than three years and there is no history of default and late payment. 
Under the license agreement, BE Consulting granted Advance Press the right to assess and publish all the latest content of the “Sing along with Kocky” series exclusively in Singapore and Malaysia for a license period of four years from 1 April 2018 to 31 March 2022. In return, BEConsulting would receive royalties at a fixed fee of US$2 million equivalent to HK$15.6 million in each year of the four-year license period plus a share at 5% of the proceeds from publishing the “Sing along with Kocky” series, which would be settled on a quarterly basis during the four-year license period. However, the royalties are subject to withholding tax in Singapore for which has to be borne by BE Consulting. The withholding tax is applied on the gross amount of the royalties received. 

### Right to use US cartoon characters “Rabbit Pomi Family” from Beyond Imagination Inc. 
On 12 March 2018, BE Consulting obtained a license from Beyond Imagination Inc. (“Beyond Imagination”), a third party in the United States, to use the images of the latter’s cartoon characters “Rabbit Pomi Family” in the Group’s educational kits for a license period of three years from 1 April 2018 to 31 March 2021. The royalties payable by BE Consulting to Beyond Imagination is US$8 per copy (equivalent to HK$62.4 per copy) of educational kit incorporating “Rabbit Pomi Family” sold in each quarter during the license period. 
During the period from 1 April 2018 to 30 June 2018, 12,400 copies of educational kit incorporating “Rabbit Pomi Family” were sold. However, according to the terms of the license agreement with Beyond Imagination, BE Consulting was required to make a non-refundable advance payment of US$300,000 (equivalent to HK$2,340,000) to Beyond Imagination on 1 April 2018 (i.e. upon commencement of the license period). The management of BE Consulting estimated that the advance payment would be fully recouped through royalties payable to Beyond Imagination within the license period. 
## Litigation and settlement 
In March 2014, BE Consulting filed a complaint in the Court of First Instance against Sunshine Academy (HK) Ltd (“Sunshine Academy”) for infringement of its copyright in the “Kocky’s animal farm” series, and seeking an injunction and monetary damages. On 13 June 2018, BE Consulting and Sunshine Academy signed a settlement agreement under which Sunshine Academy agreed to pay BE Consulting HK$1.2 million in full and finalise settlement of all claims against Sunshine Academy. HK$1.2 million represents the estimated loss of sale during the period from February 2013 to March 2014. The litigation against Sunshine Academy was dismissed by a court order on 20 June 2018. The agreement eliminated the need for any further court proceedings or decisions relating to damages or injunctive relief. BE Consulting received the compensation of HK$1.2 million from Sunshine Academy on 4 July 2018.

## Expansion plan of the Group 
In order to prepare for the listing of the Group, the Management has actively expanded the 
business in Hong Kong, mainland China and other countries. 
### Online mobile application 
To further promote the Group in Hong Kong and expand its sources of revenue, BEHL established a new subsidiary BE Technology in January 2018. BE Technology entered into license agreement with BE Consulting, under which BE Consulting would grant the right to publish the mobile games developed by BEConsulting exclusively in BE Technology’s platform at a fixed fee of HK$120,000 for each game. BE Technology has also engaged an independent computer programmer in Hong Kong as a consultant to assist the initial set up of a mobile application platform. The contracting fee for the consultant is HK$250,000. 
### Expanding into mainland China 
The Sales Director of BE Trading noted that more online sales were made to customers in mainland China. In September 2017, Mr Simon Hung, Sales Director of BE Trading, was assigned to lead a team to explore BE Trading’s market expansion plan in mainland China. During the period from 1 October 2017 to 30 June 2018, Mr Hung and his teammates travelled to three first-tier cities in mainland China, and stayed in each city for two weeks to four weeks at most. No business trips are planned for the second half of 2018. 

### Strategic alliance with Beyond Imagination Inc. 
As part of the Group’s long term strategies, the Group has acquired 10 million ordinary shares, representing 1% of issued share capital of Beyond Imagination, from Mr Stephen Patterson, the founder and controlling shareholder, at approximately US$1 million (equivalent to HK$7,800,000) on 8 February 2018. The fair value of the shares was US$1.2 million (equivalent to HK$9,360,000) on 30 June 2018. 
### Plan to expand to Thailand 
The Group also considers expanding its business in Thailand and is now in the process of formulating the expansion strategy by either setting up a new company or through acquisition in Thailand. 
As the Group has established a long-term relationship with SGL, Ms Kelly Tang, the Chief Financial Officer (“CFO”), suggested to expand through acquisition of 75% equity interest in SGL (the “Acquisition”). The Acquisition will be at the consideration of THB80 million settled in cash. 
Based on this proposed acquisition, assuming that the cash flows take place at the end of the year and the growth rate is insignificant, it is estimated that this investment will generate a return of THB10 million, THB12 million, THB30 million and THB35 million after one, two, three and four years of the investment, respectively; and no cash flow starting from year 5 onwards. The required rate of return on the investment is 8%. 
The Group also performed the below assessment as if the Acquisition has taken place on 31 August 2018: 
* based on the terms of the license agreement previously signed between the Group and SGL, it was assessed that it could receive a sharing of 5% (instead of 3%) if the Group entered into a similar license agreement with other parties. Both the Group and SGL did not record any assets or liabilities in relation to the license agreement. 

* the fair value of the signed license agreement was THB20 million, which includes an amount of THB6 million by which the contract is unfavourable for the estimated sharing in the future, which are below the current market rates. 

* the fair value of net assets of SGL on 31 August 2018 was THB60 million (excluding the fair value of the license agreement). 
The Group planned to elect to measure the non-controlling interest in SGL at the non-controlling interest’s proportionate share of SGL’s identifiable net assets and estimated to incur acquisition-related costs of HK$800,000.
