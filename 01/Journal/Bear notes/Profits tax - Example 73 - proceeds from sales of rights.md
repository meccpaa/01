# Profits tax - Example 73 - proceeds from sales of rights
Co HK purchased a registered trade mark at a cost of $2 million during 2014/15. The trade mark was was used partly in the production of chargeable profits, and the allowable proportion is 50% and 60% for 2014/15 and 2015/16 respectively. Deductions of the purchase cost in the amount of $200,000 and $240,000 would be allowed for 2014/15 and 2015/16 respectively, calculated as follows:

![](Profits%20tax%20-%20Example%2073%20-%20proceeds%20from%20sales%20of%20rights/B6CB1A91-0A5F-4F69-A1F9-554896C1133A.png)

During 2016/17, Co HK sold the trade mark for $3 million. The relevant sale proceeds are $1.65 million ($3m x 55%). The unallowed amount is $660,000 [($2m – $800,000) x 55%], which is less than the relevant sale proceeds. The deemed trading receipt in 2016/17 is $990,000 ($1.65m $660,000), but is restricted to the deduction previously allowed of $440,000 (s.16EB(2)(b)).
