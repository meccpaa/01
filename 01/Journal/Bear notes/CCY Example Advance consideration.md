# CCY Example: Advance consideration
Ashby entered into a contract on 18 August 20X6 to purchase a machine for use in its business at a cost of £210,000. A 25% deposit was payable on 31 August 20X6 and the balance of payment was to be made 60 days after delivery. Delivery of the machine was made on 1 November 20X6. Ashby's functional currency is the Hong Kong Dollar.

Exchange rates were as follows:

18 August 20X6 		HK1: £0.10 
31 August 20X6 		HK1: £0.12
1 November 20X6 	HK1: £0.115
30 November 20X6	HK1: £0.11

Required
What journal entries are made in respect of the transaction in Ashby's accounts in the year ended 30 November 20X6?
- - - -
### Solution

**31 August 20X6**
DEBIT 	Prepayment (25% x £210,000/0.12) = $437,500
CREDIT	Bank
To recognize the advance consideration paid.

**1 November 20X6**
DEBIT	PPE				$437,500
CREDIT	Prepayment		$437,500
To transfer the advance consideration to PPE when the asset is delivered and

DEBIT 	PPE	(75% x £210,000 / 0.115) = $1,369,565
CREDIT	Payable
To recognize the balance of payment for the PPE.

The PPE is therefore initially measured at $1,807,065 (437,500 + 1,369,565)

The payable is a monetary item and is retranslated at the reporting date to $1,431,818 (75% x £210,000/0.11).

**30 November 20X6**
DEBIT 	Exchange loss (1,431,818 - 1,369,565) = $62,253
CREDIT	Payable
