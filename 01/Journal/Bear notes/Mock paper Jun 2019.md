# Mock paper Jun 2019
[[HKICPA QP FE Jun 2019 Mock paper: case]]
### Question 1 (25 marks – approximately 45 minutes) 
Assume you are Mabel Chan, the Finance Manager of Brilliant Group. With reference to the relevant financial reporting standards, advise the CFO, Ivan Luk, on the followings: 
Required: 
(a) the initial recognition and subsequent measurement of the lease arrangement and the underlying property to: 
* (i)  the financial statements of MESL 
* (ii)  the financial statements of SML 
* (iii)  the consolidated financial statements of Brilliant Group 
Provide supporting calculations. Journal entries are not required.  (13 marks) 
 
(b)  the accounting treatment of the convertible bonds, including its conversion, from 1 April 2018 to 31 March 2019 in the financial statements of SBL. Journal entries are not required.  (6 marks) 

* (c)  apart from part (b) above, the effect of the conversion of the convertible bonds at 31 March 2019 in the consolidated financial statements of Brilliant Group. Journal entries are not required. 
(6 marks) 

### Question 2 (6 marks - approximately 11 minutes) 
Assume the CFO has adopted the accounting treatment suggested by the Financial Manager of Brilliant Group and classified the lease as operating lease. 

A&B Co., an international CPA firm, offers tax advisory services and audit services to Brilliant and its subsidiaries. 

Assume A&B Co. is appointed as the component auditor of MESL and you are the audit manager auditing the financial statements of the MESL for the year ended 31 March 2019. 

Required: 
As the component auditor of MESL, propose substantive audit procedures to verify the classification assertion of the lease arrangement.  (6 marks)

### Question 5 (6 marks – approximately 11 minutes) 
MESL has branches in PRC, Hong Kong, Taiwan and New Zealand in order to provide its services in different locations. MESL has exposed tax in many countries. Due to the complexity of tax, MESL has approached A&B Co. to provide tax advisory service. 

Required: 
Identify and explain the ethical issues that A&B Co. may encounter for providing tax advisory service to MESL and discuss any safeguards or actions A&B Co. could implement. (6 marks) 


### Question 6 (5 marks – approximately 9 minutes) 
After discussion with the financial controller of MESL, you noted that MESL has invested HK$10 million on a project for developing a new model of diving equipment which is planned to be launched for sales in the coming 3 to 4 years. 

Required: 
Assume the management recognises the costs as intangible asset, propose the substantive audit procedures to verify whether the HK$10 million meets the recognition criteria as an asset. (5 marks)
