# Source of Rental Income
![](Source%20of%20Rental%20Income/image.png)

## Immovable property
[[Situs (or location) test]] [[DIPN 21 (Revised)]]
The relevant test in determining the source of rental income from immovable property is the situs test. Activities occurring elsewhere in connection with the letting of the property such as negotiation and conclusion of the lease agreement are not taken into account. DIPN 21 (Revised) indicates that the location (the situs) of the immovable property is the source of the rental income.

## Movable property
1. Ships and aircrafts [[Special businesses]] s.23B, 23C, 23D and 23E
2. sums received by or accrued to a person by way of hire, rental or similar charges for the use of, or the right to use, movable property in Hong Kong: s.15(1)(d) ([[s.15(1)(d) - movable property]])
(sums are not otherwise chargeable to profits tax)
- - - -
3. In general, where the machines were used by the lessee [[Situs (or location) test]]
4. However, it is possible viewed as arising in the place **where the taxpayer provides the movable property** to the lessee if there are ~a number of short-term leases~ and that the lessor ~exercises no control on the place(s)~ where the movable property is to be used.
- - - -
6. From machinery or plant: non-taxable in cases where the recipient is not allowed to claim depreciation allowance in respect of the property by virtue of s.39E. [[DIPN 15 (revised)]]
7. Disallow depreciation allowances on leased machinery or plant if any of the independent conditions are satisfied {S.39E)
	- One such condition is that 'the machinery or plant, not being a ship or aircraft ... is, while the lease is in force, used wholly or principally outside Hong Kong by a person other than the taxpayer'.
