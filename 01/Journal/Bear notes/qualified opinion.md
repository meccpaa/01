# qualified opinion
A qualified opinion is expressed when:
(a) The auditor, having **obtained** [[sufficient appropriate audit evidence]], concludes that misstatements, individually or in the aggregate, are **material**, but ::not:: [[pervasive - HKSA 705]], to the financial statements.

(b) The auditor is ::unable to obtain:: sufficient appropriate audit evidence on which to base the opinion, but the auditor concludes that the **possible effects** on the financial statements of ~undetected misstatements~, if any, could be **material** but ::not:: [[pervasive - HKSA 705]].
