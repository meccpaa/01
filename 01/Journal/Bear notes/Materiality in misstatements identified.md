# Materiality in misstatements identified
Auditors should assess whether the aggregate of uncorrected misstatements that have been identified during the audit is material in all aspects when evaluating whether the financial statements have been prepared within an ~applicable financial reporting framework~. We will discuss this further when studying HKSA 265 (Clarified) in Chapter 15. 

If the auditor has identified a material misstatement resulting from error, the auditor should **communicate** the misstatements to the appropriate level of management and consider the need to report it ~to those charged with governance in accordance~ with HKSA 260 (Revised).
