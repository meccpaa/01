# Practical expedient
A lessee may ::elect not to separate:: ~non-lease components~ and instead to account for each lease component and related non-lease components **together**, ~as a lease component~.

As a result, the **initial measurement** of a lease asset and liability are ~increased~.

A lessee may make the election to use the **practical expedient** by ~class of underlying assets~. It is not available in respect of [[HKFRS 9: embedded derivatives]] that must be separated from a host contract in order to comply with HKFRS 9.
