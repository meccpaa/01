# Profits tax - Example 13 - manufacturing business
A company carries on a manufacturing business in Hong Kong, and operates a server (at its disposal) located outside Hong Kong. The company supplies details of its products and prices, negotiates the terms with clients, accepts orders and receives payments through the server. If the company's manufacturing processes are carried out in Hong Kong, the profits are sourced in Hong Kong even though the goods are sold to overseas customers. In this case, the income is chargeable to Hong Kong profits tax.
