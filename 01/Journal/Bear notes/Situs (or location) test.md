# Situs (or location) test
For other passive income earned by the possession of asset or properties, such as dividends or rent, the operations test will also not be appropriate as the taxpayer **does not need to perform any activities** other than making and holding the investment. In such circumstances, the situs test may be used. The test is ' ~the situation/location of the asset or property from which income is derived~'.
