# Change in functional currency
The functional currency of an entity can be changed **only if there is a change to the underlying transactions, events and conditions that are relevant to the entity**. For example, an entity's functional currency may change if there is a change in the currency that mainly influences the sales price of goods and services.

Where there is a change in an entity's functional currency, the entity translates all items into the new functional currency **prospectively** (i.e., from the date of the change) using the exchange rate at the date of the change.
