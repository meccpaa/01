# s.20ACA exemption for special purpose vehicles SPVs
Exemption is extended to the profits of a SPV derived from a transaction in securities in an interposed SPV or an eligible private company, i.e. gains from the disposal of an EPC or from the disposal of a SPV that owns an EPC (s.20ACA(1)).

For this purpose, the SPV may be a corporation, partnership, trustee of a trust estate or any other entity incorporated, registered or appointed in or outside Hong Kong; and must be wholly or partly owned by a non-resident person (i.e. an offshore fund). Moreover, the SPV is not itself an EPC (s.20ACA(2)).

However, the SPV **must be** ~established solely for the purpose of holding~ (directly or indirectly) and ~administering one or more EPCs~, and ::must not:: ~carry on any trade or business in Hong Kong~ (s.20ACA(2)). The SPV ::should not:: engage in an active business with trading transactions; and cannot derive service fees from the non-resident person for the provision of services. It is expected to only derive passive dividend income from one or more EPCs (DIPN 51, para 48). Hence, **activities of the SPV** are restricted to:
(a) Reviewing financial statements of EPCs;
(b) Attending shareholders' meetings of EPCs;
(c) Opening bank accounts to collect dividends and investment disposal proceeds; and
(d) Appointing company secretary and auditor (DIPN 51, para 49).
