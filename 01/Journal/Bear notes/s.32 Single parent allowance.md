# s.32 Single parent allowance
## Scope
A single person who has the sole or predominant care of a child

## Conditions
- The taxpayer must be eligible to claim [[s.31 Child allowance]];
- A person shall not be entitled to single parent allowance if at any time during the year of assessment the person was ::married and not living apart from the spouse::;
- Single parent allowance can be **apportioned** by reference to the periods of the sole or predominant care of the child.
