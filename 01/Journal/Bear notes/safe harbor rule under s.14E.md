# safe harbor rule under s.14E
If a CTC undertakes other activities in Hong Kong, it may still be regarded as a QCTC if it satisfies the prescribed safe harbour rule. A CTC satisfies the sale harbor rule for a year of assessment if it falls within the 1-year safe harbor or the multiple-year safe harbour (s.14E(1)).

(a) **1-year safe harbour** – passes both the corporate treasury profits ('CTP') and corporate treasury assets ('CTA') tests for the year of assessment concerned (ss.14E(1)(a) & (2));

(b) **Multiple-year safe harbour** – the average percentages of the CTP and CTA for the year of assessment concerned and the preceding one* or two years of assessment pass both tests (ss.14E(1)(b), (3) and (4)).

(* if the CTC carries on a business in Hong Kong for less than 2 years)
- - - -
The CTP test is satisfied if the CTP are not lower than 75% of the total profits, which is calculated based on the accounting profits irrespective of the source of profits. If there is substantial loss, the IRD would consider excluding the loss when computing the percentage for the sale harbour rule.

The CTA test is satisfied if the CTA are not lower than 75% of the total assets, which is calculated based on audited accounts irrespective of the location of the assets. The CTA include fixed assets that are used to carry out the corporate treasury activities, e.g. office equipment. The CTA are apportioned if they are partly used for carrying out a corporate treasury activity and partly for another purpose (s.14E(9)).

If a holding company also acts as a CTC, the Commissioner is prepared to exclude the equity investments in associated corporations and dividends from the denominators (i.e. total profits or assets) in calculating the CTP and CTA percentages.

## Commissioner's determination
If neither of the above conditions are satisfied, the Commissioner may, on application by the taxpayer, exercise his discretion to determine that a CTC is a QCTC if he is of the opinion that the conditions specified in s.14D(3) or the safe harbour rule under s.14E would, in the ordinary course of business of the corporation, have been satisfied for the year of assessment (s.14F(3)).
