# Caishui [2014] No. 43
The Ministry of Finance and the SAT issued Caishui [2014] No. 43 on 29 April 2014 under which VAT reform programme is expanded to include telecommunication sector starting from 1 June 2014. Caishui [2014] No. 43 stipulated an 11% VAT rate for basic telecommunication services and a 6% VAT rate for value-added telecommunication services.
