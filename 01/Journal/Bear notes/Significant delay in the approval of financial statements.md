# Significant delay in the approval of financial statements
When there is a significant delay in approving the financial statements, the auditor shall enquire as to the reasons for the delay. If the auditor believes that the delay could be related to events or conditions relating to the going concern basis of accounting, the auditor shall perform those additional audit procedures necessary, as well as consider the effect on the auditor's conclusion regarding the existence of a material uncertainty.
