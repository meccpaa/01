# HKAS 16: Property, plant and equipment - PPE 
![](HKAS%2016%20Property,%20plant%20and%20equipment%20-%20PPE/0AC28B77-92EC-4352-BE6C-10D6B92136DB.png)

`HKAS 16`
For ‘tangible’ non-current assets

[[PPE]] are tangible items that are:
* Held for use in the production or supply of goods or services, for rental to others, or for administrative purposes
* Expected to be used during more than one period 

1. [[Recognition of PPE]]
2. [[Measurement of PPE]]

### Impairment
An [[impairment loss]] should be treated in the same way  as a Revaluation decrease.

### Retirements and disposals
When an asset is permanently withdrawn from use, or sold or scrapped, and no future economic benefits are expected from its [[disposal]], it should be withdrawn from the statement of financial position.
