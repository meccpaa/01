# Exam Module C: Dec 18 - Q4 construction-in-progress
Q4. ELL had a piece of land and certain ~construction-in-progress~ which were recorded as **non-current assets** in its financial statements as at 31 December 2018. The construction-in-progress represented the construction materials purchased and the wage payables to the workers for the construction of ELL’s new factory premises. Although the construction had been commenced before the year end date, the **land certificate** was ~not yet issued~ by the local land bureau. ELL did not have any **official documents** to prove that it had the ~rights and obligation~ to that piece of land at the year end date.
- - - -
(a) Explain the audit objective of the rights and obligation assertions. In view of the specific situation faced by ELL, propose procedures to audit these assertions of the land at the year end date. (5 marks)
[[Tangible non-current assets - audit procedures]]
(b) Propose procedures to audit ELL’s construction-in-progress at the year end date. (5 marks)
- - - -
## Solutions
(a) The audit objective of the rights and obligations assertions is to ensure ELL has rights to the land purchased and recorded at the year end.

If the land certificate was not obtained, it may indicate that ELL did not have the rights to the land purchased at the year end.

The audit team should enquire with the management to understand why the land certificate could not be obtained, whether it was due to any legal issues from executing the sale and purchase agreement or the usual practice for the registration process which requires a longer time to process.

The audit team should ask ELL to consult its legal counsel to provide an opinion whether ELL had the rights to that piece of land.

The audit team should examine such legal opinion to conclude if ELL had such rights at the year end. If it is concluded that ELL did not have such rights at the year end date, the audit team should discuss with the management of ELL to ascertain if appropriate adjustment or additional financial disclosures should be made to the financial statements.
- - - -
(b) The audit team should perform the following procedures:
- Obtain from management the ~schedule~ which lists all the **costs incurred** for the construction in progress, perform casting and cross casting.
- **Agree** the total construction costs from the ~schedule~ to the balance recorded in the ~ledger~.
- For **material items** purchased from third parties, examine the ~underlying invoices, agreements~ or any documentary evidence to verify the amounts.
- For **wages** incurred by the construction workers, examine any documentary evidence or worker confirmations to verify the wages incurred. Consider whether the workers’ wages are eligible for capitalisation.
- Examine the **subsequent year end payments** to ensure ~proper cut-off~ of the construction costs recorded.
- Obtain from management all the ~construction contracts~ to ensure if all the related costs have been recorded at the year end date.
- Review the ~nature~ of the ~construction costs incurred~ to ensure they are related to the constructions.
- Review any ~construction progress report~ or ~perform inspection~ to ensure the construction was in place.
