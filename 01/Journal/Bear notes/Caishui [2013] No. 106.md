# Caishui [2013] No. 106
The Ministry of Finance and the SAT issued Caishui [2013] No. 106 on 13 December 2013. VAT reform programme was expanded to include railway transportation and postal services starting from 1 January 2014.

According to Caishui [2013] No. 106, the applicable VAT rates will be as follows:

- Land transportation, railway transportation, air transportation – 11% 
- Postal services – 11%
- R&D and technology services, technical consulting, technical testing and technical training services – 6%
- Information technology services, business processing management services, audit and tax services – 6%
- Cultural and innovation services, design services – 6%
- Pick-up and delivery services – 6%
- Logistics ancillary services – 6%
- Certification, attestation and consulting services, language translation and interpretation services – 6% 

Taxpayers may choose to calculate their VAT liability under a simplified taxation method. The applicable VAT rate is 3%. No input VAT is creditable.
