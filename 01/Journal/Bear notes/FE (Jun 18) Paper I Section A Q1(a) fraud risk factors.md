## Answer 1(a) fraud risk factors
Fraud risks factors:
Incentive – performance bonus is calculated with reference to KPIs of the Group

Pressure – because of the listing plan, the management may have pressure on meeting the financial thresholds and manipulate the financials to meet the minimum profit requirements

Opportunity – no interface between systems and certain operational / finance personnel who have access to adjust the data in the systems

Fraud risks may happen in the following areas:
(i) Revenue recognition
	- various revenue streams with **different recognition methods** based on different terms and conditions, some of which are rather **complex**; and
	- **no interface** between systems, existence and completeness of transactions depends on manual reconciliation.
(ii) Significant unusual transaction outside the normal course of business
	- **donation** of significant amount of musical instruments, ~existence~ of such transaction and the ~valuation~ of the instruments donated would be a concern; and
	- no such donation has been made previously and so the ~business rationale~ of such a transaction would be a concern.
(iii) Related party transactions
	- various related transactions with a different nature, terms and details, which may be manipulated to impact the Group’s financials.
(iv) Payroll
	- **existence** of over 150 full-time and part-time **instructors**; and
	- the ~fees are shared~ between the Group and the instructors on a ~negotiated basis~.
(v) Business development
	- the Group has **expanded quickly** in both Hong Kong and mainland China, the ~existence / substance~ of ~new branches~ and the existence of ~students~ would be a concern.
