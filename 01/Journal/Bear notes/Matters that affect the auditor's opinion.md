# Matters that affect the auditor's opinion
HKSA 700 (Revised) requires that the auditor shall modify the opinion in the auditor's report in accordance with HKSA 705 (Revised) Modifications to the Opinion in the Independent Auditor's Report when either of the following circumstances exist and, in the auditor's judgment, the effect of the matter is or may be material to the financial statements:

(a) The auditor is **unable to obtain** sufficient appropriate audit evidence to conclude that the financial statements as a whole are free from material misstatement (this is also referred to as a limitation on scope)

(b) The auditor concludes that, based on the audit evidence obtained the financial statements as a whole are **not free from material misstatement**
