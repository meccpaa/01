# Financing - Example 1: Overdrafts
For example, suppose that a business has the following working capital:
![](Financing%20-%20Example%201%20Overdrafts/1D134A4E-954D-4711-B674-313445937F23.png)
It now buys inventory costing $25 million for cash, using its overdraft. Net working capital remains the same, $60 million, although the bank's financial stake has risen from $10 million to $35 million.
![](Financing%20-%20Example%201%20Overdrafts/4DF4E10F-AD57-4465-952B-D9C4A9216020.png)
A bank overdraft provides support for normal trading finance. In this example, finance for normal trading rises from $(100m x 30m) = $70 million to $(125m - 30m) = $95 million and the bank's contribution rises from $10 million out of $70 million to $35 million out of $95 million.
