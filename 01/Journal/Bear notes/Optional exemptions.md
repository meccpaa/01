# Optional exemptions
Optional exemptions are available in areas in which HKICPA believes that the cost of applying HKFRS retrospectively may exceed the benefit. In these cases a first-time adopter may choose to apply policies prospectively from the date of transition.

HKFRS 1 specifies the alternative accounting to be applied when preparing the statement of financial position at the transition date if an exemption is taken.

Please see the transition arrangements in each standard for further details. See HKFRS 2 for transition arrangements.

## 3.4.1 Short-term exemption relating to HKFRS 
`HKFRS 1 Appendix E`

HKFRS 1 currently provides a short-term exemption, in relation to the requirement to restate comparative information for HKFRS 9.

The exemption allows that, where an entity's first HKFRS reporting period begins before 1 January 2019 and it applies HKFRS 9 as issued in 2014, the comparative information presented in the first HKFRS financial statements need not comply with HKFRS 7 Financial Instruments: Disclosures or HKFRS 9 to the extent that the disclosures required by HKFRS 7 relate to items within the scope of HKFRS 9.

Instead, an entity applying this exemption should apply the requirements of its previous GAAP in respect of items within the scope of HKFRS 9 and disclose this fact. Any adjustments between amounts reported in the comparative statement of financial position and the HKFRS statement of financial position at the start of the first HKFRS period are treated as arising from a change in accounting policy and HKAS 8 should be applied.
