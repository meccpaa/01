# FV Example: unit of account
When determining the fair value of an entity's 75 investment in the 100,000 equity shares of another company, the unit of account may either be:

- Each individual share, such that total fair value of the investment is 75,000 u fair value per share, or
- The 75 shareholding, such that the total fair value may be determined to include a control premium.
