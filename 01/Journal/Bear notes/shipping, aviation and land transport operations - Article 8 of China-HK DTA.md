# shipping, aviation and land transport operations - Article 8 of China-HK DTA
Article 8 of China-HK DTA provides that income arising from the operation of ships, aircrafts or land transport vehicles by an enterprise of One Side shall be exempt from tax in the Other Side (except where the ships, aircrafts or land transport vehicles are solely operated between places of the Other Side). The taxes exempt in the mainland of China include Corporate Income Tax and Business Tax. The tax exempt in Hong Kong refers to Profits Tax.
