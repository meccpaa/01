# Communicating with group management and those charged with governance
`HKSA 600.46`
The following matters should be communicated to group management:

(a) Deficiencies in the design or operating effectiveness of group-wide controls.

(b) Deficiencies that the group engagement team has identified in internal controls at components that are judged to be significant to the group.

(c) Deficiencies that component auditors have identified in internal controls at components that are judged to be significant to the group.

(d) Fraud identified by the group engagement team or component auditors or information indicating that a fraud may exist. Timely reporting should be communicated to the group management.

(e) Group management may need to keep certain material sensitive information confidential.
