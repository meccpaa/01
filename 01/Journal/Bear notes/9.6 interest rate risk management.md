# 9.6 interest rate risk management
Interest rate risk can be managed using 
- internal hedging in the form of 
	- asset and liability management, 
	- [[9.6.1 matching and smoothing]] 
-  external hedging instruments such as 
	- [[9.6.2 forward rate agreements (FRAs)]] and 
	- derivatives
	- [[9.6.3 interest rate futures]]
	- [[9.6.4 interest rate options]]
	- [[9.6.5 interest rate swaps]]
