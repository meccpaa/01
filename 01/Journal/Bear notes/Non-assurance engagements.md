# Non-assurance engagements
No assurance is given for compilation or agreed-upon procedures engagements.

HKSRS 4400 Engagements to Perform Agreed-Upon Procedures Regarding Financial Information applies to an agreed-upon procedures engagement where the professional accountant is to carry out procedures of an audit nature, which the professional accountant, the entity and any appropriate third parties have agreed and to report on factual findings.

HKSRS 4410 (Revised) Compilation Engagements governs a compilation engagement where it is for the professional accountant to use his accounting expertise to collect, classify and summarise financial information.

No assurance is expressed for the above engagements.
