# s.16A Special payment under an approved retirement scheme
Pursuant to s.16A, **employers** carrying on a trade, profession or business in Hong Kong are entitled to deductions for:

(a) **Contributions** (other than ordinary annual contributions) to a fund established under a ~RORS~ ([[recognized occupational retirement scheme]]); or
(b) **Premiums** (other than ordinary annual premiums) in respect of a contract of insurance under a ~RORS~; or
(c) **Contributions** (other than regular contributions) paid to a ~MPFS~.

The deduction is allowable on a straight-line basis over five years (i.e. 20% per year of assessment).
