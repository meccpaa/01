# General comparison of HKSAR and PRC tax systems
- Duty on commodities
	- HK: Customs and excise duties on **liquors, tobacco, hydrocarbon oil and methyl alcohol.**
	- PRC: Import customs duty on a wide range of commodities and capital goods; Value-added tax on most locally consumed products and imported commodities; Consumption tax on 14 categories of items, mainly luxury items such as tobacco, alcoholic and non-alcoholic beverages, cosmetics, jewellery, motor vehicles, motor cycles, car tyres, gasoline, diesel, fireworks, golf balls and equipment, premium watches (selling price above RMB10,000), yachts, disposable wooden chopsticks and solid wood flooring; Refund of value-added tax and consumption tax on export goods; Export customs duty on a few goods.
- VAT:
	- HK: None
	- PRC: VAT is a tax on **turnover** rather than on profits. All units and individuals who are engaged in the sales of goods, the provision of processing, repair and replacement services, or the importation of goods, within the PRC territory, are subject to VAT. —- Since 1 August 2013, China has kicked off the national implementation of the VAT reform programme. For example, provision of transportation services, modern services and telecommunication services has since been subject to VAT. —- Furthermore, starting from 1 May 2016, VAT reform programme has expanded to cover the sales of immovable properties, leasing of immovable properties and transfer of land use right, leasing of tangible movable properties as well as provision of construction services, financial services and consumer services. Refer to Chapter 13 for details.

- Rates and Government rent
	- HK: Rates at 5% on rateable value of immovable property; Government rent at 3% on rateable value of certain immovable property after 1 July 1997.
	- PRC: Property tax on leasing property with no rental income charged at 1.2% on a percentage (70% – 90%) of the rateable value of the property.
- Estate duty
	- HK: Charge on property in Hong Kong passing on death of people who died before 11 February 2006. Maximum duty rate is 15%.
	- PRC: None
- Stamp Duty
	- HK: Charge on instruments (including counterparts and duplicates) relating to Hong Kong immovable property, Hong Kong stock and Hong Kong bearer instruments. Duty ranges from HK$3 to 8.5% on the higher of stated consideration and market value.
	- PRC: Charge on legal and financial documents (including a wide range of contracts, business licences and books of account) executed or received in the PRC Duty ranges from RMB5 to 0.1% on contract value
- Capital duty
	- HK: Charge at 0.1% of the authorised capital of a corporation (maximum $30,000). Capital duty levied on Hong Kong companies under the Hong Kong Companies Ordinances was abolished with effect from 1 June 2012.
	- PRC: none
- Fiscal year
	- HK: From 1 April to 31 March
	- PRC: From 1 January to 31 December
- Basic scope of charge
	- HK: Territorial basis
	- PRC: Worldwide basis on residents. (PRC sourced income for non-residents with permanent establishment in the PRC. Withholding basis on passive income such as dividends, interest, royalties that is derived from the PRC for non-residents without permanent establishment in the PRC.)
