# home loan interest
Home loan interest, in relation to a person claiming a deduction in respect of a dwelling, means interest paid by the person as a **sole owner**, or as a joint tenant or tenant in common of the dwelling for the purposes of a [[home loan]] to:

(a) The Government;
(b) A financial institution;
(c) A credit union registered under the Credit Unions Ordinance (Cap. 119);
(d) A money lender licensed under the Money Lenders Ordinance (Cap. 163);
(e) The Hong Kong Housing Society;
(f) An employer of the person; or
(g) Any [[recognised organisation or association]].
