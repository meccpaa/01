# share-based payments with a choice of settlement
Accounting for share-based transactions with a choice of settlement ~depends on which party has the choice~.

**[[Counterparty has choice of settlement]]**
- HKFRS 2: Where the counterparty has a choice of settlement, the entity is deemed to have granted a ~compound instrument~, and a liability component and an equity component are identified.
- non-employee - direct method: equity component = residual value (service - debt)
- employee - 
[[SBP Example: choice of settlement]]

**[[Entity has choice of settlement]]**
HKFRS 2: Where the entity has a choice of settlement, the whole transaction is treated **either**, as ~cash-settled~ or as ~equity-settled~, depending on whether the entity has an **obligation** to settle in **cash**.
- has obligation to settle in cash - e.g. prohibited from issuing shares —> liability —> [[cash-settled share based payment transactions]]
- no present obligation - [[equity settled share-based payment transactions]]
