# s.26G: Contributions to recognised retirement schemes
With effect from December 2000, a deduction of the contributions paid by a person to a recognised retirement scheme as an employee to the scheme during any year of assessment is allowable under salaries tax.

The amount of deduction allowable in respect of any contributions to a recognised retirement scheme shall be the smallest of the following three amounts:
- The [[amount specified in Schedule 3B of the IRO]]; or
- The amount of **contributions by the taxpayer** as an employee to the scheme; or
- The amount of the mandatory contributions that the taxpayer would have been required to pay as an employee if at all times while an employee during the year of assessment in question he had contributed as a participant in a MPF scheme.

The Legislative Council passed the amendment of the minimum [[relevant income level - MPF]] and the maximum [[relevant income level - MPF]] under the Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes Ordnance. 

On the other hand, **employers** also need to contribute 5% of the employees' relevant income to the MPF scheme. Such mandatory contributions are allowable under profits tax. For further details of allowable deductions under profits tax, please refer to section 8 for details.

> **Note**. Although the MPF scheme took effect on 1 December 2000, employees were only required to make contributions after 30 days from the effective date. With effect from 1 February 2003, the Mandatory Provident Fund Schemes Ordinance was amended. The employees were not required to make contributions for the period between the first day of his/her employment and the calendar month in which the 30 th day of employment falls. For instance, for an employee who commenced work on 15 April 2016, he/she will not be required to make any mandatory contribution for April and May 2016. The obligation for mandatory contribution commences from 1 June 2016.
