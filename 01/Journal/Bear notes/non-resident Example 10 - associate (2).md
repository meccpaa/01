# non-resident Example 10 - associate (2)
(Adapted from DIPN 43 (Revised), Example 13)

T Ltd held 90% of the issued shares of U Ltd which in turn held 70% of the issued shares of V Ltd. V Ltd held 50% of the issued shares of I-Fund Ltd, a tax-exempt offshore fund. T Ltd, U Ltd and V Ltd were all resident companies.

Under the deeming provisions of s.20AE, the deemed assessable profits representing 50% of the exempt profits of I-Fund Ltd would be attributed to V Ltd.

Under s.20AE(9), ::no deemed assessable profits:: would be attributed to T Ltd and U Ltd notwithstanding that they both held indirect beneficial interests of more than 30% (T Ltd: 90% x 70% x 50% = 31.5%; U Ltd: 70% x 50% = 35%) in I-Fund Ltd. Deemed assessable profits in respect of the same exempt profits of I-Fund Ltd had **already been imposed on V Ltd**.
