# occupational retirement scheme
'Occupational retirement scheme' is a scheme, comprised in one or more instruments or agreements, which provides (or is capable of providing) benefits in the form of pensions, allowances, gratuities or other payments on the termination of service, death or retirement of employees. Insurance contracts under which benefits are only payable upon the death or disability of the insured do not qualify as occupational retirement schemes.
