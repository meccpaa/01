# Apportionment or aggregation
Where it is necessary to adopt as a basis period only a portion of the adjusted profits or loss of a longer accounting period or to aggregate such a portion with a portion of the adjusted profits or loss of another accounting period, apportionment or aggregation may be made on the basis of days or months in the respective periods (i.e. on a time basis). However, if it is inappropriate to apportion profits or loss on a time basis, e.g. when a large proportion of profits or loss fell within a specific part of an accounting period, the Commissioner may direct some other basis (s.18E(3)).

It should be noted that apportionment is made in respect of the adjusted profits or loss before depreciation allowances. Depreciation allowances are not apportioned, but are recomputed in accordance with the new basis period (s.40(1)) (see section 9.1 on 'Basis period under s.40(1)' and Example 93).
