# Inventory Example 3: Inventory valuation (1)
Arneson Co. trades in widgets and has bought and sold the following inventory during the month of September 20X1:

![](Inventory%20Example%203%20Inventory%20valuation%20(1)/99711A62-8B84-412D-A9EF-298DD597A932.png)

What is the cost of inventory at 30 September using:
(a) FIFO?
(b) Weighted average cost (period end)?
(c) Weighted average cost (continuous)?
- - - -
## Solution
![](Inventory%20Example%203%20Inventory%20valuation%20(1)/D031A649-DD38-42F5-A03C-23228238432C.png)

Sales are matched to the earliest purchases such that closing stock is made up of 75 units purchased on 25 September. Closing stock is therefore measured at 180 (75 units x 2.40).

### Weighted average (period end)
![](Inventory%20Example%203%20Inventory%20valuation%20(1)/5EED8968-FCD4-407D-B8D6-756FBD09F56C.png)

The weighted average cost of stocks during September is:
![](Inventory%20Example%203%20Inventory%20valuation%20(1)/95FABA9A-FE9B-4AB1-AB4A-91DA40470F69.png)

Therefore weighted average cost per unit 2.24
Closing stock is therefore measured at 168 (75 units x 2.24)

Therefore, closing stock is measured at 172.50 (75 units x 2.30)
