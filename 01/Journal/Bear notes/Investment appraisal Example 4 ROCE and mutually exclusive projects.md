# Investment appraisal Example 4: ROCE and mutually exclusive projects
Arrow Ltd wants to buy a new item of equipment which will be used to provide a service to customers of the company. Two models of equipment are available, one with a slightly higher capacity and greater reliability than the other. The expected costs and profits of each item are as follows:
![](Investment%20appraisal%20Example%204%20ROCE%20and%20mutually%20exclusive%20projects/459DCB62-1939-4127-ADD8-68F91DDD1718.png)
ROCE is measured as the average annual profit after depreciation, divided by the average carrying amount of the asset. Decide which item of equipment should be selected, if any, if the company's target ROCE is 30%.
- - - -
## Solution
![](Investment%20appraisal%20Example%204%20ROCE%20and%20mutually%20exclusive%20projects/4081A0B7-9AD2-4424-BAFA-0A882E4E2209.png)
Both projects would earn a return in excess of 30%, but since item X would earn a bigger ROCE, it would be preferred to item Y, even though the profits from Y would be higher by an average of $100,000 a year.
