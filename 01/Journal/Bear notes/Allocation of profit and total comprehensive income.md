# Allocation of profit and total comprehensive income
The following items must also be disclosed in the statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income as allocations for the period:

(a) Profit or loss for the period attributable to:
	* (i) Non-controlling interests
	* (ii) Owners of the parent.

(b) Total comprehensive income for the period attributable to:
	* (i) Non-controlling interests
	* (ii) Owners of the parent.
