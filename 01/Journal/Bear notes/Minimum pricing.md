# Minimum pricing
A minimum price is the minimum selling price for an item that must be charged in order to achieve a stated target. The target may be a minimum profit on the sale, or it may be a price that will leave total profit unchanged.

Minimum price is calculated from the incremental and relevant costs of making the sale.

The relevant cost of an item or action is the increase in future net cash outflows that will occur as a consequence of making or selling the product, or performing the action. (The same principles of relevant costing are used to estimate cash flows for discounted cash flow analysis, which is described in a later chapter.)
