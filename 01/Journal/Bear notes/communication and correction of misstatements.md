# communication and correction of misstatements
The auditor shall communicate on a **timely basis** all misstatements accumulated during the audit with the ~appropriate level~ of management, unless prohibited by law or regulation. The auditor shall request management to ~correct~ those misstatements.

The auditor shall communicate with those ~charged with governance~ **uncorrected misstatements** and the **effect** that they, individually or in aggregate, may have on the opinion in the auditor's report, unless prohibited by law or regulation and the effect of uncorrected misstatements ~related to prior periods~ on the relevant classes of transactions, account balances or disclosures, and the financial statements as a whole. The auditor's communication shall identify material uncorrected misstatements **individually**.
- - - -
## 8.4.1 Considerations Specific to Public Sector Entities
In the case of an audit of a public sector entity, the evaluation whether a misstatement is material may also be affected by the auditor's responsibilities established by law, regulation or other authority to report specific matters, including, for example, fraud.

Furthermore, issues such as public interest, accountability, probity and ensuring effective legislative oversight, in particular, may affect the assessment whether an item is material by virtue of its nature. This is particularly so for items that relate to compliance with law, regulation or other authority.
