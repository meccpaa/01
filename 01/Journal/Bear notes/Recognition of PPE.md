# Recognition of PPE
An item of PPE should be recognised in the statement of financial position as an [[asset]] when

### Application to specific types of asset
* [[Small separate assets]] 
* [[Spare parts and stand-by equipment]]
* [[Safety and environmental equipment]]
* [[Complex assets]]
* [[Inspections and overhauls]]

### Subsequent expenditure
Subsequent expenditure on a non-current asset **may be capitalised** where the expenditure **enhances the economic benefits** of the asset beyond its current standard or performance. This may be achieved through extension of the asset's life, improved quality of output or an increased operating capacity. Day to day servicing costs, repairs and maintenance do not meet this criteria and therefore must be expensed to profit or loss.
