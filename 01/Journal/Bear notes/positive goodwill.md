# positive goodwill
Positive goodwill acquired in a business combination is recognised as an asset and is initially measured at cost. Cost is the excess of the cost of the combination over the acquirer's interest in the net fair value of the acquiree's identifiable assets, liabilities, and contingent liabilities.

After initial recognition goodwill acquired in a business combination is measured at cost less any accumulated impairment losses. It is not amortised. Instead it is tested for impairment at least annually, in accordance with HKAS 36 Impairment of Assets.
