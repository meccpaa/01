# Source of profits
![](Source%20of%20profits/image.png)
Under s.14, only '**profits arising in or derived from Hong Kong**' are taxable.

It is important to ascertain the source of a person's profits. In general, there are two main sources of income or profits:
1. [[Active income]]: Rendering services or performing activities (e.g. commission income); and
2. [[Passive income]]: Holding of property (e.g. interest).

- The BOR, in [[CIR v Hang Seng Bank [(1990) 1 HKRC 90–044}]] differentiated active and passive income.
- [[United Aircraft Corporation [(1943) 7 ATD 318}]]

## 4.1 Traditional tests of source of profits
### 4.1.1 The board guiding principle
The Privy Council in Hang Seng Bank provided a general guiding principle for the determination of source: ' ~one looks to see what the taxpayer has done to earn the profit in question~'. [[CIR v Hang Seng Bank [(1990) 1 HKRC 90–044}]]

The following tests are therefore relevant in determining the source of income:
(a) [[Operations test]] - service, commission, money lending business
(b) [[Contract effected test]]  - trading, reinsurance
(c) [[Provision of credit test]] - passive interest income
(d) [[Situs (or location) test]] - possession of asset or properties
(e) [[Activities test]] - manufacturing business
