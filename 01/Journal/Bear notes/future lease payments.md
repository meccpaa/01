# future lease payments
Future lease payments may include:
- Fixed payments less [[lease incentives]]
- Variable payments if:
	- [[variable payments are fixed in substance]], or
	- [[variable payments depend on a rate or index]]

- The exercise price of a purchase option if it is reasonably certain to be exercised 
- Penalties to terminate the lease if the lease term reflects early termination 
- Amounts payable under residual value guarantees 

Future lease payments for the purpose of calculating the lease liability ::do not include:::
- Payments in respect of **non-lease components** (unless the [[Practical expedient]] is applied) 
- Variable lease payments that depend on ~future performance~ or use of the asset.

Variable lease payments that are ::excluded:: from the measurement of the lease liability are ~recognised in **profit or loss**~ in the period that they are triggered.
