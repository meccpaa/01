# s.14B (re)insurance of offshore risks as a professional reinsurer / an authorised captive insurer
Assessable profits of a corporation, to the extent they are assessable profits of the corporation derived from the business of

(i) Reinsurance of offshore risks as a professional reinsurer within the meaning of ss.23A(2) and (3) (discussed in section 14.3.1 on 'Qualifying business of reinsurance of offshore risks'); or

(ii) Insurance of offshore risks as an authorised captive insurer within the meaning of ss.23A(2A) and (3), effective from the year of assessment 2013/14 (discussed in section 14.3.2 on 'Qualifying business of insurance of offshore risks').

An election for the tax concession, once made, is irrevocable.
