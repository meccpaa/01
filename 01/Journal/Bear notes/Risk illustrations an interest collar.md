# Risk illustrations: an interest collar
Suppose in the previous example Zuma is offered the cap at 10% interest, but the treasurer regards the cost of 1% per annum (pa) as too expensive. Upon negotiation, he discovers that bank is prepared to buy an 8% floor from Zuma for a premium cost of 0.75% per annum payable quarterly in arrears. Zuma purchases the 10% cap for 1% pa and sells the 8% floor for 0.75% pa giving a net cost of the collar of 0.25% pa.
