# ascertainment the deemed assessable profits - offshore fund
The amount of deemed profits is ascertained by reference to 
	- (1) the percentage of the resident person's beneficial interest in the offshore fund and 
	- (2) the length of ownership within the relevant year of assessment.
- - - -
The amount of deemed assessable profits imposed on a resident person for a year of assessment is ascertained in accordance with Part 1 of Schedule 15 of the IRO by adding up the assessable profits of the non-resident person which are tax exempt for each day in the period in that year of assessment during which the resident person has a direct or indirect beneficial interest in the nonresident person (ss.20AE(6) and (7)).

The exempt profits of the non-resident person for a particular day in a year of assessment (which is the deemed assessable profits of the resident person) are calculated using the following formula set out in the Part 1 of Schedule 15 of the IRO.

where:
A: means the **exempt profits** of the non-resident person for a particular day in a year of assessment
B: means the extent of the resident person's **beneficial interest** in the non-resident person on the particular day
C: means the **exempt profits** of the non-resident person for the accounting period of the non-resident person in which the ~particular day falls~
D: means the **total number of days** in the accounting period of the non-resident person in which the particular day falls

Ascertainment of deemed assessable profits is made by reference to the year of assessment from 1 April to 31 March, irrespective of the accounting dates of the non-resident person and the resident person.

Non-taxable items (e.g. capital gains, dividends and interests) are excluded in computing the deemed assessable profits. On the other hand, there is no deduction for the expenses incurred by the resident person in generating the deemed assessable profits.

However, the deeming provision ~will not by itself deem the resident person to be carrying on a business in respect of his other activities~, will not impose any new tax, and will not be invoked in respect of offshore profits, capital gains nor dividend income.
