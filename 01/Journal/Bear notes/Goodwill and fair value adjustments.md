# Goodwill and fair value adjustments
Goodwill arising under HKFRS 3 Business Combinations from the acquisition of a foreign operation should 
1. **initially** be calculated in the **functional currency of the subsidiary**. 
2. It is then treated as an **asset** of the foreign operation and should be translated at the **closing rate each year**.

Similarly, any **fair value adjustments** made on consolidation are treated as an asset or liability of the foreign operation and are translated at closing rate each year.

The **exchange differences** arising are recorded as ~other comprehensive income~ in the consolidated accounts and accumulated in group equity.
