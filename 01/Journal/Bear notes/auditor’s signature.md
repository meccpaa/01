# auditor’s signature
The report shall contain the auditor's signature, whether this is the auditor's own name or the audit firm's name.

Under section 409 of the new Companies Ordinance, an auditor's report must be signed:

(a) If the auditor is a natural person, by the auditor; or

(b) If the auditor is a firm or body corporate, by a natural person authorized to sign the auditor's name on the auditor's behalf.

An auditor's report shall state the auditor's name.
