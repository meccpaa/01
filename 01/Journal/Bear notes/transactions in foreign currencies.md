# transactions in foreign currencies
For example, an Indian company might buy materials from Canada, pay for them in US dollars, and then sell its finished goods in Germany receiving payment in Euros, or perhaps in some other currency. If the company owes money in a foreign currency at the end of the accounting year, or holds assets which were bought in a foreign currency, those liabilities or assets must be translated into the local currency (in this Learning Pack ), in order to be shown in the books of account.
