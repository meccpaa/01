## Personal allowances
Part 5 of the IRO prescribes the allowances which shall be granted to persons chargeable to salaries tax under Parts 3 (Salaries Tax) and 7 (Personal Assessment) and the circumstances in which such allowances are grantable. An allowance claimed under Part 5 must be made in the specified form and an allowance will be granted only if the claim contains the relevant information supported by such documentary evidence as the Commissioner may require.
