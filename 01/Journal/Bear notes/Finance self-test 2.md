# Finance self-test 2
CDE Bank is a listed company and needs to increase its capital by $230 million. CDE Bank’s current share price is $18.75. It has decided to carry out a rights issue and has appointed advisors to handle the issue who have estimated the costs of the issue at $10 million. Due to a volatile equity market CDE has been advised that the shares should be offered at a 20% discount to current market price. There are currently 48 million shares in issue.

Required

(i) Determine the terms of the rights issue that would be necessary for CDE to raise the required funds.

(ii) Brian Lam is a small shareholder with 300 shares, calculate the value of the rights if he were to sell them rather than subscribe for new shares.

- - - -
## Answer 2

CDE needs to raise sufficient funds to cover the required increase in capital and the costs of issue.

$230m + $10m = $240m

Since the current share price is $18.75 and there will be a 20% discount the rights issue will be priced at $15. Thus the number of shares to issue is $240m/$15 = 16 million

As there are currently 48 million shares in issue the rights issue would take place on a 1 for 3 basis.

ii. If Brian Lam has 300 shares he will receive 100 rights. To calculate the value of these rights we first have to establish the theoretical ex-rights price of a CDE Bank share.

Since a right permits the purchase of a share worth $17.81 for $15 it will be worth the difference in these two figures of $2.81. Since Brian Lam will receive 100 rights he will be able to sell these for $281.
