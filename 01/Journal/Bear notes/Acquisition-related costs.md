# Acquisition-related costs
Under [[HKFRS 3 (revised)]] costs relating to an acquisition must be recognised as an **expense** at the time of the acquisition. They are **not regarded as an asset**or as part of the cost of acquisition. Costs of issuing debt or equity are to be accounted for under the rules of [[HKFRS 9]] (financial instruments)
