# Appointment to fill casual vacancy - new CO s.397
Appointment to fill casual vacancy (section 397 of new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)

The directors may appoint a person to fill a casual vacancy in the office of auditor of the company. If the directors have not done so within one month after the casual vacancy occurs, the members may, by a resolution passed at a general meeting, appoint a person to fill the casual vacancy.
