# s.15(1)(ia) deeming provisions for interest income and other gains from intra-group financing businesses
The new legislation includes two deeming provisions to tax interest and other gains that arise through or from the carrying on of an intra-group financing business by a corporation in Hong Kong in the same way a FI would be taxed, effective from 3 June 2016.

Under s.15(1)(ia), interest income received by or accrued to a corporation (other than a FI) through or from the carrying on in Hong Kong of its intra-group financing business are **deemed taxable**, ::even if:: the moneys in respect of which the interest is received or accrues are ~made available outside Hong Kong~. In other words, the provision of credit test will no longer apply, and corporations which have applied this test to determine the source of interest will be affected.

The IRD reiterated in DIPN 52 that the operations test applies to determine the source of interest income for intra-group financing business, while the provision of credit test applies to determine the source of interest income for simple inter-company loans not made in the ordinary course of an intra-group financing business.
