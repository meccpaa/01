# WC self-test 2
RST Limited is a producer of specialist plastics. One of its key production inputs is crude oil and over the course of a year it expects to use 10,000 barrels. RST has its own oil storage facilities onsite which cost $2,000,0000 to build and are being depreciated on a straight line basis over 25 years, depreciation is absorbed into the cost of the total number of barrels used in a year. RST has calculated that the cost of placing an order is $137, made up of $127 of variable costs and $10 of fixed costs absorbed. RST estimates that the cost of the funds tied up in its oil inventory total 2.5%. The price of oil is currently $55.

Required

Calculate the amount of oil that RST should purchase each time it places on order and establish how frequently orders should be placed.
