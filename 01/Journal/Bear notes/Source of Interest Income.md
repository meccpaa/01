# Source of Interest Income
![](Source%20of%20Interest%20Income/image.png)
- - - -
For persons other than a FI, the IRD indicates in [[DIPN 21 (Revised)]] that the locality of their interest income will be determined on the basis set out in [[DIPN 13 (Revised)]].

1. Interest received by a business, other than a FI or money lender, carried on in HK is assessable: [[Provision of credit test]] - place where the sum of money is first made available to the borrower
2. **Money lending business**: [[Operations test]] 
3. **FIs**: deeming provision of s.15(1)(i) [[Special businesses]] ([[s.15(1)(f), (g), (i), (ia) - interest]])
4. Interest on **trade debts**: same as that of the trading profits
5. **Bank deposits**: location of the branch with which the account is maintained [[Provision of credit test]]
6. **Local bank deposits**: could be exempt from tax under s.87
7. [[Corporate treasury centre]]: money lent to a non-HK associated corporation in the ordinary course of an intra-group financing business: may be subject to concessionary tax rate of 8.25%
