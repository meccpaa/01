# Profits tax - example 57 interest flow-back
The $7 million loan in Example 56 was sub-participated by Co N for only six months during the basis period of the year of assessment.

Disallowable interest expense = $1m × $7m / $10m × 182.5 days / 365 days = $350,000

Total interest deduction = $1m – $350,000 = $650,000
