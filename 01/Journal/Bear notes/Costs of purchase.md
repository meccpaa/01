# Costs of purchase
HKAS 2 states that the costs of purchase may include all of:

- Purchase price Import duties and other irrecoverable taxes
- Transport, handling and any other cost directly attributable to the acquisition of finished goods, services and materials
- Costs of purchase should be net of trade discounts, rebates and other similar amounts when measuring inventory.
