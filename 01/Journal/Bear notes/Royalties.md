# Royalties
Consideration in a contract to licence intellectual property may be a royalty that is measured by reference to sales or usage. In this case, the seller recognises revenue when the **later of the following events** occurs:

* The subsequent ~sale or usage~ arises.
* The ~performance obligation~ to which some or all of the sales or usage based royalty has been allocated has been ~satisfied~ (or partially satisfied).
