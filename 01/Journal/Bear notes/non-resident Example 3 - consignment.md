# non-resident Example 3 - consignment
B Ltd, a Taiwan company manufacturing watches for sale in Asia, consigns watches to C Ltd for sale in Hong Kong. C Ltd keeps stocks in Hong Kong, from which it regularly fills orders. Sales for the year of assessment 2016/17 were as follows:

1st quarter 	$400,000   
2nd quarter	$450,000
3rd quarter	$550,000
4th quarter	$600,000

## The Hong Kong tax implications are as follows:
C Ltd is required to submit quarterly returns (Form BIR 52B) in respect of the above sales to the Commissioner and remit tax of $2,000, $2,250, $2,750 and $3,000 respectively for the four quarters to the IRD.

As C Ltd keeps stocks from which it regularly fills orders, it constitutes a permanent establishment and B Ltd is regarded as carrying on business in Hong Kong and chargeable under s.14. Its assessable profits will be ascertained in accordance with IRR 5. However, in practice, 'consignment tax' is usually regarded as the final liability.
