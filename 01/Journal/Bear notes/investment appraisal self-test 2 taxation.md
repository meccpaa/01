# investment appraisal self-test 2: taxation
A company is considering the purchase of a machine for $150,000. It would be sold after four years for an estimated realisable value of $50,000. By this time tax-allowable depreciation of $120,000 would have been claimed. The rate of tax is 16.5%.

What are the tax implications of the sale of the machine at the end of four years?
- - - -
## Answer 2

There will be a balancing charge on the sale of the machine of $(50,000 – (150,000 – 120,000)) = $20,000. This will give rise to a tax payment of 16.5% x $20,000 = $3,300.
