# Investment appraisal Example 18: Single period rationing with non-divisible projects
Short Ltd has capital of $95,000 available for investment in the forthcoming period. The directors decide to consider projects P, Q and R only. They wish to invest only in whole projects, but surplus funds can be invested. Which combination of projects will produce the highest NPV at a cost of capital of 20%?
![](Investment%20appraisal%20Example%2018%20Single%20period%20rationing%20with%20non-divisible%20projects/B6360CB0-35D7-44E0-83F5-318DE6194269.png)
- - - -
## Solution
The investment combinations are the various possible pairs of projects P, Q and R.
![](Investment%20appraisal%20Example%2018%20Single%20period%20rationing%20with%20non-divisible%20projects/44098EEF-13C0-4FA3-A29D-A88B9D440B9D.png)
The highest NPV will be achieved by undertaking projects Q and R and investing the unused funds of $20,000 externally.
