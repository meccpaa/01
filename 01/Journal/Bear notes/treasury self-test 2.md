# treasury self-test 2
The JKL Group of companies has a number of trading subsidiaries which carry out a high volume of purchases of raw materials and customer sales in a number of different currencies.

Required

How could treasury functions be structured to minimize transaction and administration costs for transactions related to these sales, and describe appropriate performance measures with which to assess this function?
- - - -
Answer 2

JKL Group could establish a centralised shared facility for processing all payments to suppliers and receipts for customers which would make collections and payments on behalf of other group companies. Centralising these repetitive tasks should reduce the total administration cost to the Group and bank fees and costs may also be minimised by ensuring a single entity interacts with external bankers. In addition by centralising payments and receipts JKL is likely to be able to more efficiently manage currency exposure and obtain better exchange rates from banks.

Appropriate performance measures could include:
- Number of transactions processed 
- Total costs per transaction 
- Bank fees and costs per transaction 
- Admin costs per transaction 
- Number of processing errors
