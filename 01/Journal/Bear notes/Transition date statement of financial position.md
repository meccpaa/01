# Transition date statement of financial position
`HKFRS 1.3, 7, 10`

When preparing the statement of financial position as at the transition date, the following general principles should be applied:

![](Transition%20date%20statement%20of%20financial%20position/3D1D17E3-B4FC-403C-8583-8660FA1F4BCF.png)
These principles reflect the requirement that HKFRS (with certain exceptions as detailed below) are applied retrospectively when first adopted; this in turn reflects the HKAS 8 accounting treatment applicable when there is a change in accounting policy.

Any adjustments resulting from the retrospective application of HKFRS are recognised directly in equity.
