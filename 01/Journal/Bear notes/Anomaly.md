# Anomaly
HKSA 530.5e: [[Anomaly]] is defined as a misstatement or deviation that is demonstrably **not representative** of misstatements or deviations in a population.

In the ::extremely rare circumstances::, when the auditor considers a misstatement or deviation discovered in a sample to be an anomaly, the auditor shall ~obtain a high degree of certainty~ that such misstatement or deviation is **not representative** of the population. The auditor shall perform ~additional procedures~ to obtain sufficient appropriate audit evidence that the misstatement or deviation does ::not affect the remainder of the population::. However, for anomalies, [[projecting misstatements]] to the population is not required.
