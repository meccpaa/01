# treasury share transactions
Where a company holds a number of its own shares, those shares are known as Treasury shares.

Guidance on treasury share transactions previously contained within HK(IFRIC)-Int 11 is incorporated into HKFRS 2 with effect from 1 January 2010.

Where an entity grants rights to its own equity instruments to employees, and then either chooses or is required to buy those equity instruments from another party, in order to satisfy its obligations to its employees under the share-based payment arrangement, the transaction is accounted for as an equity-settled transaction.
