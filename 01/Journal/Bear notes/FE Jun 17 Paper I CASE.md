# FE Jun 17 Paper I CASE
Tasty Wine Holdings Company Limited (“Tasty Wine”, or the “Company”) is a company incorporated in Bermuda with limited liability and it was listed on the Growth Enterprise Market (“GEM”) of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange (“HKSE”) a few years ago. The Company and its subsidiaries (the “Group”) mainly engage in the wine trading business. The Group has an annual financial reporting date of 31 March each year. Tasty Wine prepares its consolidated financial statement in accordance with Hong Kong Financial Reporting Standards (“HKFRSs”). The Group’s functional currency and presentation currency is the Hong Kong dollar.

## West Wine (H.K.) Company Limited
West Wine (H.K.) Company Limited (“West Wine”) and its subsidiaries (the “West Wine Group”) were incorporated in Hong Kong which were set up by Mr Henry Tse and were included into the Group through a restructuring upon listing.

West Wine purchases wines from different countries around the world. The cost of the wines ranges from a few hundred to a few thousand Hong Kong dollars per bottle and some of the wine may cost even more.

West Wine sells the wine either to its distributors in Hong Kong and mainland China through West Wine Wholesales Company Limited (“West Wine Wholesales”), or in its retail shops which are managed by West Wine Retail Company Limited (“West Wine Retail”).

During the year ended 31 March 2016, West Wine sold wine totaling HK$50 million and HK$30 million to West Wine Wholesales and West Wine Retail, respectively and at a margin of 20% based on the selling price. As at 31 March 2016, HK$10 million and HK$5 million of wine remained unsold by West Wine Wholesales and West Wine Retail, respectively.

Part of the sales are consignment sales on behalf of overseas suppliers and West Wine will charge the suppliers 10% to 30% of the selling price as the commission. West Wine also has some other income. As its warehouses are well renovated to ensure the wine is kept under appropriate temperatures, some of the customers pay a monthly fee to West Wine to keep their wine in the warehouses after they have purchased it.

West Wine and its subsidiaries used to perform an inventory count during the last few days around the year end. Starting from April 2015, they also performed an inventory count on a monthly basis. Inventory count instructions and standard count sheets are prepared and the inventory count is executed by a combination of experienced staff in the warehouses, sales department and accounting department. Any differences identified will be adjusted with the management’s approval. Previously, the inventory record was maintained and the weightedaverage inventory costing was computed using two separate systems and there was no interface between the systems. A new in-house developed inventory system was implemented during the year for maintaining the inventory record as well as calculating the weighted-average inventory costing. The new system has been tested in West Wine Wholesales for two months before rolling it out to all of the West Wine Group’s subsidiaries.

West Wine has been quite reputable regarding the quality of its wine. However, a significant amount of goods returns was noted in both West Wine Wholesales and West Wine Retail in April 2016 in relation to the wine imported from France because of the negative news that the wine may have been polluted while it was delivered from France to Hong Kong two months ago. The management estimated that it has to suffer a loss of around HK$0.8 million because of the goods returns and other related costs. Adjustments were made in the financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2016.

Based on the discussions up to 15 June 2016, the supplier in France sent an email and agreed to share 30% of the loss but the management was not satisfied with this and did not take this into consideration in the financial statements for the year ended 31 March 2016. After several further tough negotiations, the supplier finally agreed with West Wine on 15 July to share up to 50% of the loss. Adjustments were made in the management accounts for the four months ended 31 July 2016.

## Wine Diner Company Limited
Wine Diner Company Limited (“Wine Diner”) operates a showroom with drinks and meals in one of West Wine warehouses. Unfortunately, the showroom was damaged by a fire in May 2016. The management estimated that the loss of assets caused by the fire amounted to HK$1.5 million, including cash of around HK$0.3 million kept in the showroom. The fire also caused some damage to several third parties’ offices and warehouses in the same building. The tenants took the case to court and sued Wine Diner to claim for compensation fees of HK$0.8 million in aggregate. In August 2016, the showroom reopened and Wine Diner was also able to settle the case with the tenants outside court by paying total compensation fees of HK$0.6 million.

Wong & Co has been the auditor of the Group since it was listed. Henry invited Wong & Co’s engagement team for dinner at the showroom as a token of appreciation for their support throughout the years as well as the completion of the annual audit. A box of six red wines was served during the dinner and Henry said that it was the sample sent from a new supplier. Two bottles of wine were consumed and Henry gave the remaining four bottles to the engagement team after the dinner.

## Business development of the Group

After listing on the GEM Board for a few years, the Group has faced strong competition in the local and mainland China markets. A number of small to medium sized wine companies have been set up in the current markets and they sell similar products with a much lower margin. Mr Andy Leung, the Chief Executive Officer (“CEO”), has recognized that it is becoming more difficult to generate new business and sustain the Group’s profitability because of the increasing competition and saturation of the current markets. After discussion with other senior management, he decides to expand its wine business to other Asian markets. The Group needs to raise funding in the bond market to expand its business in other Asian markets.

In addition, Henry also would like to expand the Group for listing on the Main Board of the HKSE. He is planning to acquire Zhou’s Wine Group (“ZWG”), which is a family business engaged in the manufacturing and selling of Chinese wine in mainland China. ZWG is solely owned by Mr Samuel Zhou. Because of its limited distribution network and the decrease in purchase of luxury products in mainland China, ZWG’s profit has decreased significantly in recent years. If ZWG is acquired by the Group, ZWG can share West Wine’s distribution network by paying an annual management fee to West Wine and the Group can manufacture their own brand of wine.

John Chan is a member of the Hong Kong Institute of Certified Public Accountants and has joined the Group as an accounting manager. He joined a project team responsible for an acquisition project to acquire ZWG. In the first working meeting, he learnt about certain nonpublic information about the future business development of the Group, including the expansion of business to other Asian markets. Ms Marie Tang, the Chief Finance Officer (“CFO”), reminded all team members including John to keep all relevant information of the project highly confidential.

On 1 April 2016, West Wine (BVI) Company Limited (“West Wine BVI”) entered into the sales and purchases agreement (the “S&P Agreement”) with Samuel to acquire 70% equity interest in ZWG (the “Acquisition”) and ZWG would become a 70%-owned subsidiary of West Wine BVI.

The Acquisition was completed on 31 May 2016 and the consideration was settled in the form of (a) cash of HK$2,000,000 and (b) 30,000,000 ordinary shares of Tasty Wine. In addition, pursuant to the S&P Agreement, if the audited net profit of ZWG for the year ending 31 December 2016 and 2017 exceeds HK$5 million (subject to a cap of HK$8 million) and HK$6 million (subject to a cap of HK$10 million), respectively, Samuel would be entitled to an additional payment, which is 10% of the aggregated net profits for the year ending 31 December 2016 and 2017.
