# Holdover of provisional tax payment
A taxpayer may apply in writing for a partial or complete holdover of provisional tax in circumstances as follows:
![](Holdover%20of%20provisional%20tax%20payment/image.png)
The application for holdover of provisional tax must be submitted to the Commissioner not later than:
- 28 days before the payment due date of the provisional tax; or 
- 14 days after the date of the notice for payment of provisional tax;

whichever is the later.

It should be noted that the Commissioner has no discretionary power to extend the time limit for a holdover.
