# Product-market mix
Ansoff drew up a growth vector matrix, describing how a combination of a firm's activities in current and new markets, with existing and new products can lead to growth.

Ansoff's original model was a four-cell matrix based on product and market, shown as the heart of the diagram below. It can be enhanced by adding the external options shown in the diagram. Withdrawal, demerger and privatisation are discussed at the end of this section.
