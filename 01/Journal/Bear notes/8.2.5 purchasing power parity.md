# 8.2.5 purchasing power parity
Purchasing power parity theory (PPPT) states that the exchange rate between two currencies is the same in equilibrium when the purchasing power of currency is the same in each country.
- - - -

Interest rate parity should not be confused with purchasing power parity. PPPT predicts that the spot exchange value of foreign currency depends on the relative purchasing power of each currency in its own country and that spot exchange rates will vary over time according to relative price changes.
