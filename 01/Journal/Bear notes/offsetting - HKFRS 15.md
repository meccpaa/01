# offsetting - HKFRS 15
HKFRS 15 makes the following points about offsetting:
- A **single contract** is presented either as a net contract asset or a net contract liability. Any **unconditional right** to consideration is presented ~separately as a receivable~.
- Contract assets and liabilities in respect of ::different customers:: are not offset.
