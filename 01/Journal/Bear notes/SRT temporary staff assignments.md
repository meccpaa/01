# SRT: temporary staff assignments
The lending of staff by a firm to an audit client may create a self-review threat. Such assistance may be given, but only for a short period of time and the firm's personnel shall not be involved in:
- Providing non-assurance services that would not be permitted under the Code; or 
- Assuming management responsibilities.

In all situations, the audit client shall be responsible for directing and supervising the activities of the loaned staff.
