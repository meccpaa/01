# Exam Module C: Dec 18 - Q1(d) substantive procedures - completeness AP
(d) Propose substantive procedures to audit the completeness of ELL’s trade payables at the year end date. (5 marks)
- - - -
[[trade payables and accruals - audit procedures]]
[[AP - completeness procedures]]

- Trace a sample from PO, to invoice, transaction records and payment to ensure there is no missing
- Reconcile the AP listings and the ledger balance
- Reasonableness testing on the change of AP, AP turnover days
- - - -
## Solution
The audit team should perform the following procedures to audit the completeness of ELL’s trade payables:
- Reconcile the listing of trade payables to the general ledger balance by casting and cross-casting.
- Examine **post year end purchase transactions** to identify if there are any unrecorded trade payables.
- Obtain **statements** from the overseas suppliers for LED chips and **trace** these back to ELL’s accounting records to identify if there are significant discrepancies.
- Obtain statements (if any) or written documentation from the **subcontractors** and **trace** these back to ELL’s records of the subcontracting fee payables to identify if there are significant discrepancies.
- Examine the ~subcontracting agreements~ for the terms of the subcontracting fees to ensure appropriate fees have been accrued for by ELL as trade payables.
- Send **confirmation** requests to the overseas suppliers of LED chips and subcontractors with ~material outstanding balances~ to confirm the year end balances due by ELL.
- Send **confirmation** requests to the ~key suppliers~ which have ~nil or small~ outstanding balances due by ELL at the year end date.
- Perform **comparison** of the current year balance for trade payables to the previous year to check for reasonableness.
