# other-AE self-test 3
Capital Engineering College (CEC) is a university in Hong Kong offering undergraduate and postgraduate degrees in engineering. The college also has its own research facility which is partly funded by industry. Research projects are based at the university but often involve travel to carry out work on sites overseas. University employees are required to submit an expenses claim form with supporting documentation for all overseas travel expenses. Once authorised, these expenses are then reimbursed through the monthly payroll.

Your firm is the external auditor at CEC and has been asked to carry out a review investigating whether expenses being reimbursed to employees are in line with the CEC travel policy. Your firm is not required to provide an opinion on whether the policy is being adhered to.

Required

(a) What matters will need to be agreed with CEC before the engagement can be commenced?

(b) Explain the key differences between the report produced at the end of this agreed-upon procedures engagement and the external auditor's report on the audit of CEC's financial statements for the period.
- - - -
## Answer 3

(a) Matters to be agreed before engagement commences Matters that need to be agreed with CEC before the engagement can commence will be included in the engagement letter. These are described in HKSRS 4400 and include the following:
- Who will receive copies of the agreed-upon procedures report. Is it for the college, the members of industry who have part funded the research facility or perhaps both?
- Nature of the engagement; specifically that it does not constitute an audit/review and no assurance will be expressed that expenses are being reimbursed in line with CEC policy.
- The purpose of the engagement is to investigate whether expenses are being reimbursed to employees in line with CEC policies.
- Identification of the financial information to which the agreed-upon procedures will be applied. In this case, this will be the information contained in the expenses and payroll accounts.
- The nature, timing and extent of the specific procedures to be applied. These are likely to include the following:
	- Reading CEC's policies regarding the reimbursement of expenses to employees
	- For a randomly selected sample of reimbursements, agree the total of the expense claim form to the amounts given on receipts or other supporting documentation
	- Verify that the expenses have been reimbursed in line with CEC policy

- Form of the report, ie it will be a report of factual findings.
- Limitations of the distribution of the report. A statement will be included confirming it is solely for the use of the specified parties and should be used solely for the purposes stated.
- The fee that will be paid to the auditors for carrying out the engagement.
- The respective roles and responsibilities of CEC and the auditors.
