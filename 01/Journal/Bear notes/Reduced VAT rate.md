# Reduced VAT rate
The reduced rate of 13% is applicable to these supplies:

- Food grains, edible vegetable oils and fresh milk;
- Tap water, heating, air conditioning, hot water, coal gas, liquefied petroleum gas, natural gas, coal/charcoal products for household use; 
- Books, newspapers and magazines; 
- Feed, chemical fertilisers, agricultural chemicals, agricultural machinery and plastic film for agricultural purposes;
- Audio-video products, such as sound recording tapes, videotapes, gramophone record, CD and VCD, which are published officially;
- E-journals;
- Diethyl ether;
- Other goods stipulated by State Council.

Note that, from 1 January 2009 onwards, there is an adjustment to the tax rate, and the metallic mineral and non-metallic ore products are subject to the basic rate of 17%.
