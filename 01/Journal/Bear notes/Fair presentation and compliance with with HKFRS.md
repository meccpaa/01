# Fair presentation and compliance with with HKFRS
HKAS 1.15,16,18-20 **Compliance with HKFRS** is presumed to result in financial statements that achieve a fair presentation.
- - - -
Most importantly, financial statements should present a true and fair view of the financial position, financial performance and cash flows of an entity. Compliance with HKFRS is presumed to result in financial statements that achieve a fair presentation and true and fair view.
