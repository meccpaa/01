# HKSA 260 (Revised) Communicating with those charged with governance
Auditors shall communicate specific matters to those charged with governance and HKSA 260 (Revised) provides guidance to auditors in this area.

7.1 [[The importance of communicating with those charged with governance]]
7.2 [[Matters to be communicated by auditors to those charged with governance]]

## 7.3 The communication process
`HKSA 260.18`
The auditor shall communicate with those charged with governance the form, timing and expected general content of communications. The auditor shall communicate with those charged with governance on a timely basis through structured presentations, written reports or discussions.
