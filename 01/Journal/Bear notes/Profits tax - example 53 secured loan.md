# Profits tax - example 53 secured loan
The two loans in Example 52 were secured by a deposit of $4 million that generated tax-free interest of $160,000. There was no other security.

Part of the deposit is used to secure the onshore loan. Allowable interest is reduced by a portion of the tax-free interest on the deposit in the amount of $40,000, calculated as:

Part of the deposit that is used to secure the two loans is $2.5m total loan /$4m deposit

The part attributable to the onshore loan is $1m onshore loan / $2.5m total loan

$160,000 x $2.5m / $4m x $1m / $2.5m = $40,000

Allowable interest = $50,000 – $40,000 = $10,000
