# Profits tax - Example 3 - business carried on in HK
A Ltd is a company incorporated and listed in Hong Kong. At all relevant times, A Ltd traded actively in listed shares in Hong Kong. To finance the share dealing and investment activities, A Ltd placed deposits with the Hong Kong branch of Bank C as security for a back-to-back loan. The deposits were usually rolled over automatically without much management.

For the year ended 31 March 2015, the turnover of A Ltd consisted of bank interest income, gains on disposal of and dividend income from the listed shares.

Required

Discuss whether the placing of deposits by A Ltd with Bank C constitutes a carrying on of business in Hong Kong.

Solution

A Ltd placed deposits with Bank C in Hong Kong so as to secure a loan for its share dealing and investment activities in Hong Kong. Following [[CIR v Bartica Investment Ltd [(1966) 4 HKTC 129}]], such on-going activities went **beyond the mere passive acquiescence** and were sufficient to constitute carrying on a business in Hong Kong.
