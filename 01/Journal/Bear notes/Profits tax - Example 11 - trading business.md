# Profits tax - Example 11 - trading business
A company carries on a business of selling books in Hong Kong, and operates a server (at its disposal) located outside Hong Kong. Its customers obtain details of books and price lists, place orders and make payment through the server. However, all other business operations of the company, including procurement, storage and physical delivery of books, are performed in Hong Kong. In this case, the profits made by the company from selling books through the server are sourced in Hong Kong, and chargeable to Hong Kong profits tax.
