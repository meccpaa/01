# instrument s.2
An 'instrument' is defined in s.2 as including 'every written document'. Provided that an instrument falls within one of the following Heads of charge, it will be stampable whether or not that instrument was executed in or outside Hong Kong:

Head 1 Immovable property in Hong Kong
Head 2 Hong Kong stock
Head 3 Hong Kong bearer instrument
Head 4 Duplicates and counterparts of chargeable instruments under Heads 1, 2 and 3
