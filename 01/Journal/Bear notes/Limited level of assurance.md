# Limited level of assurance
Engagement risk must be reduced to an acceptable level under the circumstances but that risk will be greater than for a reasonable assurance engagement.

For example: A review performed in accordance with HKSRE 2400 (Revised) Engagements to Review Historical Financial Statements is a limited assurance engagement.

The conclusion is expressed in 'negative terms', for example:

'Based on the procedures performed and evidence obtained, nothing has come to our attention that causes us to believe that the entity has not complied, in all material respects with XYZ law' (Framework para 86).
