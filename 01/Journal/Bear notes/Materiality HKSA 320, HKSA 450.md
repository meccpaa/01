# Materiality HKSA 320, HKSA 450
![](Materiality%20HKSA%20320,%20HKSA%20450/image.png)
Materiality should be calculated at the **planning stages** of all audits. The calculation or estimation of materiality should be based on ~experience and judgment~.

Materiality should be **reviewed throughout** the audit and revised if necessary. An item might be material due to its nature, value or impact on the readers of the financial statements.

**Assessing** whether an ~omission or misstatement~ may influence the **decision-making** by users, requires consideration of the characteristics of those users and how the information may be used.
