# Profit-sharing schemes and bonus plans
HKAS 19 requires that an entity recognises the expected cost of profit-sharing and bonus payments only when:
- The entity has a present **legal or constructive obligation** to make such payments as a result of **past events**, [[obligation (legal or constructive)]] and
- A **reliable estimate** of the obligation can be made.

In this case an expense and liability should be recognised.

Often conditions are attached to ~bonus~ payments, for example an employee must still be in the entity's employment when the bonus becomes payable.

Therefore, an estimate should be made based on the expectation of the level of bonuses that will ultimately be paid. A reliable estimate can only be made when:
- The **formal terms** of the plan include a formula to determine the amount of the benefit;
- The entity determines the **amount payable** before the financial statements are authorised for issue; or
- **Past practice** provides clear evidence of the amount of a [[constructive obligation]].
