# Measurement - new CF
Two main measurement bases are referenced in the Conceptual Framework:
- Historical cost; and
- Current value (which includes fair value, value in use, fulfilment value and current cost).

Guidance on choosing a measurement basis is provided, with an emphasis on providing relevant information that faithfully represents an item. Faithful representation is affected by measurement inconsistency and measurement uncertainty.
