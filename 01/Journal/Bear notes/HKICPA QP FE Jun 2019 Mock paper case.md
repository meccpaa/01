# HKICPA QP FE Jun 2019 Mock paper: case
Brilliant Marine Services Limited (“Brilliant” or the “Company”) was incorporated in PRC in 1983. The A shares of the Company were listed on the Shanghai Stock Exchange in 1990 and its H shares were listed on the main board of the Hong Kong Stock Exchange on 19 December 2010. Brilliant and its subsidiaries (“Brilliant Group” or the “Group”) offer a range of high quality marine services to large multinational customers and governments around the world. The Group provides solutions to customers through the use of specialist equipment operated by expertise in the marine industry. The Group has offices throughout the world and employs over 1,800 staff worldwide onshore and offshore. Brilliant and its subsidiaries in the Group all have the financial year end date of 31 March.

Management of Brilliant often consider how to broaden the product and service range or geographical coverage. The Group is now engaged in the following lines of businesses: 
* Marine Support and Consultancy Services - MCSL mainly provides consultancy and support for the less mature markets in ship engineering, construction, shipyard selection, ship-to-ship transfers, offshore terminal services, and integrated marine services, etc. MCSL provides products, services and solutions to end market sectors including marine, oil and gas, ports, construction and renewables. 

* Testing and Monitoring Services - This focuses on products and services that measure and monitor structural stress, instrumentation and materials testing. MTSL is the leading provider of using a range of applications to monitor the structural integrity of infrastructure in the construction, maintenance and transport sectors. Moreover, MTSL also designs and manufactures testing and monitoring equipment for customers. 

* Specialist Engineering and Technical Business – The specialist engineering and technical businesses offered by MESL involves supply diving equipment and services, submarine rescue services and engineering solutions etc. MESL also provides specialist technical consultation in hazardous environments in the nuclear industry. To facilitate the provision of services, other than the Hong Kong Office, MESL has established branches in PRC, Taiwan and New Zealand. 

* Ship Broking Services – SBL provides services in ship chartering, seeking employment for the ships, and negotiating shipping rates and routes for its clients. In addition, SBL also monitors the implementation of voyage instructions, payments, preparation of contracts and related documents for its clients. 

- Ship Management Services - The ship owners may outsource regular operational functions to professional third-party ship managers to manage costs and operations. SML is a well- established professional ship management services provider offering services in organizing crew and supplies, dry-docking, regular maintenance, regulation compliance, staff recruitment, insurance, work permits, and staff training. 

Two weeks later, the CFO of Brilliant Group, Ivan Luk, needs to have a meeting with some of the board members of the Company. Ivan requests the Finance Manager of the Group, Mabel Chan, to write a memo on the financial reporting and risk management matters to be discussed during the meeting. 

### Lease of property 
On 31 March 2019 Marine Engineering Supply Limited (“MESL”) acquired a property at a price of $30,000,000 which is determined by a professional valuer – Property Valuation Ltd. (“PVL”) engaged by MESL. The property had an estimated remaining useful life of 40 years. 

On the same day, MESL rented out this property to Ship Management Limited (“SML”) on a three- year lease with lease payment of $1,000,000 per annum, payable in advance at the commencement and start of each period. Implicit interest rate is 8% per annum.
