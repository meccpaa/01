# s.14D Concessionary profits tax rate for qualifying corporate treasury centres
Qualifying income derived from corporate treasury activities carried on by a qualifying corporate treasury centre, which accrued on or after 1 April 2016 (discussed in section 5.7.1 on 'Concessionary profits tax rate for qualifying corporate treasury centres').

An election for the tax concession, once made, is irrevocable.
