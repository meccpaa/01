# Consideration transferred
Forms: cash, other assets, ordinary or preference instruments, debt instruments
Immediate or deferred (may be contingent upon an event or certain financial / non-financial goals)

The basic principle of HKFRS 3 (revised) is that consideration transferred in a business combination should be measured at **fair value**.
