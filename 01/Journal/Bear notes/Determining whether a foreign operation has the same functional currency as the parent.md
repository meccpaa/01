# Determining whether a foreign operation has the same functional currency as the parent
Where a parent has a foreign operation a number of factors are considered in order to decide whether the functional currency is the same as that of the parent:

(a) Whether the activities of the foreign operation are carried out as an **extension** of the parent, rather than being carried out with a significant degree of **autonomy**.

(b) Whether **transactions with the parent** are a high or a low proportion of the foreign operation's activities.

(c) Whether **cash flows** from the activities of the foreign operation **directly affect the cash flows of the parent** and are readily available for remittance to it.

(d) Whether the activities of the foreign operation are **financed** from its own cash flows or by borrowing from the parent.

Where these indicators are mixed, management must use judgment, giving priority to primary factors.
