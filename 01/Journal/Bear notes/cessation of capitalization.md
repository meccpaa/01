# cessation of capitalization
Once all the activities necessary to prepare the qualifying asset for its intended use or sale are substantially complete, the capitalisation of borrowing costs should come to an end. This will normally be the case when the physical construction of the asset is completed, though routine administrative work and minor modifications may still be outstanding.

An asset may be completed in parts or stages and each part can be used while construction is still in progress on the other parts. Capitalisation of borrowing costs should be terminated for each part as it is completed. The example quoted in the standard is a business park consisting of several buildings.
