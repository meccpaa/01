## 6 Current development
The IASB issued proposed amendments to IFRS 3 (HKFRS 3) in June 2016 to address a concern that arose during the post-implementation review of the standard, which was completed in June 2015.

The proposed amendments aim to clarify the definition of a business contained within the standard. This is important because the accounting treatment applied to the acquisition of a business is different from the accounting treatment applied to the acquisition of a group of assets; in particular goodwill arises on the acquisition of a business.

It is proposed that IFRS 3 (HKFRS 3) is amended to specify that in order to be considered a business, an acquired set of assets and activities must:

1. Not have a fair value of gross assets acquired concentrated in a single identifiable asset (or group of similar identifiable assets) and
2. Include an input and a substantive process that together mean that an output can be created.

The proposed amendments provide detailed guidance to be applied when determining whether a process is substantive.
