# corporate treasury service
'Corporate treasury service' is defined in s.1 of Schedule 17B to mean any of the following services provided to an associated corporation:
(a) Managing the cash and liquidity position;
(b) Processing payments to the vendors or suppliers;
(c) Managing relationships with FIs;
(d) Providing corporate finance advisory services;
(e) Advising on the management of the investment of the funds;
(f) Managing investor relations;
(g) Providing guarantees, performance bonds, standby letters of credit or other credit risk instruments, or services in relation to remittances;
(h) Providing advice or service in relation to the management of interest rate risk, foreign exchange risk, liquidity risk, credit risk, commoditiy risk or other financial risk;
(i) Providing assistance in merger or acquisition;
(j) Providing advice or service in relation to compliance with accounting standards, internal treasury policies or regulatory requirements;
(k) Providing advice or service in relation to operations of the treasury management system; and
(l) Providing business planning and co-ordination in connection with any of the above activities.
- - - -
Cash forecasting and cash pooling (including physical cash pooling and notional cash pooling) are within the scope of corporate treasury service, but re-invoicing transactions are neither corporate treasury services nor corporate treasury transactions, and the half rate concession will not apply (DIPN 52, para 63 and 67).

Where a QCTC enters into a corporate treasury service contract with a non-Hong Kong associated corporation which in turn enters into a back-to-back service contract on the same terms with a Hong Kong associated corporation, the IRD takes the view that the corporate treasury service is, in substance, provided by the QCTC to the Hong Kong associated corporation. Such service is not considered as a qualifying corporate treasury service and the half rate concession will not apply (DIPN 52, para 83).
