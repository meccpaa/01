# new SIT: serving as a director or officer of an audit client
A partner or employee of an assurance firm should not serve as a director or officer of an assurance client (HKICPA Code of Ethics: para. R523.3).

It may be acceptable for a partner or an employee of an assurance firm to perform the role of company secretary for an assurance client, if:
- The role is essentially administrative, with no managerial decision making; and
- This practice is specifically permitted under local law and professional rules.
(HKICPA Code of Ethics: para. R523.4).

Although a partner or employee cannot serve on a client's board, it is possible for them to attend board meetings. This is common practice, and moreover, may be necessary if there are issues that need to be raised with management.
