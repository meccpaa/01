# suspension of capitalization
Capitalisation of borrowing costs should be suspended for any extended periods where active development is interrupted.

Any borrowing costs incurred during such periods should be expensed through profit or loss.

Capitalisation of borrowing costs is not normally suspended during:
- A period when substantial technical and administrative work is being carried out
- A temporary delay occurs which is a necessary part of getting an asset ready for intended use, e.g. while inventories are maturing
