# Effect on shareholder wealth - Corporate finance EPS
- Basic EPS = net P/L for the period attributable to ordinary shareholders / weighted average number of ordinary shares outstanding during the period

One measure of gearing uses earnings per share.

The relationship between these two figures can be used to evaluate alternative financing plans by examining their effect on earnings per share over a range of operating profit levels. Its objective is to determine the operating profit indifference points amongst the various alternative financing plans.
