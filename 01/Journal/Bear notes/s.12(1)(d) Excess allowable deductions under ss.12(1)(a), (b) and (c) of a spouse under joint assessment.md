# s.12(1)(d): Excess allowable deductions under ss.12(1)(a), (b) and (c) of a spouse under joint assessment
The excess of allowable deductions of a spouse under ss.12 (a), (b) and (c) not fully utilised by that person can be deducted from the assessable income of the other spouse under **joint assessment**.
