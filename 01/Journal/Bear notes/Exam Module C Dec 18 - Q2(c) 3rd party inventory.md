# Exam Module C: Dec 18 - Q2(c) 3rd party inventory
Other than the inventories which were kept in the ELL’s warehouse and factory premises, identify any other inventories that ELL had recorded at the year end date. Given the fact that no inventory count for these inventories was arranged by ELL, advise measures or procedures that the audit team could perform in order to obtain reliable evidence to confirm their existence. (7 marks)

## Solution
Other inventories that ELL had recorded at the year end date included inventories ~held by the subcontractors~ and ~goods-in-transit~.

For inventories held by the subcontractors, if **material**, the audit team can perform the following procedures subsequent to the reporting date to confirm their **existence**:
- Obtain ~direct confirmations~ from the subcontractors to confirm the quantities and ownership of the inventories held by them on ELL’s behalf.
- ~Inspect any documentation~ regarding the goods held by the subcontractors.
- If possible, request ELL or subcontractors to perform an ~inventory count~ and such count will be attended by the auditor.
- Assess the ~effectiveness of the control~ implemented by the subcontractors regarding their inventory count procedures.
- Perform ~roll-backward procedures~ to **reconcile** these inventories at the year end date.

For **goods-in-transit**, if ~material~, the audit team can perform the following procedures:
- Obtain the ~shipping or other delivery evidence~ to verify the existence of the goods in transit.
- Examine the **evidence** of the ~subsequent goods received~.
