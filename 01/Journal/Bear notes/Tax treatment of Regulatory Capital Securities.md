# Tax treatment of Regulatory Capital Securities
The Inland Revenue (Amendment) (No. 2) Ordinance 2016 clarifies the tax treatment of regulatory capital securities issued by financial institutions to comply with Basel III capital adequacy requirements. The Basel III capital adequacy requirements are the minimum standards promulgated by the Basel Committee on Banking Supervision. Financial institutions should hold certain amount of regulatory capital expressed as a percentage of their total risk-weighted assets.

Any repayments under a regulatory capital security which are not repayments of principal are to be treated as interest for both deduction and taxation purposes. These include coupon payments, premium paid and discount given.

Specific anti-avoidance measures were issued to prevent financial institutions from issuing regulatory capital securities for tax avoidance purposes.

DIPN 53 was issued in February 2017 setting out the IRD's interpretation and practice on the application of related provisions in the IRO including ss.17A to 17G.

Refer to chapter 3 for details.
