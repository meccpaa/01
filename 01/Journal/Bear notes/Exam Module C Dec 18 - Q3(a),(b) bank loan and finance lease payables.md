# Exam Module C: Dec 18 - Q3(a),(b) bank loan and finance lease payables 
Q3. ELL had recorded a bank loan and finance lease payables in its 2018 financial statements. Both the bank loan and finance lease payables were classified and presented as non-current liabilities, with a current portion of finance lease payables being classified as current liabilities. The principal of the bank loan is repayable by 2023 and interest is repayable on a bi-annual basis. The finance leases are repayable by monthly instalments until 2022.
- - - -
**(a)** Propose procedures to audit the classification and presentation assertions of (i) bank loan and (ii) finance lease payables. (5 marks)
[[Specific audit procedures]] [[non-current liabilities - audit procedures]] [[classification and presentation]]
**(b)** Propose procedures to audit the accuracy and completeness assertions of interest expenses recorded by ELL relating to its (i) bank loan and (ii) finance leases.

(i) bank loan
- Review agreement with bank
- Ascertain the bank loan period
- Ensure the condition of liquidity ratio is not exceeded


(ii) finance lease payables

## Solution
(a) The audit team should perform the following procedures to audit the **classification and presentation** assertions of ELL’s bank loan and finance lease payables:

(i) Bank loan
- Examine the **bank loan agreement** to confirm the repayment of the loan is repayable ~beyond 12 months~ from the reporting date.
- As the loan agreement stipulates conditions with which ELL **must comply a current ratio** of greater than 1, the audit team should obtain evidence to ~confirm whether ELL has complied~ to such conditions. Otherwise, a demand of the ::immediate repayment:: of the loan would trigger the reclassification of such a loan from non-current to current liabilities.
- Obtain **direct confirmation** from the respective bank to confirm the ~due date~ for the repayment of the loan.

ii) Finance lease payables
- Examine the **respective agreement(s)** to confirm the ~repayment schedules~ of all finance lease payables.
- **Recalculate** the ~current portion~ of the finance lease payables.
- Examine the terms of respective agreement(s) to confirm the arrangements ~satisfied the definition of finance leases~ in accordance with the relevant **accounting standards**.
- - - -
The audit team should perform the following procedures to audit the **accuracy and completeness** assertions of interest expenses of the bank loan and finance leases:

(i) Bank loan
- As the **interest** is payable on a bi-annual basis, **examine** the ~two interest **payments**~ to the **bank advices** to confirm the amount of interest expenses to ensure it is accurately recorded.
- Examine the bank statements to **trace** the amount of interest expenses recorded by ELL to ensure the completeness.
- **Recalculate the** interest expense by applying the market (or agreed) interest rate on the principal loan balance and assess if there are significant discrepancies when comparing the recorded interest expenses.
- Examine the **subsequent interest payment** to ensure no significant unrecorded interest.

(ii) Finance leases
- Examine the finance lease **repayment schedules**, aggregate the total interests for the year and compare to the recorded interest expenses.
- **Compare** the current year interest expenses to that of the prior year and ensure if the changes are reasonable.
- Check the monthly **bank statements** and agree the portion of interest expenses paid and aggregate repayments of principal.
- Examine the ~annual statement sent by the lenders~ which may state the annual interest payment for the respective financial lease arrangements, and compare such annual interests to the recorded interest expenses.
