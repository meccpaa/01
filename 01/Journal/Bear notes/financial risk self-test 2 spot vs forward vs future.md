# financial risk self-test 2: spot vs forward vs future
Why might a non-financial trading company choose to:
(a) Buy or sell currency at spot rather than arrange forward FX contracts
(b) Arrange forward exchange contracts rather than hedge currency risk with currency futures?
- - - -
## Answer
(a) Buying or selling currency at spot rather than arranging forward contracts is likely to be preferred when:
- The value of the foreign currency transactions is small, or 
- The exchange rate is expected to move favourably, or to remain fairly stable, or 
- Settlement date for the transaction is very close.

(b) Arranging forward contracts to hedge FX is more likely than using futures for companies that do not have an active treasury team, because:
- Forward contracts are easier to arrange than futures transactions
- Futures positions may require management (for example, regular payments of cash with the futures exchange, to cover movements in the market price of the futures)
- Futures contracts may not be available in the currency pairs in which the company trades.
