We will be looking at six accounting standards in this and the next three chapters. HKFRS 10 to 12 and the revised versions of HKAS 27 and 28 (known as HKAS 27 (2011) and HKAS 28 (2011)) were issued in June 2011 as a package of five standards and have been applied since 1 January 2013:

- HKFRS 3 (revised) Business Combinations
- HKFRS 10 Consolidated Financial Statements
- HKFRS 11 Joint Arrangements
- HKFRS 12 Disclosure of Interests in Other Entities
- HKAS 27 (2011) Separate Financial Statements
- HKAS 28 (2011) Investments in Associates and Joint Ventures 
HKFRS 9 was covered in Chapter 17.
