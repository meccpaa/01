# tolerable rate of deviation
HKSA 530: Tolerable rate of deviation is the rate of deviation from prescribed [[Internal control and tests of controls]] procedures set by the auditor in respect of which the auditor seeks to obtain an appropriate level of assurance that the rate of deviation set by the auditor is ::not exceeded by the actual rate of deviation in the population::.

#QP/C/Audit/evidence

The **tolerable deviation rate** is the largest percentage variance experienced in audit sampling that an auditor will accept in order to rely upon a specific control. If the **deviation rate** is higher than this threshold value, then the auditor cannot rely upon the control.
