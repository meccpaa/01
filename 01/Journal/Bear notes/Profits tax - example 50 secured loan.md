# Profits tax - example 50 secured loan
The $1 million loan in Example 47 was secured by a deposit of $2 million that generated tax-free interest of $80,000.

Part of the deposit is used to secure the loan. Allowable interest is reduced by a portion of the taxfree interest on the deposit, calculated as:

$80,000 × $1m loan / $2m deposit = $40,000

Allowable interest = $50,000 – $40,000 = $10,000
