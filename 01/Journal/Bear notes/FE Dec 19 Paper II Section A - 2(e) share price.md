# FE Dec 19 Paper II Section A - 2(e) share price
**(e)  The Management is concerned with the share price due to the CSR issue. Besides improving the image of the Group, recommend to the Management three measures, in detail, on how to possibly maintain the share price.**
**(6 marks)**
- - - -
## Answer
Measures that possibly maintain the share price: 
* (i)  Maintain a stable dividend payout policy 
Shareholders expect a consistent dividend policy with either stable dividends each year or steady dividend growth. A large rise or fall in dividends in any year can unduly influence the company’s share price. 

* (ii)  Avoid issuing new shares 
An issue of new shares would affect the share price. Generally speaking, the share price will decline when new shares are issued. 

* (iii)  Good news related to the company. Make good news (launch of the online platform) available to the public as soon as possible. 
Good news has a direct impact on the share price. Generally speaking, good news will have a positive impact on share price and vice versa. 

* (iv)  Pay off the loans or debts 
If the credit rating of the Group is rated as excellent, then the Group is likely to meet its obligation and may not have major trouble paying back the debts or loans whereas a low credit rating suggests the Company may have trouble paying back the debts or loans. Paying off the loans or debts can improve the liquidity and solvency position of the Group that can improve the credit rating of the Group; and generally speaking, the share price will maintain stable. 

* (v)  Avoid share split (for example 2 for 1 share split) 
A share split occurs where each ordinary share is split into two shares; therefore, creating a larger number of shares will consequently affect the share price.
