# Head 1 Immovable property in Hong Kong
The stamping of immovable property is governed by Head 1 in the First Schedule:

Head 1 Immovable property in Hong Kong
Head 1(1) COS chargeable with AVD
Head 1(1AA) COS chargeable with SSD
Head 1(1AAB) COS chargeable with BSD
Head 1(1A) AFS chargeable with AVD
Head 1(1B) AFS chargeable with SSD
Head 1(1C) AFS chargeable with BSD
Head 1(2) Lease

In accordance with s.3 of the Interpretation and General Clauses Ordinance, 'immovable property' means:
(a) land, whether covered by water or not;
(b) any estate, right, interest or easement in or over land; and
(c) things attached to land or permanently fastened to anything attached to land.

The definition includes both tangible land and buildings, and intangible rights in or over any land and buildings.

There are normally three instruments involved in the sale of immovable property, namely a provisional (temporary) AFS ('PAFS'), a formal AFS and a COS. Typically, the PAFS (or the formal AFS if it is executed within 14 days of signing the PAFS) of an immovable property is subject to AVD while the COS (if it is executed in conformity with the duly stamped AFS) is subject to a fixed duty of $100.
