# Salaries Tax - Exam Practice 3 John Chan
John Chan

18 minutes

John Chan acquired Property A in January 2002 just before his wedding. The funds for financing the purchase were provided by his father as a wedding gift to his son.

John and his wife moved into Property A after they returned from their honeymoon.

John's wife, Mary, gave birth to a baby boy on 10 October 2009. They moved into a larger apartment, Property B. They used the proceeds from the sale of Property A and a mortgage loan obtained from a local bank to finance the purchase of Property B.

Required

(a) Is the gain on Property A taxable? (3 marks)

(b) John is preparing his salaries tax return for the year of assessment 2010/11 and called you to inquire what allowances/deductions are available to him? (7 marks)
