# Example: Factoring
A company makes annual credit sales of $200m. Credit terms are one month, but its debt administration has been poor and the average collection period has been two months with 2% of sales resulting in bad debts which are written off. A non-recourse factor would take on the task of debt administration and credit checking, at an annual fee of 6% of sales receipts. The company would save $8m a year in administration costs. The factor would also provide an advance of 75% of invoiced debts at once at an interest rate of 3% above the current base rate which is at 10% and the remaining 25% is payable in normal credit term. The company normally obtains an overdraft facility to finance its debtors at a rate of 2% over base rate.
