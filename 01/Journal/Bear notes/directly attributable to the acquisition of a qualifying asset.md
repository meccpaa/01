# directly attributable to the acquisition of a qualifying asset
HKAS 23 states that the determination of the amount of borrowing costs that are directly attributable to the acquisition of a qualifying asset is difficult and the exercise of judgment is required.

This is particularly the case where:
- Loans are denominated in foreign currencies and exchange rates fluctuate
- Financing of projects is co-ordinated centrally
- A group uses a range of debt instruments to borrow funds at various rates and lends those funds to other entities in the group on various bases.
