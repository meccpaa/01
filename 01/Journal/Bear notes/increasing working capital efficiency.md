# increasing working capital efficiency
It is important not to forget that an internal source of finance is the **savings** that can be generated from ~more efficient management of trade receivables, inventory, cash and trade payables~. Efficient working capital management can **reduce bank overdraft and interest charges** as well as increasing cash reserves. Actions might include:

- Accelerating collection of accounts receivable 
- Expanding accounts payable 
- Reducing inventory levels 
- Reducing expenses 
- Selling any surplus assets.
