# probability weighting
The estimate of expected credit losses should reflect an unbiased and probability-weighted amount that is determined by evaluating a range of possible outcomes.   

In simple cases there may be no more than two possible outcomes (a credit loss occurs and a credit loss does not occur), whereas in other, more complex cases, a number of scenarios will be identified in which the amount and timing of expected cash flows, and so the amount of credit loss, varies.  

A probability is assessed for each possible outcome or scenario and applied to the credit loss arising in each scenario in order to calculate a probability-weighted expected credit loss.
