# s.80(2) of IRO
Any person who, without reasonable excuse:

(a) Makes an incorrect return by omitting or understating anything in respect of which he is required to make a return;

(b) Makes an incorrect statement in connection with a claim for any deduction or allowance;

(c) Gives any incorrect information in relation to any matter or thing affecting his own liability for tax or the liability of any other person;

(d) Fails to comply with the requirements of a notice given to him under s.51(1) or s.51(2A) (i.e. fails to submit a return by the due date); or

(e) Fails to comply with s.51(2) (i.e. to inform the Commissioner of his chargeability to tax).

Commits an offence and is liable on conviction to a fine at level 3 and a fine of treble the amount of tax that has been undercharged or that would have been undercharged if such failure had not been detected.
