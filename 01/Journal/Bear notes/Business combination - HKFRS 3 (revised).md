# Business combination - HKFRS 3 (revised)
![](Journal/Bear%20notes/Doc/Business%20combination%20-%20HKFRS%203%20(revised)/Attachment.jpeg)
provides guidance on the **measurement** of net assets acquired in a business combination, the non-controlling interest and goodwill arising on a business combination.

- ~**A. Recognition and measurement of**~
	* The assets and liabilities acquired when a parent company **achieves control** over a subsidiary or other business
	* Any NCI in the subsidiary or business
	* Goodwill arising on the acquisition or a subsidiary or business
- B. Necessary ~disclosures~ to provide information to evaluate a business combination
