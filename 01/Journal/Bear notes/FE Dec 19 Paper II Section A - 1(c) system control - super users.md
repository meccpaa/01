# FE Dec 19 Paper II Section A - 1(c) system control - super users
**(c) Regarding the issue that the newly employed inventory warehouse manager has “super user” access to the Group’s various systems, advise the procedures you would perform in order to assess whether the “super user” access would impact the audit of the Group for the current year.**
**(7 marks)*
- - - -
## Answer 1(c)
In relation to the matter that the newly employed inventory warehouse manager has “super user” access to the Group’s various systems, the auditor should: 

Understand the extent of the “super user” access on the Group’s various systems; whether the inventory manager can edit or make changes to the program design and data, or if he is supposed to only read the information kept in the Group’s various systems; and whether the inventory manager’s right to access the various systems is appropriate considering the job responsibilities of the inventory manager; and whether approval is made by management. 

Understand how the access rights to various systems were defined and established by appropriate levels of IT and business unit management to achieve relevant control objectives, including segregation of duties objectives in both IT and in business processes. 

Understand how the Management designed security administration controls to ensure that access rights to various systems are properly granted, changed and removed as needed, only upon approval by appropriate management personnel. 

Understand whether there is control in place on periodic reviews of user access to ensure that access remains commensurate with job responsibilities. 

Understand whether there is a formal approval process in place to control data changes. For example, whether formal approvals are obtained for all changes, from the requesting parties and IT (to document the change was needed and why) and user management (to verify the change was made correctly). 

Understand whether there is, and if so test the, mitigating controls in place during the year. For example, whether all entries made are logged by the systems; whether control is in place for reviewing the log periodically by the Management. If we are able to demonstrate that the inventory warehouse manager had not used that “super-user” access inappropriately during the year, then the risk associated with this issue is low.
