# Example: financial targets
Lion Grange Co. has recently introduced a formal scheme of long-range planning. Sales in the current year reached $10,000,000, and forecasts for the next five years are $10,600,000, $11,400,000, $12,400,000, $13,600,000 and $15,000,000. The ratio of net profit after tax to sales is 10%, and this is expected to continue throughout the planning period. Total assets less current liabilities will remain at around 125% of sales. Equity in the current year is $8.75m.

It was suggested at a recent board meeting that:
(a) If profits rise, dividends should rise by at least the same percentage.
(b) An earnings retention rate of 50% should be maintained i.e. a payment ratio of 50%.
(c) The ratio of long-term borrowing to long-term funds (debt plus equity) is limited (by the market) to 30%, which happens also to be the current gearing level of the company.
You are required to prepare a financial analysis of the draft long-range plan.
