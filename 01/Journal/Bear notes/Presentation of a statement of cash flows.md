# Presentation of a statement of cash flows
According to HKAS 7, reported cash flows during a period should be **classified** into operating, investing and financing activities in a statement of cash flows.

The way of presenting cash flows under operating, investing and financing activities is **dependent on the nature of the reporting entity**. The classification enables users to understand the impact of each activity on cash and cash equivalents and their interrelationship.
