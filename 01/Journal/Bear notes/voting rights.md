# voting rights
In some cases assessing power is straightforward, for example, where power is obtained directly and solely from having the majority of voting rights or potential voting rights, and as a result the ability to direct relevant activities.

Potential voting rights may arise from convertible instruments or options. When considering these in the context of determining power, the purpose and design of the instrument should be considered, alongside the purpose and design of any other involvement with the investee. Potential voting rights may, alone, or in combination with other rights, give the ability to direct relevant activities.
