# Tangible non-current assets - audit procedures 
![](Tangible%20non-current%20assets%20-%20audit%20procedures/image.png)
![](Tangible%20non-current%20assets%20-%20audit%20procedures/image%202.png)
These are key areas when testing tangible non-current assets:
- Confirmation of ownership 
- Inspection of non-current assets 
- Valuation by third parties 
- Adequacy of depreciation rates
- - - -
**1.1 [[Audit objectives for tangible non-current assets]]**
1. [[existence]] and [[occurrence]] - 
	- Additions represent assets acquired in the year and disposals represent assets sold or scrapped in the year
	- Recorded assets represent those in use at the year-end
2. [[Completeness]]
	- All additions and disposals that occurred in the year have been recorded
	- Balances represent assets in use at the year-end
3. [[rights and obligations]]
	- The entity has rights to the assets purchased and those recorded at the year-end
4. [[accuracy, valuation and allocation]]
	- Non-current assets are correctly stated at cost less accumulated depreciation
	- Additions and disposals are correctly recorded
5. [[classification and presentation]]
	- Disclosures relating to cost, additions and disposals, depreciation policies, useful lives and assets held under finance leases are adequate and in accordance with accounting standards
- - - -
**1.2 [[Internal control considerations for non-current assets]]**
1. The **[[non-current asset register]]** is a very important aspect of the internal control system. It enables assets to be identified, and comparisons between the general ledger, non-current asset register and the assets themselves provide evidence that the assets are completely recorded.

2. Another significant control is **procedures over acquisitions and disposals**, that acquisitions and disposals are properly authorised, and proceeds are accounted for.

3. **Other significant aspects** are whether:
	- Security arrangements over non-current assets are sufficient 
	- Non-current assets are maintained properly 
	- Depreciation is reviewed every year 
	- All income is collected from income-yielding assets
