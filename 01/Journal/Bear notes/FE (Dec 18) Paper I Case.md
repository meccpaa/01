# FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Case
Shine Stars Holdings Inc (“SSHI” or the “Company”) was incorporated in Hong Kong and the Company is **planning to be listed** on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange. SSHI and its subsidiaries (the “Group”) are ~controlled and 70% owned~ by the Shine Family. The Group has a financial year end of 31 December and the financial statements are prepared in accordance with the Hong Kong Financial Reporting Standards. The Group is subject to Hong Kong ~profit tax at 16.5%~.

The Group has been engaging in selling health care products, such as Men’s and Women’s Nutrition, Vitamins and Minerals, Joint Support and Nutritional Oil and Juice etc. for more than 15 years and is the leader in the Hong Kong market. It is well-known for its Vitamins and Minerals products and many tourists from mainland China purchase these products when they visit Hong Kong.

**Andy Shine** is the ~founder~ of the Group. He is the ~Chairman~ of the Board, ::~Executive Director~::, and is a member of the ~Nomination Committee~ and ~Remuneration Committee~ of the Group. Andy is responsible for the strategic direction and overall performance of the Group. The Board also consists of **two** ~other Independent Non-Executive Directors~, Tom Wong and Peter Lee. **Tom** is also a member of the ~Audit Committee~ and ~Nomination Committee~ of the Group. **Peter** is also the Chairman of the Nomination Committee and a member of the Remuneration Committee of the Group.

### In-house Developed Inventory System
Since the products are sold through **over 150 selling points** including supermarkets, pharmacy stores and distributors, the Group uses its in-house developed integrated systems to ~manage the movement of inventory~ and to ~calculate the weighted-average costing of inventory.~ It is the Group’s policy that an **inventory count** will be arranged in different selling points on a ~rotation basis~ throughout the year. The Group will also perform a year-end inventory count at the Group’s warehouse in the last few days of the year.

The in-house developed integrated systems were upgraded during the year to rectify the ::deficiencies:: identified in the last year’s audit in relation to the inaccurate calculation of weighted-average costing. Before the systems were upgraded, remedial actions (manual checking and corrections) were taken by the management to address the deficiencies identified. The management represented that the data in the original systems were checked and corrected if necessary, and they were all transferred to the upgraded systems.

### The Management Discussion and Analysis Report, and Group Financial Statements
The Group, as a socially responsible company, focuses heavily on customers’ needs and has developed various products two years ago, such as the Joint Support supplements, which fit customers’ needs.

On the Management Discussion and Analysis Report attached with the draft group financial statements, the management provides the information about customer demographics, products offered as well as the sales and profit margin etc. The Vitamins and Minerals products are always the best-selling products of the Group. However, the management particularly stressed that the ~Joint Support supplements~ are the best-selling products due to the aging problem and the Sales and Promotion Team will focus on these products in the coming years.

In the management meeting held on 12 December 2017, it was proposed that a **one-off special bonus of HK$8 million**, which is calculated based on certain percentage of profit before tax, be paid to the employees. Based on the proposal which is subject to the approval of the directors, the bonus will be payable in ~four equal instalments~ of HK$2 million on 30 June 2018, 31 December 2018, 30 June 2019 and 31 December 2019, and the payment of each instalment is conditional upon the individual employees ~remaining in employment~ with the company. A current liability of HK$8 million has been recognised as at 31 December 2017. The bonus arrangement is not stipulated in the employment contracts. Subsequent to the issuance of the financial statement for the year ended 31 December 2017, the bonus proposal was approved by the directors on 1 April 2018 and written notices have been made to the employees on the same day.

### Expansion Plan
In one of the recent meetings, the management discussed an expansion plan in Hong Kong and the overseas market.

For the expansion plan in Hong Kong, the management discussed and met with the following parties:

The management met with the managers of supermarkets and pharmacy stores requesting **favourable product placement** (e.g. eye level position, near the entrance) for three years with the total fee at HK$2 million (“ ~Slotting fee~ ”). The Slotting fee is **non-refundable** and upon the supermarkets and pharmacy stores placing the first sales order with the Group, payment of the Slotting fee will be made. ~The supermarkets and pharmacy stores will ::not be committed:: to any minimum purchases quantities in the future~ upon the payment of the Slotting fee. The Finance Manager proposed that the slotting fee is a one off prepaid item; and the Head of the Sales and Promotion Team proposed to the managers of supermarkets and pharmacy stores that the exchange of products is not permitted.

The management also met with the distributors and discussed the **consignment arrangement**. The management understands that the design and renovation of each store is different due to unique specifications; therefore, the Group will sponsor the distributors for a design and renovation fee. In order to be entitled to the sponsorship of the design and renovation fee, the distributors are required to ~commit to a minimum sales quantity~ for the coming three years. If the distributors fail to meet the minimum quantity, the distributors are required to ~make a refund of the sponsorship~ of the design and renovation fee to the Group on a ~pro rata basis~. During the year ended 31 December 2017, the Group has sponsored the **design and renovation fee** with HK$4 million.

Besides, the Group will provide training to the distributors about the ingredients and functions of the products as well as training on how to sell the products of the Group. The management and the distributors agreed that exchange of products is allowed but a refund is not permitted.

The management analysed the collected data and found that the Joint Support supplements were particularly welcomed by the customers aged between 40 to 45 years old. With this analysis, the Head of the Sales and Promotion team proposed a new project (“Project A”) that requires HK$80 million as the initial investment. There was an argument between the Head of the Sales and Promotion Team and the Finance Director about the substantial amount of the initial investment. Under this circumstance, the Finance Director requested a budget in the next meeting that outlines the cash outflow and cash inflow with respect to Project A.

For the expansion plan outside Hong Kong, the management planned to raise capital in Hong Kong by various means, such as requesting a bank loan from a bank, issuing debts, preference shares and ordinary shares. The Group considered many factors in several countries for possible expansion, such as, labour cost and foreign currency fluctuation etc. and the Group has identified that the United Kingdom (“UK”) is the optimal market for overseas expansion.

In the meeting, the management also discussed the following issues:
- The implementation and impact on corporate social responsibility practices
- Review of the Key Performance Indicators (“KPI”), such as sales turnover and profit margin etc.
- Sustainability and Integrated Reporting
- Introduction of a membership discount programme and redemption programme in order to attract more customers
- Different ways to establish its business in the UK, such as merger and acquisition, joint venture as well as license and franchise agreements etc.

In addition, Andy Shine and the Company are currently under negotiation with a potential strategic investor, Moonlight Investment Limited (the “Pre-IPO Investor”), to enter into a share purchase and subscription agreement (the “Sale and Subscription Agreement”).

Below sets out the draft key terms of the Sale and Subscription Agreement:
- Andy will sell 1 million ordinary shares (“Sale Shares”) to the Pre-IPO Investors at a cash consideration of HK$20 million (“Sale Consideration”); with an option granted to the Pre-IPO Investors that Andy would purchase the Sale Shares from the Pre-IPO Investor if the Company failed to complete the IPO and be listed by 31 December 2020 at the Sale Consideration plus 10% interest per annum.
- the Company will issue 1 million new ordinary shares (“Subscription Shares”) to the Pre-IPO Investor at a cash consideration of HK$20 million (“Subscription Consideration”) with an option granted to the Pre-IPO Investors that the Company would purchase the Subscription Shares from the Pre-IPO Investor if the Company failed to complete the IPO and be listed by 31 December 2020 at the Subscription Consideration plus 10% interest per annum. 
- the Company will grant an option (“Subscription Option”) to the Pre-IPO Investor to subscribe 1 million new ordinary shares at a consideration of HK$28 million between 1 January 2019 and 31 December 2019 and the Company will receive a payment of HK$1.5 million from the Pre-IPO Investor for the Subscription Option.
