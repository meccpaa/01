# No level of assurance
Here, the professional accountant is giving no assurance at all for the engagement.

(a) Agreed-upon procedures (HKSRS 4400) – the professional accountant and the entity determine the procedures to be performed and professional accountants will provide a report of factual findings as a result of undertaking those procedures.

(b) Compilation (HKSRS 4410 (Revised)) of financial or other information – professional accountants use their accounting expertise to collect, classify and summarise financial information, users of the compiled information will gain some benefits because professional accountants carry out their work with professional competence and due care.

(c) Preparation of tax returns.

(d) Management consulting and advisory services.
