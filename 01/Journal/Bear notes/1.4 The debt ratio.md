# 1.4 The debt ratio
Another measure of financial risk is the debt ratio. There are a number of definitions used, of which one possibility is:
![](Journal/Bear%20notes/Doc/1.4%20The%20debt%20ratio/7D597510-B5DA-4932-9CF4-6A0CCF32EED7.png)
Debt does not include long-term provisions and liabilities such as deferred taxation.

A variation of the debt ratio is the debt-equity ratio, which can be calculated in a number of ways:
![](Journal/Bear%20notes/Doc/1.4%20The%20debt%20ratio/41B48BE9-692C-4C66-B458-892B86BAA1AD.png)
There is no firm rule on the maximum safe debt ratio, but as a general guide, you might regard 50% as a safe limit to debt.
