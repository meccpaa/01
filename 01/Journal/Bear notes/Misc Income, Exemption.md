# Misc Income, Exemption
![](Misc%20Income,%20Exemption/image.png)
Certain sums, such as recovery of bad debts and proceeds from sale of machinery and plant, previously allowed as deductions are specifically chargeable to profits tax while other sums, such as profits arising from the sale of capital assets, are specifically exempt from profits tax.

## 5.1 Sums specifically chargeable to profits tax
S.15 extends the scope of charge of s.14 by providing that **certain sums 'shall be deemed to be receipts arising in or derived from Hong Kong from a trade, profession, or business carried on in Hong Kong'**. It applies where the receipts are not otherwise chargeable to profits tax under s.14. The deemed trading receipts cover several types of income which are generally subject to **withholding tax** in other jurisdictions (e.g. interest, royalties and rent). These deeming provisions were brought into the IRO on the recommendation of the Second Inland Revenue Ordinance Review Committee, in accordance with a proposal advanced by the then Commissioner.

The [[deemed trading receipts]] are discussed below:
- [[s.15(1)(a), (b), (ba); s.21A, s.20B - IPRs]]: Copyrights, trade mark etc.
- [[s.15(1)(c) - grants]]: grants, subsidies
- [[s.15(1)(d) - movable property]]: Hire or rental - use movable property in HK
- [[s.15(1)(f), (g), (i), (ia) - interest]]: Interest
- [[s.15(1)(h) - refund RORS]]: refund of contributions
- [[s.15(1)(j), (k), (l), (la) - CD]]: certificate of deposit
- [[s.15(1)(m), s.15A - right to receive income]]: transfer of the right to receive income
