# Salaries Tax - Example 7: Employment packages
## Package 1

Annual salary $4,000,000, no fringe benefits.

Salaries tax payable = $580,000 (i.e. (15%  $4,000,000) – $20,000)

(Note. Tax computed based on progressive tax rates on net chargeable income (i.e. after deduction of personal allowance for single person) is $625,560 (after the deduction of $20,000 for 2016/17), which is higher than the tax computed at 15% on the net assessable income before the deduction of personal allowance for single person.)

Tax savings = $580,000 – $310,000 = $270,000

The IRD has not provided any guidance on the proper amount or proportion of the accommodation benefits in a remuneration package. Proper documentary evidence should be kept and substantiated. The proportion of accommodation benefits may be challenged by the IRD.
