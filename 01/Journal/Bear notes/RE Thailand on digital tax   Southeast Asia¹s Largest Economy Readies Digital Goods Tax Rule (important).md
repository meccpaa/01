Hi Adrian

The new Thai VAT rule is relevant to us because I understand that the contract with Apple/Google is entered into by OTT HK, but not OTT Thai.

Under the current situation, service fee is remitted from Apple/Google to OTT HK and then OTT Thai recharges OTT HK for the revenue from Thai customers.  Currently no VAT is charged to Thai customers.  However, there should be Thai VAT implications as the customers are located in Thailand.  Previously, I proposed that adding the VAT in the price charged to Thai customers so that the VAT collected will pass to OTT Thai and OTT Thai will pay the VAT to Thai tax authorities.

With the new Thai VAT rule, while we are waiting for detailed guidelines, I would expect that the arrangement would be similar to Singapore – Google and Apple will withhold the VAT before remitting the fee collected from customers to OTT HK.  If we would like to maintain the same level of revenue, we need to add VAT to the price we charged to the customers.  However, we have to seek further clarification about whether OTT Thai will be exempt from charging VAT when it recharges OTT HK for its revenue share.

I would say the current arrangement is not ideal.  The most simple solution is that we use OTT Thai and contract with Apple and Google.  I think we should do so because we already have a Thai entity.

Regards
Frederick
