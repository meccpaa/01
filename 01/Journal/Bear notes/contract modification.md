# contract modification
A contract modification is accounted for as a **separate** contract if:
(a) The scope of the contract changes because **additional distinct goods or services** are promised (see section 2.3.2), and
(b) The increase to the contract price reflects the [[stand-alone selling price]] of the additional goods or services promised.
