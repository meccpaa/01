# IT audit self-test 3
A customer forgot to retrieve his cash from the dispenser after an ATM (automatic teller machine) transaction in the Nathan Road Branch of The Bank of San Carla, Tsimshatsui. Ten minutes after the transaction, the man returned to the branch and found that someone was using the ATM he had used but his cash was not there. He was anxious to get his money back and told the bank's Hotline Customer Service Representative that no cash came out from the cash dispenser. After the police investigated the case and reviewed the branch records, a near-by street sleeper was arrested on charges of theft.

Required

For the ATM cash withdrawal activities, suggest a total of six general controls or application controls, in a computer-related environment, that the auditors expect to be present to protect the bank and customers from the theft of cash and disputes with customers. (6 marks) HKICPA June 2014


- - - -
## Answer 3
### General computer controls
- Proper approval of testing acceptance of ATM hardware and software application before deployment.
- Updated user manual and training of staff operating ATMs.
- Physical protection of ATMs from deliberate destruction or intrusion.
- CCTV designed to capture activity at ATM machines.
- Controlled cash count and replenishment procedures e.g. two or more staff involved in cash count and delivery of cash.
- Indemnity agreement signed by ATM customer.
- Customer access by card and password.
- Firewall or hacker protection measures.
- Data transfers between an ATM and the Main computer system are encrypted and processed properly through secured communication lines.

### Application controls 
- Transaction activity log.
- Computer sensor and programming to forfeit cash left idle in the cash dispenser at expiry of waiting period.
- Each ATM is linked to the others so that a person cannot obtain the maximum cash withdrawal from each machine at once.
