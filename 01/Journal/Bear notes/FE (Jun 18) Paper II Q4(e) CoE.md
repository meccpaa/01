# FE (Jun 18) Paper II: Q4(e) CoE
Jane Wong, the audit manager of Team B, had lunch with her friend, Kate Chan, and noted that Kate has joined a tour organised by WT Tours. They talked about those ongoing litigations during the lunch. Consider whether or not Jane can discuss the litigations with Kate.

(2 marks)
- - - -
## Solutions
Jane’s discussion with Kate of those litigations should only be limited to **information already available** in the ~public~ sphere.

Jane should ::not disclose:: that ~she is **involved** in the audit of WT Tours~ and she should not disclose any **confidential information** related either to WT Tours or the litigations.
