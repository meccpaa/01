# Purchase and sale of unlisted securities
In general, the situs test is not applicable to unlisted securities. According to DIPN 21 (Revised), the contract effected test applies. Locality of the profit derived from the purchase and sale of unlisted securities will be taken as the place where the contracts of purchase and sale are effected (except FIs in instances where s.15(1)(l) applies).
