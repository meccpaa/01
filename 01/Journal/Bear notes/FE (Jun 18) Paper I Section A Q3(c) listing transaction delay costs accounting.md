# FE (Jun 18) Paper I: Section A Q3(c) listing transaction delay costs accounting
(c) The accounting treatment for the IPO costs incurred due to the delay or postponement of the IPO.
(2 marks)
## Answer
In the case of a delay or postponement of the IPO timeline and additional costs are incurred, the costs relating to the earlier attempt that can no longer be leveraged on should be expensed in the profit or loss.
