# right-of-use asset
An asset that represents a lessee's right to use an [[underlying asset]] for the [[lease term]].
- - - -
## 3.3.3 Initial measurement of the right-of-use asset
The right-of-use asset is initially measured at cost:

- initial measurement of [[lease liability]]
- [[lease payments]] made by lessee before commencement of lease [[commencement date of the lease]]
- [[initial direct costs incurred by the lessee]]
- estimated [[dismantling and restoration costs]] at the end of the lease term (payable by the lessee)
- LESS: [[lease incentives]] received
= **Cost of right-of-use asset**

## 3.3.4 subsequent measurement of the right-of-use asset
A right-of-use asset is measured at cost less accumulated **depreciation** less accumulated impairment losses. 
	- The ~depreciation period~ of a right-of-use asset is the **shorter of** [[useful life]] of the underlying asset and [[lease term]]. If ownership of the underlying asset transfers to the lessee, the depreciation period is useful life.
	- [[HKAS 36 Impairment]] is applied to determine whether a right-of-use asset is **impaired** and to account for any loss. 

::Two exceptions:: exist to this measurement rule:
	- If the **underlying asset is PPE** and belongs to a ~class of asset~ that the lessee measures using the ~revaluation model~, the lessee may elect to measure all right-of-use assets in that class of asset using the HKAS 16 revaluation model.
	- If the **underlying asset is investment property** and the lessee applies the ~fair value model~ to investment properties, the right-of-use asset ~must be~ measured in accordance with the ~HKAS 40 fair value model~.
