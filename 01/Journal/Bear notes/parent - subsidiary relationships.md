# parent - subsidiary relationships
Relationships between parents and subsidiaries must be disclosed irrespective of whether any transactions have taken place between the related parties. An entity must disclose the name of its parent and, if different, the ultimate controlling party. This will enable a reader of the financial statements to be able to form a view about the effects of a related party relationship on the reporting entity.

If neither the parent nor the ultimate controlling party produces financial statements available for public use, the name of the next most senior parent that does so shall also be disclosed.
