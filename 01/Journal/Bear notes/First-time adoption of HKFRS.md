# First-time adoption of HKFRS
HKFRS 1 provides guidance on the transition to HKFRS from another set of accounting standards.

HKFRS 1 First-time Adoption of HKFRS sets out the procedures that a reporting entity should follow when it adopts HKFRS for the first time. In particular it provides guidance on the preparation of an opening statement of financial position at the date of transition to HKFRS that is used to provide brought forward balances to which HKFRS are applied.

The objective of the standard is to ensure that an entity's first HKFRS financial statements contain high quality information that:

(a) Is transparent and comparable over all periods presented;
(b) Provides a suitable starting point for applying HKFRS; and
(c) Can be generated at a cost that does not exceed the benefits.

In order to achieve the final objective, the standard provides a number of exemptions from the full application of HKFRS when preparing an opening statement of financial position at the transition date.
