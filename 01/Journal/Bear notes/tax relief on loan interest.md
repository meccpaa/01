# tax relief on loan interest
As far as companies are concerned, debt capital is a potentially attractive source of finance because **interest charges reduce the profits chargeable to tax**.

A new issue of bonds is likely to be preferable to a new issue of preference shares (preference shares are shares carrying a fixed rate of dividends).

Companies might wish to avoid dilution of shareholdings and increase gearing (the ratio of fixed interest capital to equity capital) in order to improve their earnings per share by benefitting from tax relief on interest payments.
