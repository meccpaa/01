# Identifying reportable segments
Reportable segments are operating segments or aggregation of operating segments that meet the 10 test.

HKFRS 8 requires entities within its scope to report financial and descriptive information about its reportable segments, so the first task is to understand which segments are reportable. Reportable segments are either individual operating segments or aggregations of operating segments (see below) that meet the specified criteria (the 10 test explained below).
