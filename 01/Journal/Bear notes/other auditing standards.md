# other auditing standards
Apart from complying with HKSAs in the conduct of the audit, an auditor may be required to conduct an audit in accordance with the auditing standards of a specific jurisdiction ('other auditing standards') as well. The auditor should ensure there is no conflict between the requirements in the other auditing standards and those in HKSAs. The auditor's report of specific jurisdictions shall identify other auditing standards when referring to law or regulation and shall be read as reference to the other auditing standards.
