# identification of specific risks
When the **corporate treasurer** understands the risks facing a company, the process of identifying and managing the various risks faced can be undertaken. **Critical** to this process is the requirement ~to have accurate, relevant and timely **information**, which is future-oriented~. The treasurer needs well-defined and reliable information to make decisions, recognising the challenge of identifying all the unknowns.

Internal information requirements comprise:
- Cash information and company systems (to determine where the company is) 
- Cash flow forecasts (to determine where the company is going)

External information requirements comprise:
- Strategic information: data on relevant macroeconomic, historical and forecast trends 
- Tactical information: data on short-term forecasts of interest rates and foreign exchange rates 
- Operational information: current online data on interest and exchange rates
