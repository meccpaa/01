# Reorganization: Self-test 1 - MBO
A venture capital has been asked to provide financial support for a management buyout of the operating division of a company.

What factors will the venture capitalist take into consideration when making a decision whether or not to invest?
- - - -
## Answer
Factors to consider:

- The **business** to be purchased: what is the business, its markets, customers and suppliers; what are the expected sales, costs and profits. What is the ~business plan~ for the business? 
- What are the skills and experience of the **management** buyout team? Do they have all the skills required to succeed? For example do they have the necessary operational and financial skills?
- **How much** capital is the venture capitalist being asked to provide, and what **form** will it take (equity shares, preference shares etc)?
- Will the business need to borrow from **other sources** (bank loans)?
- How much capital will the management team provide, and what **share of the equity** capital do they want for this?
- Why is the division being sold to the management buyout team? (**Reason for sale**) 
- What is the likely ‘**exit route**’ for the venture capitalist’s investment? Trade sale or a stock market listing, for example? When is this likely to occur?
- What **alternative investments** are available to the venture capitalist, and how do these compare to the MBO?
