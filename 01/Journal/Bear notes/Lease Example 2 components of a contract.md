# Lease Example 2: components of a contract
Fauna Co., a construction company, enters into a contract with a heavy plant manufacturer to obtain use of a digger and crane for a period of two years in exchange for annual payments in advance of $200,000 and an arrangement fee of $20,000. The terms of the contract require the manufacturer to service both vehicles annually.

Standalone prices have been determined as follows:
![](Lease%20Example%202%20components%20of%20a%20contract/ABADB42A-70F5-4842-92A6-080629F0A12B.png)
Required

Identify the components of the contract and allocate the consideration to them from Fauna's perspective, assuming that the practical expedient is not applied.
- - - -
## Solution
The contract contains an ::arrangement fee::, which is ~not a separate component~ of the contract.

The contract also conveys the right to use two assets. These assets are **not highly dependent** on one another, and each can be **benefitted from alone**, therefore they form ~two separate lease components~.

The two year service plans for each asset form non-lease components and these are aggregated to be a single non-lease component.

Therefore, for accounting purposes, the contract contains two lease components and a non-lease component.

The total of the standalone prices is $440,000 (100,000 + 250,000 + 30,000 + 60,000); the contract price is $420,000 (200,000 + 200,000 + 20,000).
