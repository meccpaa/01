# s.20ACA exemption provision for offshore private equity funds
Before 1 April 2015, profits tax exemption will only be provided in respect of a non-resident person's profits from dealing in specified transactions that are carried out through or arranged by specified persons. However, the definition of ‘[[specified transactions - schedule 16 of IRO]]' did not include securities of a **private company**. Therefore, the profits tax exemption for offshore funds did not apply to **PEFs** that typically invest in private companies. Moreover, ‘[[specified person]]’ referred only to a **corporation licensed under the SFO** while ::PEFs might not be managed by corporations licensed or registered under the SFO::. On 17 July 2015, the Inland Revenue (Amendment) (No. 2) Ordinance 2015 was enacted with retrospective effect from the year of assessment 2015/16 to:

(a) Extend the [[exemption to transactions in securities in EPCs]];
(b) **Waive** the requirement for transactions to be carried out through or arranged by ~persons licensed by the SFC~; and
(c) **Extend the exemption to SPVs** which are established to hold offshore investments. [[s.20ACA exemption for special purpose vehicles SPVs]]

It is expected that more PEF managers will set up or expand their business in Hong Kong, which will be conducive to the future development of Hong Kong's asset management industry.
