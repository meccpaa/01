# FE (Jun 18) Paper II: Q1 share options accounting
Assume that you are Ms Eleanor Chan, the CFO of the Group.

(a) Write a memo to Mr Hugo Kwok, the Director and CEO to advise as to the accounting impact of the proposed grant of the share options under the three proposals.

(12 marks)

(b) Assume the share option was granted under Proposal A on 1 July 2016 and one of the non-director employees has left the Group on 1 March 2017 before vesting and he is no longer entitled to the 50,000 share options granted to him. On the same date, the Group agreed with him and granted a discretionary award of 35,000 share options, which are fully vested immediately, as a part of the package of benefits on termination of his employment. The fair value of each share option is HK$5 and HK$7 as at 1 July 2016 and 1 March 2017, respectively.

Advise as to the accounting impact of the lapse of the share options and the discretionary awards granted to this non-director employee upon the termination of his employment for the year ended 31 December 2017.

(3 marks)
[[HKFRS 2: Share-based payment]]

## Answer
To: Mr Hugo Kwok, CEO
From: Ms Eleanor Chan, CFO
Date: DD / MM / YY
Subject: The accounting impact of the proposed share option schemes

### Proposal A
Since the share options are granted to the directors and employees with no cash settlement alternatives, they are equity-settled share awards and the fair value of the awards (i.e. the share options) would be measured at the grant date.
[[equity settled share-based payment transactions]]

In addition, the requirement of remaining for two years in employment is a [[service condition]].

Under HKFRS 2, service condition, which is a  [[non-market condition]], is not taken into account when estimating the ::fair value:: of the share options at the grant date. Instead it shall be taken into account by adjusting the **number of equity instruments** (i.e. share options) included in the measurement of the transaction amount so that, ultimately, the amount ~recognised for goods or services received~ (i.e. services provided by grantees) as consideration for the equity instruments (i.e. share options) granted shall be based on the number of equity instruments (i.e. share options) that eventually vest.

Since the Group expected 5% of the non-director employees would leave the Group before vesting, the ~fair value of the awards~ should be adjusted to reflect the **best estimate of employee retention**.

### Proposal B
In addition to the consideration under Proposal A, the extra share entitlements would be considered as vesting conditions which include both service condition (2-year service period) plus a [[market condition]] (share price target). In accordance with HKFRS 2, the market condition (share price target) is taken into account when **estimating the fair value** of the ~share option on the grant date~ and the **cost of the extra** share award of 500,000 options will be ~expensed over the two-year period~.

### Proposal C
There is a ~clear shared understanding on the terms of the awards for the Tranche 1~ Options only that will be potentially vested at the end of **Year 1**. Meanwhile there is no clear understanding on the terms of the Tranche 2 Options which will be potentially vested at the end of **Year 2** because the vesting depends on the ::revenue targets which have not yet been set::.

Accordingly the **grant date** for Tranche 1 and Tranche 2 Options would be the **beginning of the Year 1 and Year 2 respectively** and each tranche has a ~vesting period of one year~ from the date of grant.

The [[vesting condition]] includes both a [[service condition]] (grantees remained in employment) and a [[non-market condition]] [[performance condition]] (revenue target). Since these vesting conditions are non market conditions, these vesting conditions will ::not be taken into consideration:: when ::estimating the fair value:: at the date of grant but the **cost of the award** would be ~adjusted to reflect the best estimate of staff retention and probability in achieving the revenue target~.

(B) 
[[SBP - Cancellation]], [[SBP - New equity instruments are granted]]
The lapse of the share options and the grant of discretionary awards may be considered as

(i) Modification of the original award (vesting conditions are waived - removal of service requirement); or
(ii) Forfeiture of the original award and granting a new award on a discretionary basis.

(i) When the lapse is treated as a modification of the original award, the Group will:
	- **accelerate the costs not yet booked** for the original award [HK$170,833 = HK$5 x 50,000 options – (HK$5 x 50,000 options x 95% x 8 / 24 months)] as there is no future service; and
	- treat the discretionary awards as a **replacement award** at incremental fair value (fair value of new award). Since the fair value of the replacement award as at 1 March 2017 (HK$245,000 = HK$7 x 35,000 options) is ::smaller than:: the fair value of the original award (HK$350,000 = HK$7 x 50,000 options), **::no incremental costs::** are required to be recorded.

OR

(ii) When the lapse is treated as ::forfeiture:: of the original award and granting a new award, the Group will record a **net expense** of HK$165,833 (HK$245,000 - HK$79,167) on 1 March 2017:
	- **reverse** the cost already booked for the award (HK$79,167 = HK$5 x 50,000 options x 95% x 8 / 24 months); and
	- **recognise** the cost of the discretionary award at the grant date (i.e. 1 March 2017) (HK$245,000 = HK$7 x 35,000 options)
