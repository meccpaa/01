# FE Dec 19 Paper II Section A - 2(d) CSR share price
**(d)  With the case background information provided, advise with reasons how Corporate Social Responsibilities (“CSR”) may affect the Company’s share price. (3 marks)**
- - - -
## Answer
Corporate Social Responsibilities (“CSR”) promotes a vision of business accountability to various stakeholders. One of the key concerns in this case is environmental protection. 
A company which practices CSR will be viewed as a good company and can attract more shareholders as shareholders would like to invest in such a company that adopts environmental friendly policies, therefore, the share price usually will increase. 
The poor practices or poor image of the Group would have an adverse impact on the share price, as it is believed that share price is positively associated with its CSR practices or image. 
The overconsumption of wood in Indonesia will usually be viewed by society (one of the stakeholder groups) negatively, as the Group does not preserve the natural resources (wood). The unsustainable use of natural resources (wood) and the foreseeable litigations and lawsuits will raise the shareholders’ concern and lead to more sell-side activities and result in decline of the share price.
