# Payroll taxes and insurance contributions paid by employers
The assessment of these costs is done annually in some countries, though they are paid at an uneven rate during the year. A large portion of the taxes is paid in the earlier phase of the year while a much smaller portion is paid in the later part of the year. This situation thus entails the ~use of an estimated average annual tax rate~ in an interim statement, not the actual tax paid since the taxes are assessed on an annual basis, even though the payments pattern is uneven.
