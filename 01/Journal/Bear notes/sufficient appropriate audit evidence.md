# sufficient appropriate audit evidence
HKSA 500 requires that the auditor shall design and perform audit procedures that are appropriate in the circumstances for the purpose of obtaining sufficient appropriate audit evidence. ‘[[Sufficiency]]’ and ‘[[appropriateness]]’ are closely linked and apply to both [[tests of controls]] and [[substantive procedures]].
