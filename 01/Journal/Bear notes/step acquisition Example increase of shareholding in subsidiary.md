# step acquisition Example: increase of shareholding in subsidiary
A parent owns an 80 interest in a subsidiary which has net assets of 4,000. The carrying amount of the non-controlling interests is 800. The parent acquires an additional 10 interest from the non-controlling interests for 500.

The parent company will already have accounted for the acquisition in its separate financial statements by:

DEBIT Investment		$500
CREDIT Cash				$500
