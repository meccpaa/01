# MOOV: Tax Exposure of inviting Singapore artist for HK concert
 
The contracting party determines the HK tax rate.  If we contract with her company in Singapore, the HK tax rate of 16.5% applies.   If we contract with the person, the rate is 15%.  So contracting with a person will save a bit.

In both case we can elect the 2-tiered tax rate (i.e. half of the tax rate applies on the first HK$2m profit), after confirming that no connected entity has made such election.  If the 2-tiered tax rate is elected, we will need to file a tax return.
 https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/faq/2tr.htm

Please note that we have to submit Form IR623 (can be downloaded from IRD’s website) for payment to non-resident entertainer.
