# Expense / asset - SBP
The debit entry is made to an account relevant to the goods or services acquired. For example, where the counterparty is an ~employee~ and the share-based payment transaction is consideration for services provided, an appropriate account is **staff costs**; where the counterparty is a ~supplier~ of goods for resale, an appropriate account is **purchases or inventory**.
