# FE (Jun 19) Paper I: Q1(b) risk assessment revenue
(b)  Perform risk assessments for revenue recognition in relation to the license agreement with Advance Press and suggest the appropriate audit procedures to address the risk and the relevant assertions. 
(14 marks) 
- - - -
## Answer
The **revenue** in relation to the license agreement with Advance Press includes a fixed fee plus a sharing of proceeds from publishing the “Sing along with Kocky” series. The **risk of material misstatement** of the ~fixed fee~ is relatively lower because the amount is fixed according to the license agreement. The risk of material misstatement of the ~sharing of proceeds~ is relatively higher because it is based on the proceeds recognised by Advance Press from publishing the “Sing along with Kocky” series. 

The engagement team should **discuss with the management to understand** how they can obtain confidence that the proceeds communicated by Advance Press is reliable. For example, whether the proceeds are audited, or whether it is disclosed in the audited financial statements, etc. 

Based on this discussion with the management, the engagement team has to **identify the relevant controls** that the management have put in place, **evaluate** whether these controls are effective and **validate** whether the controls are executed as expected. For example, whether the management have implemented any controls to review the information provided by Advance Press and ensure the appropriate amounts are used to calculate the royalty and the calculation is correct. Also, since the gross amount of royalties is subject to the **withholding tax** in Singapore for which has to be borne by BE Consulting, the royalty revenue may not be properly recognised after taking into account of the withholding tax. The engagement team should discuss with the management to obtain further details to ensure they understand how the withholding tax is assessed and calculated, and they should access whether and how it would affect the royalty revenue recognised. 

The **completeness and occurrence** can be assessed by performing the following substantive procedures: 
* reviewing the license ~agreement~; 
* arranging ~confirmations~ with Advance Press; and 
* checking the ~bank statements~ on the amount of royalties received during each quarter. 

The **accuracy and cut-off** can be assessed by performing a reasonableness test.
