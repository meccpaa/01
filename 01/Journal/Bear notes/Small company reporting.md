In Hong Kong, there are therefore two options for simplified reporting for smaller, non-listed entities:
- The SME-FRF and SME-FRS - certain unlisted entities
- The HKFRS for Private Entities - any private entity

## SME-FRF and SME-FRS
**SME-FRF** - provides a **conceptual framework** for small company reporting
**SME-FRS** - sets out recognition, measurement, presentation and disclosure requirements for companies reporting under it

## HKFRS for Private Entities
### Eligibility to use the HKFRS for Private Entities
Council has approved the adoption of the HKFRS for Private Entities as a financial reporting option for companies that:
(a) Do not have [[public accountability]]; and
(b) Publish [[general purpose financial statements]] for external users.

A **subsidiary** of a group which applies full HKFRS may use the HKFRS for Private Entities provided that the subsidiary ~itself meets the eligibility criteria~.

### Contents of the HKFRS for Private Entities
The HKFRS for Private Entities is a self-contained standard incorporating accounting principles that are based on full HKFRS, but which have been simplified to suit the private entities within its scope.
