# HKFRS 15 - scope
HKFRS 15 does not apply to revenue arising from:
- HKFRS 16 Leases; [[HKFRS 16 Leases]]
- HKFRS 4 Insurance Contracts;
- Financial instruments and other contractual rights or obligations within the scope of HKFRS 9, HKFRS 10, HKFRS 11, HKAS 27 and HKAS 28 (2011); and
- Non-monetary exchanges between entities in the same line of business to facilitate sales to customers or potential customers.
