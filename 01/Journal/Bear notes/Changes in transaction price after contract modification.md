# Changes in transaction price after contract modification
The change in price is allocated to the performance obligations identified in 
1. The **original** contract - if the variable consideration was promised before the contract modification;
2. The **modified** contract - if the variable consideration was promised after the contract modification and the modification was not accounted for as a separate contract.
