# FE Dec 19 Paper I case
## CASE
Moments Photography Company Limited (“Moments” or the “Company”) is a company incorporated in Hong Kong. The principal activities of Moments and its subsidiaries (collectively the “Group”) include: 
* Provide portraits photography services in Hong Kong, Shanghai and Hangzhou 

* Operate online platforms for outdoors and event photography services 
The Group has a financial year end of 31 December, and the group structure of Moments as at 31 December 2018 is as follows:

The Company is a wholly-owned subsidiary of Picturesque Hong Kong Limited (“PHKL”), a company incorporated in the ::British Virgin Islands::. The sole shareholder of PHKL is Ms Gabriella Fung, a world renowned master photographer. 

### Photography services in Hong Kong, Shanghai and Hangzhou
The Group currently has three, four and two studios in Hong Kong, Shanghai and Hangzhou, respectively; the photography services are taken in the Group’s studios; in particular, the Group is famous for family and pet photography. 

### Unauthorised use of the images

The Group respects the privacy of the customers and ::would not use the customers’ image after the service:: (say for promotion and publicity or joining the photography competition) unless prior consent was obtained from the customers. However, on 7 September 2018, the Group received a ::complaint:: from Ms Gigi Cheung, a customer of MPHK who took pictures in the Group’s studio earlier, that certain images of her were used by Mr Johnny Yip, a full-time employee photographer of MPHK, in his personal social media accounts without her consent. Immediately after receiving the complaint, the Group requested Johnny to remove all the unauthorised images from his social media accounts, terminated Johnny’s employment with MPHK and ~proposed~ to provide Gigi a compensation of HK$100,000 to settle the incident. However, Gigi is still unhappy about this incident and ~demands~ compensation of HK$600,000 from MPHK. After rounds of negotiation, Gigi is willing to ~accept~ a compensation of HK$200,000 to settle the incident on 1 March 2019, which is before the finalisation of audited financial statements.

### Non-competing arrangement entered with Gary Lau
Mr Gary Lau has worked for MPHK as a full-time employee photographer for about five years. He is one of the most popular photographers of MPHK and his bookings are often full. On 26 September 2018, he demanded MPHK to double his base salary otherwise he would resign and join the competitor of MPHK. After weeks of negotiation, MPHK entered into **covenants** with Gary, that, where in exchange for payment of HK$3 million, Gary undertook that (1) he will ~not compete~ with MPHK in Hong Kong for five years after he left; and (2) he will ~not solicit customers and colleagues of MPHK~ by using the knowledge he gained during the course of his employment with MPHK. 

### Leasing of 3D scanning and 3D printing machines
On 1 March 2018, MPHK has leased a set of 3D scanning and 3D printing machines for a term of ~three years~ at a consideration of US$500,000 from Photo Innovation Inc (“PII”), an ~unrelated third party~ company in Shenzhen, for its new service offering to create personalised miniature figures for customers. PII has no other business presence in Hong Kong. 

### Acquisition of the 30% equity interest of MPSH from Mr Raymond Yuan
As at 1 January 2018, 30% of the equity interest in Moments Photography (Shanghai) Limited (“MPSH”) is owned by Mr Raymond Yuan, and 30% of the equity interest in MPSH is owned by Mr Francis Chen. Notwithstanding that the Group’s equity interest in MPSH is 40%, 
it was accounted for as a subsidiary by virtue of an agreement entered with **Raymond**, which he agreed to ~follow the Group’s actions and votes~ in the shareholders’ meeting of MPSH. Raymond would like to retire, and on 15 June 2018, MPHK entered into an agreement to ~acquire~ his equity interest of MPSH (the “Acquisition”) at a ~cash consideration of RMB7 million plus a vehicle of MPSH currently used by him~. The transaction was completed on 1 August 2018, and the ~carrying amount and fair value~ of the vehicle were RMB800,000 and RMB500,000, respectively. The carrying amount of the ~non-controlling interest~ of MPSH (60%) was RMB3 million immediately before the completion of the Acquisition as at 1 August 2018. 

### Online platforms for outdoor and event photography services

Moments Online Limited (“MOL”) is the 70% owned subsidiary of the Company and the other 30% is owned by Mr Lawrence Fung, the brother of Gabriella. MOL maintains a website and also a mobile application which both serve as platforms for outdoor and event photography in Hong Kong. Various ~photography packages~ are offered on the website for the customer to choose. Requests (including date, time, location, style of the photography, and other special requirements) are made by the customers on the website / mobile application for photo shooting. Once the request is received, MOL will check the ~availability~ of photographers to match with the demand / specification made by the customers. Once the matching is done, ~notice~ will be sent to inform the customers to arrange ~payment~ to confirm their ~bookings~. Once the booking is confirmed and payment is made, the photo shooting session is ~non-cancellable~ and the payment is non-refundable unless the photo shooting session does not take place (e.g. bad weather) and MOL will negotiate with the customers for ~rescheduling or refund.~ 
MOL is considering to **expand** the platform business to mainland China, Taiwan, Singapore and other Asian countries; MOL will conduct a more detailed analysis about different locations and ways to establish its business. 
In order to test the market response, MOL planned to enter the market in ~Singapore~ first. Under the contemplated expansion plan, business operations will be highly ~automated~. As such, MOL will not need to establish an office or send its employees to Singapore. It will only rent a ~webserver~ located in Singapore which will host a new website. Through this website, customers can obtain details of photographers, their service offerings and availabilities in the next three months; place request for photography services; lodge enquiries; and make payments. MOL will charge the photographers a commission for each request fulfilled. To ensure the quality of services, MOL will only allow ~verified photographers~ to be listed in its website. The verification of photographers, the answering of customers’ enquiries, as well as the day-to-day maintenance of the webserver and website, will be ~conducted by the staff of MOL in Hong Kong~. 
However, Ms Gabriella Fung is concerned about the ~fluctuations in the exchange rate~ between the Hong Kong Dollar and Singapore Dollar; therefore, Gabriella would like to explore the need to manage the exchange rate risk. 

### Loan to PHKL
On 1 January 2019, an unsecured interest-free loan has been made by the Company to PHKL **amounting to HK$5 million which will be payable in full** at the end of the three-year period (i.e. 31 December 2021). The Company intends to hold the loan until maturity, and the market interest rate for a loan with similar terms would be 8% on 1 January 2019.

### Auditors of the Group

KK Cheung & Co Ltd is newly appointed as the auditor of the Group in the current year and Mr Fabio Leung is the audit manager for the annual audit of the Group. Upon receiving the draft financial statements and consolidation schedules from the Management, Fabio noted that MPHZ (whose operation is based in Hangzhou, China) accounts for 35% and 40% of the revenue and net profits of the Group, respectively, in the current year. While in the prior year, MPHZ accounted for 10% and 5% of the revenue and net profits of the Group, respectively. The Management explained that the predecessor auditor did not perform any audit work or contact the local auditor of MPHZ last year for the Group audit purposes, and expected the same from Fabio this year. 
Prior to the year end, the audit team obtained a list of bank balances and relationship from the Management of the Group, which included seven banks located in Hong Kong, five in mainland China and two in Taiwan. Confirmations were arranged with all the banks but closer to the audit report date, the audit team still have not received the confirmation reply from the two banks in Taiwan, where the Group has kept bank balances of TWD1,000,000 and was granted banking facility of TWD12,000,000 (unutilised). The Management alternatively provided you with the year end bank statements downloaded from the online banking system as evidence to support the bank balances. 
Fabio also noted that the Group has a material receivable balance from Cherish Photograph Limited (“CPL”) as at 31 December 2018. CPL is a new corporate customer that entered into an agreement with MPHK in July 2018, and per the agreement, on a monthly basis commencing August 2018, CPL would refer customers to MPHK for photography service. For each referral, CPL would collect the photography fee from customers and after retaining 10% as agency fee, transfer the remaining 90% of the photography fee to MPHK upon completion of photography service. CPL has not settled the balance with MPHK for the photography service provided by MPHK from September 2018 to December 2018. During the audit, when Fabio discussed with the Management in respect of the receivable balance with CPL, the Management has requested Fabio not to arrange for confirmation with CPL, but to confirm the balance with the Management as at 31 December 2018.
