# 20200410 hiking from Tsing Shan to Ha Pa Lai
It was an adventurous day. We woke up late this morning. Originally intended to finish the last two sections of Wilson trail, but changed to go to Ching Shan. 

We took 42A bus and then transferred to 66X. Took around 1 hour travel. After arriving Tuen Mun, we entered the park nearby to go to toilet. Then started the hiking. We followed the same trail last time we hike. From Through the monastery to the peak with the signal station. Then turn right to the sandy area. The first section need power to climb. The second section need agility and skill to conquer. These 2 parts were fun. 

Then I thought the last section to Ha Pa Lai would be boring. But we found a path along the 青大石澗 can go to the same destination. 

It was a fun journey. It was the first time I along a river. 

It took awhile to arrive the destination. And timing was good, it was about to sunset. We wait a short while and see the sunset. 

Then as we saw a long queue to wait for minibus to the urban area, we decided to walk back. 

It took shorter time, as we walk the formal path this time. 

Around 8:00 when we arrived Tuen Mun. We ate there and rush back,... to watch TV.

To prevent slippery, I can lower my center of mass. I sprang my ankle a few weeks ago, haven’t recovered yet. So, at the beginning, I was very careful to enter the Tsing Shan inner area. Because that region is sandy. 

However, when I lower my Centre of gravity, I felt easier to move forward.
