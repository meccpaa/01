# Testing 100% of items in a population - sampling
In some circumstances, auditors may decide that it will be appropriate to test the entire population of items. Auditors are unlikely to test 100% of items when carrying out tests of controls, but 100 % testing may be appropriate for certain substantive procedures. 100% examination may be appropriate in the following circumstances:

(i) If the population is made up of a small number of high value items;

(ii) There is a significant high risk of material misstatement and other means do not provide sufficient appropriate audit evidence, then 100% examination may be appropriate; or

(iii) The repetitive nature of a calculation or other process performed automatically by an information system makes a 100% examination more effective.
