# stamp duty Example 1
A owes B $5 million and is in financial difficulties. The parties agree that A will assign to B his immovable property with a value of $4.5 million as full settlement of the debt.

Stamp duty payable on the conveyance will be based on $5 million. However, the duty-payer may apply to the Collector to have the stamp duty payable based on the value of the property of $4.5 million.
