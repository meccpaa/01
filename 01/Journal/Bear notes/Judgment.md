# Judgment
Judgment means the board making decisions that enhance the prosperity of the organisation. This means that board members must acquire a broad enough knowledge of the business and its environment to be able to provide meaningful direction to it. This has implications not only for the attention directors have to give to the organisation's affairs, but also the way the directors are recruited and trained.

The complexities of senior management mean that the directors have to bring multiple conceptual skills to management that aim to maximise long-term returns. This means that corporate governance can involve balancing many competing people and resource claims against each other; although, as we shall see, risk management is an integral part of corporate governance, corporate governance is not just about risk management.
