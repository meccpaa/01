# HKAS 38 Scope - exceptions
- Financial assets (HKAS 32) 
- The recognition and measurement of exploration and evaluation assets (HKFRS 6)
- The development and extraction of minerals, oils, gas and other non-regenerative resources (HKFRS 6)
- Intangible assets held for sale in the ordinary course of business (HKAS 2)
- Deferred tax assets (HKAS 12) x Leases within the scope of HKFRS 16
- Assets arising from employee benefits (HKAS 19) 
- Goodwill acquired in a business combination (HKFRS 3 (revised))
- The recognition and measurement of insurance contracts (HKFRS 4)
- Intangible assets within the scope of HKFRS 5 Non-current Assets Held for Sale and Discontinued Operations
