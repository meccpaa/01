# Profits tax - example 49 secured loan
The $1 million loan in Example 47 was secured by a deposit of $500,000 that generated tax-free interest of $20,000 and some shares which were worth $500,000.

The whole of the deposit is used to secure the loan. Allowable interest is reduced by the tax-free interest of $20,000 on the deposit.

Allowable interest = $50,000 – $20,000 = $30,000
