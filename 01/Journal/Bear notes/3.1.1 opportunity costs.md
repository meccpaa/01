# 3.1.1 opportunity costs
These are the costs incurred or revenues lost from **diverting existing resources from their best use**.
- - - -
### Illustration: Opportunity costs

If a salesman, who is paid a fixed annual salary of $300,000, is **diverted** to work on a new project and as a ~result existing sales of $500,000 are lost~, the opportunity cost to the new project will be the loss of contribution on the $500,000 of lost sales. The salesman's salary of **$300,000** is not an opportunity cost since it ~will be incurred however the salesman's time is spent~.
