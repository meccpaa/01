# contract asset
Entity's right to consideration in exchange for goods or services that it has transferred to a customer when that right is conditional on something other than the passage of time (for example the entity's performance), i.e. if an entity transfers goods or services to a customer before the customer pays consideration, the entity shall present the contract as a 'contract asset', unless it is presented as a receivable. A contract asset is assessed for impairment in line with HKFRS 9 guidance.
