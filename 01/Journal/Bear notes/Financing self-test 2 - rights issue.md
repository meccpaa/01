# Financing self-test 2 - rights issue
CDE Bank is a listed company and needs to increase its capital by $230 million. CDE Bank’s current share price is $18.75. It has decided to carry out a rights issue and has appointed advisors to handle the issue who have estimated the costs of the issue at $10 million. Due to a volatile equity market CDE has been advised that the shares should be offered at a 20% discount to current market price. There are currently 48 million shares in issue.

Required

(i) Determine the terms of the rights issue that would be necessary for CDE to raise the required funds.

(ii) Brian Lam is a small shareholder with 300 shares, calculate the value of the rights if he were to sell them rather than subscribe for new shares.
