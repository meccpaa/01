# The new Companies Ordinance - auditor’s report
### 1.1.1 Auditor's duty to report

Under section 405 of new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), a company's auditor **must prepare a report for the members** on any financial statements prepared by the directors, a copy of which is laid before the company in general meeting or is sent to a member before general meeting or otherwise circulated, published or issued by the company, during the auditor's term of office.
