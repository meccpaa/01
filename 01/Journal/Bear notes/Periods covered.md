# Periods covered
As required by the standard, financial information for the following periods or as at the following dates should be provided in interim financial reports:

(a) Statement of financial position data ~as at the end of the current interim period~, and **comparative** data as at the end of the most recent financial year.

(b) Statement of profit or loss and other comprehensive income data for the current interim period and cumulative data for the current year to date, together with comparative data for the corresponding interim period and cumulative figures for the previous financial year.

(c) Statement of changes in equity data should be for both the current interim period and for the year to date, together with comparative data for the corresponding interim period, and cumulative figures, for the previous financial year.

(d) Statement of cash flows data should be cumulative for the current year to date, with comparative cumulative data for the corresponding interim period in the previous financial year.

In March 2017 the IASB issued an exposure draft of proposed amendments to IFRS 8 (HKFRS 8). An amendment to IAS 34 (HKAS 34) was also proposed, which would require that, in the first interim report following a change in composition of reportable segments, all interim periods of the current and prior financial years were restated and presented.

For example, an entity reports quarterly and has a year end of 30 -une. On 1 October 20;7 the entity changes its reportable segments. The first interim report after the change is for the quarter ending 31 December 20;7. In that interim report, the entity would include restated segment information for each of the quarters in the year ended 30 -une 20;7 and the first quarter of the current year (i.e. the quarter ended 30 September 20;7).
