# Caishui [2011] No. 110 and Caishui [2011] No. 111
The PRC Ministry of Finance and the SAT jointly issued tax notices, Caishui [2011] No. 110 and Caishui [2011] No. 111 on 16 November 2011 on VAT reform programme. Under the reform program, BT will be replaced by VAT in certain circumstances.

Caishui [2011] No. 110 outlines the general principles for the VAT reform programme, which has been implemented in the Mainland since the year 2012. It stipulated that the VAT reform programme is to be carried out in two phases. The first phase will be applicable to specific sectors in Shanghai. The second phase is to be rolled out as programmes to other regions for specific sectors.

Caishui [2011] No. 111 sets out the specific programme to be implemented in Shanghai since 1 January 2012. Under the programme, service providers will be classified as 'general tax payers' and 'small-scale tax payers'. A taxpayer with annual taxable service turnover of at least RMB5 million should apply for the status of a general tax payer.

Based on the above two tax notices:

The applicable VAT rates for the general tax payers (service providers) will be as follows:

Leasing of movable property and tangible goods - 17%
Provision of transportation and construction services - 11%
Provision of other specific 'modern services' - 6%

'Modern services' are defined in Caishui [2011] No. 111 to include research and development and technology services, information technology services, design services, intellectual property services, advertising services, meeting and exhibition services and certification services.

The VAT rate for small-scale VAT payers (service providers) will be 3%.

Taxpayers covered by the VAT reform programme should pay VAT at the location where the enterprise is located. BT paid in other regions can be deducted when calculating the VAT payable.
