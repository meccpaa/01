# revenue - audit procedures
For verification of revenue, auditors have to be aware of bill and hold arrangements and consignment arrangements.
- - - -
Revenue is a material figure in the financial statements where the auditor devotes special attention. In most cases, an auditor would perform [[Analytical procedures]] review at the beginning in order to predict the relationship of revenue to other figures in the financial statements.

The auditor wants to ensure revenue is completely and accurately recorded.

For financial statement assertions that are relevant to revenue, the auditor would concentrate on: **completeness**, **accuracy** and **cut-off**.
