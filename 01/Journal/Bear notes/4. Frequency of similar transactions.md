# 4. Frequency of similar transactions
If there are a number of similar transactions, it is likely that a trade is being carried on.

In Martin v Lowry [(1927) AC 312], a wholesale machinery merchant purchased a large quantity of Government surplus aircraft linen and disposed of the linen in a number of lots. It was held that a trade was carried on with regard to the methods adopted for the resale of the linen, the number of operations entered into and the time occupied by the resale.
