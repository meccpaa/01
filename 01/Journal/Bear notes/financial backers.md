# financial backers
**Financial backers** of the buy-out team. The main financial backers will usually want an equity stake in the bought-out business, because of the **venture capital** risk they are taking. Often, several financial backers provide the venture capital for a single buy-out. The main financial backers, for example, may persuade a bank to provide venture capital funding in the form of loans.
