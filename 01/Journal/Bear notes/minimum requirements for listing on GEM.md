# minimum requirements for listing on GEM
- Type of company: Companies who do not meet the requirements for a Main board listing but meet the requirements for a GEM listing.
- Minimum trading record: At least two financial years.
- Financial requirement: Must pass a financial test 
	- No profit requirement 
	- Market capitalisation of at least HK$100 million at the time of listing.
	- Positive cash flow from operating activities of at least HK$20 million in aggregate for the two preceding financial years.

- Minimum continuity of management: At least the two preceding financial years.
- Minimum continuity of ownership: For at least the preceding full financial year.
- Minimum market capitalisation for shares: At least HK$100 million at the time of listing. At least 25% (subject to a minimum of HK$30 million) to be held by the public.
- Underwriting requirement: No underwriting requirement.
- Method of offering shares for a new listed company: Placing
- Minimum number of shareholders at time of listing who are regarded as the 'public’: 100
