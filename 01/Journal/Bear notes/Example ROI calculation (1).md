# Example: ROI calculation (1)
A new company has non-current assets of $460,000 which will be depreciated to nil on a straight line basis over 10 years. Net current assets will consistently be $75,000, and annual profit will consistently be $30,000. ROI is measured as return on average net assets each year. Calculate the company's ROI in years 2 and 6.

Solution

Year 2 – Net assets = 460,000 – (46,000 + 46,000 x 6/12) + 75,000 = 466,000 
ROI 30,000/466,000 = 6.4% 

Year 6 – Net assets = 460,000 – (46,000 x 5) – (46,000 x 6/12) + 75,000 = 282,000 
ROI 30,000/282,000 = 10.6%
