# Incidental to the construction or development 
The standard also states that income and related expenses of operations that are incidental to the construction or development of an item of property, plant and equipment should be recognised in profit or loss. These include:

* Costs of opening a new facility 
* Costs of introducing a new product or service 
* Costs of conducting business in a new location or with a new class of customer 
* Administration and other general overhead costs
