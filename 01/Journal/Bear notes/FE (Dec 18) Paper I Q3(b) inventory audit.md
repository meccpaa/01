# FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Q3(b) inventory audit
(b) Based on the information provided in the case, advise with regard to (i) the risk assessment procedures and (ii) further audit procedures at the assertion level for the audit of the Company’s inventory. In terms of assertion, you only have to focus on existence, completeness and accuracy.

(13 marks)
- - - -
[[FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Case]]
### In-house Developed Inventory System
Since the products are sold through **over 150 selling points** including supermarkets, pharmacy stores and distributors, the Group uses its in-house developed integrated systems to ~manage the movement of inventory~ and to ~calculate the weighted-average costing of inventory.~ It is the Group’s policy that an **inventory count** will be arranged in different selling points on a ~rotation basis~ throughout the year. The Group will also perform a year-end inventory count at the Group’s warehouse in the last few days of the year.

The in-house developed integrated systems were upgraded during the year to rectify the ::deficiencies:: identified in the last year’s audit in relation to the inaccurate calculation of weighted-average costing. Before the systems were upgraded, remedial actions (manual checking and corrections) were taken by the management to address the deficiencies identified. The management represented that the data in the original systems were checked and corrected if necessary, and they were all transferred to the upgraded systems.

### (i) Risk assessment procedures
- Based on the ~knowledge built up~ from previous audits, **discuss** with the management during the planning stage to obtain updates for the current year in particular to obtain an **understanding** of whether there are ~any changes in risks~ in relation to ~inventory~ as well as the ~respective controls~ used to mitigate the risks.

- Obtain the latest financial information to perform **risk assessment analytics** during the planning stage to identify the risk of material misstatement related to ~inventory~.

- ~Assess the risk arising from~ the **deficiencies identified** in the in-house developed systems in relation to the ~inaccurate calculation~ of weighted-average costing.

### (ii) Further audit procedures at the assertion level
**Assertion level – existence**
- Perpetual inventory count
	- ~Evaluate~ the ~effectiveness of controls~ for the perpetual inventory count.
	- ~Test the controls~ for the perpetual inventory count executed throughout the year.
	- ~Observe the perpetual inventory count~ at all / a sample of selling points during the year.
- ~Observe the inventory count~ at the Company’s **warehouse** at the year-end.
- For the inventory counts observed, select ~items from the inventory listing~ generated from the in-house developed integrated systems and perform test counts.
- Evaluate the ~effectiveness of controls~ over the record of inventory movement between the year-end inventory count and the year-end.
- Perform **roll-forward testing** on intervening transactions to ensure the movement of inventory is properly recorded.
- Arrange **confirmations** to confirm the inventory items kept in the supermarkets, pharmacy stores and distributors.

### Assertion level – completeness
- For the inventory counts observed, select items from the selling points / warehouse, perform test counts and **trace to the inventory listing** generated from the in-house developed integrated systems.
- Evaluate the effectiveness of **controls** over the record of ~inventory movement~ between the year-end inventory count and the year-end.
- Perform **roll-forward testing** on intervening transactions to ensure movement of inventory is properly recorded.

### Assertion level – accuracy
- Vouch **a sample of inventory** items against supplier’s invoices to ensure the purchase price is ~correctly inputted~ into the systems.
- Select a sample of inventory items and ~calculate the weighted-average costing~.

### Assertion level – accuracy (in relation to in-house developed integrated systems)
- Assess whether the **remedial actions** taken by the management have properly addressed the deficiencies ~before the system was upgraded~.
	- Assess whether the system upgrade has been ~properly tested~ by the management.
	- Assess whether the system upgrade has been ~properly approved~ by the management.
	- Assess whether the upgrade of the systems has ~properly rectified the deficiencies.~
	- Assess whether data and information have been ~properly maintained / transferred~ before and after the systems were upgraded.
- **Test** the information technology ~general and automatic controls~ of the system separately ~before and after~ the systems were upgraded.
