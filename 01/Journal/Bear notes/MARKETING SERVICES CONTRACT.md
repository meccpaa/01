# MARKETING SERVICES CONTRACT
 
 #work/Vlogger/Co/VF
#work/fin/Audit/Vlogger
DATE: 22nd January 2018
 
 
BETWEEN:
 
SuperV Limited whose registered office is at Room 3, 6/F, Central Industrial Building, 57-61 Ta Chuen Ping Street, Kwai Chung, Hong Kong and Company number is 2529443, (hereinafter referred to as the " Company”),
 
AND:
 
Vformula Limited whose registered office is at 7th Floor, Yau Lee Centre, No. 45 Hoi Yuen Road, Kwun Tong, Hong Kong and company number is 2644756, (hereinafter referred to as the “Service Provider”).
 
 
WHEREAS:
 
I.             The Company would like to engage the Service Provider and the Service Provider will provide marketing services for the Company, (hereinafter referred to as the "Services”) and that the Service Provider has the sufficient and necessary knowledge, experience, personnel and equipment as to comply with the obligations contained herein.
 
II.            Both Parties undertake to observe the following clauses:
 
 
1.            SCOPE OF SERVICES
 
The Service Provider is obliged to promote the marketing of the products, in particular, to advertise the products by way of utilising multiple channels as listed below:
 
1.1              KOL engagement strategy
The Service Provider shall understand the brands, industry trend and target customers of the Company in order to develop a unique Key Opinion Leaders (the “KOLs”) engagement strategy of the Company. The Service Provider shall identify the Right KOLs and ensure there is a good fit between the Company and the relevant KOLs.
 
1.2              Exhibition participation with KOLs promotion
The Service Provider shall  act as the coordinator and in charge for at least three exhibitions or trade shows in Hong Kong. The Service Provider shall assist in obtaining booth area, design and production of the booth for the Company to sell the products. The exhibition booth shall be promoted by KOLs by way of social media and attendance at relevant exhibition in person.
 
1.3              Recommendation by KOLs for potential hero products of the Company
The Right KOLs will be engaged to promote the potential hero products of the Company by posting news feed (in form of video or article) on their social media platforms.
 
1.4              Content of advertisement
The Service Provider shall be responsible to liaise with the KOLs for the content of promotion, produce videos and copy write articles for the potential hero products of the Company.
 
1.5              Advertorial on media
To enhance the effect of engaging KOLs, the Service Provider shall further post advertorial on other media to increase the appearance of the potential hero products of the Company.
 
1.6              Consultancy on marketing strategies on social media
The Service Provider shall provide the Company with recommendations on updating or creating a comprehensive social media marketing strategy.
 
1.7              Product sourcing and merchandising
The Service Provider shall involve in the process of the Company attains a product to sell and all promotional activities used to sell the products of the Company.
 
1.8              Recommendation on pricing strategy
To coordinate with the promotional activities, the Service Provider shall further provide recommendations on the pricing strategy of particular products to be promoted.
 
 
2.            CHARACTERISTICS OF THE PRODUCTS
 
2.1          The Products that shall be subject to the Services will be electronic products and related accessories (the “Products”).
 
 
3.            PROPERTY OF THE PRODUCTS
 
3.1          The Company will have the full property of the Products, such property will not be assigned in any circumstance or any way whatsoever to the Service Provider.
 
3.2          The Service Provider will not assert any ownership rights on the Products and therefore shall not lease, transfer or by any other title dispose of them.
 
 
4.            PERFORMANCE OF THE SERVICES
 
The Services to be performed by the Service Provider must comply with the following:
 
4.1          The personnel used by the Service Provider for the execution of the Services must be experienced and skilled, as well as capable and duly qualified in connection to the nature of the Services to be performed accordingly to the principles generally accepted in the industry.
 
4.2          The Service Provider will not perform any services on the order acceptance, transportation, distribution and storage of the Products as well as collection of receivables from the customers.
 
 
5.            OBLIGATIONS OF THE SERVICE PROVIDER
 
The Service Provider must comply with the following obligations:
 
5.1          Perform actions under the law and under the Contract in good faith.
 
5.2          Observe the legal requirements in effect related to the purpose of this Contract.
 
5.3          Utilise promotional materials provided to the Service Provider by the Company for the purpose of marketing the Products.
 
5.4          Comply with the Company’s instructions related to marketing and promotion on the Products.
 
5.5          Inform the Company, as soon as possible, any circumstances changes reasonably relevant to the Company’s marketing strategies.
 
 
6.            RESPONSIBILITIES OF THE SERVICE PROVIDER
 
The Service Provider hereby expressly accepts and acknowledges that the responsibility of the Service Provider derived from the breach of its obligations hereunder for any cause, reason or circumstance, shall be subject to the following:
 
6.1          The Service Provider shall not be liable for the breach of its obligations resulting from causes not directly attributable to the Service Provider including Force Majeure, or any breach of the Company.
 
6.2          The Service Provider will only be liable for damages to the Products directly caused by the Service Provider or its employees due to its fault, deceit or negligence.
 
 
7.            OBLIGATIONS OF THE COMPANY
 
The Company must comply with the following obligations:
 
7.1          Pay the Service Provider the Fees set forth in Article 8 of this Contract.
 
7.2         The Company is fully responsible for any credit loss in case of the customers failing to settle its outstanding amount.
 
7.3          Provide to the Service Provider all commercial and technical data reasonably required by the Service Provider to conduct activity on the promotion, data analysis and advertising suggestion.
 
 

 
8.            FEES
 
The amount that the Service Provider shall receive from the Company for the provision of the Services shall be in accordance with the following:
 
8.1          For the performance of the Services, the Company agrees to pay the Fees at 26% of the sales amount of the Products. Nevertheless, the Parties agree that such Fees will be revised in the case of the existence of facts, circumstances, or causes not foreseen by the Parties.
 
8.2          The Services provided by the Service Provider to the Company will be invoiced monthly and the Company must pay the amount due within 30 (thirty) calendar day period from the date the respective invoice was issued.
                
 
9.            TERM OF THE CONTRACT
 
9.1          Parties agree this Contract shall be in force from the date of its execution by both parties for a 1 (one) year.
 
9.2          The Contract shall be renewed automatically for periods of 1 (one) year at a time, unless either Party should wish to terminate it by written notification to the other Party at least 30 (thirty) calendar days in advance.
 
 
10.          TERMINATION OF THE CONTRACT
 
This Contract may be terminated in advance before the end of the first year, for any of the following causes:
 
10.1       By agreement between both Parties.
 
10.2       In the event that due to Force Majeure it is impossible for the Parties to comply with the purpose of this Contract.
 
 
11.          BREACH OF THE CONTRACT
 
11.1       Any breach of any of the obligations derived from this Contract by any Party shall entitle the other Party to notify in writing the applicable breach to the defaulting Party in order to remedy or correct such breach within a term not to exceed 5 (five) business days from the date of notification thereof.
 
11.2       In the event that the defaulting Party should not have not remedied the respective breach of the term provided above, the other Party shall be entitled, in its discretion, to demand the mandatory performance of this Contract or to rescind it by full right, upon written notice with immediate effect, and in any case, to claim the payment of damages and losses accrued by the breach of the applicable Party.
 
 

 
12.          NON EXCLUSIVENESS
 
The Parties agree and acknowledge that this Contract does not imply exclusiveness for any Party. Therefore, the Company may hire with any third party the Services, and the Service Provider can provide the Services to any other third party with absolute freedom, in connection with all types of products, merchandise, whether they are similar or related in any way whatsoever to the Services or the Products, agreed herein, including without limitation the merchandise or goods of the Company’s competitors.
 
 
13.          ASSIGNMENT OF RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS
 
None of the Parties may negotiate, assign or transfer in any manner the rights and obligations acquired under this Contract, without prior written consent of the other Party. Any act, agreement or assignment which might contravene the provisions of this Clause shall have no legal effect.
 
 
14           APPLICABLE LAW AND COMPETENT JURISDICTION
 
14.1       The Parties shall exercise their best efforts to resolve by negotiation any dispute, controversy o difference between them arising out or relating to this Contract.
 
14.2       This Contract and all acts and transactions pursuant hereto shall be governed, construed and interpreted in accordance with the laws of Hong Kong.
 
 
By signing the present Contract in duplicate, both Parties express their complete conformity thereto.
 
This Contract enters into force the date written above.
