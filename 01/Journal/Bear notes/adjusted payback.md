# adjusted payback
The payback method of investment appraisal recognises uncertainty in investment decisions by focusing on the near future. Short-term projects are preferred to long-term projects and liquidity is emphasised.

Adjusted payback **uses discounted cash flows**.

One way of dealing with risk is to **shorten the payback period** required. A maximum payback period can be set to reflect the fact that risk increases the longer the time period under consideration. However, the disadvantages of payback as an investment appraisal method mean that adjusted payback cannot be recommended as a method of adjusting for risk.
