# Unbundling
Unbundling is a [[portfolio restructuring]] strategy which involves the **disposal and sale** of assets, facilities, production lines, subsidiaries, divisions or product units.
- - - -
Unbundling can be either **voluntary** or it can be ::forced:: on a company. A company may voluntarily decide to divest part of its business for strategic, financial or organisational reasons. An involuntary unbundling on the other hand may take place for regulatory or financial reasons. The main forms of unbundling are:

- [[2.1 Divestments]]
- [[2.2 Demergers]] 
- [[2.3 Sell-offs]]
- [[2.4 Spin-offs]] 
- [[2.5 Carve-outs]] 
- [[3. Management buy-out (MBO)]]
