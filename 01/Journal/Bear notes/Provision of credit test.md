# Provision of credit test
In the case of **passive interest income** from the lending of money, the source is 'where is the place where the credit is made available to the borrower?' ([[CIR v Lever Brothers & Unilever Ltd (14 SATC 1)]]). The ::operations test:: may not be appropriate as the taxpayer does not need to perform any activities.

However, in the case of a **money lending business**, the taxpayer's business would normally encompass a ~broader range of activity~, including the borrowing and/or lending of money. For this type of business, the IRD will apply the **operations test** instead of the ::provision of credit test:: in determining the source of the interest income (see DIPN 13).
