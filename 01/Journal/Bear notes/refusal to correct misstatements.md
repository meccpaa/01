# refusal to correct misstatements
`HKSA 450.9`
The auditor shall request management to correct those misstatements accumulated during the audit. If management refuses to correct some or all of the misstatements communicated by the auditor, the auditor shall **obtain an understanding** of management's reasons for not making the corrections and shall take that understanding ~into account~ when evaluating whether the financial statements ~as a whole are free from material misstatement~.
