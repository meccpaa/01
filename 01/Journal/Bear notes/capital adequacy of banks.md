# capital adequacy of banks
Capital adequacy is a regulatory issue that deals with the **level of equity that a bank must have** relative to its loan and deposit base. Banks with capital adequacy problems may be ~restricted by regulatory agencies~ in the type and amounts of loans they can grant. In addition, most of the bank regulatory agencies have mandated broad increases in the quality of loans in financial institutions' portfolios, further restricting bank lending.
