# Employee share-based benefits
![](Employee%20share-based%20benefits/086029A9-D655-42EA-9AFA-BD3AA65D90D7.png)
S.9(1)(d): Any **gain realised by the exercise of**, or by the assignment or release of, a right to acquire shares or stock in a corporation obtained by a person as the holder of an office or an employee of that or any other corporation.
- - - -
## Stock option gains
![](Employee%20share-based%20benefits/50166C16-394D-48E5-B2A8-A1E6392BC54D.png)
Any gain realised by the exercise of, or by the assignment or release of, a right to acquire shares or stock in a corporation obtained by a person as the holder of an office in or an employee of that or any other corporation is chargeable to salaries tax. The salaries tax liability arises ~when the stock option is exercised, assigned or released~, **notwithstanding that no actual gain is made** at that time or a loss may be suffered when the shares are being disposed of later.

### Formula to learn
The **notional gain** chargeable to salaries tax is computed as follows:

1. **Market value** of the shares at the ~date of the exercise of the option~ 
~LESS~ **Consideration paid by the employee** for the shares and the share option Or
2. **Consideration received** on assignment or release of the right
~LESS~ **Consideration given** for the grant of the right
- - - -
Share options have become very popular in recent years. The IRD issued DIPN No. 38 (Revised), which provides helpful guidance on employee share option benefits. The following are some of the main points:

- **Source of gain:** The **date of grant** governs the source of gain to be taxed. Other factors include the ~period of services~ in Hong Kong and, the ~vesting period~ of the option, etc. (Note. The IRD considers that **apportionment** of gain from share options is ~only applicable to non-HK employment~. In cases where services are partly performed in Hong Kong and partly outside Hong Kong and the employment is a Hong Kong employment, the IRD will, in general, not take into account the period of services performed outside Hong Kong in determining the taxable gain for salaries tax purpose. However, one may argue that the period of services and the vesting period are also important in determining the amount of taxable gain under Hong Kong employment and apportion the gain as indicated in the example below); 
- **Timing**: The **date of exercise**, assignment or release of a right to option determine the timing of the taxability;
	- Exercise of share option gain after cessation of employment or after leaving Hong Kong will not extinguish the tax liability. Furthermore, the gain should be ~assessed in the year of exercise~ and not be treated as a gain in the last day of employment;
- There will be no salaries tax on the ::grant of stock options::;
- **Gains** from **unconditional** stock options granted ::before providing Hong Kong services:: are not taxable even when the options are exercised when rendering services in Hong Kong;
- Gains from **conditional** stock options granted for non-Hong Kong employment will be ~prorated if partially sourced in Hong Kong~;
- Notional expenses (e.g. brokerage, stamp duty) can be claimed as a deduction from the notional gain.

## Shared award benefits
![](Employee%20share-based%20benefits/115DC048-E337-417F-BF99-D008368CF36C.png)
The IRD issued DIPN No. 38 (Revised) in March 2008 to include the salaries tax treatments on share award benefits. Essentially, Part 1 of DIPN No.38 (Revised) covers the share option benefits. Part 2 of DIPN No.38 (Revised) classifies share award benefits in two categories namely: 'Upfront' and 'Back End'.

It has been accepted that share award benefits are taxable as perquisites received by an employee during his/her employment. The essential element is to determine the timing of receipt of such perquisite, (i.e. the timing when the employee would be fully entitled to the ownership of the share award benefits) and the value attached to the perquisite. The characteristics of the two categories can be summarized as follows:
![](Employee%20share-based%20benefits/2376C0CA-BCF8-436E-A455-9AC2FA589CB0.png)
Similar to the share option gain, share award benefits can be determined by the locality of employment, i.e. Hong Kong vs used earlier non-Hong Kong employment. In addition, taxpayers leaving Hong Kong permanently can make a deemed vesting election relating to the 'Back End' approach share award benefits.

## Share option rights - specifically exclusion
 ::excl. s.9(5)::
The **notional gain** on exercise or release of the share option rights is chargeable to salaries tax pursuant to s.9(1)(d) (Please refer to section 3.5.1), while the ::grant of a right to acquire shares:: is not subject to salaries tax. Please refer to explanations in DIPN No. 38 (Revised).
