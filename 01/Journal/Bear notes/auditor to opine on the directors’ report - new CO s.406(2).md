# auditor to opine on the directors’ report - new CO s.406(2)
Section 406(2) of the new Companies Ordinance (CO) (Cap. 622) requires the auditor to opine on the **directors' report**:

- If a company's auditor is of the opinion that the information in a directors' report for a financial year is ::not consistent:: with the financial statements for the financial year, the auditor-
	- (a) Must state that opinion in the auditor's report
	- (b) May bring that opinion to the members' attention at a general meeting

The auditor who is considering the course of including a paragraph after the audit opinion in the auditor's report describing the **material inconsistency** between the financial statements and the other information or the material misstatement of fact in the other information (with the exception of reporting inconsistencies with the director's report) is advised that the ::qualified privilege:: (ie the defence to an action for defamation) which an auditor's report normally enjoys ~may not extend to comments on items of the other information~ which appear to be misleading. Similarly, ::no qualified privilege:: may ~attach to statements made by the auditor on such matters at a general meeting~ pursuant to his right under section 411 of the new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622).

The auditor would also bear in mind the provisions of sections 417 and 424 of the new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) before **deciding whether resignation** from the engagement would be likely to resolve the problem. The auditor would consider ~seeking legal advice~ both as to which course of action he would adopt and as to the precise form of any wording to be used.

When management ::refuses to revise:: the other information, the auditor's possible further course of action may involve using his right under section 411 of the new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) to **be heard at any general meeting** of the members on any business of the meeting which concerns him as auditor.
