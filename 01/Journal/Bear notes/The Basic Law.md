# The Basic Law
The Basic Law states the tax relationship between Hong Kong and the mainland of China.

It is worth noting that none of the tax treaties entered into by the Mainland Government with the other countries is applicable to Hong Kong since Hong Kong and China maintain two separate tax systems. DIPN 29 summarises the principle 'one country, two systems' and outlines the tax relations between Hong Kong and the Mainland. Under Article 151 of the Basic Law, the HKSAR has concluded CDTAs with 37 jurisdictions under 'Hong Kong, China' as of 1 June 2017.
