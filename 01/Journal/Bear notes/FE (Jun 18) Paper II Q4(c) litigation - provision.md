# FE (Jun 18) Paper II: Q4(c) litigation - provision
Assuming you are the audit manager of Team B. Analyse the **audit implications** in relation to the litigations of WT Tours with reference to relevant ethical requirements.
- - - -
## solutions
Responding to non-compliance with laws and regulations (“NOCLAR”)

- New HKICPA **Code of Ethics** for Professional Accountants.
- ~HKSA 250~ Consideration of Laws and Regulations in an Audit of Financial Statements (effective from 15 July 2017).
- ~Group communications~: **Team B** has to consider reporting to **Team A** on the NOCLAR.
- Determination of whether **further action** is needed in the public interest: ::no disclosure:: was made in the notes to the financial statement, Team B has to ~discuss the matter with management and / or those charged with governance~, **consider their response** and determine whether **further action** is needed in the public interest.
- **Documentation** as a result of the public interest consideration: Team B has to ~document the consideration~ of whether further action is needed in the public interest.
