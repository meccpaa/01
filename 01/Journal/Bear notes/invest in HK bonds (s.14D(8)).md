# invest in HK bonds (s.14D(8))
### excluded from qualifying profits - fund invest to bond issued in HK as issuer can claim tax deduction
Where any sum payable to the CTC in respect of any of the above [[corporate treasury activities]] can be claimed by any person as a ~tax deductible expense~ in Hong Kong, such sum will be ::excluded:: from the qualifying profits (s.14D(8)). Therefore, when a QCTC obtains funds from a [[non-Hong Kong associated corporation]] and ~invests the funds in bonds~ issued by a corporation ~carrying on business in Hong Kong~, the interest income would ::not qualify:: for the concessionary tax rate. This is because the **bond issuer would claim a tax deduction** in Hong Kong in respect of the interest payment.
