# Auditor's opinion on financial statements, directors' report, etc.
Under `section 405` of new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622), an auditor's report must state, in the auditor's opinion:

(a) Whether the financial statements have been properly prepared in compliance with the new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622).

(b) In particular, whether the financial statements:
	 - (i) In the case of annual financial statements of a company that does not fall within the reporting exemption for the financial year, give a **true and fair view** of the financial position and financial performance of the company as required by `section 380` of new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622).
	 - (ii) In the case of annual consolidated financial statements of a company that does not fall within the reporting exemption for the financial year, give a true and fair view of the financial position and financial performance of the company and all the subsidiary undertakings as required by `section 380` of new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622).

(c) If a company's auditor is of the opinion that the information in a **directors' report** for a financial year is **not consistent** with the financial statements for the financial year, the auditor ~must state that opinion~ in the auditor's report and may bring that opinion to the members' attention at a general meeting.

(d) If the financial statements do not comply with `section 383` of new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622) (ie **disclosure of directors' information** in the notes to financial statements), the auditor must include in the auditor's report, so far as the auditor is reasonably able to do so, a statement giving the particulars that are required to be, but have not been, contained in the financial statements.
