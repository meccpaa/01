# Exam Module C: Dec 18 - Q5 corporate governance, audit committee
Chemical Green Limited (“Chemical Green”) is a **family** business specialising in chemical trading and distribution. John Wong has been the **Chairman** and the **Chief Executive Officer** of Chemical Green for decades since Chemical Green was set up twenty years ago. Joe Wong, **brother** of John, is the Chief Operation Officer (“COO”) of Chemical Green. Elizabeth Wong, **daughter** of John, is the Chief Financial Officer of Chemical Green. Both Joe and Elizabeth report to John. The board of directors of Chemical Green consists of **three executive directors** who are John, Joe and Elizabeth. John determines the remuneration of Joe, Elizabeth and himself.

Chemical Green has plans to **list** on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange in the coming one or two years. For preparation of the listing, an **internal audit team** was set up six months ago and reports to the COO. In a recent ::surprise count:: carried out by the internal audit team, inventories of HK$5 million, representing 15% of the total inventories, were found lost. The COO has been directing the investigation relating to the inventory loss.

Required:
(a) Advise five improvement recommendations to Chemical Green in accordance with the corporate governance requirements in Hong Kong. (5 marks)
[[Corporate Governance - Listing]]

(b) Assume Chemical Green became a listed company in Hong Kong and an audit committee was set up by Chemical Green, propose actions of the audit committee in discharging its corporate governance responsibilities in relation to the inventory loss. (5 marks)

## Solution
Answer 5(a)
The five improvement recommendations include the following:
- The Chairman and the Chief Executive Officer (“CEO”) of Chemical Green should ~not be the same person~. John should resign as either the Chairman or the CEO.
- The composition of the board of directors should be extended to ~at least one-third~ of the directors as **independent non-executive directors**.
- A remuneration committee should be established. A majority of the members of the remuneration committee should be independent non-executive directors. The Chairman of the remuneration committee should be an independent non-executive director. The general policy on the remuneration of executive directors and the specific remuneration packages of each director should be determined by the remuneration committee but not to be determined by the Chairman and CEO of Chemical Green.
- An audit committee should be set up. The internal audit team should report to the audit committee instead of the Chief Operation Officer, who is also an executive director of the board.
- The **audit committee** should consist ~entirely of non-executive directors~ and there should be at least three non-executive directors on the committee. At least one member of the audit committee is an independent non-executive director who has appropriate professional qualifications, or accounting or related financial management expertise. A majority of the audit committee members must be independent non-executive directors and the Chairman of the audit committee must be an independent non-executive directors as well.

Answer 5(b)

In response to the inventory loss, the audit committee should consider the following actions:
- Discuss with the ~board of directors~ to **set up** an **independent investigating team** to find out the causes of the inventory loss.
- **Direct** the independent investigating team to understand the ~root causes~ of the inventory loss.
- **Supervise** the **internal audit team** to perform a thorough review of the company’s ~inventory management~ processes and controls in place, evaluate and identify possible ~control deficiencies~ and improvement areas on the inventory management procedures.
- Discuss with the **external auditor** their historical and current inventory count findings.
- Review with the **finance and accounting team and internal audit team** the **quantification** of inventory loss and the sufficiency of disclosures made in the financial statements.
- Consider if there is any **management fraud** involved in the inventory loss and suggest appropriate follow up and remediated actions.
