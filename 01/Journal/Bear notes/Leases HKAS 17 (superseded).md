# Leases HKAS 17 (superseded)
![](Leases%20HKAS%2017%20(superseded)/image.png)
HKAS 17 required that leases were classified as operating or finance leases and accounted for accordingly:

- A lease expense was recognised in profit or loss on a straight line basis in respect of operating leases; and
- The leased asset and a corresponding lease liability were recognised in the statement of financial position in respect of finance leases.

The determination of whether a lease was operating or finance in nature was ::subjective:: and required an assessment of risks and rewards of ownership. As a result entities were able to structure leasing arrangements to be classified as operating leases so resulting in ~a source of 'off-balance sheet' financing~.
