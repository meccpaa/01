# Recognition of inventory as an expense
An expense related to inventory is recognised in profit or loss in the following circumstances:

(a) When inventories are **sold**, the carrying amount of those inventories is recognised as an expense by:

DEBIT Cost of sales
CREDIT Inventories

(b) Any write down of inventories to NR9 and all losses of inventories are recognised as an expense in the period the write down or loss occurs by:

DEBIT Expense account (e.g. Inventory written down)
CREDIT Inventories

The exact expense account is not defined by HKAS 2. Depending on the circumstances of the write down, a company may use an inventory write down account, cost of sales or some other account.

(c) Any reversal of a previous write down of inventories, arising from an increase in NR9, is recognised as a reduction in the amount of inventories recognised as an expense in the period in which the reversal occurs by:

DEBIT Inventories
CREDIT Expense account

Again, the expense account is not defined, but it is likely to be the same as that account in which the previous write down was recognised.
