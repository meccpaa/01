# approved investment advisers - s.20AA(6)
'Approved investment adviser' means:

(a) a corporation **licensed** to carry on a business in ~advising~ on securities or asset management under `Part V of the SFO`; or

(b) an **authorised FI registered** for carrying on such a business under that part, only to the ~extent~ that the FI ~carries on such a business~.
