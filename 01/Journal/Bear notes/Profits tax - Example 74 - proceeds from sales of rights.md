# Profits tax - Example 74 - proceeds from sales of rights
The facts are the same as Example 73 except that the trade mark is sold for $1 million. The relevant sale proceeds are $550,000 ($1m x 55%). The unallowed amount is still $660,000, which exceeds the relevant sale proceeds. The excess of $110,000 ($660,000 – $550,000) is deductible in 2016/17 (s.16EB(2)(a)).
