# FE (Jun 19) Paper I: Q1(a) revenue accounting
(a) Analyse the accounting treatment for revenue recognition in relation to the license agreement with Advance Press using the five-step model. 
(12 marks) 
- - - -
## Answer
**Step 1: Identify the contract with a customer**
A contract exists on the date of contract inception (1 February 2018) as: 
* The contract is approved by the entity (BEConsulting) and the customer (Advance Press) upon the date of contract inception and they are committed to perform the relevant performance obligations. 

* The contract has commercial substance – BE Consulting’s cash flows change as a result of this contract, i.e. it receives royalty payment from the Advance Press. 

* The right of BE Consulting and the customer (Advance Press) regarding the promised goods and services can be identified – BE Consulting granted Advance Press the right to assess and publish all the latest content of “Sing along with Kocky” series exclusively in Singapore and Malaysia for a license period of 4 years and BE Consulting received royalty income as return. 

* There are identifiable payment terms for the promised goods or services – BE Consulting would receive royalties at a fixed fee of US$2 million in each year of the 4-year license period plus a sharing at 5% of the proceeds from publishing the “Sing along with Kocky” series which would be settled on a quarterly basis during the 4-year license period. 

* It is probable that BE Consulting will collect the consideration to which it is entitled – considering Advance Press is a subsidiary of a listed company in Singapore with sound financial background and with good credit history, the Group has a valid expectation that the royalty payment would be collected when due. 
**Step 2: Identify the performance obligations in the contract**
In accordance with para 27 of HKFRS 15, BE Consulting assesses the goods and services promised to Advance Press to determine which goods and services are distinct. There is no other performance obligations (“PO”) other than the promise to grant a licence to Advance Press, i.e. the right to assess and publish all the latest content of the “Sing along with Kocky” series exclusively in Singapore and Malaysia for a license period of 4 years. 

BE Consulting assessed that its promise in granting the licence is a promise to provide the right to assess the intellectual property (the “Sing along with Kocky” series) after considering the following criteria in accordance with HKFRS 15: 
* (a)  The contract requires, and the customer (Advance Press) has reasonable expectation that BE Consulting would undertake activities (e.g. adding new videos content and characters to the series) that significantly affect the intellectual property to which Advance Press has rights (i.e. the latest content of the “Sing along with Kocky” series). 

* (b)  The rights granted by the licence directly expose the customer to any positive or negative effects of the entity’s activities, as the contract provides the customer access to the latest content of the “Sing along with Kocky” series. 

* (c)  Even though Advance Press may benefit from those activities through the rights granted by the licence, they do not result in the transfer of a good or a service to the customer as those activities occur. 

Therefore, it is concluded that BE Consulting’s promise is to provide Advance Press unlimited access and use of the intellectual property as it exists throughout the license period. 
**Step 3: Determine the transaction price**
The transaction price is the royalties received from Advance Press, which is the annual fixed fee of US$8 million (US$2 million x 4 years) plus the share at 5% of the proceeds. For the 5% share, it is a form of variable consideration and hence sales-based royalty exception applies. No estimation of the variable consideration is required, as it is a form of sales-based royalty for the licence of intellectual property and BE Consulting shall recognise revenue only when the subsequent sales occurs. 
In relation to the fixed fee of US$8 million, no significant financing components was noted, as the Company would receive the royalty fee of US$2 million each year. In relation to the 5% share, there was also no significant financing components since the amount and timing of considerations varies on the future sales that is not substantially within the control of BE Consulting and Advance Press pursuant to para 62(b) of HKFRS 15. 
**Step 4: Allocate the transaction price to performance obligations in the contract**
Since there is only one PO in the contract, no allocation of transaction price is required. 
**Step 5: Recognise revenue when (or as) the entity satisfies a performance obligation**
As mentioned in Step 2 above, the nature of the promise is the right to assess BE Consulting’s intellectual property as it exists throughout the licence period and hence revenue should be recorded over time. 
Because the contracts provide Advance Press with unlimited assess of the latest content for a fixed term of 4 years, the fixed consideration of US$8 million might be recognised as revenue using a time-based method. In relation to the 5% share, as mentioned in Step 3 above, sales-based royalty exception applies and it shall be recognised as revenue when subsequent sales occurs.

## Panel
Question 1(a) – 12 marks 
The question required candidates to analyse the accounting treatment for revenue recognition in relation to the license agreement with Advance Press using the five- step model. 
The candidates’ performance was fair. Students demonstrated basic knowledge on HKFRS 15, and could apply the five-step model to the case. However, most of the candidates failed to identify and apply the specific guidance of HKFRS 15 in relation to the licensing of intellectual properties in the case; the candidates were also not aware of the “sales-based royalty exception” in the transaction price determination and hence not required to estimate the variable consideration. This demonstrated that the candidates had some, but not strong, understanding of the accounting standard.
