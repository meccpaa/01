# Application of time extension and tax payments - CIR v Maple Ridge Holdings Limited [DCTC 701/2016}
The taxpayer has spent $8.5 million to purchase plant and machinery in 2012 and provided the same to its PRC subcontractor company to use in its manufacturing process. In July 2013 and Aug 2014, the taxpayer claimed initial and annual depreciation allowance totalling $5.7 million and $2.1 million respectively for 2012/13 and 2013/14 in respect of the acquisition of fixed assets in 2012.

In Feb 2015, the IRD rejected these claims and the two profit tax assessments were issued with these claims disallowed.

The IRD relied on s.75(4) of IRO to argue that for the recovery of tax the court shall not entertain any plea that the tax is "excessive" or "incorrect", subject to objection or under appeal. The taxpayer argued that there would be no tax payable by, or tax refund to, the taxpayer if the claimed depreciation allowances in the original profits tax computations are allowed.

The court rejected the taxpayer's argument and agreed with the IRD's submission.
