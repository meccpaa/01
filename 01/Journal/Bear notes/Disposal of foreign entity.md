# Disposal of foreign entity
A parent may dispose or part dispose of its foreign subsidiary. For this purpose, a **[[full disposal]]** includes:
	- A disposal of all interests held in a subsidiary 
	- Any disposal where control over the subsidiary is lost.

A **[[part disposal]]** is any other reduction in ownership in a foreign subsidiary i.e. where control is retained.
