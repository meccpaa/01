# short-term leases
Criteria:
	- Lease with a term of 12 months or less.
	- Leases with a purchase option cannot be short-term.

Election: by class of underlying asset ie simplified accounting is applied to all qualifying assets in the same class.

Accounting treatment: Lease payments are recognised on a straight-line basis over the lease term.
