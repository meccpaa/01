# hedging instrument
Contracts that can be designated as hedging instruments include: 

- [[Derivative]]  
- Non-derivative financial assets or liabilities measured **at fair value** through ~profit or loss~ (with the exception of financial liabilities designated at fair value through profit or loss for which changes in fair value attributable to credit risk are presented in OCI) 

For the hedge of a foreign currency risk [[foreign exchange risk]], the foreign currency risk component of a non-derivative financial asset or liability may be designated as a hedging instrument provided that it is not an investment in an equity instrument measured at FVTOCI by election.
