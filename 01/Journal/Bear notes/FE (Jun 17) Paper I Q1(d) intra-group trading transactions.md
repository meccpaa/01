# FE (Jun 17) Paper I: Q1(d) intra-group trading transactions
### (d) During the audit, the audit junior has asked you about the accounting treatment of the intra-group trading transactions amongst West Wine, West Wine Wholesales and West Wine Retails.

#### (i) Explain to the audit junior the concept of unrealized profit adjustments (including the respective deferred tax adjustments) for the unsold inventories of West Wine Wholesales and West Wine Retail purchased from West Wine from the perspective of separate financial statements and consolidated financial statements of West Wine. (4 marks)

West Wine, as an individual company, recorded the profit from the sale of wine to West Wine Wholesales and West Wine Retail. However, from the Group’s perspective, the profit has not yet been realized until the wine is sold outside the Group. Therefore, an adjustment was made to eliminate the unrealized profit for the unsold wine in accordance with HKFRS 10 Consolidated Financial Statements.

In addition, West Wine is taxed on the profit from the sale of wines to West Wholesales and West Wine Retail (including the unrealised profit eliminated). After the unrealized profit adjustment, the carrying amount of the inventories would be reduced while the tax base remained unchanged. Since the tax base is larger than the carrying value, a deferred tax asset adjustment is recorded assuming it is probable (more likely than not) that sufficient profits would be available against which any deductible temporary difference can be utilized in accordance with HKAS 12 Income Taxes.
- - - -
#### (ii) Explain if there is an additional accounting consideration of the intra-group transactions if West Wine Wholesales and West Wine Retail are the selling companies while West Wine is the buying company. (2 marks)

West Wine Wholesales is a 90%-owned subsidiary and West Wine Retail is a 70%-owned subsidiary. If they are the selling companies, unrealized profits and the respective deferred tax adjustments should be allocated to the non-controlling interest for its share of the unrealized profits and deferred tax assets.
