# S.9(1)(aa)
Lump sum payments (representing the employer's contributions) from an unrecognised retirement scheme
- - - -
So much of an amount (other than a pension) as is attributable to the **employer's contributions to a fund**, scheme or society other than a recognised occupational retirement scheme or mandatory provident fund ('MPF') scheme.
- - - -
Notwithstanding its capital nature, such payment from an unrecognised occupational retirement scheme is chargeable to salaries tax.
