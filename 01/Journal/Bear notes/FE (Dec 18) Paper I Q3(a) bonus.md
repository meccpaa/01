# FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Q3(a) bonus
YK & Co has been the auditor of the Company for several years. Assume you are the audit manager.

(a) (i) Advise as to the accounting treatment and the audit implication of the bonus proposal on the audit of the consolidated financial statements of SSHI for the year ended 31 December 2017. (7 marks)

(ii) Assuming the Group has the practice for bonus payment to the employees in prior years and the bonus is not conditional upon remaining employment with the Group.

Advise as to the accounting treatment and auditing implication of the bonus proposal on the audit of the consolidated financial statements of SSHI for the year ended 31 December 2017.

(6 marks)

Note: For both (i) and (ii), when you consider the audit implications, you have to consider whether the management, if there is any misstatement, will adjust or not adjust the financial statements. You are not required to cover the impact on the auditor’s report in your discussion.

## Solutions
Considering (i) the employment **contracts** did not stipulate any bonus arrangement and (ii) the bonus proposal has been approved by the directors and written notice has been made to employee after year end date on 1 April 2018, there is **no legal or constructive obligation** for the Group to make the bonus payment as at 31 December 2017.

Furthermore, since the bonus payment is **conditional upon future employment**, the bonus payment might be considered as retaining the employee for future services. No obligation has arisen until the future services rendered by the employees have been received by the Group.

Accordingly, ::no liability or expenses:: should be recognised for 2017.

As such, the HK$8,000,000 current liability recognised as at 31 December 2017 should be identified as a ::factual misstatement:: during the audit. YK & Co should ~communicate~ on a timely basis the misstatement with the management and request the management to correct the misstatement.

Since this misstatement represents 19.48% of the PBT and 3.97% of net current assets, it is material to the consolidated financial statements. YK & Co have to ~evaluate the effect~ of this misstatement on the audit and assess whether the overall audit strategy and the audit plan need to be revised.

If the management ~correct~ the misstatement, ~additional audit procedures~ should be performed to assess whether the misstatement has been properly corrected. 

If the management ::did not correct:: the misstatement, YK & Co should ~obtain an understanding~ of management’s reasons for not making the corrections and should take that understanding into account when evaluating whether the consolidated financial statements ~as a whole are free from material misstatement.~ Also, YK & Co should **reassess the materiality** to confirm whether it remains appropriate in the context of the Company’s actual financial results. In addition, YK & Co should **communicate** with those charged with governance this uncorrected misstatement and the effect it may have on the auditor’s opinion.
- - - -
(ii) Assuming (i) the Group has a past practice regarding the bonus payment and (ii) the payment of the bonus is not conditional upon future employment, it may be appropriate to conclude there is a **constructive obligation** for the proposed bonus as at 31 December 2017. This is because the Group has ~created a valid expectation~ that it will honour the bonus payment as promised and the change in the Group’s informal practices would cause ::unacceptable damage:: to the relationship with employees.

Accordingly, the Group would recognise an **expense and a liability** for the bonus as at 31 December 2017, which is measured at the **present value** of the 4 equal instalments of HK$2,000,000. 

As such, the difference between the actual total payable amount of HK$8,000,000 recognised as a current liability and the present value of the 4 equal instalments of HK$2,000,000 should be identified as a factual misstatement during the audit. Although this misstatement may not be as material as the one identified if the company has no obligation on the bonus, similar audit procedures should be performed by YK & Co.

If management did not correct this misstatement and YK & Co conclude that the **uncorrected misstatement is not material**, they should request a **written representation** from the management, and where appropriate, those charged with governance whether they believe the effects of uncorrected misstatements are ~immaterial~, individually and in aggregate, to the consolidated financial statements as a whole.
