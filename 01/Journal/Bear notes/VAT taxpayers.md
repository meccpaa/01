# VAT taxpayers
The payers of VAT are divided into general taxpayers and small-scale taxpayers. The general taxpayers adopt general method (as discussed in section 8) to calculate VAT payable while the small-scale taxpayers adopt a simplified system to calculate VAT payable.

Article 1 of PRVAT provides that all units and individuals engaged in the sales of goods, the provision of processing, repair and replacement services and the importation of goods within the territory of the People's Republic of China are subject to VAT.

Starting from 1 August 2013, China has been implementing a VAT reform programme applying to various services on a nationwide basis. The majority of services such as transportation services, modern services and telecommunication services have been subject to VAT instead of BT.

With effect from 1 May 2016, the VAT reform programme has expanded to cover sales of immovable properties, leasing of immovable properties and transfer of land use right, as well as provision of construction services, financial services and consumer services (see section 14 below for details).

Units' are defined under PRVAT Article 9 as enterprises, administrative units, public institutions, military units, social organisations and other units. 'Individuals' are defined widely to include individual business operators and other individuals.

In practice, taxpayers adopt different methods of calculating VAT according to their scale of operations. The taxpayers are divided into two categories namely: the general taxpayers (who adopts the general calculation method as discussed in section 8 above); and the small-scale taxpayers (who adopts a simplified calculation method).
