# Consider the characteristics of the population from which the sample will be drawn - sample design
	- (i) For [[tests of controls]], the auditor should make an **estimate of the expected rate of deviation** based on expectation through investigation of the relevant controls or on the examination of a small number of items from the population. If the ::expected rate of deviation is above a certain level::, the auditor will normally reject further tests of controls.
	- (ii) For [[tests of details]], the auditor may decide to test a large sample size or test 100% if the ::expected misstatement is high::.
