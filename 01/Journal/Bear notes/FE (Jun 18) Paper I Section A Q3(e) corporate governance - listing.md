# FE (Jun 18) Paper I: Section A Q3(e) corporate governance - listing
(e) Identify three improvements that the Grand Music Group need to make in order to fulfil the listing requirements in relation to corporate governance. (3 marks)

## Answer
The current board of directors only comprises ~major shareholders with only music-related expertise~. A board of directors should include a **balanced composition** of executive and non-executive directors (including independent non-executive directors) with a ~wide range of relevant expertise~. Since the existing directors might not have any **financial or accounting expertise**, it is important to invite someone with accounting or financial expertise to join the board.

An **audit committee** should be established to report to the board of directors on their decisions or recommendations in respect of auditing, financial reporting and financial control matters. Having a separate audit committee would help Grand Music Group board of directors to focus on strategic and other non-financial matters, leaving the audit committee to oversee the detailed auditing, financial reporting and financial control matters.

An **internal audit department** should be established to evaluate and improve the effectiveness of Grand Music Group’s governance processes, risk management and internal controls.
