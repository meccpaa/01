### Solution

Inventory must be measured line by line rather than as a whole, taking lower of cost and NR9 in each case:

![](Inventory%20Example%204%20Inventory%20valuation%20(2)/9CCDF79F-5939-482E-A8D1-BFBCF9C756C7.png)

Therefore it is measured at $34.5m and should be written down by 1,500,000. This is achieved by ($):

DEBIT Inventory written down 1,500,000 
CREDIT Inventories 1,500,000 

To recognise the write down of inventories to net realisable value.
