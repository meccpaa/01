# 8.5.1 Hedge accounting
Disclosures must be made relating to hedge accounting, as follows:
(a) An explanation of risk management strategy and how it is applied to manage risk.
(b) How the entity's hedging activities may affect the amount, timing and uncertainty of future cash flows.
(c) The effect that hedge accounting has had on the entity's statement of financial position, statement of profit or loss and comprehensive income and statement of changes in equity.
