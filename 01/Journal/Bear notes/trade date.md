# trade date
The trade date is the date on which an entity commits to purchase or sell an asset, and trade date accounting results in: 
- The recognition of an asset to be received and liability to pay for it on the trade date, and 
- The derecognition of an asset to be sold and corresponding receivable on the trade date (together with any gain or loss).

When trade date accounting is used interest does not start to accrue until the settlement date when title passes.
