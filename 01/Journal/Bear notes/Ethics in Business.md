# Ethics in Business
![](Ethics%20in%20Business/image.png)
Ethical dilemmas involve situations concerning right and wrong where values are in conflict.

The problems that confront business executives are many. Some of the most complex and difficult involve situations concerning right and wrong where values are in conflict. These situations are called ethical dilemmas.

With corporate scandals and billion-dollar bankruptcies dominating headlines, ethics has almost become a hotter topic than earnings in the business world.
