# FE Dec 19 Paper II Section A - 1(b) Key audit matters
**(b)**Thomas has previously asked what are key audit matters. 
**(i) Explain to Thomas what are key audit matters.**
**(ii) Prepare an abstract of two key audit matters to be included in the Key Audit Matters section of the Group’s audit report for the current year. In describing how you are going to address those matters, state three audit procedures for each key audit matter.**
**(12 marks)**
- - - -
## Answer
* (i)  Per Hong Kong Standard on Auditing 701 *Communicating Key Audit Matters in the Independent Auditor’s Report*, key audit matters are those matters that, in the auditor’s professional judgment, were of most significance in the audit of the financial statements of the current period. Key audit matters are selected from matters communicated with those charged with governance. 

* (ii)  Abstract of key audit matters to be included in the Group’s audit report. 

### Key audit matter - Working capital sufficiency 
Refer to note x to the consolidated financial statements. Working capital sufficiency is determined to be a key audit matter for the audit of the Group for the current year due to significant judgement and estimates have been made by the Group in relation to the working capital forecast. In addition, significant audit effort has been spent on validating the working capital sufficiency. 
In relation to the working capital sufficiency, we have performed the following procedures during the audit: 
* Understand management’s plan for how they are going to maintain sufficient working capital for daily operation of the Group, including funding Project A or Project B that requires HK$30 million as the initial investment. 
* Obtain the working capital forecast and evaluate the reasonableness of the assumptions made by management in the forecast (e.g. sales growth rate, projected sales volume etc).
* Circulate confirmation to confirm bank loan and facilities available at the year end and assess the probability of facility renewal during the forecast period. 
* Examine the bank loan agreement to note for any bank covenant terms, and assess management’s compliance with those terms based on the working capital forecast. 
* Evaluate the sensitivity of the working capital forecast. 

### Key audit matter - Revenue recognition – cutoff 
Refer to note x to the consolidated financial statements. The Group recognised revenue of HK$155,000,000 for the year ended 31 December 2018. Revenue recognition is determined to be a key audit matter for the audit of the Group for the current year due to significant risk has been identified related to the cutoff assertion during the audit. In addition, significant judgment and estimates have been made by the Group on the timing of revenue recognition, and significant audit effort has been spent on validating the timing of revenue recognition during the audit. 
In relation to the revenue recognition, we have performed the following procedures during the audit: 

* Understand, evaluate and test the controls relating to cutoff of revenue recognition. 
* Obtain a list of goods delivered prior to and subsequent to the year end and test whether the transactions have been properly recorded in the appropriate period by tracing to the shipping documents (such as customer signed receipts). 
* Obtain a list of goods returned subsequent to the year end and test whether the returned transactions have been properly recorded. 

### Key audit matter - Provision for closure of a warehouse in Fo Tan and a store in Shatin 
Refer to note x to the consolidated financial statements. The Group recorded provision for closure of a warehouse in Fo Tan and a store in Shatin of HK$xxx as at 31 December 2018. Provision for closure of a warehouse and a store is determined to be a key audit matter for the audit of the Group for the current year due to significant risk has been identified related to the provision during the audit. In addition, significant judgment and estimates have been made by the Group on the determination of provision, and significant audit effort has been spent on the assessment of the provision during the audit. Also, the financial impact of this provision is significant to the consolidated financial statements of the Group for the current year. 
In relation to the provision for closure of the warehouse in Fo Tan and the store in Shatin, we have performed the following procedures during the audit: 
* Understand and evaluate management’s process and control in determining the provision. 
* Obtain the schedule for provision for employees laid off, and assess the reasonableness or check the calculation of the provision for redundancy according to the Labour Law in Hong Kong. 
* Obtain the lease contract for the store and the schedule for provision of loss, and assess the reasonableness or check the calculation of the loss for terminating the lease contract. 
* Obtain the list of property, plant and equipment related to the warehouse and store and understand their planned use, and assess if any loss/impairment should be recorded due to closure of the warehouse and store (e.g. disposal or resale value of property, plant and equipment). 

### Key audit matter - Existence of inventory 
Refer to note x to the consolidated financial statements. The Group held inventory of HK$89,900,000 as at 31 December 2018. The existence of inventory is determined to be a key audit matter for the audit of the Group for the current year due to the balance of inventory is significant to the consolidated financial statements of the Group for the current year. In addition, significant audit effort has been spent on auditing the inventory balance due to the various locations. 
In relation to the existence of inventory, we have performed the following procedures during the audit: 
* Understand and evaluate the effectiveness of controls for the inventory count. 
* Observe the inventory count at the Group’s warehouse and store at the year-end. 
* For the inventory counts observed, select items from the inventory listing generated from the client’s inventory system and perform test counts. 
* Evaluate the effectiveness of controls over the record of inventory movement between the year-end inventory count and the year-end inventory list that agreed to the accounting system.
