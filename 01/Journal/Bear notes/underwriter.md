# underwriter
New issues of shares must **usually be fully underwritten**. This means that there are ~investors who have formally agreed~ to buy any of the shares of the company that are not purchased by other investors. The reason for this rule is that if the share issue is fully underwritten, the company is able to plan on the basis that it will receive the amount of funds it is expecting (and will not raise insufficient money because investors decide not to buy the shares).
