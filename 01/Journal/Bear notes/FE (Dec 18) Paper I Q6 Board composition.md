# FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Q6 Board composition
SSHI plans to be listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange and the management is aware of the Corporate Governance issues. The Corporate Governance code contains some general principles and Code Provisions and Recommended Best Practices. Board composition is one of the key provisions. With the case background information, identify the weakness of the corporate governance structure that relates to the board composition; and advise how to tackle the weakness.

(5 marks)
- - - -
[[FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Case]]
**Andy Shine** is the ~founder~ of the Group. He is the ~Chairman~ of the Board, ::~Executive Director~::, and is a member of the ~Nomination Committee~ and ~Remuneration Committee~ of the Group. Andy is responsible for the strategic direction and overall performance of the Group. The Board also consists of **two** ~other Independent Non-Executive Directors~, Tom Wong and Peter Lee. **Tom** is also a member of the ~[[Audit Committee]]~ and ~[[Nomination Committee]]~ of the Group. **Peter** is also the Chairman of the Nomination Committee and a member of the Remuneration Committee of the Group.
- - - -
## Answer
The Board should include a **balanced composition** of executive and non-executive directors (including independent non-executive directors) with ~relevant skills and experience~ so that there is a strong **independent** element on the board that can effectively exercise independent judgment.

In this case, there are ::only two independent non-executive directors::; therefore, it cannot satisfy the listing requirement.

With the plan to list on the stock exchange and in order to tackle this weakness to comply with the listing requirement, ~one-third of the board members~ and ~at least three members~ have to be independent non-executive directors.

SSHI should **invite at least one more individual** who is of good calibre and experienced as an independent non-executive director.
