# financial risk self-test 1: insurance
If an airline company fully insures the hull of an airliner against all risks for its full value of US$150m has it totally eradicated this risk?
- - - -
## Answers
Even if a risk has been fully insured this does ~not totally eradicate risk~, rather it converts the risk into [[credit risk]] related to the ::insurer’s ability to meet potential claims::. Thus if the airliner is destroyed the airline is, in fact, only protected from the loss if the insurer has the resources to meet the claim. Therefore when selecting insurance cover the treasurer should consider the **financial strength of insurers** as well as the **premiums offered**.
