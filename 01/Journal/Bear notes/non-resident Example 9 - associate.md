# non-resident Example 9 - associate
P Ltd, a resident company, held 20% of the issued shares of E-Fund Ltd, a tax-exempt offshore fund and an associate of P Ltd.

Although the beneficial interest held by P Ltd was less than 30%, the deeming provisions under s.20AE would continue to apply as it held some beneficial interest in E-Fund Ltd which was its associate.
