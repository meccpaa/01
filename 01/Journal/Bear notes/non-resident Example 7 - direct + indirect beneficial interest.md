# non-resident Example 7 - direct + indirect beneficial interest
(Adapted from DIPN 43 (Revised), Example 4)

M Ltd, a resident company, directly held 20% of the issued shares of C-Fund Ltd, a tax-exempt offshore fund. Through other companies, M Ltd also indirectly held 15% of C-Fund Ltd.

The deeming provisions under s.20AE could apply to M Ltd. Its direct and indirect beneficial interests in C-Fund Ltd (20% + 15% = 35%) exceeded the '30% or more' threshold. Hence, 35% of the exempt profits of C-Fund Ltd would be deemed to be the assessable profits of M Ltd.
