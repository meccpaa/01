# Perf-sys Example - sales margin
Look at the following examples.
(a) Golden Press, a printer
![](Perf-sys%20Example%20-%20sales%20margin/6D16F304-D3C1-4CB0-8D87-A90C12F73B0E.png)
Cost of sales comprises direct material cost, such as paper, and direct labour. Distribution and administrative expenses include depreciation. Sales margin = 32%.

Sales margin at least shows the contribution that is being made, especially when direct variable costs are very significant.

(b) Blue Line, a bus company
![](Perf-sys%20Example%20-%20sales%20margin/BE8E42AD-770D-4BFD-9EA2-24B866344C72.png)
Sales margin = 16%. Clearly a higher percentage of costs are operating costs.
