# Contingent on both future earnings and future market price of the ordinary shares
The number of ordinary shares contingently issuable may depend on future earnings and future prices of the ordinary shares. In such cases, the number of ordinary shares included in the diluted EPS calculation is based on both conditions (i.e. earnings to date and the current market price at the end of the reporting period).

CIOS are not included in the diluted EPS calculation unless both conditions are met.
