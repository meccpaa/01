HK does not have a tax treaty with Australia.  We should use SG to run the business with NZ and Australia.

SG will need to do a GST registration and be responsible for GST in Australia if it sells directly to Australian consumers (“B2C”) and the turnover exceeds A$75,000.  However, if the sales are made **through an operator** of an electronic distribution platform (EDP) or online marketplace, such as an app store that supplies imported digital products to Australian consumers, the EDP / App store will handle the GST and the merchant (i.e. PCCW) will not need to do GST registration.  Similar rules apply to NZ, and the registration threshold is NZ$60,000.

The standard GST rates are 10% for Australia and 15% for NZ.
