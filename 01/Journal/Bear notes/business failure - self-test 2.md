# business failure - self-test 2
What actions could the directors of a company facing financial difficulties take to prevent insolvency?
- - - -
## answer
Since insolvency is the result of a company’s inability to meet its financial obligations when due, the directors need to raise cash. The following are examples of actions that the directors could take:

* Sale of assets to realise cash. A sale-and-leaseback, as covered in Chapter 9, would also allow the company to retain use of the asset whilst realising its value.
* Corporate restructuring as discussed in Chapter 19 could also generate cash. For example a divestment, spin-off or carve out could generate cash for the company to meet its obligations.
* Raise additional equity capital, for example a rights issue, could raise the necessary cash from existing shareholders to solve the company’s financial issues.
* Obtain additional borrowing. A loan could be obtained, if the financial difficulty is expected to be short term working capital issue, arranging an overdraft may be sufficient.
* As mentioned in Chapter 8, if a company facing a cash shortage and has existing borrowings, it could contact lenders to discuss the possibility of rescheduling to reduce the size of repayments. For example, if a company has a 3 year loan from a bank, the bank may be prepared to extend the term to 5 years, in order to reduce repayments to a level the company can meet. Lenders may be prepared to vary the terms of lending in this way if they believe that it will result in a better outcome than insolvency.
