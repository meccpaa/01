Dear Frederick Leung,

It’s nice to meet you . Regarding to your questions, please see my response below.


1. The TP disclosure form is the separate form of tax return;  this is the new requirement for the Company with related party transactions who have annual revenue of THB 200 million.

The  due date is the same as due of tax return, which is about end of May 2020.


2. In term of TP Report, at current stage though it is not required to submit to RD; but we would urge to have this in place. In case of the reassessment or tax investigation, tax authority would looking for the substantial evidence of the reason for the charges (market value or normal value) and of the benefits receive; and the TP report could be the good reference.



3. For TP documentations, in practices, the related transactions are subject to close scrutiny by the tax authority as to their reasonableness/transfer pricing.  Therefore, we recommend the Company to ensure that there are evidences to support these services render at Thai Office. The evidences shall not be only invoices, but also agreements, correspondence, minutes of meetings, memos, reports, written advice to prove that the services are actually rendered to and received by the Company, and certification on the reliability of allocated costs by an external auditor.  Furthermore, the sufficient details of each allocated expenses should be made available.



Apart from TP requirement as mentioned above, we would like to draw your attention on the VAT exposure, which become common trend of tax audit especially for the e-commerce business especially from service providers abroad and provide services via Digital Platform to customers located in Thailand.
