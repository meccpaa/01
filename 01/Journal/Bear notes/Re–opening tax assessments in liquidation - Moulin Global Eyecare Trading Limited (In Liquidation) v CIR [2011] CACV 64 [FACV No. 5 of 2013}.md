# Re–opening tax assessments in liquidation - Moulin Global Eyecare Trading Limited (In Liquidation) v CIR [2011] CACV 64 [FACV No. 5 of 2013}
The Court of Appeal allowed the CIR's appeal against the CFI's decision (HCAL 29/2010). The taxpayer has been granted leave to appeal to the CFA.

On 13 March 2014, the CFA dismissed the appeal. The liquidators cannot rely on s.64(1) and s.70A to re–open tax assessments.
