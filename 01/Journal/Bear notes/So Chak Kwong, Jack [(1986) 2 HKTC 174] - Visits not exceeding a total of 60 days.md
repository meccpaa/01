# So Chak Kwong, Jack [(1986) 2 HKTC 174] - Visits not exceeding a total of 60 days
The taxpayer was employed by a Hong Kong company but seconded to the United Kingdom. During the year of assessment 1981/82, he visited Hong Kong for 108 days of which 28 days were spent on rendering services in connection with his employment. The taxpayer's total emoluments were assessed to salaries tax.

The Board of Review decided in favour of the taxpayer that his income should be exempt from tax as he had not rendered services in Hong Kong for more than 60 days. The High Court decided in favour of the Commissioner on the grounds that the words 'not exceeding a total of 60 days' **qualified the word 'visits'** rather than ::the words 'services rendered'::. All the taxpayer's **emoluments were taxable** as he visited Hong Kong for more than 60 days.

[[S.8(1B) ≤ 60 days]]
