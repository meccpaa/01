# term of office of auditor - new CO s.402, 403
Generally a person appointed as auditor of a company holds office in accordance with the terms of the appointment or until the company holds its annual general meeting or by a written resolution passed when the annual general meeting is not held (section 402 of new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)).

If the company is not required to hold an annual general meeting in respect of a financial year and at the end of the appointment period in relation to the next financial year, no person has been appointed as auditor of the company for that next financial year, the person who is the auditor of the company as at the end. of that appointment period is **deemed to be reappointed**, at that time, as auditor of the company for that next financial year on the same terms of appointment. (section 403 of new Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622)).
