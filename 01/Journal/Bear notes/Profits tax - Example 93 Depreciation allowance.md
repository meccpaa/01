The business is a post-1974 business (i.e. a new business), and the basis periods for the years of assessment 2016/17 and 2017/18 are 12 months to 31 December 2016 and 12 months to 30 November 2017, respectively (see the discussion on basis period in section 11). Therefore, the period from 1 December 2016 to 31 December 2016 forms the basis period for the two years of assessment 2016/17 and 2017/18. Pursuant to s.40(1), capital expenditure on machinery and plant incurred in the overlapping basis period is treated as incurred in the basis period of the first year of assessment (i.e. 2016/17).
