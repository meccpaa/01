# FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Q5 NPV
In the last meeting, the Head of the Sales and Promotion team proposed a new project (“Project A”) that requires HK$ 80 million as the initial investment. With the updated information and assuming that the return of Project A is 8%, it is forecasted that Project A will provide a net cash flow of HK$ 5 million to the Company at the end of the first year and the cash flow from the second year onwards is projected to grow annually at a constant rate of 3% indefinitely.

Required:

(a) The Head of the Sales and Promotion team proposes to the management that Project A should be accepted. Assess and justify with calculation as to whether Project A should be accepted. (4 marks)

(b) By reviewing SSHI’s operations and the macroeconomic environment, the Finance Director has doubts about the 3% growth rate in view of the economic downturn. The Finance Director is very conservative and in order to provide more information to facilitate the management decision-making process, calculate the constant growth rate (expressed as a percentage) at the breakeven level. (2 marks)
- - - -
## Answer
Since the future cash inflows of Project A will continue forever, therefore, we apply the perpetuity concept. The PV of the future cash flows from Project A is:

PV of cash inflows 
= C / (R - g)
= HK$5,000,000 / (0.08 - 0.03)
= HK$100,000,000

NPV of Project A
= initial investment + PV of cash inflows
= -HK$80,000,000 + 100,000,000
= HK$20,000,000

With the time value of money and risk being considered, the NPV is positive and it adds value to SSHI, therefore, we would accept Project A.

Answer 5(b)

In order to determine the minimum growth rate, we would set the NPV of Project A as zero in the PV of a growing perpetuity:

NPV = 0 = -HK$80,000,000 + 5,000,000 / (0.08 - g)

g = 0.0175 = 1.75%
