# SIT: gifts and hospitality
This is a notoriously difficult area; in some parts of the world, offering gifts or corporate hospitality is an accepted part of business life; in others, it falls into a grey area somewhere between inducement and downright bribery! In general, unless the **value** of the gift or hospitality is **trivial and inconsequential** a member of an engagement team should ::decline:: any offers which may be seen to be intended to influence the judgment of a professional accountant.
