# Procedures - reviews
In a review engagement, the auditor will rely more heavily on procedures such as inquiry and analytical review than on more detailed substantive testing such as testing accounting records through inspection, observation or confirmation. There are two reasons for this:

(1) He is seeking a lower level of assurance than for an audit, so these forms of evidence are sufficient due to risk being lower.

(2) Such techniques provide indicators to direct work to risk areas and from which to draw conclusions; they are quick and, therefore, cost-effective.
