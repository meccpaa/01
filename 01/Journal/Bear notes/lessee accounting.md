# lessee accounting
HKFRS 16 requires that a single lease accounting model is applied to all leasing arrangements, with limited exception. The accounting model requires that an asset and liability are recognised in the statement of financial position, thereby addressing the criticism of HKAS 17, the previous standard on leases, that it resulted in 'off-balance sheet' financing.

[[simplified accounting - lease]] may be applied to certain leases.
- - - -
## 3.3 Lessee accounting model
At the start of a lease to which [[simplified accounting - lease]] is not applied, a lessee should recognise:

1. A [[lease liability]], representing the obligation to make lease payments to the lessor, and 
2. A [[right-of-use asset]], representing the right to use the underlying asset for the lease term.
- - - -
## 3.3.5 Presentation in lessee’s financial statements
In the statement of financial position:
- A [[right-of-use asset]] must be ~presented separately~ from other assets; 
- A [[lease liability]] must be ~presented separately~ from other liabilities; 
- A [[lease liability]] must be **split** between ~current~ and ~non-current~ amounts.
In the statement of profit or loss, ~depreciation~ on the right-of-use asset and ~finance costs~ relating to the lease liability must be ~presented separately~.
