# Judicial review on CIR's refusal to extend time to lodge objection - Chan Chun Chuen v CIR [2012] FAMV 23
The CFI held that the CIR did not properly consider the evidence submitted and have therefore acted unreasonably in refusing to grant an extension of time for the taxpayer to lodge an objection. The Court of Appeal allowed the CIR's appeal.

On 15 April 2013, the taxpayer's application for leave to appeal to the CFA was dismissed.
