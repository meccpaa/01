# Depreciation allowance for machinery or plant
[[Machinery or plant]] attracts much more generous depreciation allowances than buildings. It is therefore important to distinguish between machinery or plant and building, and to identify what assets qualify as machinery or plant and what assets are excluded.

Implements, utensils and articles under the second part of IRR 2 (including belting, crockery and cutlery, kitchen utensils, linen, loose tools, soft furnishings, surgical and dental instruments, tubes for X-ray and infra-red machines) are specifically excluded from the definition of machinery or plant. Instead, capital expenditure on such assets is allowed on a replacement basis under s.16(1)(f), i.e. initial purchase cost is neither deductible nor ranking for depreciation allowance while replacement cost is fully allowable.

Capital expenditure on assets used for R&D purposes is allowed as a deduction under s.16B (see section 8.6.3 on 'Expenditure on research and development: s.16B').

From the year of assessment commencing 1 April 1998, capital expenditure on PFAs (e.g. manufacturing equipment, computer) or the written down value of PFAs brought forward from 1997/98 is allowed as a deduction under s.16G (see section 8.6.10 on 'Capital expenditure on the provisions of a prescribed fixed asset: s.16G').

Capital expenditure on EPM and EPIs incurred on or after 27 June 2008, and capital expenditure on EFVs incurred on or after 18 June 2010 is allowed as deductions under ss.16H to K in the year of purchase (see section 8.6.11 on 'Capital expenditure on the environmental protection facilities: ss.16H-K').

### 9.4.2 Qualifying person

Depreciation allowances for machinery or plant are available to:

(a) Persons carrying on a trade, profession or business, and the machinery or plant is used in the production of assessable profits (ss.18F and 19E).

(b) Persons chargeable to salaries tax, and the use of machinery or plant is essential for the production of assessable income (ss.12(1)(b) and (5)).

### 9.4.3 Qualifying expenditure

Capital expenditure, as defined under s.40(1):

(a) **Includes** purchase price, freight, insurance, delivery and installation costs, capital expenditure on alterations to an existing building incidental to the installation of that machinery or plant, and interest paid and commitment fees incurred in respect of a loan made for the sole purpose of financing the provision of machinery or plant; but

(b) **Excludes** expenditure which is reimbursed by way of or is attributable to any grant, subsidy or similar financial assistance, and those allowed under ss.16B(1)(b), 16G and 16I(2) and (3), and those on implements, utensils and articles.
