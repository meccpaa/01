# Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO)
The Securities and Futures Ordinance (SFO) lays down the overall parameters which govern corporate finance activities.
- - - -
The SFO is the main body of law relating to the securities and futures industry and markets. The powers and authority of the [[Securities and Futures Commission (SFC)]] are derived from the SFO, which allows the SFC to make rules with respect to participants in the markets, including rules regarding applications for listing.
