# CoC self-test 2: WACC
The following financial information is available about a private company:

- In the statement of financial position, total assets are $462m. These consist of $387m of non-current assets and $75m of current assets. Total liabilities are $165m: the proportion of long-term liabilities (interest bearing) and current liabilities (non-interest bearing) are 80:20. Equity amounts to $297m.
- Companies of a similar business nature and financial position have to pay 8.5% to raise long-term financing and have an equity a beta of 1.18.
- The corporate tax rate is 16%.
- The yield on a 10-year government bond is 5.5% 
- The equity risk premium is 8%.

Required

Based on the above information, calculate the WACC of this company.
