# Preliminary engagement activities
Planning activities ~establish~ an **overall audit strategy** for the engagement and develop an **audit plan** in order to ~reduce audit risk to an acceptably low level~.

`HKSA 300` requires that, at the beginning of the **current audit** engagement, the auditor must perform the following ~preliminary engagement activities~:

- Perform procedures regarding the ~continuance~ of the client relationship and the specific audit engagement (HKSA 220)
- Evaluate compliance with ~ethical~ requirements such as **independence** (HKSA 220) 
- [[Establish an understanding of the terms of the engagement (HKSA 210)]]

Performing the preliminary engagement activities ~assists the auditor~ in **identifying and evaluating** events or circumstances that may **adversely affect** the auditor's ~ability to plan and perform~ the audit engagement.
