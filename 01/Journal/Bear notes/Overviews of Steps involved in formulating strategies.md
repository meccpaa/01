# Overviews of Steps involved in formulating strategies
1. **[[Strategic position]]**
	- Conduct a [[Corporate appraisal: environmental analysis]] (assess the internal and external environment of the organisation). 
	- Set mission and objectives – the **long-term aims** of the organisation may be broken down into ~shorter-term, more measurable targets~.
	- [[Gap analysis]] – compare forecast performance with strategic objectives to expose 'gaps' which need to be filled by new or different strategies.
2. **[[Strategic choice]]** – generate options and evaluate them to decide on which ones have the potential to succeed.
3. **[[Strategy implementation]]** – carry out the strategy at corporate, business and functional level.

Each of these will be considered in more detail as we progress through the chapter.
