# corporate treasury transaction
'Corporate treasury transaction' is defined in s.2 of Schedule 17B to mean any of the following transactions that is entered into by a corporation on its own account and related to the business of an [[associated corporation]]:
(a) Providing guarantees, performance bonds, standby letters of credit or other credit risk instruments;
(b) Investing the funds of the corporation or associated corporation in deposits, certificates of deposit, bonds, notes, debentures, money-market funds or other financial instruments for managing the cash and liquidity position;
(c) Entering into contracts (e.g. contracts for difference, foreign exchange contracts, forward/futures contracts, swap contracts, options contracts) for hedging interest rate risk, foreign exchange risk, liquidity risk, credit risk, commodity risk or other financial risk; and
(d) Factoring or forfaiting transactions.
- - - -
Corporate treasury transactions may be concluded with Hong Kong entities (e.g. placing a deposit with a Hong Kong bank) as long as they are related to the business of a [[non-Hong Kong associated corporation]].
