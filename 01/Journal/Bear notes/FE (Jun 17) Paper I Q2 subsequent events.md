# FE (Jun 17) Paper I: Q2 subsequent events
## Question 2 (9 marks – approximately 17 minutes) Advise as to the impact of the subsequent events of the Group in the case and explain the auditor’s responsibility accordingly. (9 marks)

### Subsequent event – goods returns and related adjustments of West Wine

Although this happened in April 2016, this was an adjusting event as the reason (i.e., the negative news that the wine imported from France may have been polluted while it was delivered from France to Hong Kong two months ago) for the goods returned already existed at the year-end date. It has a direct effect on the financial statements as at and for the year ended 31 March 2016 and adjustments should be made to the financial statements as at and for the year ended 31 March 2016.

Since adjustments were made based on the management’s estimates, the auditor has to:

	* (i) obtain sufficient and appropriate audit evidence regarding the estimates;
	* (ii) determine whether the estimates have been made in accordance with the requirements of the applicable financial reporting framework; and
	* (iii) determine whether the method used by the management for making the accounting estimates was appropriate.

Up to 15 June 2016, the supplier in France sent an email and agreed with West Wine to share 30% of the loss. Since the auditor’s report and the financial statements have not yet been issued, the adjustments previously made should be updated based on the agreement reached with the suppliers.

However, as the management did not update the adjustment, the auditor has to:

	* (i) assess the impact of this unadjusted misstatement on the financial statements and the auditor’s opinion;
	* (ii) communicate with the management and those charged with governance the effect of this unadjusted misstatement; and
	* (iii) request written representation from the management as to whether they believe the effects of this uncorrected misstatement are immaterial to the financial statements.

On 15 July 2016, the supplier in France agreed with West Wine to share up to 50% of the loss and adjustment was made in West Wine’s management accounts for the month ended 31 July 2016.

Since this was confirmed after the financial statements had been issued, the auditor has no obligations to perform procedures or make inquiries regarding the financial statements. However, if the auditor considers that had the fact been known at the date of the auditor’s report it may have led to a different outcome, the auditor should discuss with the management and assess whether an amendment should be made to the financial statements.

### Subsequent event – the fire accident and being sued by other tenants of Wine Diner

This is a non-adjusting event which happened between the financial statement date and the auditor’s report date and it has no direct effect on the Group’s financial statements as at and for the year ended 31 March 2016. However, since the loss caused by the fire was significant to the Group, it should be disclosed in the notes to the financial statements for the users of the financial statements to make proper evaluations and decisions.

The auditor has to obtain sufficient and appropriate audit evidence regarding the subsequent event and ensure the disclosure has been made in accordance with the requirements of the applicable financial reporting framework.

In August 2016, Wine Diner settled the case with the tenants outside court by paying total compensation fees of HK$0.6 million. The auditor had no obligations unless the fact had been known at the date of the auditor’s report which may have led to a different outcome, the auditor should discuss with the management and assess whether an amendment should be made to the financial statements.

### Subsequent event – Acquisition of ZWG by West Wine (BVI)

This is a non-adjusting event and should be disclosed in the notes to the financial statements. The auditor has to obtain sufficient and appropriate audit evidence regarding the subsequent event and ensure the disclosure has been made in accordance with the requirements of the applicable financial reporting framework.
