# Depreciation allowances
![](Journal/Bear%20notes/Doc/Depreciation%20allowances/image.png)

Depreciation allowances are available for capital expenditure incurred on (i) industrial buildings or structures; (ii) commercial buildings or structures; and (iii) machinery or plant.

Depreciation allowances are available for capital expenditure incurred on:
(a) Industrial buildings or structures;
(b) Commercial buildings or structures (buildings or structures other than industrial buildings or structures); and
(c) Machinery or plant (other than assets qualifying for deductions as PFA and EPFs; and assets used for R&D purposes).
