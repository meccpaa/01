# Haphazard selection
The auditors selects the sample **without following a structured technique**, avoiding any conscious bias or predictability and thus attempting to ensure that all items in the population have a chance of selection. This method is ::not appropriate when using [[statistical sampling]]::. 

#QP/C/Audit/evidence

Haphazard sampling involves the auditor selecting sampling units **without any conscious bias**, and in a manner that can be expected to be representative of the population.
