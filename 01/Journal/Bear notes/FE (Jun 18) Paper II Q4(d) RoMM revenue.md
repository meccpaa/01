# FE (Jun 18) Paper II: Q4(d) RoMM revenue
Analyse the risk of material misstatement of different types of advertising revenue of WT Advertising and propose the audit approach for the audit of advertising revenue in relation to the assertions of completeness and accuracy.

(5 marks)
- - - -
[[FE (Jun 18) Paper II: case]]
- - - -
## Solutions
**Fixed amounts** of advertising revenue determined based on the advertising period – the risk of material misstatement is **relatively lower** because the ~calculation is straight-forward~. **Test of details** can be performed to assess the ~completeness~ by reviewing the contracts and minutes while the ~accuracy~ can be assessed by performing a **reasonableness test**.

**Variable amounts** of advertising revenue determined based on the ~number of clicks~ on the particular advertisement – it has a ::higher risk:: of material misstatement as its completeness and accuracy depends on whether the relevant information is **properly captured** by the system. **Test of general IT controls** [[general controls]] should be performed to assess whether ~the system is properly maintained~ by WT Advertising. **Test of application controls** [[application controls]] should be performed to assess whether ~the relevant information is completely captured~ and the ~revenue is accurately calculated~ as expected by the system. In addition, some [[Substantive procedures]] or test of details such as a **reasonableness test** or checking to the data used to the source information should be performed to address the [[significant risks]] regarding completeness and accuracy.
