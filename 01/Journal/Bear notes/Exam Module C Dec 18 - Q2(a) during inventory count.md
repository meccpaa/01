# Exam Module C: Dec 18 - Q2(a) during inventory count
(a) Propose procedures that the audit team should perform during the observation of ELL’s inventory count on 31 December 2018. (5 marks)

	- focus on high value items - LED chip

- Inventory 3rd party held - subcontractors, separately identified

- - - -
## Solution
The audit team should perform the following procedures during the inventory count:
- Observe whether ELL’s staff are **following instructions** as this will help to ensure the count is complete and accurate.
- Observe whether all the movements and production processes are **suspended** so that there are no movement of the stock items during the inventory count.
- Ensure **procedures to identify damaged**, obsolete and slow moving inventory are executed.
- Obtain ~information on the inventories’ age~, condition and usage, and in case of work-in-progress, its stage of completion to ensure that it is later valued properly.
- Obtain the **last serial numbers** of the goods received and dispatched for the upcoming ~cut-off test~ at the final audit.
- **Conclude** whether the inventory count is properly carried out and is sufficiently reliable as a basis of determining the existence of the inventories. For example, whether the inventories ~held on behalf of third parties~ are separated, whether discrepancies were found during the test count.
- Gain an **overall impression** of the ~levels and values of inventories~ held so that the auditor may, in due course, judge whether the figure of inventory appearing in the financial statements is reasonable.
