# s.12(1)(b): Depreciation allowances on machinery or plant
Depreciation allowances on machinery or plant essentially used by a taxpayer **in the production of assessable income** are allowable.

According to BOR D61/06, the meaning of the word "essential" in s.12(1)(b) of the IRO is consistent with "necessarily" in s.12(1)(a). [[test of necessity]]

In practice, the IRD holds the view that it is the employer's obligation to provide all facilities **essential to the performance of the services of the employee**.

If the employee acquires machinery or plant ::for his own convenience::, no depreciation allowances will be granted for salaries tax purpose.
