# Dividend cover
The dividend cover is the number of times the actual dividend could be paid out of current profits.
![](Journal/Bear%20notes/Doc/Dividend%20cover/0CA94514-5E47-4EAA-A3B8-A0C97262762E.png)

To judge the effect of increased gearing on dividend cover, you should consider changes in the dividend levels and changes in dividend cover. If earnings decrease because of an increased burden of interest payments, then:

(a) The directors may decide to make corresponding reductions in dividend to maintain levels of dividend cover.

(b) Alternatively, the directors may choose to maintain dividend levels, in which case dividend cover will fall. This will indicate to shareholders an increased risk that the company will not be able to maintain the same dividend payments in future years, should earnings fall.
