# s.14D Concessionary profits tax rate for qualifying corporate treasury centres (QCTCs)
[[Qualifying corporate treasury centres (QCTCs)]] can enjoy 50% reduction of the profits tax rate, i.e. 8.25% for the **qualifying income** from [[corporate treasury activities]], which accrued on or after 1 April 2016.

## Election for the concessionary tax rate
An election in writing to apply the **concessionary tax rate** must be made, which is ~irrevocable~ as long as the CTC remains as a QCTC (ss.14D(5)(b) and (6)).

If a CTC which previously qualified as a QCTC **fails to qualify** as a QCTC in a given year of assessment, it will not qualify for the concessionary tax rate for that year of assessment and the subsequent year of assessment despite that it may meet the conditions of a QCTC in the subsequent year of assessment (s.14D(7)). Once disqualified, the election ceases to be effective and the CTC needs to make a fresh election when it qualifies as a QCTC again.

If a QCTC incurs a **tax loss** from corporate treasury operations, it would not be disqualified but the tax loss can only be set off against its other types of profits taxed at half rate; or against other profits taxed at normal rates after making the adjustment as required under ss.19CA and 19CB (discussed in section 13.6 on 'Unabsorbed loss from normal and concessionary trading receipts').
