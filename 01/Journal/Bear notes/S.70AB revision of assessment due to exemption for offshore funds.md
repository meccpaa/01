# S.70AB revision of assessment due to exemption for offshore funds
The exemption is applicable with retrospective effect from the year of assessment 1996/97. An application for revision of a previous year's assessment can be made under `s.70AB` within 
	- (i) 12 months after 10 March 2006 or 
	- (ii) 6 years after the end of the relevant year of assessment, whichever is the later.
