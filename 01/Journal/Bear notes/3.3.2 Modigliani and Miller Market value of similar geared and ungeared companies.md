# 3.3.2 Modigliani and Miller: Market value of similar geared and ungeared companies
Allowing for tax relief on interest, MM argued that the weighted average cost of capital of a company falls as its level of financial gearing increases. It therefore follows that the market value of a geared company will be higher than the market value of a company which is identical in every respect except that it has a lower level of gearing. The difference in value is attributable to the tax shield arising on debt.
