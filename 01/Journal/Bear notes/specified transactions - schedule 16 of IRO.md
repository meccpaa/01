# specified transactions - schedule 16 of IRO
Schedule 16 to the IRO contains the list of 'specified transactions':

(a) A transaction in securities;
(b) A transaction in futures contracts;
(c) A transaction in foreign exchange contracts;
(d) A transaction consisting in the making of a deposit other than by way of a money-lending business;
(e) A transaction in foreign currencies; and
(f) A transaction in exchange-traded commodities.

[Appendix B of DIPN 43 (Revised) sets out the IRD's view on whether particular transactions are covered by the 'specified transactions'. The IRD is of the view that the holding of debentures, loan stocks, bonds or notes to earn interest income is not a transaction in securities since such holding does not involve two transacting parties and cannot be regarded as a transaction. The interest derived therefrom could only be considered as derived from incidental transactions and not specified transactions (DIPN 43 (Revised), para 24).]

The terms 'securities', 'futures contract', 'foreign exchange contract' and 'deposit' are extensively defined in Schedule 16 of the IRO; whereas 'exchange-traded commodity' is narrowly defined to comprise only gold or silver traded on a commodity exchange in Hong Kong.

However, to avoid non-resident persons dealing in underlying assets of a private company (e.g. landed property) through the transaction in shares of that company, shares, debentures and related rights, options etc. of a private company, which is not a special purpose vehicle ('SPV') or an excepted private company ('EPC'), are excluded from the definition of 'securities' under Schedule 16 of the IRO.
