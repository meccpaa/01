# FE (Jun 18) Paper II: Q3(f) cost of debt, WACC
After the discussion with the CFO and FD, the CEO plans to issue additional ordinary shares with the current share price of HK$20 per share. With the issuance of these new shares, the total market value of the equity is HK$60 million. The required return of shares is 10%. At the same time, the Group plans to issue HK$70 million bonds with a 4% annual coupon payment at par. The Group is subject to Hong Kong profits tax at 16.5%.

Calculate the cost of debt and determine the weighted average cost of capital.

(5 marks)
- - - -
[[Cost of capital]]
[[3.3.4 Adjusted cost of capital (WACC)]]

The cost of debt is 4%
The market value of equity is 3M x $20 = $60M
The market value of debt = $70M
The market value of equity and debt = $130M

WACC = (60 / 130) x 0.1 + (70 / 130) x 0.04 x (1 – 0.165)
WACC = 0.0462 + 0.018
WACC = 0.0642
WACC = 6.42%
