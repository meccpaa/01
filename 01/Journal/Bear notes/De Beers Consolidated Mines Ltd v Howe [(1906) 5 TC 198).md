# De Beers Consolidated Mines Ltd v Howe [(1906) 5 TC 198)
it was held that 'a company resides for purposes of income tax where its real business is carried on … and the real business is carried on where the central management and control actually abides'. Therefore, traditionally as a general rule, a company carries on a business in Hong Kong if its central management and control is in Hong Kong, which is usually the place of directors' meeting.
