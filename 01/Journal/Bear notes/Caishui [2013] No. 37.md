# Caishui [2013] No. 37
The Ministry of Finance and the SAT issued Caishui [2013] No. 37 on 24 May 2013. The VAT reform programme for transportation and modern services would be rolled out nationwide starting from 1 August 2013.

Caishui [2013] No. 37 formalizes the national implementation of the VAT reform programme and sets out detailed implementation rules. Caishui [2011] No. 111 has been abolished since 1 August 2013.

Under Caishui [2013] No. 37, the following servies are zero-rated:

- International transportation servies carried out by entities and individuals in China relating to the transport of cargo and passengers from China to overseas, from overseas to China or outside China. An international transportation operation licence is required;
- Transportation services from/to/in Hong Kong, Macau and Taiwan. A transportation operation licence is required;
- Research and development services provided to overseas entities; and 
- Design services provided to overseas entities (excluding design services related to immoveable property in China).

A PRC entity or an individual that provides services eligible for zero-rating can opt to waive such treatment and apply for the VAT exemption treatment. Once the zero-rating treatment is waived, the taxpayer cannot elect for zero-rating for the subsequent 36 months.

Under the zero-rated method, no VAT is charged on the invoice to overseas customers whilst input VAT associated with the services provided is fully recoverable.

Under the VAT-exemption treatment, no VAT is charged on the invoices to overseas customers whilst the input VAT associated is not recoverable.
