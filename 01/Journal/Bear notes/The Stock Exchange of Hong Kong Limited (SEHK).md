# The Stock Exchange of Hong Kong Limited (SEHK)
The SEHK, a wholly-owned subsidiary of HKEx, is a recognised exchange company under the SFO. It operates the stock market in Hong Kong and is the primary regulator of stock exchange participants on trading matters and of companies whose securities are listed on the Main Board and Growth Enterprise Market (GEM) of the Stock Exchange. Companies wishing to become listed companies must apply to the Listing Division of the Stock Exchange and must comply with the Listing Rules of the Exchange.
