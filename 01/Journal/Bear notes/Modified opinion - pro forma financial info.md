# Modified opinion - pro forma financial info
Where relevant law or regulation precludes the publication of a prospectus with a modified opinion but the auditor concludes that a modified opinion is required the practitioner should discuss the situation with the responsible party. If the responsible party does not make the necessary changes the practitioner must:
- Withhold the report 
- Withdraw from the engagement 
- Seek legal advice

Where law or regulation does not preclude the publication of a prospectus with a modified opinion the practitioner should include a modified opinion in accordance with HKSAE 3000(Revised).
