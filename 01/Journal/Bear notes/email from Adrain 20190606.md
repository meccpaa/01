Further to your correspondence with Shane Barber, we've been considering the four issues you've highlighted for "Stage 1" work, and make the following broad initial observations:

Stage 1
1)	Government permit/license requirement for the above intended set up;

Most OTT services in Australia are not subject to specific licensing obligations, and based on the material you have provided, we would expect this would be the case for Viu.

2)	Censorship and consumer protection;

Australia does not impose a strict censorship regime, although programs may be subject to classification (ie, rating for age-appropriateness) in some circumstances.  Australia's consumer protection laws are likely to apply to the service itself (but their application to issues with delivery of OTT services can be limited by the reliance upon Internet for delivery).

3)	Privacy and data collection/use; and

Australia does have a comprehensive privacy regime (although less onerous than the regime in, for example, Europe), and it will likely be of relevance to this service.

4)	Any advantage for operating on onshore mode (setting up a local entity there).

Because "carrying on business" in Australia will require a foreign company to register with, and report to, Australia's corporate regulator in any event, as a practical matter the advantages of setting up an Australian business can outweigh conducting a business from overseas (particularly in the longer term).
