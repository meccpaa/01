# Additional statement of financial position
HKAS 1 (Revised) requires that a statement of financial position is presented as at the beginning of the preceding period when an entity:

(i) Applies an accounting policy **retrospectively**, makes a retrospective restatement of items in its financial statements or reclassifies items in its financial statements, and

(ii) The retrospective application or restatement or the reclassification has a **material** effect on the information in the statement of financial position at the beginning of the preceding period. In effect, this will result in the presentation of three statements of financial position when there is a material prior period adjustment: one at the end of the current period, one at the end of the preceding period and one at the start of the preceding period.
