# s.79(3) tax overpaid by non-resident persons
Pursuant to s.79(3), when a non-resident has been assessed in the name of another person under ss.20A or s.20B and the tax has been paid by the other person, either that other person or the nonresident person may claim repayment of any tax overpaid (see DIPN 17 (Revised), para15).
