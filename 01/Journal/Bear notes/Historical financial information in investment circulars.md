# Historical financial information in investment circulars
HKSIR 200 Accountants' Reports on Historical Financial Information in Investment Circulars is effective for engagements where the investment circular is dated on or after 1 July 2017 (although early application is permitted).

The key objective of HKSIR 200 Accountants' Reports on Historical Financial Information in Investment Circulars is to establish specific standards and provide guidance for reporting accountants engaged to issue an accountant's report on historical financial information for inclusion in an investment circular. Including an accountant's report in a prospectus is required by the Companies Ordinance and the Listing Rules and GEM Rules also list circumstances where an accountant's report is a requirement.

An accountant's report on historical financial information should contain an clear expression of opinion on the historical financial statements. Appendix One of HKSIR 200 provides examples of an Accountant's Report on Historical Financial Information in Investment Circulars.
