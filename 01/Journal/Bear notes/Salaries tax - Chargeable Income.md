# Salaries tax - Chargeable Income
![](Salaries%20tax%20-%20Chargeable%20Income/image.png)
S.9 gives an **inclusive definition** of income that is chargeable to salaries tax. S.9(1)(a) covers the **[[General emoluments s.9(1)(a)]]** that are chargeable to salaries tax.

S.9(1)(a): Any wages, salary, leave pay, fee, commission, bonus, gratuity, perquisite, or allowance, whether derived from the employer or others, — subject to subsection (2A), any amount paid by the employer to or for the credit of a person other than the employee in ~discharge of a sole and primary liability of the employer~ to that other person, not being a liability for which any person was surety.

## Items specifically chargeable to salaries tax
[[retirement benefits]] s.9(1)(aa), (ab), (ac), (ad), (ae) | ::excl. s.8(2)(c), (cb), (cc)::
[[Accommodation benefits]] s.9(1)(b), (c) | ::excl. 9(1A)(a)::
[[Employee share-based benefits]]  s.9(1)(d)  | ::excl. s.9(5)::
- - - -
Amounts included in ss.9(1)(a)-(d) are assessable except if paid by employer to a third party to discharge employer's liability unless specifically assessable under s.9(2A).
- - - -
S.9(2A) specifically states that the following items are not to be excluded from income from any office or employment:
	- (a) Any benefit capable of being converted into money by the recipient.  [[Other fringe benefits]]
	- (b) Any amount paid by an employer in connection with the education of a child of an employee. [[Education benefits]]
	- (c) Any amount paid by an employer in connection with a holiday journey. [[Holiday journey benefits]]
