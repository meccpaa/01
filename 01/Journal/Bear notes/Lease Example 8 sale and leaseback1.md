# Lease Example 8: sale and leaseback1
Osborne Co. owned a property with a carrying amount of $8,400,000 on 1 March 20X8. On this date Osborne Co. sold the property to a bank for $13,200,000, being the fair value of the asset, and then undertook to lease it back under a 20-year lease. Annual lease payments were $400,000 in arrears and the present value of future lease payments at the 6% interest rate implicit in the lease equaled $4,588,000 at 1 March 20X8.

The transfer qualifies as a sale and Osborne's accounting reference date is 28 February.

Required

How should the transaction be accounted for by Osborne Co. in the year ended 28 February 20X9?
- - - -
Solution

Part of the carrying amount of the asset is allocated to be a right-of-use asset retained. This is calculated based on the right-of-use asset (lease liability) as a proportion of fair value:
![](Lease%20Example%208%20sale%20and%20leaseback1/A947EC28-DEB7-4872-8D4C-54682F9B77FF.png)
The remaining carrying amount of $5,480,364 (8,400,000 - 2,919,636) represents the transferred asset.

The **overall gain** on disposal is $4,800,000 (13,200,000 - 8,400,000); only that part of the gain relating to the transferred asset is recognised:
![](Lease%20Example%208%20sale%20and%20leaseback1/7C306EE2-DDBD-4A3F-A589-A50AD2044B46.png)
Therefore the **recognised gain** relating to the ~transferred rights~ is $3,131,636 (4,800,000 - 1,668,364).
