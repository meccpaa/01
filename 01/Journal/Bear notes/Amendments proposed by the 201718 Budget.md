# Amendments proposed by the 2017/18 Budget
Under the 2017/18 Budget, the following tax measures were proposed:
- Reduce profits tax, salaries tax and tax under personal assessment for 2016/17 by 75%, subject to a ceiling of $20,000 per case.
- Increase the width of marginal tax bands from $40,000 to $45,000 from 2017/18. See section 5.2 above for details.
- Increase the disabled dependant allowance from $66,000 to $75,000 and the dependent brother or dependent sister allowance from $33,000 to $37,500 from 2017/18 and onwards.
- Raise the deduction ceiling for self-education expenses from $80,000 to $100,000 from 2017/18 and onwards.
- Extend the entitlement period for home loan interest deduction from 15 to 20 years of assessment from 2017/18 and onwards.
- A rate waiver for all four quarters, subject to a cap of HKD1,000 per quarter, for each rateable property.

The Inland Revenue (Amendment) Bill 2017 was passed by the Legislative Council on 25 May 2017 to give effect to the tax measures proposed in the 2017/18 Budget.
