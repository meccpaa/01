# general approach
Under the general approach:
At initial recognition —> recognise 12-month [[expected credit losses]]

After initial recognition, a **~three stage approach~** is taken:
**Stage 1**
[[Credit risk]] has ~not increased~ significantly since initial recognition —> recognise **12-month** expected credit losses

**Stage 2**
[[significantly increased credit risk]] since initial recognition —>
- recognise **lifetime** expected credit losses
- ~interest~ calculated on ~gross~ amount of asset

**Stage 3**
There is objective evidence of impairment at the reporting date *(including the significant financial difficulty of the borrower anßßd breach of contract.)* —>
- recognise **lifetime** expected credit losses
- ~interest~ calculated on ~net~ amount of asset ![](general%20approach/bear_sketch@2x.png)#QP/A/Fin-Rpt/FI#
