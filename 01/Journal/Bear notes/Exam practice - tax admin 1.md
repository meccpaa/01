# Exam practice - tax admin 1
## Mr. Brown
18 minutes

Mr. Brown, a property agent, advises you that he is being investigated by the IRD. Since he is unable to substantiate his business expenses amounting to $300,000, he has been required by the IRD to pay tax of $48,000 under an additional assessment.

Required

(a) Explain the difference between additional assessment and additional tax. (3 marks)

(b) Explain the difference between tax avoidance and tax evasion. (3 marks)

(c) Advise Mr. Brown on the possible consequences if the IRD takes legal action against him under s.82(1). (4 marks)

   (Total = 10 marks)
