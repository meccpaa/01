# perquisite
The general commercial understanding of what constitutes a ‘[[perquisite]]’ is relatively wide, including any emolument or reward of value (i.e. benefits in kind) to a person in addition to salaries and wages.

Following the decision of the Privy Council in CIR v David Hardy Glynn [(1990) 1 HKRC 90-032], there was a change to the IRO in the year 1991 to limit the scope of charge on taxable emoluments.
