# stamp duty Example 2
E sells the shares in C Ltd to D. C Ltd owns a property with a market value of $5 million and an outstanding mortgage of $2 million. D agrees to acquire the shares in C Ltd by paying E $3 million and undertaking repayment of the $2 million liability.

Stamp duty payable on the conveyance will be based on $5 million, being cash paid plus value of the debt assigned.
