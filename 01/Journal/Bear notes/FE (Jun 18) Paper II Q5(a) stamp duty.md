# FE (Jun 18) Paper II: Q5(a) stamp duty
(a) Upon the disposal of the shares of WT Tours, advise (i) the amount of stamp duty payable; (ii) the timing of stamping if the transfer takes place outside of Hong Kong; (iii) the penalty for late stamping if it is late by 1.5 months; and (iv) the penalty for late stamping if it is late by one year.

(4 marks)
- - - -
[[FE (Jun 18) Paper II: case]]
## Solutions
(i) 0.2% of the higher of the consideration or market value of the shares is payable on the bought note (0.1%) and sold note (0.1%).

In addition, HK$5 is payable on the instrument of transfer.

(ii) For the transfer of shares outside Hong Kong, stamping should be made within 30 days from the date of transfer.

(iii) Four times the stamp duty is payable as the penalty for late stamping exceeding one month but not more than two months.

(iv) Ten times the stamp duty is payable as the penalty for late stamping ~exceeding two months~.
