# Profits tax - Example 8 - services - associated agent
Adapted from [[DIPN 21 (Revised)]]

A Hong Kong company holds the Far East Area sales or purchase responsibility for a product or group of products sold into the area or sourced in the area by principals who are associated concerns. The Hong Kong company and the associates are **members of a group under the control of a common parent organisation**. The Hong Kong company is ~appointed agent~ for the area, and is remunerated by a ~commission~ on all sales and/or purchases in the area. The Hong Kong company may either:

(a) Actively solicit orders ex-Hong Kong, on behalf of its principals by sending employee representatives overseas or by employing sub-agents overseas; or

(b) Factually do nothing whatsoever, either itself or through sub-agents.
