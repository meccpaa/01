# CoC Example: Cost of convertible debt
A company has issued 8% convertible bonds which are due to be redeemed in five years' time. They are currently quoted at $820 per $1,000 nominal. The bonds can be converted into 25 shares in five years' time. The share price is currently $35 and is expected to grow at a rate of 3% pa. Assume a 16.5% rate of tax.

Calculate the cost of the convertible debt.
