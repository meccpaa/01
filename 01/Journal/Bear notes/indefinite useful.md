# indefinite useful
An intangible asset with an indefinite useful life **should not be amortised**, instead HKAS 36 requires that such an asset is tested for impairment at least annually.

The useful life of an intangible asset that is not being amortised should be **reviewed each year** to determine whether it is ~still appropriate~ to assess its useful life as indefinite. Reassessing the useful life of an intangible asset as finite rather than indefinite is an indicator that the asset may be ::impaired:: and in these circumstances it should be tested for impairment as well as being amortised over its remaining life.
