# subsequent measurement of goodwill
**Subsequent accounting**

**Purchased [[positive goodwill]]** is retained in the statement of financial position as an intangible asset under the requirements of HKFRS 3 (revised). It must then be ~reviewed for impairment annually~.
* Initial: recognised as an asset and is initially measured at cost
* Subsequent: at cost less any accumulated impairment losses

A **[[bargain purchase]]** is reassessed. If a bargain purchase remains, it is recognised as a ~gain in profit or loss~.
