## Method of investment appraisal
The payback method of investment appraisal and the ROCE/ARR/ROI methods of investment appraisal are popular appraisal techniques despite their limitations (of which you should be aware).

1. [[The Payback period]] - timing cash inflow = cash outflow
2. [[Return on capital employed (ROCE)]] avg annual profit / avg investment ::(capital cost + disposal value) /2:: - ::accounting not cash flow::
3. [[discounted cash flow (DCF)]]
	- [[NPV and IRR compared]] - IRR easier to understand; NPV more flexible and superior
