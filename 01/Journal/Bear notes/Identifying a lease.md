# Identifying a lease
HKFRS 16.9,10, B9, B13-14, B20
A contract is, or contains, a lease if the contract conveys the right to control the use of an [[identified asset]] [[for a period of time - lease]] in exchange for consideration.
	- [[right to control an asset]]
		- 1. [[right to obtain substantially all economic benefits]] from the use of the asset, and
		- 2. [[right to direct use]] of the asset.
	- [[identified asset]] - identified, physically distinct / all the capacity, 
		- ::[[supplier’s substantive right to substitute an identified asset]]:: 
	- [[for a period of time - lease]] - time or units use
		- [[lease term]]
