# obtain written representations and documentation
`HKSA 450.14-15`
The auditor shall ~request a written representation~ from management and, where appropriate, those charged with governance whether they believe the **effects of uncorrected misstatements are immaterial**, individually and in aggregate, to the financial statements as a whole. A summary of such items shall be included in or attached to the written representation.

The auditor shall include in the ~audit documentation~:
- The ~amount~ below which misstatements would be regarded as ~clearly unimportant~
- All ~misstatements accumulated~ during the audit and ~whether they have been corrected~
- The auditor's ~conclusion~ as to whether uncorrected misstatements are **material**, individually or in aggregate, and the basis for that conclusion
