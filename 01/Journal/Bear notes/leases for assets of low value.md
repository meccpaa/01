# leases for assets of low value
Criteria: 
- Low value is not defined. The Basis for Conclusions of HKFRS 16 refers to assets with a value of **US$5,000 or less**.
- The ~value of the asset when new~ is the relevant value.
- An asset is ~assessed individually~; simplified accounting may be applied to ~several identical low-value assets~ that together have a high-value.
- An asset can only be low-value if it can be ~used alone~ or ~with other available resources~ and isn't dependent on another asset.

Election: on a lease-by-lease basis
Accounting treatment: Lease payments are recognised on a straight-line basis over the lease term.
