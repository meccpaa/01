# methods of elimination of double taxation - Article 21 of CHD
Article 21 of China-HK DTA provides that **taxes paid** on One Side shall be allowed as **a credit against taxes payable on the Other Side** in respect of the same item of income. However, the amount of ::tax credit shall not exceed the amount of tax computed:: in respect of that income in accordance with the taxation laws and regulations of the resident's home jurisdiction.
