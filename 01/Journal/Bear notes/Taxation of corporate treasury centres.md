# Taxation of corporate treasury centres
A corporate treasury centre ('CTC') is an in-house bank within a group. A typical CTC performs the functions of:
(a) Intra-group financing;
(b) Multi-currency cash management and liquidity management;
(c) Cash pooling;
(d) Central or regional processing of payments to vendors or suppliers;
(e) Conducting transactions for financial or treasury-related risk management; and
(f) Supporting the raising of capital by the group.

To attract multinational and Mainland enterprises to establish global or regional CTCs in Hong Kong, the Inland Revenue (Amendment) (No. 2) Ordinance 2016 was enacted on 3 June 2016 to:
(a) Provide a [[s.14D Concessionary profits tax rate for qualifying corporate treasury centres (QCTCs)]];
(b) Introduce new [[s.16(2)(g) deduction rules for interest expenses incurred by intra-group financing businesses]];  and
(c) Introduce new [[s.15(1)(ia) deeming provisions for interest income and other gains from intra-group financing businesses]].

The IRD issued DIPN 52 in September 2016 to provide guidance on the application of the profits tax provisions in relation to CTCs.
