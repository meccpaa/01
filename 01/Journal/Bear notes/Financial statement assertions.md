# Financial statement assertions
![](Financial%20statement%20assertions/image.png)
**Audit procedures** are designed to obtain evidence about the financial statement [[assertion]]s. Assertions relate to classes of transactions and events, account balances at the period-end, and presentation and disclosure.

> HKSA 315.4a - Assertions are representations by management, explicit or otherwise, that are embodied in the financial statements, as used by the auditors to consider the different types of potential misstatements that may occur.   

The auditor will carry out procedures that are designed to ~test the assertions~ made by management. HKSA 315 (Revised 2016) identifies these as follows:
