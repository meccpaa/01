# Disclosure of a change in accounting estimate
Where there is a change in accounting estimate, an entity must disclose the **nature and amount** of the change that has an effect in the current period or is expected to have an effect in future periods, unless it is ::impracticable:: to estimate that effect.

If the amount of the effect in future periods is not disclosed because estimating it is impracticable, that fact must be disclosed.
