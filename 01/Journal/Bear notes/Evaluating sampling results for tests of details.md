# Evaluating sampling results for tests of details
Any ::unexpected high misstatements:: found in a **sample** 
1. may cause the auditor to believe that a class of transactions or an account balance is ~materially misstated~. 
2. The auditor should **consider** whether the sample results provide a ~reasonable basis~ for conclusions about the population and 
3. should **further consider** the likeliness of actual misstatement in the population.

The auditor should 
1. consider the results of **other audit procedures** in order to assess the risk of actual misstatements in the population. 
2. The auditor may further request management to investigate the identified misstatements and consider whether management shall make any necessary adjustments. 
3. In addition, the auditor shall reconsider the nature, extent and timeliness of further audit procedures.
