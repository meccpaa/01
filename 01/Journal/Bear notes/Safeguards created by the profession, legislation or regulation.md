# Safeguards created by the profession, legislation or regulation
Examples of safeguards created by the profession, legislation or regulation:
(a) Educational training and experience requirements for entry into the profession
(b) Continuing professional development requirements
(c) [[Corporate Governance Code]]
(d) Professional standards
(e) Professional or regulatory monitoring and disciplinary procedures
(f) External review by a legally empowered third party of the reports, returns, communication or information produced by a professional accountant

HKICPA issues ethical standards, quality control standards and auditing standards which work together to ensure independence is safeguarded and quality audits are carried out.
