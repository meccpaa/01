# Objection and holdover - Nam Tai Group Management Limited v CIR (2011) DCTC 458
The CIR claimed against the taxpayer for profits tax due and payable under s.75 of the IRO. The taxpayer's defence was that the CIR should have exercised his discretion properly when deciding whether to holdover the tax pending objection, and since he did not, the tax should not be payable.

It was held that the taxpayer did not have a reasonable defence, which was struck out accordingly.
