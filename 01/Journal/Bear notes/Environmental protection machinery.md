# Environmental protection machinery
'Environmental protection machinery' means (s.16H(1) and Part 1 of Schedule 17):

(a) Low noise construction machinery or plant registered under the system administered by the Environmental Protection Department ('EPD');
(b) Air pollution control machinery or plant in compliance with the requirements under the Air Pollution Control Ordinance;
(c) Waste treatment machinery or plant in compliance with the requirements under the Waste Disposal Ordinance; and
(d) Waste water treatment machinery or plant in compliance with the requirements under the Water Pollution Control Ordinance.
