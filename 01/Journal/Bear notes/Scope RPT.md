# Scope: RPT
`HKAS 24.3`
- related party transactions and outstanding balances are to be presented in **separate financial statements** of a parent, venturer or investor as well as in consolidated financial statements. Disclosures should be in accordance with HKAS 27 and HKFRS 10.
- Related party transactions and outstanding balances with other entities in the group are to be disclosed in an entity's financial statements. However, ::no intragroup:: transactions and balances except for those between an investment entity and its subsidiaries are to be shown as they are eliminated in the preparation of consolidated financial statements.
- An investment entity is a particular type of parent company defined in Chapter 26.
