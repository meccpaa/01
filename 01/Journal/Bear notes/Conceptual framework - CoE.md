# Conceptual framework - CoE
The conceptual framework in the Code requires a professional accountant to identify, evaluate and address [[threats to compliance with the fundamental principles]].

A professional accountant has an obligation to evaluate any threats to compliance with the [[Fundamental principles - CoE]]. They should take into account both qualitative and quantitative factors when considering the significance of a threat.

When the threats are identified and the threats are clearly **significant**, a professional accountant should where appropriate, apply **[[safeguards - CoE]]** to eliminate the threats or reduce them to an ~acceptable level.~

A professional accountant should **decline or discontinue** the service if no safeguards can be implemented.

A professional accountant shall use professional judgment in applying this conceptual framework.
