# Productivity measures
In many organisations, particularly those in manufacturing industries, productivity is an important measure. **Productivity is a measure of output per labour hour or output per machine hour**.

An organisation may have a target for productivity in its operations. Productivity measures output (in units, standard hours of work or sales revenue) per unit of resource – typically, per labour hour worked or per machine hour worked. Actual productivity can be compared with target productivity , and the reasons for any differences can be analysed.
