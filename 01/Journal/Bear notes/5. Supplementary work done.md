# 5. Supplementary work done
If there is work done to enhance the value of the asset to make it more marketable, it is likely that a trade is being carried on.

In Cape Brandy Syndicate v IRC [(1921) 12 TC 358], the taxpayers purchased a quantity of brandy which was blended and re-casked and then disposed of in lots. It was held that there was a trade carried on by the taxpayers.
