# FE Dec 19 Paper II Section A - 3 Revenue
**With reference to the relevant accounting standards, advise Mr Paul Lee, the Finance Director, on the revenue recognition in relation to the sale of 2,000 dining tables under the three proposals. You are only required to focus on the following steps of the five–step model:**
* **(i) Step 3: Determine the transaction price.**
* **(ii) Step 5: Recognise revenue when (or as) the entity satisfies a performance obligation.**
- - - -
## Answer
### Step 3: Determine the transaction price 
In accordance with HKFRS 15, the consideration from GAL are considered as **variable consideration** which is estimated by considering the expected return for all of the three proposals and the **restocking fee** for the items expected to be returned under Proposal Z: 

The Group then considered the requirement on constraining estimates of variable consideration to determine whether the estimated amount can be included in the transaction price and the cap on products return represented the variable component of the consideration. 

Proposal X – The Group first estimated the consideration as HK$9,000,000 (HK$10,000,000 x 90% or HK$5,000 x 1,800 tables). Given the expected return rate (10%) is the same as the return cap (i.e. maximum amount of the products could be returned), the transaction price for the contract with GAL is HK$9,000,000. 

Proposal Y – The Group first estimated the consideration as HK$9,450,000 (HK$10,500,000 x 90% or HK$5,250 x 1,800 tables), considering the expected return rate is 10%. The Group has no previous working relationship with GAL, and the Group does not have any previous experience in estimating returns; and the uncertainty would not be resolved within a very short time frame (180 days); the Group might conclude it is unable to assert that it is highly probable that a significant reversal in the cumulative amount of revenue recognised will not occur. Therefore, the excess portion of 20% (return cap of 30% – expected return rate of 10%) might need to be constrained and the transaction price would then be HK$7,350,000 (HK$9,450,000 – HK$10,500,000 x 20%). 

Proposal Z – The Group first estimated the consideration as HK$9,120,000 [HK$8,820,000 (HK$9,800,000 x 90% or HK$4,900 x 1,800 tables) + HK$300,000 (HK$1,500 x 200 tables)]. As with Proposal X, the transaction price would be HK$9,120,000 given the expected return rate is the same as return cap. 

### Step 5: Recognise revenue when (or as) the entity satisfies a performance obligation 
#### Revenue recognition 
Since none of the criteria under para.35 is met in relation to the sales of dining tables to GAL, revenue would be recognised at a point in time (measured at the transaction price above) when the control of the products are passed to GAL, which is assessed upon the products being delivered to the customers. 
#### Refund liabilities 
Furthermore, a refund liability would be recorded for the amounts that the Group does not expect to be entitled due to the existence of product returns. 

- Proposal X – HK$1,000,000 (HK$10,000,000 x 10% or HK$5,000 x 200 tables) 
- Proposal Y – HK$3,150,000 (HK$10,500,000 x 30% or HK$5,250 x 600 tables) 
- Proposal Z – HK$680,000 (HK$4,900 - HK$1,500) x 200 tables

#### Assets for its right to recover products from customers on settling the refund liabilities 
The assets shall initially be measured by reference to the former carrying amount of the products (HK$6,000,000 / 2,000 tables = HK$3,000 per table) less any expected costs to recover those products (including potential decreases in the value to the entity of returned products) (HK$600), and shall present the asset separately from the refund liability. 

The corresponding adjustment would be made to cost of sales. 
* Proposal X – HK$480,000 ((HK$3,000 - HK$600) x 200 tables) 
* Proposal Y – HK$1,440,000 ((HK$3,000 - HK$600) x 600 tables) 
* Proposal Z – HK$480,000 ((HK$3,000 - HK$600) x 200 tables)
