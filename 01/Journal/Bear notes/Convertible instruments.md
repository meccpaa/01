# Convertible instruments
## 1.6.6 EPS
Convertible loan stock may be dilutive or anti-dilutive. In order to ascertain which the case is, a stand-alone earnings per share amount should be calculated as:

(Post tax interest saved on conversion of loan stock) /  (Maximum number of potential ordinary shares in respect of the stock)

If the amount calculated is less than basic earnings per share, the loan stock is dilutive and should be included in diluted earnings per share.
