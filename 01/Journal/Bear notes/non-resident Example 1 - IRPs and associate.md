# non-resident Example 1 - IRPs and associate
A Ltd develops and owns a trade mark in Hong Kong. A Ltd intends to sell the trade mark to B Ltd, a non-resident, for $10 million and then license it back for use in its manufacturing business carried on in Hong Kong at $1 million per annum.

### The Hong Kong tax implications are as follows:
Any gain/loss on disposal of the trade mark made by A Ltd is capital in nature and therefore will not be taxable/deductible. The license fee paid by A Ltd will be tax deductible as it is incurred in the production of assessable profits.

B Ltd will be deemed to have derived a profit from licensing the trade mark for use in Hong Kong under s.15(1)(b).

::If the deemed profit is 30% of the payment, A Ltd and B Ltd as a whole will enjoy a tax benefit of $115,500 ($1m x 16.5% – $1m x 30% x 16.5% = $115,500).::  —> wrong (deductible cost > assessable profits)

If the deemed profit is 100% of the payment, A Ltd and B Ltd as a whole will get **no tax benefit** from the arrangement ($1m x 16.5% – $1m x 100% x 16.5% = Nil).
