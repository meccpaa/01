# Construction of s.64(1) and s.70A - Good Mark Industrial Limited v Commissioner of Inland Revenue [HCAL 88/2012][CACV 90/2014}
Tax return for 2002/03 was submitted by the taxpayer to the IRD. The original assessment was issued in September 2004 based on the tax return filed. An additional assessment was issued in January 2005 disallowing certain bad debts. No objection was raised by the taxpayer against the Sep 2004 and the Jan 2005 assessments. The IRD made an additional assessment for 2003/04 in March 2010. On 15 April 2010, the applicant notified the IRD to object the March 2010 assessments for 2003/04 and reopen the previous 2 assessments for 2003/04 based on s.70A.

The Commissioner refused to entertain the s.70A claim in respect of the Sep 2004 assessment and the Jan 2005 assessment. Good Mark applied for judicial review. The judge of the CFI refused the application.

Good Mark appealed to the Court of Appeal. However, the Court of Appeal upheld the CFI's decision
