# Lease Example 1: Identifying a lease
On 1 March 20X7 Schmitt Retail Co (SRC) enters into a ~three-year contract~ [[for a period of time - lease]] with a property owner to use **a retail unit** in a shopping centre for a five-year period. SRC can ~make all decisions about the use of the unit~ during the period of use, including the mix of goods sold and quantity of inventories held. SRC also ~controls physical access to the unit~ when the shopping centre is open. The ::property owner:: provides ~maintenance and security services~ throughout the term of the agreement. **SRC** is required to ~make fixed payments~ to the property owner as well as ~a variable amount~ that is a percentage of sales made from the unit.

During the three years of the agreement, the property owner can require SRC to ::relocate to another unit:: in the same shopping centre. In that case the property owner must provide SRC with a ~unit of similar quality~ and specifications to the original unit and must ~pay SRC's relocation costs~. SRC would **benefit economically** from relocating SRC only if a major new tenant decided to occupy a large amount of retail space at a premium rate. At the inception of the contract it is ::possible, but not likely,:: that those circumstances will arise.

Explain whether the contract is a lease.
- - - -
## Solution
### Right to control - substantially all economic benefits
SRC has the right to ~obtain substantially all economic benefits~ over the three-year period of use. The fact that SRC is required to pay a variable amount to the property owner that is linked to sales does not prevent SRC from having the right to obtain substantially all economic benefits.
### Right to control - direct the use
SRC makes the relevant decisions about ~how and for what purpose~ the retail unit is used and it has the ~right to change~ these decisions during the three-year term. The fact that SRC can only access the unit ::when the shopping centre is open:: does not affect this assessment; SRC can direct the use of the unit ~within the parameters of the contract~.
### Identified asset
The ~retail unit~ is the identified asset. The property company has ::the right to substitute the asset::, however, this is not a substantive right as, at the inception of the contract, the circumstances in which the property company could benefit economically from substitution are **not likely** to arise.
### Period of time
The period of time is specified as three years.
