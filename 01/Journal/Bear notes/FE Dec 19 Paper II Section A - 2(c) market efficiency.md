# FE Dec 19 Paper II Section A - 2(c) market efficiency
**(c)  The Company is listed on the Hong Kong Stock Exchange; and there are a lot of factors that affect the daily movements of the share price. Discuss and comment with reasons about the form of market efficiency of the Hong Kong stock market.**
**(3 marks)**
- - - -
## Answer
The Hong Kong stock market has a semi-strong form of efficiency in which share prices reflect all past and publicly available information about a company. 

It is observed that share prices traded in the Hong Kong stock market continuously react to both past and present public information quickly due to efficient trading system, low trading cost, analysts and investors conduct research on companies, and high level of transparency (i.e. free flow and easy access of company information).
