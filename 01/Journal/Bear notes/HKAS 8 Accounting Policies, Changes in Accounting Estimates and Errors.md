# HKAS 8 Accounting Policies, Changes in Accounting Estimates and Errors
![](HKAS%208%20Accounting%20Policies,%20Changes%20in%20Accounting%20Estimates%20and%20Errors/E2068684-0C64-4B44-813A-00CBFC15E67D.png)
HKAS 8 deals with the treatment of changes in accounting estimates, changes in accounting policies and errors.
- - - -
## 1.1 Objective
The objective of HKAS 8 is to prescribe:
- The criteria for selecting and changing accounting policies
- The accounting treatment and disclosure of:
	- Changes in [[Accounting policies]],  - (retrospectively)
	- Changes in [[Accounting estimates]], and  (prospectively)
	- [[Corrections of errors]]. (Retrospectively)

The standard is intended to enhance the relevance and reliability of an entity's financial statements, and the comparability of those financial statements over time and with the financial statements of other entities.

The standard was extensively revised in October 2008. The new title reflects the fact that the material on determining profit or loss for the period has been transferred to HKAS 1.
