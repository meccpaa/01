# Key audit matters HKSA 701
**Listed company** auditor's report includes a description of the key audit matters.

Key audit matters are those matters that, in the auditor's professional judgment, were of **most significance** in the audit of the financial statements of the ~current period~. Key audit matters are selected from matters ~communicated with those charged with governance~. 
- - - -
3.1 [[Key audit matters]]

### The auditor's objectives are as follows.
The objectives of the auditor are to **determine** key audit matters and, having formed an **opinion** on the financial statements, to **communicate** those matters by describing them in the auditor's report.

KAMs are communicated after the opinion. This is because the reported KAMs ~do not include matters which have resulted in a modified opinion~ – any explanations in relation to these issues would already have been included in the 'Basis for' modified opinion paragraph.

The auditor must do four main things:
	1. Determine the matters which should be described as KAMs 
		- 3.2 [[determining KAMs]] 
		- 3.3 [[choosing not to include a KAM]]
	2. 3.4 [[communicating KAMs in auditor’s report]]
		- KAMs vs auditor’s opinion
		- [[HKSA 570 (Revised) Going concern]] - not as a KAM
		- [[modified opinion]] - not in KAM
		- [[KAM vs Other Matter paragraphs]] - not overlap, one related to FS, the other not
		- [[KAM vs Emphasis of Matter]] - need to draw users’ attention, but not significant auditor attention
	3. [[Communicate the KAMs to those charged with governance]] 
	4. Keep appropriate [[audit documentation - KAMs]]
