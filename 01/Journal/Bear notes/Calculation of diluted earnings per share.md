# Calculation of diluted earnings per share
Diluted earnings per share is calculated as:

(Profits in basic EPS + effect on profit of dilutive potential ordinary shares) / (Number of shares in basic EPS + dilutive potential ordinary shares)

This calculation is achieved in steps, with each group of potentially dilutive ordinary shares added in turn from the most dilutive to the least dilutive. Options and warrants are generally included first as they do not affect the profits part of the calculation.

After each addition, diluted EPS is calculated and the diluted earnings per share is the lowest figure calculated at any stage in the sequence.
