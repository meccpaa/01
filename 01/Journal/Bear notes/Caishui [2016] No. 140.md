# Caishui [2016] No. 140
In addition to the above, on 22 December 2016, the Ministry of Finance and the SAT jointly issued Caishui [2016] No.140 clarifying various industry issues for the financial services, real estate, modern services and consumer services. The major provisions in Caishui [2016] No.140 took retrospective effect from 1 May 2016.

Salient points of Caishui [2016] No.140 are as follows:

### Financial services industry

- Asset management products held to maturity do not fall into the categories of trading of financial products.
- The manager of asset management is the VAT payer in connection with VAT taxable activities.

### Real estate industry

- The deduction scope for VAT was expanded. Land price includes land requisition and relocation compensation, early stage land development costs and land grant fee paid to the government.
- Relocation compensation paid to entities and individuals can also be deducted from the sales amount.

### Consumer service industry
- Sale of take away food by taxpayers in catering business would be subject to VAT at 6%.
- The classification of VAT taxable category should be aligned with the underlying economic activities. For example, when a taxpayer provides conference venue and supporting services at the same time, it should pay VAT under the category of convention and exhibition services instead of leasing of real property.
