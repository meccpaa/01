# Lease Example 4: variable payments linked to a rate
On 1 January 20X8, Weir Kitching Co (WKC) entered into a lease agreement to obtain use of an asset for five years, although WKC may terminate the lease after 3 years in exchange for payment of $35,000. Lease payments are required annually in advance; the first payment on 1 January 20X8 is $20,000 and payments in future years increase by the rate of HIBOR (1.5% at 1 January 20X8). WKC expects to exercise the option to terminate the lease early. The interest rate implicit in the lease is 4%. The asset has a fair value of $210,000 at the inception of the lease.

Required

How is the lease liability measured?

- - - -
## Solution
WKC expects to exercise the option to terminate the lease early and therefore the lease term is three years.

The lease liability is measured at the start of the lease term based on the current HIBOR of 1.5%, and lease payments (including the termination penalty) are discounted at 4%.
