# sole or dominant purpose
'Sole or dominant purpose' is not defined in the IRO. 'Sole' is defined in the Oxford Dictionary as 'one and only' and 'dominant' is defined as 'occupying a commanding position'. A sole purpose is therefore the only purpose for entering into a transaction or an arrangement whereas a dominant purpose is an 'overwhelming' purpose (not just the main or principal purpose).
