# Obtaining evidence about the appropriateness of the pro forma adjustments
The practitioner is required to assess whether the pro forma adjustments illustrate the impact of the event or transaction and whether they are in accordance with the applicable criteria. The practitioner must determine whether the adjustments are:

(a) Directly attributable to the event or transaction

(b) Factually supportable

(c) Consistent with the applicable financial reporting framework
