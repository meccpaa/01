# FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Q1 sales incentive
You are Sean Chan, the Finance Manager of SSHI; advise Steven Lee, the CFO, on the accounting for the slotting fee of HK$2 million and the design and renovation fee of HK$4 million on the consolidated financial statements of SSHI, with reference to the relevant accounting standards.
[[FE (Dec 18) Paper I: Case]]
(7 marks)
- - - -
## Answer
### Slotting fee of HK$2 million
The Slotting fee is paid for the favourable product placement service. Generally the Slotting fee **does not provide a distinct good or service** to the Company as the Company would ~not obtain any rights or receive any benefit from the product placement services without selling the products~ to the customers (supermarkets and pharmacy stores).

The Slotting fee, in substance, represents a ::sales incentive:: made to the customers and should be treated as a **reduction of the transaction price** for sales to customers (i.e. revenue).

Given that the Slotting fee is **non-refundable** in nature and the customers (supermarkets and pharmacy stores) are not committed to make any future purchases with the Group after the first sales order, the Slotting fee should be **netted off against the revenue upon the first sales order** ::instead of capitalised:: the amount as a prepaid item.

- - - -
### Design and renovation fee of HK$4 million
Similar to the Slotting fee, the design and renovation fee ::may be considered as a sales incentive:: made to customers and is treated as a reduction of the transaction price for sales to customers (i.e. revenue).

Having considered (i) the customers are required to commit to a **minimum sales quantity** before the Company would sponsor the design and renovation fee; and (ii) the customers are **required to refund** to the Company if the customer failed to meet the sales target, the design and renovation fee `may be capitalised as a prepaid incentive and amortised `based on the pattern of the **agreed minimum sales targets** over three years.

The carrying amount of the prepaid design and renovation fee (prepaid sales incentive) represent the unamortised portion of the design and renovation fee less impairment, if any.

Should the customers fail to meet the sales target and subsequent refund is received by the Group, the respective portion of the prepaid incentive would be reduced with a corresponding increase in cash receipt. In addition, the Group shall also consider whether there any impairment allowance is necessary for the unamortised portion of the prepaid incentive, if any.
