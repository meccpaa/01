# a reasonable basis for conclusions about the population
The auditor may perform the following **procedures** when he concludes that audit sampling has ::not provided a reasonable basis:: for conclusions about the population tested:
	- (a) **Request management** to ~investigate~ identified misstatements, potential misstatements and to make necessary  ~adjustments~
	- (b) Modify nature, extent and timing of **further audit procedures** to achieve desired assurance

When determining whether the sample provides a reasonable basis for the conclusions about the population as a whole, the auditor should first set a **level of [[tolerable misstatement]]**. If when the results of the test sample are projected for the population as a whole, and any anomalous results taken into account, this level is ::exceeded::, then the sample must be considered as ~not providing a reasonable basis~ for conclusions about the population as a whole.
