The YA2021/22 profits tax return is similar to that of last year.  For AM’s easy reference, I have recapped the key points previously made (see below).  Grateful if you could forward this email to the relevant AMs for their attention.

**A.**     **2021/21 PROFITS TAX RETURN – FORM BIR 51**

**1.**      **Two-tiered Profits Tax Rates**

A group of connected entities can only nominate one entity within the group to enjoy the two-tiered rates for a given year of assessment.  All HK operating entities except HKT Limited have to check “No” to Box 2.3 of the Form BIR 51.

**2.**      **Standard Industrial Classification Code**

Taxpayers are required to state in Box 4.4 of the Form BIR 51 their principal activity by reference to the 6-digit industry code.  The Industry code can be accessed via the following link:

[https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/hkstat/un/class/hsic/search/index.jsp](https://www.censtatd.gov.hk/hkstat/un/class/hsic/search/index.jsp)

![cid:image008.jpg@01D60C3B.C9FAF800](file:///C:/Users/01257542/AppData/Local/Temp/msohtmlclip1/01/clip_image002.jpg)

If a taxpayer is engaged in more than one principal business activity, it should provide only the industry code of the largest principal business.  If a taxpayer does not have any business activity during the year, they should state “000000” as their industry code.

**B.**      **SUPPLEMENTARY FORMS S1 TO S15**

In addition to completing Form BIR 51, certain categories of taxpayers will be required to provide the IRD with additional detailed information, by way of completing and filing certain supplementary forms, numbering from S1 to S15. 

![](file:///C:/Users/01257542/AppData/Local/Temp/msohtmlclip1/01/clip_image003.png)

These supplementary forms will not be enclosed together with the hardcopy Form BIR 51 sent to taxpayers by the IRD.  Instead, you have to download the PDF files from the IRD’s website:

[https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/tax/bus_cpt.htm#sf](https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/tax/bus_cpt.htm#sf)

Then, fill in **electronically** and save the forms in local drive, print the completed forms for signature and submit the **signed hard copies together with the return**.  Please note that the supplementary forms must be signed by the **same person** signing the tax return.

**1.**      **Form S1 – Person Electing for Two-tiered Profits Tax Rates**

For those HK entities which are subsidiaries of PCCW Group, they are not required to submit this form.  In case you have to prepare profits tax return for entities which are not PCCW subsidiaries, please check with Group Tax separately. 

**2.**      **Form S2 – Transfer pricing**

A taxpayer is required to complete this form if **any** of the below three conditions are met during YA2021/22:

(i)                 The company had transactions with non-resident associated persons / other parts of the non-HK resident person; and/or

(ii)               The company has entered into an advance pricing arrangement that covers the company’s transactions; and/or

(iii)             The company belongs to a multinational enterprise group that is required to file a country-by-country report in Hong Kong or elsewhere. 

If condition (i) is met, please complete Section 1 – Transactions with non-resident associated persons / other parts of the non-HK resident person.  Item 1.1 is self-explanatory.  For Item 1.2, please check the relevant box after assessing the FY2021 position of your responsible entities.  A HK operating entity is likely to subject to Local File and Master File reporting if it has presence in two or more jurisdictions **and** its certain parameters exceed the exemption threshold values.  Please find attached an extract from PwC slides (except the footnotes on the first page) which can assist AMs in assessing whether their responsible entities are subject to mandatory documentation requirements.  For further details, you can refer to paragraphs 25-47 of the DIPN 58 on the IRD website ([https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/pdf/dipn58.pdf](https://www.ird.gov.hk/eng/pdf/dipn58.pdf)).  Should you have any questions, kindly seek assistance from your contact point in Group Tax.

**C.**      **Engagement of Service Provider to “Furnish” Tax Returns**

Pursuant to the new law enacted in June 2021, it is only when a service provider **signs a tax return for and on** **behalf of the taxpayer** for submission to the IRD will such an act constitute “furnishing a return”.  Thus, a person engaged by a PCCW entity for undertaking only preparatory work such as preparing profits tax computations and other supporting documents, filling in the return form for the taxpayer’s review, is not a service provider engaged for “furnishing a return” under the new law if the person does not also sign the return for and on behalf of the taxpayer.  Please note that where a service provider “furnishes” a return, the completed Form IR 1476 must be submitted together with the 2021/22 profits tax return.
