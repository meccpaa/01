Please be informed that the new procedure for Request for Payment (RFP) vendor registration will take effect from **1 July 2026**. Email has been sent to Senior Management of BU and FU.

Kindly refer to the detailed guidelines available on the CAP homepage and ensure that all steps and requirements are followed accordingly. For your convenience, the supplier profile creation form is attached for reference.

Please note the following updates to the new procedure:

- **Effective 1 July 2026:** 

- Document required for non-exempt supplier: 

- Business Registration (BR) / Certificate of Incorporation (CI), or
- Alternative document: Bank statement

- If the required documents cannot be provided, BU/FU must obtain additional approval from BB3 or above and provide the justification.

- **Effective 1 September 2026:** 

- Additional approval for non-exempt supplier:

- Payments Unit will obtain approval from the Head of GP&S.
- Payments Unit will obtain additional approvals from the Director of-Group Finance if required document for non-exempt supplier cannot be provided.
- The SLA for new supplier creation is approximately two weeks for non‑exempt suppliers.

Thank you for your cooperation. Please also assist in forwarding this email to all relevant colleagues involved in the RFP supplier profile creation process.
