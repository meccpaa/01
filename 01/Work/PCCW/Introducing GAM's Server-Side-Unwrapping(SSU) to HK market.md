Server-Side-Unwrapping(SSU) feature provide below helps:- 

- Minimize VAST 303 Errors: SSU resolves VAST 303 error responses by sending pre-auction requests to all VAST Tag creatives, ensuring they contain a valid ad response before proceeding.

- Reduce Video Fallback & Wastage: Only SSP VAST Tags with valid ad responses will participate in the GAM auction for dynamic competition. This significantly minimizes video fallback and impression wastage across all line items.

- Boost Sell-Through Rate (STR): With less programmatic wastage, GAM can deliver a higher STR and optimize revenue yield, provided that overall demand (including AdX) is sufficient.

- Reduce Operational Overhead: With fewer VAST 303 errors, the Ad Ops team will no longer need to closely monitor VAST tag campaigns to prevent wastage issues, which often lead to the under-delivery of other adjacent campaigns.

- Proven Market Readiness: Top SSPs like PubMatic, Magnite, and Nexxen are fully integrated, tested, and actively running in our other markets for SSU operations.
