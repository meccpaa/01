Dear Colleagues,

As we continue to embrace AI to boost productivity, lately we have soft launched HKT.AI. Given different AI tools at HKT, please find the guidance below to ensure safe and compliant use.

**What to use and when:**

- **For general daily support, personal productivity, learning, experimentation, and non-sensitive exploratory purposes: you may use HKT.AI.  
    **Employees must not input, upload, process, or disclose confidential, sensitive, regulated, customer-related, personal, or internal restricted information through HKT.AI unless the use case has been formally assessed and approved.

- **For confidential or sensitive work: Always use our in-house tools — Bot Builder or other approved enterprise AI tools where appropriate.  
    **These tools operate under HKT controls, terms and access protections to support approved business use.

**Good practices when using AI tools:**

- Do not enter personal data, customer information, credentials, proprietary materials, source code, commercial information, or other restricted data into AI tools unless both the tool and use case are approved.
- Review AI-generated outputs carefully before relying on, sharing, or using them in business communications or deliverables.
- Follow the Group’s AI usage guidelines, IT security policies, and applicable approval or use case registration requirements.
