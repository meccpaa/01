Dear Frederick and Joyce,

We refer to your various emails, the latest dated 5 January 2023, and our letter to the Comptroller of Income Tax (CIT) for the Years of Assessment (YsA) 2018 to 2021 dated 6 October 2022 whereby the Company have written in to inform the CIT of the inadvertent omission of the Section 19B writing down allowance (WDA) claim on qualifying intellectual property rights (IPRs) and the Company’s intention to revise the YsA 2019 to 2021 tax computation to make the relevant claims under Section 93A of the Income Tax Act (ITA). In this regard, we are pleased to append our scope of work and proposed fee for your consideration and agreement:

Scope of work

1.  Liaising with the company to obtain and clarify on additional information required for the purpose of claiming Section 19B WDA claims on expenditure incurred on qualifying IPRs (i.e. content assets) of the Company;
2.  Revising the tax computations for YsA 2019 to 2021 to claim Section 19B WDA claims on qualifying IPRs, including flowing through of relevant changes to the YA 2022 tax computation;
3.  Preparing the forms for the declaration for the purpose of claiming WDA for IPRs under Section 19B of the ITA;
4.  Submitting the revised tax computations for YsA 2019 to 2022 and the Forms to the CIT on the Company’s behalf upon the Company’s review and approval.

Proposed fee

We estimate our fee for the above scope of work to be **S$8,000** (exclusive of our out-of-pocket expenses, administration and technology charges calculated at 2% of the fees and Goods and Services Tax at the prevailing rate, which would be billed separately).

For the avoidance of doubt, please note that our proposed fee does not cover the following:

-   Attending to subsequent queries that may be raised by the CIT after the submission of the YsA 2019 to 2022 revised tax computations; and
-   Verifying and advising the company on any Notices of Assessment raised subsequently by the CIT.

As you are aware, our fees are generally based on the complexity of the issues involved and the time and effort required of the professionals who will be performing these services. In the event that there is a substantial increase in our time cost due to additional work required in unforeseen circumstances or we encounter situations which require substantially more time to render the services, we will discuss with you and seek your agreement before an additional fee is billed to the Company. Please be assured that we will endeavour to keep our costs to a minimum as this is in the Company's interest as well as ours. The Company's co-operation in providing us with the necessary information will help to keep our cost to the minimum.

We trust that our proposed fee is acceptable to you and look forward to your written agreement to the above fee quote via a return email as soon as possible.

If you require any clarification, please do not hesitate to contact us. 

Thank you.
