## Notes
**New Guideline of Hong Kong Payroll Bank Account**

In accordance with the new banking system update initiative driven by Hong Kong Interbank Clearing Limited (“HKICL”), the beneficiary names would be validated and beneficiary banks may return payments if there is mismatch. 

Under the employment record, your legal name will be used as beneficiary names for payroll payment.  In this regard, please ensure your beneficiary name of payroll bank account updated in Connect are accurate and matched with your legal name. 

If you wish to update your bank account information, please update in Connect with supporting document (e.g. Bank statement) no later than 18:00 pm on 12 August 2022 (Friday), HR Shared Services (HRSS) will approve your changes by 15 August 2022 (Monday).

If you have any questions, please raise a ticket via [Ask HR 2.0](https://hktserviceslimitedhelpdesk.freshservice.com/support/tickets/new).
