Q1.         If we do not restrict the air date in the agreement, as the result, licensee can choose (or control) the start date of the content, in such case, would the revenue recognition date become the content delivery date?

(e.g. license period is from the date start on the platform for ## years, but the content shall not be aired later than ddmmyyyy,

A1           If the licensee can choose to air the content immediately upon the delivery of content by the licensor, yes, the revenue shall be recognized upon the delivery of the content, which is the same date when the content is able to be aired by the licensee.

Q2.         if such contract has limitation on the launch date of the content, but before that the licensee can use the content for promotion (e.g. 1-3 min of the content), and such content could earn ad revenue from e.g. YouTube or Facebook), could we state that the licensee start to get the benefit of the content. Or even if we can book, we can only allocate and book a small portion of the revenue from the licensee?

A2.         In your example, the license contains 2 POs: 1) the right to use the content for promotion (with licence period start earlier); 2) the right to use the full content (with licence period start later).

                You will have to allocate the transaction price according to the Standalone Selling Price of the respective POs.
