**Background of the Tax Assessment:**

**Tax for Non-resident foreign corporations**

General tax implication of income payment received by a nonresident foreign corporation (NRFC) from a domestic corporation.

Section 28(B)(1) of the Tax Code, as amended, prescribes that a NRFC is liable for tax equal to 30% on income derived from all sources within the Philippines. This is in nature of a final withholding tax (FWT).  Thus, NRFC is subject to Philippine income tax (and thus, also withholding taxes) on Philippine-sourced income. **(25% Beginning Jan 1, 2021)**

In addition to the 30% FWT, note further under Section 108(A) of the Tax Code, as amended, prescribes a 12% VAT on the ‘sale or exchange of services’ performed in the Philippines. Thus, income payment received by a non-resident affiliate for services rendered in the Philippines is subject to 12% final withholding VAT (WVAT).

**Megamobile's Case**:

PDI/MM was assessed by the Bureau of Internal Revenue (BIR) for deficiency Final Withholding Value Added Tax (WVAT) for the calendar year 2018 to 2019, 2020 on the ground that PDI failed to withhold and remit the said 12% Final VAT arising from its income payment for services rendered by PCCW SG.
