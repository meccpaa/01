### **14. Other Terms and Conditions**
#### A. Release Date and Windowing

The Parties agree that the Licensed Program shall be released on the below dates and thereafter distributed in accordance with the below windows:

a)     Window :

            i.          Episode 1 and 2 of the Licensed Program shall be released on the Licensed Platform in all of the Licensed Territories on June 23 and 24, 2022, respectively. Subsequent Episodes on the Licensed Platform shall be distributed on the Thursday and Friday of each week thereafter; and

           ii.          Episodes of the Licensed Program shall be released on iWantTFC and TFC IPTV in USA and Canada beginning 16 July 2022

b)     Window 2: Episode 1 and 2 of the Licensed Program to be respectively released by the Licensor on June 25 and 26, 2022 on:

             i.        the Free TV channels _A2Z_ and/or _TV5_, and/or any future free TV channels owned or controlled by Licensor during the Licensed Period; and

            ii.        the Pay TV channels _Kapamilya Channel_, _Jeepney TV,_ and/or other linear television channels owned or controlled by Licensor during the Licensed Period.

Subsequent Episodes shall be distributed on the above Free TV and/or Pay TV channels specified above on the Saturday and Sunday of each week thereafter. In all cases described under this Window 2, Licensor shall ensure that all Episodes of the Licensed Program shall be distributed on the Free TV and/or Pay TV channels described under this Window 2 no earlier than 48 hours after the relevant Episode is distributed as specified under Window 1.

PCCW understands, acknowledges, confirms, and agrees that the windows herein (i) may be exercised by Licensor’s Affiliates; (ii) may be assigned or sublicensed by Licensor to its Affiliates; and (iii) shall otherwise inure to the benefit of Licensor’s Affiliaites which own, operate and/or distribute the abovementioned platforms, stations, and media.

#### B. Original Sound Tracks (OST)

The parties agree that all OSTs to the Licensed Program shall constitute part of the Delivery Materials, and ABSCBN shall deliver the OSTs (and clips showing the ‘behind-the-scenes’ making of the OST) (collectively, the “**OST Materials**”) at the same time that Licensor is required to deliver Episode 1 of the Licensed Program to PCCW, which PCCW may use in its marketing and promotion of the Licensed Program, whether on the Licensed Platform, within the Territories, or otherwise.

#### C. Marketing and Press Release

The parties undertake to carry out their respective obligations relating to marketing and press release and other promotional materials as specified under Schedule D provided that any such marketing, press release and other promotional materials shall always be aligned with and subject always to the prior written approval of Licensor (such approval not to be unreasonably withheld).

#### D. Licensee’s use of Licensed Program and Publicity Materials

a)     When carrying out marketing activities (whether to sell advertising inventories or for VIU PH’s own publicity), Licensee shall also comply with the following sales guidelines (“**Sales Guidelines**”):

	i. Licensee may sell sponsorship titles for advertiser brands, provided that such title sponsorships appear only on VIU PH;
	ii. Licensee may use screen capture or recording from the Licensed Program on/within:
		·   VIU’s digital ads;
		·   VIU’s branded headers;
		·   VIU’s push notifications; and
		·   Clips curated by Licensee

b)     The above Sales Guidelines and each and every implementation thereof, including those in Schedule D, shall be subject always to the prior written approval of Licensor (such approval not to be unreasonably withheld);

c)     Licensee shall at all times comply with the Sponsorship and Sales Guidelines set out by Licensor’s Licensor;

d)     Licensor’s licensor reserves the right to charge Licensee a reasonable fee for the promotional use of the Licensed Program, Delivery Materials, and Promotional Materials.

#### E. Disclosure of Controversial Issues

Licensor shall provide to Licensee full and prompt disclosure of all potential issues (including but not limited to COVID situation on set or of any potential controversial issues) that could damage the reputation of the Licensee and/or VIU PH.

#### F. Morality Clause

ABSCBN represents and warrants that during the Term of this Agreement and five (5) years thereafter, all cast members (lead and supporting members as agreed between the parties) (“**Cast Members**”) shall conduct themselves at all times with due regard to public conventions and morals, and to refrain from any objectionable behaviour. In the event that any Cast Member(s) in the Licensed Program is considered: (i) to have committed any acts and/or omissions considered immoral, deceptive, scandalous or obscene or that would otherwise injure, tarnish, damage or negatively affect the reputation and goodwill of the Licensed Program and/or the VIU brand, or (ii) if a Cast Member in the Licensed Program has committed any act involving unlawful, immoral or ethical issues (including but not limited to dishonesty, misconduct, violence, abuse, theft, any form of misappropriation), or (iii) if the Cast Member has committed any act which could adversely impact the Licensed Program or the VIU brand, PCCW shall have the right to require Licensor to replace the Cast Member which violates this clause with another artist to be approved by PCCW, and reshoot those scenes of the  Licensed Program which included the faulted Cast Member.[[AFE6]](#_msocom_6) 

#### G. Proof of Ownership

Licensor shall provide authorization letter of the Licensed Programs as documentary proof of ownership or control of the Intellectual Property Rights subsisting in the Licensed Programs.

#### H. Licensee’s Exercise of Licensed Rights

The exercise of the Licensed Right under Section 2 (c), (d), (e), (f), (h) of the General Terms, and any other Licensed Right which involve in any way altering the Licensed Program shall be subject to the prior written approval of Licensor and its licensor (either way approval not to be unreasonably withheld and Licensor shall use its best efforts to obtain the head licensor’s approval). Furthermore, Licensor’s licensor reserves the right to charge PCCW a reasonable fee for any such exercise.

#### I. Licensor’s Licensor Take Down Rights in Licensed Territories:

If Licensor’s Licensor is able to sell the format adaptation rights in the Licensed Territory pursuant to Licensor and CJ ENM’s international syndication arrangement, there will be no takedown of the Licensed Program from the Licensed Platform in the Licensed Territory provided that PCCW ensures that the Licensed Program as exhibited in the Licensed Territories are revised and reverted to English subtitles within the first six (6) months from premiere of such separate local version in the Licensed Territory.[[AFE7]](#_msocom_7) 

#### J. VIU Naming Rights

VIU has the right to brand the Licensed Program as a “VIU Original Adaptation” on the Licensed Platform in the Territories. Licensor shall ensure that the “VIU Original Adaptation” name accompanies the Licensed Program for all distribution of the Licensed Program anywhere in the world (including by Licensor in its distribution of the Licensed Program on the Window 2 Exploitation Media (specified under Schedule B) and by CJ ENM in all of its syndication), in all cases in accordance with regional branding guidelines advised by PCCW from time to time.

#### K. OBB/CBB Credits

Licensor shall ensure that the Licensed Program contains OBB (opening bill board) and CBB (closing billboard) credits to PCCW and PCCW personnel who have worked on the collaboration under this Agreement, with the specific OBB and CBB credit language to be confirmed by PCCW.

#### L. Continuity of Content

PCCW  has right of first refusal to license for exhibition any future derivatives of the Licensed Program, with future production costs not exceeding 30% more of initial production cost.  Pursuant to this, should Licensor desire to license to any third party the exhibition of any future derivatives of the Licensed Program, Licensor shall give PCCW written notice describing the terms and conditions of any such intended transaction and PCCW shall have the right to accept the same on the terms stated in the notice in writing within thirty (30) days from receipt thereof.

#### M. Quality Checks

Licensor shall keep PCCW informed of the production status (including but not limited to all details related to pre-production, principal photography and post-production of the Licensed Program, cast, production team personnel, set, look and feel, episode outline, script, pilot episode, weekly episodes etc) of the Licensed Program, and allow PCCW sufficient opportunity to confirm its satisfaction of all foregoing items same pursuant to Item 9 of these License Terms.  

#### N. Applicable Law

The applicable laws, regulations, codes, guidelines, referred to in the General Terms and throughout this Agreement, including but not limited to 10.2(k), (l), 12.1(d) shall only mean the applicable laws, rules and regulations, codes, and guidelines of the Philippines. For avoidance of doubt, PCCW shall be responsible for obtaining all necessary permissions, clearances, authorizations and complying, and shall obtain and comply with any and all appropriate and necessary regulations, codes, and guidelines with respect to the Licensed Program and the exhibition thereof within the other Licensed Territory(ies) and shall hold Licensor free and harmless from any claims or losses arising from any such failure to obtain and comply thereto. 



### **15. PCCW Revenue Share**

A. It is acknowledged by the Parties that it is PCCW’s interest and intention to secure the Licensed Rights to the Licensed Program for the Licensed Platform worldwide for and in exchange of the License Fee stated in Schedule A hereof and that due to existing contractual obligations of Licensor, PCCW has instead agreed to limit the Licensed Territory as enumerated herein for the same License Fee, in consideration of PCCW’s receipt of the revenue share below.

PCCW shall have the right to receive fifty percent (50%) of Net Revenues from Licensor (“PCCW Revenue Share”). “Net Revenues” shall mean gross revenues from any and all promotion and syndication of the Licensed Program worldwide, less CJ ENM Partner Share and less applicable taxes, actually received by Licensor.

“CJ ENM Partner Share” shall mean fees, commissions, revenue share of Licensor’s head licensor under Licensor and CJ ENM’s relevant international syndication agreement.

B. Reports: Semi-Annual. Licensor shall submit a certified annual statement of revenues received during the semi-annual period preceding each report not later than thirty (30) days after the end of June and December of each calendar year. The PCCW Revenue Share due shall likewise be paid and remitted upon submission of said annual statement.
