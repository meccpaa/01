Dear Frederick, Joyce and Kathy,

We refer to our Microsoft Teams discussion on 12 August 2022 and the following documents issued by the Comptroller of Income Tax (CIT):

1.  CIT’s letter for the YsA 2018 to 2021 dated 10 August 2022;
2.  Notice of Additional Assessment (Notice) for the YA 2019 dated 8 August 2022;
3.  Notice of Amended Assessment for the YA 2020 dated 8 August 2022;
4.  Notice of Amended Assessment for the YA 2021 dated 8 August 2022; and
5.  Notice of Original Assessment for the YA 2018 dated 4 January 2019.

Based on our preliminary check with the Inland Revenue Authority of Singapore (IRAS) officer, the unabsorbed losses carried forward from the year ended 31 December 2017 of S$1,589,448 have been disregarded on the basis that there was no previous objection lodged against the YA 2018 Notice. The YA 2018 Notice have disregarded the aforementioned unabsorbed losses on the basis that the Company’s registered business activity was that of an investment holding company in the Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA) records. This is notwithstanding that the Company have recently submitted the YA 2021 NOA objection letter dated 28 January 2022 clarifying that the Company’s unabsorbed losses from the various years, including the year ended 31 December 2017, should not have been disregarded on the basis that the Company is in fact a normal trading company and not an investment holding company. The Company have also since corrected its principal activity in the Accounting and Corporate Regulatory Authority (ACRA) records.

In view of the above, the Company may make an application for relief in respect of error or mistake under Section 93A of the Singapore Income Tax Act to request for the IRAS not to disregard the unabsorbed losses carried forward from the year ended 31 December 2017 of S$1,589,448 given that the Company was previously incorrectly assessed as an investment holding company by the IRAS. Accordingly, an objection will also have to be lodged against the Notices for the YsA 2019 to 2021 as well. Please note that objection to a Notice, if required, must be lodged with the IRAS within two (2) months from the date of Notice. Otherwise, the assessment will be considered as final and conclusive. In this regard, the due date to object to the above YsA 2019 to 2021 Notices is 7 October 2022 (Friday).

In view of the above, we will be pleased to assist the Company to draft the Section 93A application and objection letter, and we append below scope of work and proposed fee for your consideration:

Scope of work

1.  Verifying and advising on the Notices for the YsA 2019 to 2021, all dated 8 August 2022;
2.  Preparing the application for Section 93A and the objection letter against the Notices for the YsA 2019 to 2021 for the Company’s review; and
3.  Submitting the objection letter to the CIT on behalf of the Company upon review and approval by the Company.

Proposed fee

We estimate our fee to be **S$4,000** for the above scope of work, excluding administrative and technology charges calculated at 2% of the proposed fee, out-of-pocket expenses and Goods and Services Tax chargeable at the prevailing rate, which will be billed separately. Our fee is payable within 30 days of the billing date.
