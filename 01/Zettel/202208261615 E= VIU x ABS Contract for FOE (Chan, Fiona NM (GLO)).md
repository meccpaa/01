Hi Fiona and Arthur,

We’ve received feedback from ABS re FOE contract. They’re proposing that we execute two (2) contracts. Please see details below:

**Contract 1: Licensing Agreement**

We’ll stick with the existing draft we have for content licensing and just include the following terms:

1.       VIU to have exclusive worldwide rights to digital/ OTT

2.       In the event that this exclusivity will not be followed, VIU will be given revenue share for all international syndication (this is where the $32,000 will fall under)

3.       Syndication to be driven by CJ and/or ABS

4.       Fine if ABSCBN will draft and turn around the document, but please note:

1.       Under a licensing arrangement:

1.       ABSCBN is the party that has acquired a license from CJ to adapt the KR version of FOE and to use the PH version of FOE produced by it – it is a licensee of CJ.

2.       ABSCBN would license its rights in the PH version to PCCW. PCCW is therefore a licensee of ABSCBN and a sub-licensee of CJ.

3.       Between ABSCBN (as licensor) and PCCW (as licensee), ABSCBN retains the rights to the PH version of FOE. As such:

1.       It does not make sense for PCCW as the licensee to acquire exclusive worldwide rights and license the same back to ABSCBN in respect of, amongst others, US and Canada. It would make sense if PCCW acquired worldwide rights and license US and Canada to ABSCBN’s affiliate entity, but this would entail a separate licensing agreement.

2.       Given that we don’t want to involve a separate ABSCBN entity and we don’t want to draw up a separate licensing agreement, the amount of US$2.6m and US$32K should be netted off and what we will pay under this agreement should be US$2.568 million.

1.       The amount to be paid by PCCW as a licensee would be called the ‘License Fee’. However in this case, US$2.568million is not solely for the acquisition of the content. It is additionally for:

1.       The right to receive a revenue share from the exploitation of the content via syndication driven by CJ and/or ABSCBN.

2.       CJ to provide marketing commitments as agreed.

1.       What is really happening now is that PCCW is paying the amount of US$2.568million in exchange for 1) acquisition of content, 2) right to receive rev share from exploitation and 3) CJ’s marketing commitment – in substance, this is no longer a License Agreement, but a co-investment/collaboration agreement.  

1.       This being the case, ABSCBN can turnaround the draft as they wish will make amendments to the agreement to make it a co-investment/collaboration agreement.

1.       Unless you specifically bring up this point with ABSCBN, you may expect that, by proceeding with the current form, ABSCBN will keep the term of the agreement to 5 years (because that is the license period and typically License Agreements only lasts as long as the license period) and PCCW’s right to receive rev share from syndication will end after 5 years.

They will input these proposed terms in the draft contract we’ve emailed them and will send it back to us for review.

**Contract 2: ‘Appointment’ Contract**

This will be provided by ABS where they appoint VIU to take the lead for brand integration. This is where the $5,000 one-time fee to CJ will be reflected.

Kindly see attached template for review.

1.       Arthur and I agreed that this is fine, although the agreement characterizes PCCW as a marketing sales agent of ABSCBN and it therefore does not really make sense for a marketing sales agent to have to pay a fee (US$5,000) to ABSCBN.

2.       The template agreement states that the relevant agreement will be executed by ABSCBN directly – please therefore expect that PCCW will not sign with the brands directly and account a rev share back to ABSCBN; all deals will be carried out in the reverse. Please expect that PCCW’s topline will reduce because we will only be able to book the amount received by ABSCBN rather than the amount we would receive from the brand owner.

3.       The template agreement also states that we are entitled to invoice ABSCBN for the amount received by ABSCBN rather than the amount that they have signed under the deals with third party. This is fairly typical of arrangements where we are paid a rev share – ABSCBN is not required to pay us unless they have been paid so we have to bear our share of bad debt as well.
