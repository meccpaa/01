Pursuant to the Revenue Code, net tax losses carried forward from accounting periods no more than **five** years before the current year are allowed to be treated as tax-deductible expenses for corporate income tax computation purposes. As a result, the Company’s accumulated tax losses carried forward from FY17-FY21 ==could be deducted against taxable profits for the year 2022==.

**Note :** In worst case scenario, if written agreement/sufficient evidence (proving it has real commitment to make payment) are not available in the event of a tax investigation, tax authority might assess the Company to treat loans as taxable income and submit the CIT additional filling in each respective year. As a result, if the Company has tax liability after adjust such deemed income from loans in the relevant year, the Company would be ==subject to 1.5% surcharge of tax shortfall per month but capped at the amount of the tax shortfal==l.
