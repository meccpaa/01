## Notes

Background Viu TH team and BEC Multimedia Co., LTD, a company who operates Thailand’s
first commercial free-to-air television station “Channel 3”, mutually agreed to broadcast Viu Original Series with advertisement airtime in exchange for the carriage and advertisement airtime fee of THB 10,500,000.

Since Channel 3 is one of the biggest and most famous TV station in Thailand, the purpose of the deal is to get an exposure of the content to mass audience on Free TV, as well as utilizing and selling 10-minute TVC spot airtime per each broadcast episode (total of 700 minutes).
