**Fiona’s Remarks**

**Extract Reference from Opinions issued by the Regulator**

Distribution of Content

Not Allowed.

For the avoidance of doubt, licensing activities (ie, content acquisition and licensing content to a licensee) are permitted, provided that PCCW OTT PH’s control is only limited to quality control (i.e. provision against improper splicing of the content, the use of the VIU watermark, the prohibition of use of content for any advertising campaign). Other obligations which are construed as mass media activities (eg, creating dubbing in Tagalog) shall be done by a mass media Licensee.

Because distribution of content must be carried out by 100% Filipino-owned company:

(i) MegaMobile, Inc. is the operator of the VIU PH platform;

(ii) Philippine Daily Inquirer, Inc. owns all advertising inventory on the VIU PH site, sells them to advertisers and distributes the advertisements on the VIU PH platform; and

(iii) PCCW OTT PH is the owner of the VIU PH platform.

In respect of licensing, PH Counsel has seen our syndication agreement with GMA Philippines and confirmed that our rights and obligations therein are compliant with PH laws.

Distribution by itself is  part of mass media activities as stated in SEC opinions:

“’…mass media’ which involves not only the transmittal but also the creation/publication, gathering and distribution of the news, information, messages and other forms of communications to the general public...” ||

Production and Distribution of a Film

Not Allowed.

For the avoidance of doubt, licensing activities (ie, content acquisition and licensing content to a licensee) as explained above are permitted.

Commercial and operational reasons aside, PCCW OTT PH is therefore required to engage a 100% Filipino owned to carry out this activity.

Attys. Marievic G. Ramos-Anonuevo,, SEC Opinion, [April 18, 1996]

Production of Programs for Film and Television

Not Allowed.

Commercial and operational reasons aside, PCCW OTT PH is therefore required to engage a 100% Filipino owned to carry out this activity.

Exerting any control/influence over the content (story, direction, camera work and editing) to be produced by a Local Production Company

Not Allowed.

For clarity, editorial suggestions are permitted if the local production company is not strictly required to observe or has the ultimate control over editing.

The Program Production Agreement for K-Love contains a section on Editorial Condition, which has been tweaked for compliance.

Mass Media; Nationalized Activities; Alien Director, SEC-OGC Opinion No. 08-11, [March 3, 2011]

Editing content during Post-Production

Not Allowed.

The Program Production Agreement for K-Love contains a section on Editorial Condition, which has been tweaked for compliance.

SEC Opinion No. 11-08, dated 03 March 2011 addressed to Atty. Nelson G. Leyco

Retaining an Artist

Allowed.

Allowed with conditions – see right hand column.

This may be allowed as long as the Foreign entity does not gain creative and corporate control over the production and production company, respectively (note the SEC Decision of In re Rappler, Inc., SP Case No. 08-17-001)

Financing a Production

Allowed.

Allowed with conditions – see right hand column.

Buying Equipment for Production

Allowed.

Allowed with conditions – see right hand column.
