## Notes
_Requirements and procedures for deregistration_

A Hong Kong private company  may apply to the Registrar for deregistration if it meets the following requirements pursuant to Sections 750(2) and 750(3) of the Companies Ordinance (Cap. 622):

(a)     all the members agree to the deregistration;

(b)     the Company has not been in operation or carried on business during the 3 months immediately before the application;

(c)   the Company has no outstanding liabilities;

(d)     the Company is not a party to any legal proceedings;

(e)     the Company’s assets do not consist of any immovable property situate in Hong Kong; and

(f)      the Company has obtained a “Notice of No Objection to a Company being Deregistered” (“**Notice of No Objection**”) from the Commissioner of Inland Revenue.

Normal procedures for deregistration of a Hong Kong private company:

1.       The company’s property has to be dealt with before making an application for deregistration since upon dissolution of the company, the company’s property, if any remaining, shall be vested in the Hong Kong government as bona vacantia.

2.       Obtain approval of all members on deregistration.

3.       The company must first apply for a written Notice of No Objection from the Commissioner of Inland Revenue if the company has satisfied all the following conditions:
	(a)      the company has already ceased business;
	(b)     the company will not resume business in the future;
	(c)      the company has disposed of all trading stock, landed property and securities, if any;
	(d)     the company has no outstanding tax liabilities;
	(e)      the company has no outstanding obligations under the Inland Revenue Ordinance;
	(f)      there are no unanswered enquiries from the Inland Revenue Department (“IRD”); and
	(g)     there are no unsettled objections or appeals in respect of assessments already raised.

4.       An application for deregistration in the specified form should be delivered to the Registrar within 3 months from the date of issue of the Notice of No Objection by the Commissioner of Inland Revenue, together with the required fee.

5.       On receiving an application for the deregistration, the Registrar will publish a notice of the proposed deregistration in the Gazette.

6.       If no objection to the deregistration is received within 3 months after the date of publication of the notice, the Registrar will deregister the company by publishing a final notice in the Gazette declaring it to be deregistered on the date of publication of that other notice.

7.       The applicant or the person nominated in the application will be notified upon deregistration of the company.

8.       A company is deregistered on the date of publication of the final notice under item 6 above. A company is dissolved on deregistration.

9.     Inform both the IRD and the Business Registration Office within one month of the cessation of business.

Normally, the whole process of deregistration takes about 5 months, excluding the processing time required for the issue of Notice of No Objection by the Commissioner of Inland Revenue (the normal processing time is within 21 working days from the date of lodgement of a valid application).

Until the company is deregistered and dissolved, the company is still required to observe its statutory obligations under the Companies Ordinance.

_Fees for deregistration_

For deregistration of a Hong Kong company, fees payable to the Companies Registry and the IRD are HK$690.
