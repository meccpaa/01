We have incorporated the updates on OTT 2023 AOP since last week. Based on this latest version, 2023 revenue is projected to be $308m (+22% YoY) while FCF before tax and working capital worsens YoY to -$178m, which is also far from the Canal plan.

In order to narrow the gap, we have looked at content and marketing cost which are the biggest cost items that move the needle. The growth of Viu’s marketing cost is already contained at 4% in 2023 (on an apple-to-apple basis adjusted for partnership cost). Based on the current version, total content cost (incl. both capitalized cost and COS) amounts to $214m, which is $19m more than 2022. Moreover, regional syndication revenue is projected to decrease from $18.9m in 2022 to $4.5m in 2023 as we haven’t budgeted for any S grade title in 2023.

Given the above, our management team have proposed the following

1.  **Contain 2023 content cost budget (inclusive of capitalized and expensed content cost) to no more than $190m**, i.e. a $24m improvement vs current submission
2.  Not to launch new Africa markets, which will have a $2m revenue impact but $1m cash savings

The above changes will improve total FCF by $25m to $153m, which is what we will submit to the Group today (see column shaded in yellow). While we will still have the flexibility to change the line items in between, we’ll need to work out a plan which sticks to -$153m FCF before the 3 Jan presentation.

-          Local acquisition: budgeted to increase by $11m YoY. What is the incremental return by acquiring more local content? Any room to further optimize the spending?

-          Content COS: what’s the performance of short-form/dubbed content? Any room to further optimize the spending? E.g. ROI of using an average voice actor vs a star or voice actors with a particular accent?

Welcome any additional ideas to help improve the bottom line. Thank you.
