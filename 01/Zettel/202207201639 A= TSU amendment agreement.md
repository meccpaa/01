**What's the contract term?**

1.3 This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and continue until the Parties’ full reconciliation of payments in accordance with Clause 3.4 hereof (both dates inclusive, the “Term”).

3.4 The reporting obligation of each Party for the Term in relation to payments to the other Party under this Agreement and the process of reconciliation for such payments shall be as follows:
	a) PCCW shall deliver a monthly statement to TSU, the first statement to be on 30 November 2021 and each subsequent statement within thirty (30) days after the end of a calendar month, detailing the following amounts, if any, for the preceding period since Effective Date (for the first statement) and the preceding period since the last statement (for subsequent statements):
		(i) Production Fee;
		(ii) Sponsorship Revenue;
		(iii) Sponsorship Procurement Fee;
		(iv) Syndication Revenue;
		(v) Syndication Sum payable to TSU;
		(vi) Other Revenues;
		(vii) Other Sum payable to TSU; and
		(viii) Total monthly amount payable to TSU (“Total Monthly Amount I”).
	b) TSU shall deliver a monthly statement to PCCW, the first statement to be on 30 November 2021 and each subsequent statement within thirty (30) days after the end of a calendar month, detailing the following amounts, if any, for the preceding period since Effective Date (for the first statement) and the period since the last statement (for subsequent statements):
		(i) Rights Fee;
		(ii) TVOD Revenue;
		(iii) TVOD Sum payable to PCCW; and
		(iv) Total monthly amount payable to PCCW (“Total Monthly Amount II”) .
	c) Within fourteen (14) days of the later Party’s receipt of its monthly statement or the last statement after the expiry or earlier termination of this Agreement from the other Party, the Parties shall reconcile Total Monthly Amount I and the Total Monthly Amount II and determine which of PCCW and TSU is the paying Party during the relevant month. The paying Party shall pay such reconciled amount to the other Party within sixty (60) days of such reconciliation. For the avoidance of doubt, this Clause 3.4 shall survive the expiry or earlier termination of this Agreement.
