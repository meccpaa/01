## Tasks

1.  **Mobile App Security Enhancement**

As the Information & Technology Security Policy has been issued by GRM&C in May, some security enhancements have been made on Connect (SuccessFactors) Mobile App to further improve your user experience. Effective on **3 July, 2022**, the following changes will be in place: 

1.  For **Mobile App Password Complexity**, your new password must contain all the followings:

-   a minimum of 1 English upper-case letter [A-Z]
-   a minimum of 1 English lower-case letter [a-z]
-   a minimum of 1 Number [0-9]
-   a minimum of 1 Non-alphanumeric character [~`!@#$%^&*()-_+={}[]|\;:"<>,./?]

-   Passwords must be at least 8 characters in length, but can be longer

2.  Upon **5 failed login attempts**, the app will be locked and you’ll be required to submit a ticket in SAP Services Portal for re-activation.

3.  **Biometric Authentication** is now available to make your login process more convenient. You can activate the fingerprint and face authentication on your mobile device.

2.  **System Maintenance Notice**

Please be informed that we will conduct regular system maintenance for connect, please find the details below:

1.  **“Time-Off”** function will not be available from 30 June, 2022 (Thursday) at 18:00 HK time to 6 July, 2022 (Wednesday) at 23:59 HK time. Hence any holiday/leave application submission shall be done offline and to be resubmitted after the system resumes. Other functionalities will remain normal.

2.  **Connect site** will not be accessible from 1 July, 2022 (Friday) at 18:00 HK time to 3 July, 2022 (Wednesday) at 23:59 HK time

We apologize for any inconvenience caused. For further assistance, please contact [your Business Unit HR Representative](https://hr-intra.pccw.com/wordpress/your-hr/meet-your-hr-members/).
