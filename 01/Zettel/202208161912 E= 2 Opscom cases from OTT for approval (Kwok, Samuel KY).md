**1) Vivo Partnership deal**

This is the 2nd deal with Vivo (one of the top handsets in Indonesia) by Viu Indonesia riding on a successful deal in 2021. In this deal, Viu will commit to pay USD1.75M for pre-installation of the Viu app on 5 million Vivo handsets with a unit cost of USD0.35.  In return, Vivo will comit to pay Viu for advertising and sponsorship of USD1.225m.  So the net cost to Viu is USD0.525m with all these 5 million handsets installed with Viu app.  We would derive a profit margin of USD0.13m or 6.6% after considering the potential platform revenue of US$0.67m through these devices.  The deal will help to boost the MAU and subscriber number.  It will also enable us to block the other players (Netflix, WeTV and Disney+) as a pre-load partner with Vivo in the Indonesia market.

**2) Simulcast & library content acquisition**

It is a regional bundle deal with US$3.07M with Korean broadcaster SBS to acquire (a) 2 simulcast variety shows ("All the Butlers" and "Mom’s Diary", total 156 episodes) and (b) 19 top performing library titles (422 hours) as well as Running man (40 episodes).  The unit cost for the 2 simulcast variety shows is US$6k-8k per episode while the unit cost for library drama is US$4.5k per hour.  It is a reasonable price and within our budget.  Without committing to acquire a high content volume with high content costs like in 2019, we are now handpicking and selecting suitable productions for Viu platform according to the cast, genre, storyline and production crew. This move will help to improve our efficiency on operation cost.  This series of library dramas will also help to maintain a healthy volume of Korean content on Viu.  The license period is 2 years with the VOD rights in most key SEA / MENA markets of Viu.
