## Notes
Dear Khun Arthur,

Thank you for your clarification krub.

According to the question no. 1 and 3, we would like to clarify that under Thai laws, the capital increase must be done in THB only and the bank confirmation letter shall also confirm the amount in the Company’s bank account in THB. Therefore, the capital shall be transferred into THB bank account.

In addition, we need to put the amount in THB on the notice calling for EGM and minutes of EGM approving the increase capital and registration documents and we cannot wait until the date of the fund transfer to know the THB amount. 

Please also note that the amount of increased capital must also be divided by the par value of each share at THB 5 to specify the amount of new issued shares.

Therefore, please try to provide us the amount in THB for the increase capital.

If the actual fund transferred after the EGM date is higher than the THB amount specified in the EGM documents, you can discuss with your accountant how to book such excess amount in the Company’s accounting book.
