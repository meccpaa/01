## Notes
Vuclip and Mytel entered into a service agreement dated 4th June 2020 to make available Viu Premium to Mytel's customers at no additional subscription charges when Mytel's customers access the Viu app on Mytel's mobile network or through Mytel's app called MyID ("Service Agreement*). The parties then signed a First Amendment on 30th December 2020 to amend the invoicing format and signed a Second Amendment on 29th January 2021 to change the unit price and revenue share followed by a Third Amendment to extend the term of the Service Agreement and change the unit price. Further to this, the parties signed a Fourth Amendment on 17th March 2022 to revise the unit price and commercial tax as per the latest regulation by Myanmar Post and Telecommunications Department ("PTD").

The parties are now entering into a Fifth Amendment to extend the term of the Service Agreement and to revise the currency exchange rate used for the conversion of MMK to USD.

The payment terms stated in Article 3.4 of Annex 1 of the Service Agreement is deleted in its entirety and replaced with the following:

"Vuclip shall invoice the Company for payments due under this Agreement in Myanmar Kyats and the Company shall make all payments to Vuclip in United States Dollars by bank transfer to Vuclip's bank account using the exchange rate published by the Bank for Investment and Development of Vietnam ("BID") on the last day of the applicable service month for which payment is due ("Relevant Exchange Rate"). The Company shall provide Vuclip with a screenshot of BID's webpage (www.bidv.com.mm) showing the Relevant Exchange Rate, or such other supporting documentation as approved by Vuclip from time to time, together with the Billing Report for that service
month."

Prior to the Fifth Amendment, the Parties used the exchange rate published by the Central Bank of Myanmar.

There are no changes made to the pricing & distribution.

Clause 10 of the Service Agreement on indemnification and limitation of liability is unchanged:
a. Each Party agrees to indemnify the other Party against any and all claims, actions, costs (including reasonable, outside legal costs) damages, demands or expenses brought against, suffered or incurred by either Party as a result of any breach or non-observance by the first-mentioned Party of any of its representations, undertakings, obligations and/or warranties contained in this Agreement.
b. In no event will either Party be liable to the other Party for any incidental, indirect or consequential loss or damage, damage for loss of use, data, profits or business opportunities or failure to achieve cost savings, in contract, tort or otherwise, arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, even if the Party has been advised in advance of the possibility of such losses, costs or damages.
c. Save in the case of any breach of the confidentiality provisions in this Agreement, either Party's total aggregate liability to the other Party under this Agreement shall in any event be limited, in respect of all claims arising hereunder, to the total amount of the US$700,000.

The revenue share between the Parties ("Revenue Share") shall be calculated as follows:

- Vuclip Net Payout (before WHT) = Net Data Revenue x Vuclip Revenue Share Percentage
- Vuclip Net Payout (after WHT) = Vuclip Net Payout (before WHT) x (100%-C)

	- Net Data Usage = Data Usage - Free Data. (Maximum Free Data = 30% of the Data Usage).
	- Free Data means any free data, data that is gifted, uncharged data, or any illegally obtained data.
	- Data Revenue = Net Data Usage x Unit Price
	- Net Data Revenue = Data Revenue x (100%+B) / (100%-A%)

In which:
Vuclip Revenue Share Percentage = 15%
A: 4% (Universal Service Fee + Annual Regulator Fee)
B: 15% (Commercial Tax)
C: 2.5% (Withholding Tax)
