## Notes

Hi Arthur, 

We would like to seek your advice regarding the bulk pins revenue recognition.

Based on the previous years, bulk pins revenue is being declared based on the number of activated pins all throughout the year. This is the basis of recognition for campaigns recorded both in PH and SG. 

However, last April 2022, OTT PH has been informed by the auditors that the bulk pins revenue can be recognized from the time that we have delivered the bulk pins to our clients. As we no longer have any obligations to clients after the delivery of pins. In this regard, all bulk pins revenue in PH is being recorded outright from the time it was delivered to client. 

Upon review of prior years' bulk pins-related revenue, there are six campaigns affected by this revenue recognition. In line with this, we would like to inquire if we're going to apply the same revenue recognition in PH for those campaigns recorded in CVD3. 

Thank you.

Regards,
