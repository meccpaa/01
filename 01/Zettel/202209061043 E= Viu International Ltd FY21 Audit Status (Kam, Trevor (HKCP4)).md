**Note 3 (c) Useful lives and amortization pattern of intangible assets (other than goodwill)**

The Group has significant intangible assets (other than goodwill) and is required to estimate their useful lives and amortization patterns in order to ascertain the amount of amortization charges for each reporting period.

The useful lives are estimated at the time of purchase of these assets after considering viewership trend, future technology changes, business developments and the Group’s strategies.  The Group performs annual reviews to assess the appropriateness of the estimated useful lives. Such review takes into account any unexpected adverse changes in circumstances or events, including declines in projected operating results, negative industry or economic trends and rapid advancement in technology.  The Group extends or shortens the useful lives according to the results of the review.

The amortization patterns are estimated based on the expected consumption of their economic benefits over their useful lives. The Group performs annual reviews to assess the appropriateness of the estimated amortization pattern. Such review takes into account any expected changes in consumption pattern over the useful lives of the intangible assets (other than goodwill).  The Group adjusts the amortization pattern according to the results of the review.
