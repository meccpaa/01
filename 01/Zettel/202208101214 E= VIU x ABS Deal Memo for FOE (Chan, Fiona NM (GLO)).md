That line has been revised based on the following:

1.  They have the authority or license to promote Flower of Evil in the Philippines

What the above is trying to say is that each of ABS CBN and PCCW has the authority or license to promote the Adaptation. My logic behind the revision is:

-   Whether and who has the authority is a confirmation that should be given by ABSCBN alone. We do not have any direct relationship with CJ (head licensor/owner of IP) and have not seen the license documentation between CJ and ABSCBN, so the confirmation should not come from PCCW.
-   ABSCBN is, firstly, confirming that it has rights to exhibit and promote the Adaptation.
-   ABSCBN is, secondly, confirming that it has the right to grant to PCCW the right for PCCW to exhibit and promote the Adaptation.
-   I understand the right to exhibit and to promote the adaptation is worldwide and not only in the Philippines, thus revised it to “worldwide”.
