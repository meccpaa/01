### A Free Man

I believe I can to fly... I believe I can touch the sky…

When I was young, I wanted to fly freely like a bird... but one day I fell…  

I fell in love with Joyce crazily in my secondary school. When I saw her back, I wanted to see her front; when I saw at her outside, I want to look in… into her inner heart. But I was just too shy and looked at her far away. I was afraid I cannot give her any happiness… I was afraid of bearing this responsibility, so I didn't take any action and let it go...

Rejected without taking any action.  

I want to love and do something, but still don't want to take any responsibility.  

I was very sad… so I went to cinema…

at there I met Stephen Chow, at that time he looked like this…
…. 
everyone I am the Saint of Gambling. Don't be sad Arthur, let me teach you one spell and you can FREE … Look carefully, "You can't see me, you can't me, you can't see me…" 

Amazing! can you see me? of course not, I don't have the supernatural ability like him, but when he was disappearing, an idea came up in my mind… what if I can hide my feeling to her, then I can give my love freely and don't need to take any responsibility! 

To hide my intention, I just need to make everything ambiguous…

Later on, I met Carol. In a Christmas, I did not only prepare a wonderful gift to her. But, at the same time, I also prepared another 2 gifts to 2 other girls…  

Another time, she was sick and I picked medicine for her from Clinic and told her you are more beautiful when you are wearing mask… 

So every time I do something good to her, I would also do something to blur my intention. 

I could give my love freely, until years later, she got a boy friend…  
  
I was upset … so I went to cinema...
 
I met Stephen Chow again… this time he transformed into Monkey King. 
Yo Arthur, don't worry, let me to help you. Take this box, it's called pandora box. You just need to yell "BoYeBoLoMe", then it could bring you to anywhere you want… 

Since Love always made me sad, I decided to get away from love... and yell "BoYeBoLoMe".  
It brought me to far east of HK, in an university department called Mechanical Engineering. At there I really able to focus on my study… I aced all the subjects, got scholarship… and didn't experience any love anymore. 

However, time passed… I lost my passion and enthusiasm in everything I did.  

It seems that something was missing… 

I gradually came to realise that not taking any action, hide it, or run away from it, would never give you true freedom. The true freedom is to face what I really love. And do what you really love to do. 

So I made a call to Carol immediately and said I love you… then I heard "thank you very much for your message, I am her husband"... 

So do what you really love … now
